The baupla fuzykamni (Language Planning Commission), also known as the ((BPFK)) or ((byfy)), is a standing committee with the charge of improving and maintaining formal descriptions of Lojban, elaborating undocumented and under-documented language features, resolving inconsistencies and errors in existing documentation, and responding to issues that arise in usage. It was initially formed as a result of the ((Official Baseline Statement|Baseline Statement)) of 2002, and ((BFPK Reauthorization|reauthorized)) in 2015.

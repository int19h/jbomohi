__Mumble__ is a program frequently used for voice communication in Lojbanistan. It is free software that runs on Windows, OS X and Linux and provides encryption and good sound quality via speex. To get started, install the [http://mumble.sf.net/|Mumble client] and connect to a server listed below.



It is a good idea to announce your presence on Mumble to the community, perhaps on ((Lojban IRC|IRC)) or other ((Lojbanic Forums|Forums)), either in advance or when you're ready to start chatting.



In fact, some lessons are given by means of Mumble, inquire in #ckule to get in on them.

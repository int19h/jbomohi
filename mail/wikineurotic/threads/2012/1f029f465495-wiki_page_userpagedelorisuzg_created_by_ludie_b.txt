Good or bad, the airport will always signify the starting of a long journey.

And any traveller will say that this time is a time filled with overwhelming emotions like fear, nervousness, trepidness and also gladness and excitement.

Each airport provides a rare experience, and this experience is felt by every single passenger who awaits 

their next flight. This is why airports need to be sure that the awaiting passengers have the ideal experience while waiting for the flight.

With the bad experience one has in the airport, the possibilities of having a great start will most likely be zero, but it would be the opposite of one was able to 

get a great airport experience.

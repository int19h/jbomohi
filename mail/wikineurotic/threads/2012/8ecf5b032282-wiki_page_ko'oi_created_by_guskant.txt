;ko'oi:((BPFK Section: Text Structure Discursives|UI6)) discursive: imperative (((Experimental cmavo|experimental cmavo)) proposed by guskant.) 

Broader term than ((BPFK Section: Irrealis Attitudinals|au, a'o, e'o, e'u, e'a, ei)).

"ko" is a short form of "do ko'oi". 



* Examples:

** do .onai ko'oi lo mi speni co'a morsi (as a translation of "あなたが死ぬか夫が死ぬか、どちらか一人死んでくれ". [http://www.aozora.gr.jp/cards/000879/files/179_15255.html|Original text in Japanese.]) 

** ra ko'oi citka lo brioco (as a translation of "Qu'ils mangent de la brioche." [http://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Les_Confessions_(Rousseau)/Livre_VI|Original text in French.])

** lo ko'oi se platu be do ku te snada (as a translation of "Puissent vos projets réussir !")

** mi ko'oi dandu janai jifsku (as a translation of "Que je sois pendu si je mens.")

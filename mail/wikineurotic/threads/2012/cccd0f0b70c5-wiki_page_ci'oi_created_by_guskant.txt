Psychomemes (proposed by ((tijlan)).) 



Converts following cmevla or zoi-quote into psychomeme. Narrower term than ((tai'i)). See also ((BPFK Section: Case sumtcita|ci'o)), ((sa'ei)), ((ki'ai)).



* Examples in some languages. 

** jpn: そわそわ (sowasowa): restlessly.

** nep: सुखदुख (sukhadukha): lit. happiness-sorrow; the human condition.

** eng: zing

There are 3 major kinds of pool heaters which differ according to the source of energy they require to do the job - gas pool heater, electric or heat pump heater and solar pool heater.

There are various aspects to consider when choosing the suitable heater for you.



Solar power Swimming Pool Heater



As its name indicates, this kind of pool heater makes use of the heat of the sun to heat up your pool water, which your pool pump provides to the solar pool heater, usually positioned in a row on the roof. You can set the pump to work throughout daylight. A solar pool heater provides heat all through the year and is ideal for recreational swimming.

It generally will cost you approximately $1000 up to about $5000 with installing priced at between $500 and $2500.





The benefit of utilizing solar pool heater to warm your pool water is always that it involves much less expense to operate as it solely depends on solar energy. The expenditure you should have will only be caused by the electricity which is used to run the pump.







Electric Swimming Pool Heater



If you like to take a dip on the swimming pool, whether its hot or otherwise, electric pool heaters are perfect for you. This specific heating system can also be perfect for houses which have no readily available structure to install a solar heater on.

Electric pool heaters work by gathering the heat from the air after which displacing it onto the water. Having said that, electric pool heaters can also be regarded as partly solar because of the fact that drier climate make the system more effective. Electric pool heaters may cost from less than $2000 to as high as $5000.

The costs for installing it will cost you from $350 to $1000. Basically, you will be billed about $250 to $500 annually when you use this type of swimming pool heater.



Gas swimming pool heater



People who just use pool heaters for a brief period may find this heater best. Even though it is highly efficient, gas-operated pool heaters may cost more to function. This specific heater is more advantageous for instances such as having another house, as a backup heater, or a spa heater.





Regardless of the high operating expenses from $1k to $1.5k yearly, gas-fueled swimming pool heaters would be the least expensive kind of heating system that cost you around $1k while the installment costs go for about $350.

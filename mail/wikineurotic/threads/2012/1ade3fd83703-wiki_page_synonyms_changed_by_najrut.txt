''In popular literary acceptation, and as employed in special dictionaries of such words, synonyms are words sufficiently alike in general signification to be liable to be confounded, but yet so different in special definition as to require to be distinguished.'' --G. P. Marsh.



----

It would be nice to provide each of the gismu clusters below with an advice on good usage

----

__se papri | cukta | se tcidu | datni__



[http://groups.yahoo.com/group/lojban/message/9717|A recent proposal] by ((Nick Nicholas)): ''se papri < cukta < se tcidu < datni''



The proposal is retracted, but the fine semantics of these gismu is obviously something that should be made explicit ''(and probably has been :-( : [http://balance.wiw.org/~jkominek/lojban/9402/msg00081.html])''



*Is a blank notebook a ''cukta'', a ''se tcidu'' or a ''datni''?

*Is a pictures only book a ''se tcidu'' or a ''datni''?

*Is a text of random words a ''datni''?



----

__klama | muvdu | litru__



''klama'' has all the places of ''muvdu'' plus x5 for vehicle, hence ''klama < muvdu''.



''litru'' lacks places for destination and origin, hence ''klama < litru'' __surely this should be the other way around? mi'e filip.__

----

__nelci | se pluka | prami| se dirba__



''prami'' differs from others in greater degree of emotional attachment. By the way, is there any difference between ''prami'' and ''se dirba''?



''se pluka'', compared to ''nelci'', has the place x3 'under conditions'. is there any reasonable way to make it work?

*there is also ''melbi'' which is similar to ''pluka''. We need to do more thorough work on this cluster --najrut

----

__drata | frica__



roda rode zo'u le du'u da de frica cu nibli le du'u da de drata

ijeku'i le du'u da de drata na nibli le du'u da de frica

i mu'a ''lo djacu dirgo na frica lo drata''



''drata'' = 'another' (not the same one), opposite to ''mintu''

''frica'' = 'different' (not of the same kind), opposite to ''dunli''



see [http://groups.yahoo.com/group/lojban/message/1954



----

__mintu] | dunli__



''mintu'' = same, identical, opposite to ''drata''

''dunli'' = of the same kind, opposite to ''frica''

----

__pikta | jaspu__



Are the following ''pikta'' or ''jaspu'' (or neither):

* foreign passport

* driver's licence

* a university's diploma

* marriage certificate

* credit card

* ''other borderline examples?''

----

__molki | fanri__



The gismu definition list says: '''molki'', unlike ''fanri'', need not produce a product'. However, even the prototypical grain mill for ''molki'' can be argued to produce flour from grain. Maybe ''molki'' puts more stress on processing/treatment rather than producing something radically different. But where does the border lie?



Are the following ''molki'' or ''fanri'' (or neither):

* grain mill

* foundry

* oil station

* hairdresser's saloon

* tattoo studio

* tailor's workshop

* ''other borderline examples?''

----

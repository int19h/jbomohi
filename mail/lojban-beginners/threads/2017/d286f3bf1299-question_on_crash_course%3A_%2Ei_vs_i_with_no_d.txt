I'm just starting to learn Lojban, using the Crash Course. I came across 
this:

*pa mlatu cu pinxe i le mlatu cu taske*
*There is a cat that is drinking. The cat is thirsty.*

The course has introduced *.i* as a sentence separator, but not a lone *i* without 
the leading dot. When I tried this on the lojban parser 
at https://lojban.github.io/ilmentufa/glosser/glosser.htm, it appeared to 
be accepted as valid Lojban, so I guess it's not a mistake.

What are the rules here? Is the dot not always required in front of the i 
sentence separator?

Forrest

The dot is implied, basically. It represents a glottal stop, but the
location of glottal stops are pretty obvious, so it becomes one of those
things which don't technically have to be written, but help with visually
parsing.

  It is not.  The . is an indicator of a glottal stop or pause.  Said pause
is mandatory in speech (before a word starting with a vowel, after a "la",
"lai" or "doi", and after any word ending with a consonant), and the dot is
there to remind you of it.  It also provides a nice visual break in long
blocks of text, but if you are putting spaces in between words, you don't
need it.  For example, the utterance you wrote can also be written
pamlatu cupinxe.ilemlatu cutaske and the parser would still understand it.

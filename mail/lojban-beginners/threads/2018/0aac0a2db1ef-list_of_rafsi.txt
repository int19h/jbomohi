  Well,there are several places.  First off, you could have looked in
jbovlaste itself.  You will note that in the second drop down box "Search
by", there is a "search by rafsi" option. Also, you can use the other
dictionary, vlasisku.lojban.org, which will look for rafsi or cmavo, or
break down words for you, all without needing special prompts.

  But if you just want a list
http://www.lojban.org/publications/wordlists/rafsi.txt

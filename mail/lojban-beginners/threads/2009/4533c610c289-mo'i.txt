> Why is "mo'i broda" not allowed?
.skamyxatra

"{mo'i}" is used to modify a FAhA {cmavo} to indicate that the {bridi} is
moving in the direction of the FAhA rather than simply being located according
to the FAhA.  Grammatically, "{mo'i}" may only appear before a FAhA {cmavo},
possibly followed by a NAI and/or VA.

mu'omi'e .kamymecraijun.

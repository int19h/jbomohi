I've been looking at the attitudinals (which are rad, btw), and have a
question about pei (!).

In CLL, it talks about using pei on its own to ask something along the
lines of "How are you?" and "How do you feel?" and that it can be
tagged to the end of other attitudinals to ask in someone feels a
specific way.

But can you use pei with a bridi to ask how someone feels about
something?  E.g., pei la djan. klama?  "What do you think about John
coming?"

Herc and I have been wondering, how would one say "Jedi Master"?

The dictionary claims noljedai to be defined as "x1 is a Jedi of power /
level / rank x2"

However, nol is a rafsi for nobli, which seems to be the closest word we
have for "master" in Lojban, so effectively noljedai already means "Jedi

Or would one say "nobli noljedai"?

"noljedai" is a type-4 fu'ivla, so what "nol" is a rafsi of is irrelevant. 
So "nobli noljedai" is like "matching matchcoats". A type-3 fu'ivla consists 
of a rafsi ending in a consonant, a hyphen-letter "r" or "n" or "l", and a 
part of foreign origin beginning with a consonant and ending with a vowel.

Hello everyone,

I'd like to ask—seeing that written cmavo are often clustered and  
Lojban is intended to be unambiguous in speech—is whitespace  
completely optional and ignorable in written Lojban (with the  
exception of dipthongs)? For instance:

   la mari,as. ninmu .i dunda fi la .alis. fe le cukta .i ta blota .i  
ti cmalu be le ka canlu bei lo'e
   ckule be'o nixli be li mu bei merko be'o bo ckule la bryklyn. loi  
pemci la mela nu,IORK.
   prenu la jecta. ni'o

    
lamari 
,as 
.ninmu.idundafila.alis.felecukta.itablota.iticmalubelekacanlubeilo'eck
    
ulebe'onixlibelimubeimerkobe'obockulelabryklyn 
.loipemcilamelanu,IORK.pren
   ulajecta.ni'o

   lamari,as.ninmu.idun
            dafila.alis.felec ukta.itabl ota.iticmalubel ekacanlub ei  
lo'eck
         ul eb e '  onix libelimubeim
      erkobe'obockulelabryklyn.loipemcila  melanu,IORK.pren
    ulajecta    .      ni'o


The latter two are a lot harder to read for humans, of course, but are  
they still valid Lojban, assuming that I didn't make a typo? (I've  
been trying out writing like this in a journal lately to force myself  
to carefully look at and parse what I write. It also makes it easier  
for me to read the text aloud, simulating the stream of sounds that  
I'd say or hear.)

No. Word breaks are ambiguous (I don't have an example handy) unless  
you either use " " or "." between words, or mark the penultimate- 
syllable stress on brivla. That is:

"lamari 
,as 
.NINmu 
.iDUNdafila 
.alis 
.feleCUKta 
.itaBLOta 
.itiCMAlubelekaCANlubeilo'eCKUlebe'oNIXlibelimubeiMERkobe'oboCKUlelabryklyn 
.loiPEMcilamelanu,IORK.PREnulaJECta.ni'o"

(or with nonstandard accent stress marks:
"lamari 
,as 
.nínmu 
.idúndafila 
.alis 
.felecúkta 
.itablóta 
.iticmálubelekacánlubeilo'eckúlebe'oníxlibelimubeimérkobe'obockúlelabryklyn 
.loipémcilamelanu,íórk.prénulajécta.ni'o")

Also, you have an unnecessary "." between "jecta" and "ni'o".

>  lamari,as.ninmu.idun
>           dafila.alis.felec ukta.itabl ota.iticmalubel ekacanlub ei  
> lo'eck
>        ul eb e '  onix libelimubeim
>     erkobe'obockulelabryklyn.loipemcila  melanu,IORK.pren
>   ulajecta    .      ni'o

This is not permitted. Words may not be broken by space. It could be  
made an unambiguous but nonstandard form by including space.

To clarify: You may not insert spaces or pauses into the middle of words.
Whitespace between two words may only be omitted if the first word is not a
{brivla} or its emphasized syllable is clearly marked.  Thus, you can omit
spaces between {cmavo}, {cmene}, and {cmavo} & {cmene}, and you may prepend a
{cmavo} or {cmene} to a {brivla} without any ambiguity (to experts of Lojban,
at least; omitting spaces before {brivla} is virtually never done), but if you
append anything to a {brivla}, that {brivla}'s emphasis must be marked.  Also,
mandatory pauses (e.g., after {cmene} or before words beginning with vowels)
must *always* be written as a period, though a period next to whitespace is
technically redundant and can be omitted without any loss of ambiguity (though
I'm not sure whether such practice is officially valid or not).

Here are some examples:
"lo jbojbe na lojbo jbena lo jbojbena" consists of three parts differing only 
by spaces in writing, and by stress in speech.
"le krataigo" means "the hawthorn". "lekrataigo", if "le" is stressed, lexes 
as "lekra tai go", which is meaningless, but is part of the syntactically 
valid sentence "mi lekra tai go do gi ko'a".

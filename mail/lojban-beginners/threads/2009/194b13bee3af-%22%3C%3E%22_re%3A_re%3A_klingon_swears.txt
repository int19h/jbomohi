>

I see.  Before Perl, the "diamond" operator ("<>") was used in languages 
like Basic to mean "is not equal to".  It's still used that way in MS Excel 
and Open Office Calc filters.
So it really is the same thing as Perl "!=".  The Perl usage of "<>" is the 
'non-standard' one.

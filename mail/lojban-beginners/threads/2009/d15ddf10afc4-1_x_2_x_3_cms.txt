That doesn't parse, and I'm not sure how to fix it. This does parse:

centre li pa lo'o fa'u li re lo'o fa'u li ci lo'o lo sraji ku fa'u lo pinta ku 
fa'u lo condi

The "ku", and probably the "lo'o", are not necessary with camxes, which 
doesn't have the LALR restrictions. "li pa fa'u re fa'u ci" also parses.

Since the original doesn't say which dimension is which, I'd just say "centre 
li pa fa'u re fa'u ci". BTW, "cms" is incorrect; it should be "cm".

According to CLL14:11:

"Forethought termsets also exist, and use ``nu'i'' of selma'o NUhI to
signal the beginning and ``nu'u'' of selma'o NUhU (an elidable
terminator) to signal the end. Nothing is inserted between the
individual terms: they simply sit side-by-side."

So I thought "li pa lo sraji" and "li re lo pinta" and the other could
sit side-by-side without "pe'e". The book also gives this example:

11.7)    mi klama nu'i ge le zarci le briju
        nu'u gi le zdani le ckule [nu'u]
    I go [start termset] both to-the market from-the office
        [joint] and to-the house from-the school
        [end termset].

No "pe'e". Or does this have to do with the connective being
forethought (whereas "je" in my example is afterthought)?

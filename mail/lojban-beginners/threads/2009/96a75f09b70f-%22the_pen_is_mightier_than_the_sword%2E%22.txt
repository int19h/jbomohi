"{lo pinsi cu zmadu lo xarci loka vlipa}" -- "The pencil* exceeds the weapon in
power."

That wasn't so hard, was it?

mu'omi'e .kamymecraijun.

* Unlike in English, "{pinsi}" ("pencil") is the more generic form of "{penbi}"
  ("pen").

y-syllables are not counted when determining penultimate stress:

 In counting syllables, however, syllables whose vowel is ``y'' or which
 contain a syllabic consonant (``l'', ``m'', ``n'', or ``r'') are never
 counted.
(http://dag.github.com/cll/3/9/)

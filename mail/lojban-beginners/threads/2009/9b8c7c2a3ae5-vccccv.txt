That's the damskrima bug. If I understand it, vlatai doesn't consider syllabic 
consonants to be syllabic if they're next to a vowel, and it doesn't consider 
a brivla valid if it has four non-syllabic consonants in a row, even if all 
the pairs are valid initial (e.g. matstraka).

Actually I thought of "tai" first, but "ko bajra tai lo nu da'i lo tirxu cu 
jersi do" doesn't make sense. An event of a tiger chasing doesn't have the 
form of a runner. "ko bajra tai lo se jersi be lo tirxu" does make sense, 
though.

x2 of tamsmi is the form. Abstractions don't run, but they can be forms, so 
does this make sense? "ko bajra setai lo nu da'i lo tirxu cu jersi do"

Having looked at the place structure of "tamsmi", I think "ko bajra tetai lo 
se jersi be lo tirxu" is better than "ko bajra tai ...".

My personal preference in those situations has been "ko bajra sepa'a
lo selje'i lo tirxu" -- "run, like one being followed by a tiger",
although it seems that xorxes generally prefers tai in those
situations.  Now, of course, I genericized the person being chased,
not specifically as YOU would, so if you wanted to me more literal,
perhaps, "ko bajra teta'i da'i lo nu lo tirxu cu jersi do" (notice
this isn't the same as Pierre's, as I'm using teta'i, not tetai)
--"Run, with a theoretic condition of the method that a tiger is
chasing you")

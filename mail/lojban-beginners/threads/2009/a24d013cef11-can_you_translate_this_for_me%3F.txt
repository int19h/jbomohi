Not to be churlish, but this is why I am uncomfortable with the shortcut {lo
broda pe ko'a} == {lo ko'a broda}. In the bridi above, you're modeling {*ro
do seldju} after {lo do seldju}. But when you do that, {do} actually binds
with {ro}, & the bridi is ungrammatical. So you need {ro lo do seldju} or
{ro seldju pe do}. And perhaps {ro seldju be do} is better, if you're not
trying to make a distinction with the previous utterance.

Also, -dju- is the rafsi for {sidju}; all those {seldju} should be
{selju'o}. [ /rant ]

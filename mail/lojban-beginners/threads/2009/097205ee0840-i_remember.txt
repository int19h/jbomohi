The first is "morji lo du'u broda"; the second is "morji lo nu broda".

mi morji le du'u lo djacu cu ki'orgrafi'ulitce* li pa
I remembered that water is 1 kg/l (when doing a wet soil problem)

mi morji le nu cadgau le toknu
I remembered to turn the oven off

mi morji le li'i litru la razgug.
I remember traveling to Brazil

jbofi'e thinks that "cadgau" means "to play". jvocu'adju says that the lujvo 
of "cando gasnu" is "cadgau". Trying "candygasnu" or "tolcadgau" shows 
that "cadgau" means "to make idle"; where does "play" come from?

*CLL and the gimste disagree about what "fi'u" is the rafsi of.

Pierre

I might say "mi morji fi le nu mi cusku lu coi ro do li'u", I
remembered something (implied: that it was important and that I should
do it) about the event of me saying "coi ro do".  That's ambiguous
though as to the relationship between the remembering and the saying.
Perhaps "mi cusku lu coi ro do li'u ki'u le nu morji", I said "coi ro
do" because of remembering, that's slightly clearer.

I also think it's a good opportunity for some specific lujvo.  There's
two good rafsi for "morji", "moj" and "mo'i".  We can base a lot of
different interesting kinds of remembering on "-mo'i"!  I'd like one
that means "to suddenly remember something you had forgotten"--
perhaps za'e "sukmo'i"?  Anyway, for remembering to do something, hmm,
"mojgau" is taken, so perhaps za'e "mojyzu'e" (morji zei zukte)?  Or,
oh, hey, looks like Pierre came up with "zukmo'i" for just this in
2002!  So then it would be, "mi zukmo'i le nu mi cusku lu coi ro do
li'u".


mi'e la stela selckiku mu'o

The difference between "mojyzu'e" and "zukmo'i" would be that the
latter's x2 is basically an idea while the former's basically an
action. So, if by "I remembered to say 'hello everybody'" you mean "I
remembered and did say 'hello everybody'", "mojyzu'e" would be more
accurate.

Hey there, folks! I'd like to ask all those who are attending Lojban
Festival at Penguicon (in 27 days) to think about what preparation you
can do in those 27 days to contribute to the fun.

Currently the highlights are a panel discussion about Lojban, and an
hour during which only Lojban may be spoken in that room. After a
certain time of the evening, there will be no more sessions in that
room, and it will be available exclusively for Lojbanists. I have been
offered a complete audiovisual recording kit, including camera,
microphones, sound mixer, a Twinpact for recording from a laptop
screen, and a digital capture laptop running Debian with software for
switching between feeds during recording.

What about more? Can someone make a set of la cibyska cards? Does
someone have a Nanofictionary set they can bring?

Do we want to do something foodlike together?

Does anyone want to man a Lojban promotional exhibit? Can someone
bring banners?

At the last Lojban Festival, there was a mural of signatures and
Lojbanic writing which is on a paper roll-- is someone bringing that?
Can someone bring more paper to expand the roll?

Would anyone be willing to translate the subtitles of "Call of
Cthulhu" into Lojban so we can show that?

Is there anything I can do for you as Conchair of Penguicon? I'm
incredibly busy, but I can try.

-Eppcott

coi rodo,

The Penguicon schedule is now finished (ready for its latest version
to update to the website Real Soon Now). Lojbanists have one of the
rooms on the Atrium all night starting at 9PM, both nights, and
lasting as late as desired. So, those of you who are attending have a
little more time to put together additional presentations if you would
like. There is a projector in the room.

Personally I'm the most excited about the documentation we can do for
podcasts and videos.

Who all is attending? Perhaps we can form a mailing list. So far I
have heard of (to the best of my current knowledge) Robin, tene,
bancus, phma, Bruce, and Jen. Clsn, how about you? komfo.amonan?
lojbab? Nora? JCowan?

-Eppcott

The selma'o seem to have non-Lojbanic names like "BAhE". How does one  
refer to a selma'o in Lojban, short of quoting?




=== 2 | 2009-08-20T00:27:39Z | Luke Bergen <lukeabergen@gmail.com> | 5715b9300908191727m76a14476x3d648cc3999c5564@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-beginners/cur/1250728059.069521b43bece3a2.jbomohi:2,S
you could do something like {lo selma'o be zo ba'e} which I think is saying
something like "the structure word class of "ba'e" " thus you're referring
to the selma'o class "BAhE" by saying "that cmavo class that contains the
word "ba'e" which is certainly lojbanic

Not sure you can do it without using some kind of quote word like {zo}
etc... though

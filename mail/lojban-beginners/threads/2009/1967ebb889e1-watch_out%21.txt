Aware by what means?  The point of the evidentials is to describe *how* you
know something, and saying that you know what you are saying is obviously
redundant.  If you do want to emphasize your awareness and make it the focus of
the sentence, though, there's always "{sanji}."

mu'omi'e .kamymecraijun.

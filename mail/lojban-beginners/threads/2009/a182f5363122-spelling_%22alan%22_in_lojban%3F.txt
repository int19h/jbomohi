I am reading through the level0 brochure, and I think I have
gathered enough information to spell my first name (Alan) in
lojban.

Given that cmene cannot have the word "la" in it[1], and reading
over the pronunciation guide[2], I think the closest spelling and
pronunciation in Lojban of "Alan" is "Alyn.".

This accounts for the stress of the first syllable instead of the
last, and sounds about right when I pronounce it: "/AH,luhn/"

How did I do?  Is there a closer approximation?

Thank you!

  You did fine.  My brother (who passed away about a year ago) was also
named Alan.  Assuming you are American, then the first "a" sound in your
name is normally pronounced like the "a" in cat, a sound which does not
exist in lojban.  Therefore, yes, you have to compromise.  You could go with
either "a" or "e" for the first vowel.

.skamyxatra

LA can take either (a) a sequence of one or more {cmevla}, or (b) a {selbri}.
"{dy dy ry}" is neither.  Section 17.12 of the CLL describes how {cmene} for
acronyms can be created; under that system, DDR would be "{dydyryr.}."

"la" expects a following string of cmevla or selbri. "dy dy ry" is 
neither. "me dy dy ry" is a selbri, so "la me dy dy ry" is valid. "dy dy ry" 
is also a valid sumti, so you could say "mi kelci dy dy ry".

> I'm trying to jbosku "This is several times more difficult than that".

>
>  ti la'u li so'o nandu zmadu ta

I think "se la'u", because "li so'o" is the se klani, not the klani.

>  ti fo li so'o nandu zmadu ta

I would say the ve zmadu is the difference in difficulty between ti
and ta, rather than the quotient.

>  ti me li so'o nandu zmadu ta
>
> I also ventured
>
>  ti nu'a pi'i li so'o nandu semau ta
>
> But this one jbofi'e won't parse. Whai is the problem?

"nu'a pi'i" is a tanru unit, so what you have is like "ti broda li
so'o nandu semau ta"

You need "ti nu'a pi'i be li so'o nandu" or "ti fi'o nu'a pi'i li so'o
nandu", depending on what you meant.

mu'o mi'e xorxes

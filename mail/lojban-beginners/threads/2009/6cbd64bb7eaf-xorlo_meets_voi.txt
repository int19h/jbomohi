Well, "voi" is extremely rarely used.  I occassionally say something
like "mi nelci lo prenu voi xrula" = "I like people who are flowers."
So I use it as if it's syntactic sugar for poi+pe'a, basically.  I
don't know if that's how other people think of it, because I never see
anyone else say it.  I'm not super attached to my interpretation, if
someone has a better one. :)

Succint explanation:
(1) "lo" is the only member of selma'o LE that you need, and "la" the
only one from selma'o LA.
(2) Never ever use "lo'e", "le'e", "lo'i", "le'i", "la'i".
(3) Avoid "loi", "lei" and "lai".
(4) Use "le" only if you are absolutely convinced that's what you want.

You may also want to look at:
<http://www.lojban.org/tiki/tiki-index.php?page=BPFK+Section%3A+gadri+as+of+25+Dec+2004>

I will leave the succint history to someone else.

mu'o mi'e xorxes

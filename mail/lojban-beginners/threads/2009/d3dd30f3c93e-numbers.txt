No. It's grammatically correct, as are such phrases as "pa pi pai ra'e ro", 
but isn't a proper number unless the base is at least 12. It's 
like "eleventy-seven" or "capítulo diecisiete, versículo diecionce" (the 
latter is my mishearing of a Bible citation which is actually two verses, 10 
and 11).

Can you figure out what number "cira'epi" is? It is valid, but in a kind of 
mathematics you may not know about.

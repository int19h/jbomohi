This is just my opinion:

   1. Get a dictionary. Either create it on jbovlaste, or else download from
   http://public.me.com/yoavetali (get ld.pdf) It's a little nicer
   formatted, and I update it every month.
   2. Read through "Lojban for Beginners", and actually do the exercises in
   writing.
   3. After that, try to translate some stuff. Start with simple things,
   like Dr. Seuss or songs (don't worry about rhymes). You'll always have to
   look things up, and that's how you learn.
   4. Reading also helps, and there's some translated stuff on the
   lojban.org website. It is tiresome at first when you have to look up
   every other word.

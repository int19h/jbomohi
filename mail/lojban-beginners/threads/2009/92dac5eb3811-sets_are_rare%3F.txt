Hey everyone.
Several times I have encountered the claim that *lojban* sets are rarely
used. This appears strange to me, for the following reason. Certain *gismu* ,
such as *cmima* , *simxu* and probably others *expect* a set for an
arguments. For instance, I was trying to translate into *lojban* "We teach
each other". The result was

*lu'i mi'o simxu le nu ctuca*

The *lu'i* is needed here to transform the mass *mi'o* into the set needed
for *simxu* . Without it, the meaning would be something like "each of us is
a set the elements of which teach each other".

So, why *are* sets rarely used?

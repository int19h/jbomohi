On Mon, Jun 8, 2009 at 12:50 PM, tijlan<jbotijlan@gmail.com> wrote:
> Is it "za'u re me'i mu broda" or "za'u re je me'i mu broda"?

The first one is grammatical. The second one (with the current
grammar) should be "vei za'u re .e me'i mu [ve'o] broda". The
terminator "ve'o" can be elided.

There is no "official" interpretation of the first one, but the
interpretation proposed here:
http://www.lojban.org/tiki/tiki-index.php?page=BPFK+Section%3A+Inexact+Numbers
would be that they are the same.

Looking at the official grammar (and confirming this with the official parser),
I don't think that's a bug in jbofi'e.  It appears that a bare {lerfu} string
cannot have any free modifiers attached to it (though KOhA {cmavo} can), so in
order for the above sentence to work, you would have to explicitly terminate
"{my.}" with "{boi}," the "{boi}" being the part that is actually
(syntactically) modified by "{sei}."

ro roi ku lo prenu cu canci

(Of course you probably don't mean literally all the time, but
hyperbole is as possible in Lojban as in English.)

> but to make sure it
> means what I want, I've been trying to paraphrase it. The paraphrase comes
> out something like: "There are many and frequent occurrences of people

lo nu lo prenu cu canci cu rapli li so'i gi'e cafne

>I also want to capture the fact that it's one disappearance
> per person.

Then you could add:

.i no prenu cu za'u pa roi canci

Or equivalently:

.i ro prenu cu su'e pa roi canci

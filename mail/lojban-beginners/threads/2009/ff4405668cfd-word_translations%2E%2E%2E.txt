> Close (as in "Close the file");

{ga'orgau} - from "{ganlo gasnu}," cause to be closed

> Module (as in "Part/A-part-of");

{pagbu} - x1 is a part/component/piece/portion/segment of x2 [where x2 is a
          whole/mass]; x2 is partly x1

> Search/Find (as in "Search for/Find <something>");

{sisku} - x1 seeks/searches/looks for property x2 among set x3 (complete
          specification of set)

{facki} - x1 discovers/finds out x2 (du'u) about subject/object x3; x1 finds
          (fi) x3 (object)

> Output/Result (as in "The output/results of the process")

{te pruce} - x3 is the output/results of input x2 to process x3, passing
	     through steps/stages x4

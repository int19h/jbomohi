I have translated some of the comics that are in the text section of
lijban.org and I would really love to see more comics translated into
lojban. It's the kind of material that could help us to retain some more
lojbanist.

Exactly to avoid any type of issue, I've asked explicit permission to the
authors. Just for the records I had authors that did not gave me permission
to redistribute the translation and I had to stop working on those.

However, if you are just translating the transcript (i.e. your not scanning
the images replacing the original text with the lojban version) I think it
would be perfectly fine to put it online.
That's the case for the Bernstein bears (no images). Alice is different as
the copyright on the text and the images has expired.

Btw, what manga is it?

what are they? In English we have, for example, Mr., Mrs., Ms., Sir, Ma'am,

well. (Since I'm taking Japanese right now, －さん, －ちゃん, －くん, －さま, etc. come

To put it another way, How do I say "Mr. Llambías" in Lojban?

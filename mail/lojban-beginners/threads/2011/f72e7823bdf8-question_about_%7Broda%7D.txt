about an inner quantifier, which means that we are speaking of all people

Me:

{ro broda} is equivalent to {ro da poi broda}
{ro lo broda} is equivalent to {ro broda}

that {lo}'s default inner quantifier is still {ro}, and this only changes if
there are no quantifiers present at all.

Yes, I'm finally on for a little bit. My assertion is that {ro broda} is 
-not- equivalent to {ro da poi broda} because {ro da} is explicitly 
EVERYTHING. {lo} does not have any default quantifiers under xorlo, which 
means that while {roda poi broda} is literally everything that brodas, {ro 
lo broda} or {ro broda} is all of an implicit number of brodas. {ro da poi 
broda} is equal to {ro lo ro broda} AFAIK.

Am I wrong on any point? Cos the xorlo article contradicts itself, and the 
CLL doesn't feature xorlo-related content.

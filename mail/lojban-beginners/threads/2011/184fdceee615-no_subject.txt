Or "pu benji di'u...", or just "benji di'u" since tense is optional.

Minor stylistic point: I join a cmevla to the following word with a period and 
no space only if the following word is also a cmevla. In some cases I use a 
space even if the next word is a cmevla, such as "la .fort.mil. 
nan.karolinas."

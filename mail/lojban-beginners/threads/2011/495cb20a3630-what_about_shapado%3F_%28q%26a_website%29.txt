I think it would be a good idea to copy all the questions and their 
answers that happen in the lojban IRC channel to Shapado, so that, 
ideally, someday, no questions will have to be asked again.
I'll try to remember doing this from now on, e.g. whenever I answer 
someones question, I will copy their question and my answer and put it 
up on Shapado (and vice versa), and of course encourage others to do the 
same. I just think this will help it grow much faster than having it all 
happen over there on the site, where no one ever seems to visit (so far).
Actually, every question that ever comes up *anywhere*, even if one asks 
oneself and finds the answer using google, it should still be put on 
Shapado for everyone else to profit from it.
If everyone did this, we would have a plethora of questions and answers 
on that site in no time. (tons of questions are being asked daily on IRC 
alone)

(sorry for the crappy phrasing, I just copied this straight from IRC 
where I first made this suggestion)

Any opinions?

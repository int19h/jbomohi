I.e., "more than" or "less than." I can only find out how to do this
with numbers, not with qualities and things like that. An explanation
would be great, but I would like most if someone would translate these
basic sentences:

"The car is bigger than the cat." (For ease: car = karce, big = barda,
cat = mlatu)

"The car is bigger than the cat is small." (Yes, I know this sounds
confusing. Think of it like there is a normal size for objects, and
the car is proportionally farther from that size than the cat is. For
ease: small = cmalu)

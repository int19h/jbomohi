mi'e fagri cipni

lo se lifri be mi be'o ku to'e cinri
My life is boring.

I'm not sure that "be" ... "be'o" is the best way to get the 
reference to "mi"; ie, referring to _my_ life in to the second 
place of "se lifri".

(BTW, this explains why I have such boring translations today.)

la solri ku fadni tarci
Sol/the sun is an ordinary star.

That one I don't think that I have made any errors in, but feel 
free to suggest improvements.

Notice that I have a new policy: The basic "rule" is that 
I avoid "cu" unless it can save me more than 2 terminators.

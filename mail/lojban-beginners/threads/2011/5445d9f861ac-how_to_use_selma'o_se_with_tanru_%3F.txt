Right. {lo mi melbi cmalu zdani} (= {lo melbi cmalu zdani pe mi}) is "My
pretty little house". You don't want se in there at all.

{mi ponse lo melbi cmalu zdani} is "I have a pretty little house".

"My pretty little house" is a sumti, not a bridi.

means roughly "to have a house, somehow connected with being pretty
and being small", so it may mean both "to be pretty little resident"

One way is {melbi cmalu zdani mi}; another (non-standard, slightly
interestingly imprecise) is {melbi cmalu zdani .i mi se go'i}

Also, you should be aware that "pretty little house", i.e. a house which is
both pretty and little, is better expressed by {lo melbi ke cmalu zdani},
while {lo melbi cmalu zdani} is closer in meaning to "beautifully little
house" because {melbi} attaches to {cmalu} first.

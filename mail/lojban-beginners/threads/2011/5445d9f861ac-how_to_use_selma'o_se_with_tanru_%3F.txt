home(s)*)4»3]1 

How can I direcly point "SE" to zdani, not to melbi ?

=== 5 | 2011-12-18T07:08:27Z | Jonathan Jones <eyeonus@gmail.com> | camkwhj3bpxeqqew5j6a1ua+xvqd7ze5zsfpah4iuojuang7mcw@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-beginners/cur/1324192107.202458668cc6928d.jbomohi:2,S
Right. {lo mi melbi cmalu zdani} (= {lo melbi cmalu zdani pe mi}) is "My
pretty little house". You don't want se in there at all.

{mi ponse lo melbi cmalu zdani} is "I have a pretty little house".

"My pretty little house" is a sumti, not a bridi.

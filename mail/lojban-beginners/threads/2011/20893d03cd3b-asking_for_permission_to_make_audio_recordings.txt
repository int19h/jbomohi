=== 2 | 2011-07-10T16:29:38Z | Remo Dentato <rdentato@gmail.com> | caaq0imof-sjeybmdtzlh2pzzz3cep_wgmbz9un8hzh-va9k9na@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-beginners/cur/1310315378.8ca5b540894542d0.jbomohi:2,S
For the children books I've translated, {le lunra jo'u le mapku} and
{le virnu smani blozeile'a} are under Creative Commons [NC-SA-BY] so
you're entitled to do it. I'm sure the authors will appreciate a note
from you if you want to share the recordings with them.

{lo dalpe'o dinsaru} Is copyrighted but the author gave me permission
to translate it as long as it was not for profit and I would not
redistribute it outside lojban.org.

So, for those three, as long as we are within the above license, I'm
sure we're fine.

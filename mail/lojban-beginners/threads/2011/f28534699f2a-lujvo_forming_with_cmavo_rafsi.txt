There are plenty of lujvo for which, if you pulled it apart into a tanru it
would seem kind of funny.  Remember, lujvo are NOT just a shortened form of
tanru.  They are their own definitions.  Using reasonable rafsi to compose
the lujvo certainly makes it easier to get at the meaning if you've never
seen the word before, but "rolcatra" for "omnikiller" seems perfectly
legitimate.

The definition could be {x1 catra x2 po'u roda}

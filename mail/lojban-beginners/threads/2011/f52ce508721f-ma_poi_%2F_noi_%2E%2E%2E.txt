I think "lo karce poi mo" or "lo karce po'u ma" or "lo mo karce" or
"lo ma karce" make more sense. "Which" asks for a restriction, not for
the set to be restricted.

In other words "which car do you use?" means "given that you use a
car, please be more specific about the car that you use", and not "of
all the things that you use, please identify one that is a car".

"poi". "do pilno ma noi karce" I can answer with anything that I use, but if 
it's not a car, you asked the question wrong. "lo karce poi mo" and "lo karce 
po'u ma" and "lo mo karce" are also good; the answers to "mo" will be 
different from those to "ma". "lo ma karce" is "Whose car?".

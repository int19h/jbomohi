I'm open to suggestions. <http://tatoeba.org/eng/sentences/show/700006>

After removing the idiom, I'd say something like, "I'm willing to accept
suggestions."

{mi fitytu'i lo se stidi} is "I am accepting suggestions."

I don't seem to be able to find a translation for "willing".

{tolxanru} - opposite-of willfully resisting
{tolfapro} - opposite-of opposing
{toldarlu} - opposite-of arguing

seem the three best candidates.

That sounds like something I would say after I have heard all the
suggestions and wish to express that I will now go and consider everything
that was told to me.

Although being open to suggestions does not mean that one is committed to
following any suggestions, it would be inaccurate to say if one were
unwilling to follow any suggestions.

Let's see if I can express myself in lojban:

.i broda cei nu la'e zoi .gy. open to suggestions .gy. .i lo nu selgi'a
nupre kei na smuni lo broda .i ku'i su I give up.

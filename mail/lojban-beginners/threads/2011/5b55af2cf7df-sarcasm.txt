As I understand it, it is *verbotten* to use cnivla sarcastically; I recall
reading this somewhere, but I don't remember where.

I've been thinking of {je'unai} as useful for indicating that the meaning of
an utterance is [intended to be] contrary to what is actually said, and I
guess {.ui je'unai} could be equivalent to a very flat "Yay.", even though
the semantic relation between two adjacent cnivla aren't clearly defined
(intentionally).

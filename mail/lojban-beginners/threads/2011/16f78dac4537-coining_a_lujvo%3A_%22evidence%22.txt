In the extremely likely event that there already exists such a lujvo, I'd
like to know it, and what I should have done to find it.

When I think of the relationship described by "evidence", it comes to
something like "x1(du'u) is evidence/suggests that x2 (du'u) is true [under
epistemology x3]".

There is no human component; unless you are in a quantum mechanical
framework, observation does not change the fact.

Evidence is weaker than proof or logical implication.

(While writing this, I realize that {nibyti'i} almost means what I want, but
I'm not sure if {lo nibyti'i} can be used to mean "evidence").

.skamyxatra

The dotside only affects the morphology of *actual* {cmevla}; under the
proposal, they get to freely contain "la", "la'i", "lai", and "doi", but every
{cmevla} must be preceded by a mandatory pause.  "{selmacygu'e}" does not end
with a consonant, and so it is not a {cmevla}, no matter how many pauses you
put around it.  It is a {lujvo} being used as a {cmene}, and since "{la}" takes
either a {cmevla} string or a {selbri}, the "{xanri}" forms a {tanru} with
"{selmacygu'e}" and is thus absorbed into the {cmene} along with it; thus, you
are referring to something named "{selmacygu'e xanri}."  To fix this, insert a
"{ku}" and/or a "{cu}" before "{xanri}."

mu'omi'e .kamymecraijun.

=== 5 | 2011-01-06T19:54:43Z | Michael Turniansky <mturniansky@gmail.com> | aanlkti=y1cmw7t6vavua-_fvksfv_fqeusxxj-g3nyfu@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-beginners/cur/1294343683.6f1912c784bd27d0.jbomohi:2,S
  It has nothing to do with dotside.  Regardless of dotside.  Extra
interword pauses are always allowed.   When a brivla is used as a
cmene, if it's followed by another brivla, they will form a tanru....
both "la blabi" and "la blabi ractu" are valid cmene.  In order to
prevent it, you have to terminate the la (it terminates with ku when
it consists of a selbri, as here) either explicitily, or put in a cu
to terminate it implicitly.

                --gejyspa

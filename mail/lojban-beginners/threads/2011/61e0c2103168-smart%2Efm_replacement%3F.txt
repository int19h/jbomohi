=== 2 | 2011-02-10T13:11:25Z | Oren <get.oren@gmail.com> | aanlktimsohgixrsj5i0ledx3bm9bbsq71xrrffpdcabs@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-beginners/cur/1297343485.0021ebe020139135.jbomohi:2,S
Others have mentioned others, but the best alternative I've found is Anki.

Main draws:

   - Lot's of free-to-download decks already available (including a gismu
   and a cmavo deck)
   - FOSS, cross-platform, and with an online and mobile version
   - Supports audio & images, and robust fact/template system
   - Easy to import/export decks as text files

Main drawbacks:

   - No way to "pause" for vacations, so cards can build up fast
   - No built-in multiple-choice function like smart.fm (yet!)
   - The audio feature seems buggy in my Ubuntu version

co'o

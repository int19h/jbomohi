Hmmm, I think there's an implicite "main bridi" lurking in the sentence
"even I wouldn't do that".  The implication is "X is so
revolting/unappealing/whatever THAT I wouldn't do it (despite the fact that
such things normally don't bother me)".  So using a more concrete example
for WHY "even I wouldn't do it"....

I wouldn't eat that = {mi na da'i citka ta}
Even I wouldn't eat that = {ko'a citka xlali sela'u lo nu mi noi so'a cidja
nelci ku'o na citka}

On Tue, May 31, 2011 at 10:28 AM, Michael Turniansky

>  Except that you have to specify (or know by some external means)

Who said anything about making "ro da poi broda" = "no da poi broda"?

What I said was that "ro da poi broda cu brode" can be expanded to "ro
da zo'u ganai da broda gi da brode".

Similarly "su'o da poi broda cu brode" can be expanded to "su'o da
zo'u ge da broda gi da brode".

And the negations of those two:

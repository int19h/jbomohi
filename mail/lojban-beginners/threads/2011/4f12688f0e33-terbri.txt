For "mi bu'u lo panka cu dansu", "bu'u" may be said to add an argument
place to "dansu" such that this selbri can now express a relation
between "mi" and "lo panka" in the same bridi. Would then "lo panka"
be considered a terbri, along with "mi"? More precisely:

lu mi bu'u lo panka cu dansu li'u cu bridi lu bu'u dansu li'u zo mi je
lu lo panka li'u


mu'o mi'e tijlan

            (term (sumti (LA-clause

                           (CMENE-clause (cmene "post"))))))))

The portion of the grammar that parses this line, sumti-6, says in
part:

  LA-clause free* relative-clauses? CMENE-clause+ free*

The '+' after the CMENE-clause being relevant.  That is the same in
both camxes[1] and jbogenturfahi[2].  I assume camxes has a bug
higher up than the grammar causing this clause to parse incorrectly.

1: https://github.com/lojban/camxes/blob/master/lojban.peg
2: http://bugs.call-cc.org/browser/release/4/jbogenturfahi/trunk/gerna.peg

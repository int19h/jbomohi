All four of those are names for issues of the LLG journal/newletter. 
The first couple of issues were under title (4), and the title is in TLI 
Loglan, since this was 1986-1987.  The third issue was per title (3), 
again in TLI Loglan since it was 1987.  Starting with issue 4 in 1988, 
the journal used title (2) which is in Lojban.  Publication continued 
until 1993, by which time I was juggling too many Lojbanic balls while 
newly parenting 2 kids.  Starting with issue #8, we took the short 
"news" portion of the journal, and published it separately as le lojbo 
karni - title (1).  Something like 120 people got the journal and 
400-500 or so got the much shorter newsletter.

You can download all of the issues unformatted from
http://www.lojban.org/files/jl/

At one point, I had a contact at the LoC who was interested in Lojban, 
and who collected the issues I sent for submittal to the library.  When 
I found out, I supplied him with all the missing issues.

More recently, I believe that copies of our two books were submitted to 
the LoC, so I don't know why they don't show up.

lojbab

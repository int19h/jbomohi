Let's pretend for the moment that it is possible to talk to a computer in
Lojban and be understood by it as a jbopre would understand.

Let us further pretend that we want to test this computer's responsiveness.
Say, add "2+2" 12 times and print the results on the monitor.

I came up wuth: {doi skami ko rapygau lonu makau sumji li re li re kei li
gai gi'e ciska lo jalge be la'edi'u lo vi vidni}.

Comments/suggestions?

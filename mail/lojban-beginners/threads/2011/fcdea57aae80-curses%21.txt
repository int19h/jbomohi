Is it just me, or is this group rather slow of late?

Well, in part to spark up some conversation on here, and in part because
I'd like to be able to curse in Lojban, I'm proposing we come up with some
Lojbanic curses.

And I don't necessarily mean shit, fuck, damn, etc. I mean more along the
lines of "You pea-brained sack of twat-juice!", or "Cum-guzzling gutter
slut!" or "Republican" (that last one was a joke).

So, to start things off, here's one from me:

tolmencrerai : x1 is a fucking retard (not in the mental disability sense)
to x2 because of x3

i.e. {do tolmencrerai fi lonu lo do carce pu daspo lo mi rozgu} = "You
fucking retard! You ran over my roses!"

This has been revisited from time-to-time and the outcome is always that *
curses* and such are deeply invested in cultural context. As such a
language that is designed to be culturally neutral does not provide curses
outside of the cultural context of the particular user. For anyone is
raised with lojban as their primary language, the concept of cursing, and
expletives will likely be an intellectual exercise, rather than an
emotional outburst.

mi'e brais

To be honest, I would be offended if someone called me that. Sometimes,
insults/curses do translate into other cultures. I believe what Mr. Jones
is trying to argue here is that we should have specific, intensified,
derogatory terms for various insults/curses that are fairly
culture-neutral, i.e., I don't think anyone would take kindly to being
called the equivalent of a "fucking retard" or the like. Concepts like
"slut" or your example of the offspring of a flea who was confused about
his nutrient sources would be harder to translate, obviously, and we might
do well to leave out those kind of curses all together.

I suppose that my confusion stems from a lack of understanding as to
*why*lojban
*needs* curses and insults? Mr. Jones states that cursing is part of *the
human condition* though my experience is that all curses come from cultural
expectations and are learned from context and the modelling of your family
unit. My example of "(derisively) you fool" is the only example that I
could come up with for a *meaningful  *insult. Other than that all we are
talking about is a desire to exhibit our emotional venting. I will point
people to the article by Arnt Richard Johnson at
http://people.fix.no/arj/lojban/swear.html if it is still up.

mu'o mi'e brais

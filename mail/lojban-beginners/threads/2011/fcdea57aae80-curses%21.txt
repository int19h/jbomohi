Is it just me, or is this group rather slow of late?

Well, in part to spark up some conversation on here, and in part because
I'd like to be able to curse in Lojban, I'm proposing we come up with some
Lojbanic curses.

And I don't necessarily mean shit, fuck, damn, etc. I mean more along the
lines of "You pea-brained sack of twat-juice!", or "Cum-guzzling gutter
slut!" or "Republican" (that last one was a joke).

So, to start things off, here's one from me:

tolmencrerai : x1 is a fucking retard (not in the mental disability sense)
to x2 because of x3

i.e. {do tolmencrerai fi lonu lo do carce pu daspo lo mi rozgu} = "You
fucking retard! You ran over my roses!"

This has been revisited from time-to-time and the outcome is always that *
curses* and such are deeply invested in cultural context. As such a
language that is designed to be culturally neutral does not provide curses
outside of the cultural context of the particular user. For anyone is
raised with lojban as their primary language, the concept of cursing, and
expletives will likely be an intellectual exercise, rather than an
emotional outburst.

mi'e brais

If you go to the lojban site you will find a couple of discussions on
curses, and swearing. Since the language is based on creating logically
verifiable statements, most curses simply do *not* parse, at least not IN
lojban. The closest you may come is *do bebna* (you are a fool) though that
does not fulfill the emotional catharsis of calling somebody a "fucking
retard" even if it is logically equivalent.

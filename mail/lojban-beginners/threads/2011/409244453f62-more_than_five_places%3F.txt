For the nintadni out there, here's how tagged jai works:

Before the selbri, you can say {jai <BAI> broda} where <BAI> is anything
under selma'o "BAI" (I'm not sure if you can put other tags in there like
"pu" and such... could be neat).  This construct changes x1 to be a sumti
that would normally be in the <BAI> position.

e.g.  {mi cusku bau la lojban} = {la lojban jai bau cusku fai mi}.

I guess the two usages kind of make sense now if you think of taggless {jai}
as a more generic form of {jai TAG selbri}.  Leave out the "TAG" and it's
just a generic sumti that gets swapped into x1.

On vlasisku.lojban.org:  baupli = x1 uses language x2 for purpose x3

pe'i The only real clear difference between selbau and baupli is that baupli
makes the definite assertion that the language is being used in this selbri
as opposed to selbau which to me is more of a "I am one who is/does/CAN be a
speaker of language x2".

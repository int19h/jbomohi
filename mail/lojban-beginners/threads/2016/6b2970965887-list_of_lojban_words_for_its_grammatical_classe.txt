In order to talk about cmavo belonging to a particular selmaho in 
Lojban, you can either:
• use {lo cmavo be lo se cmavo be zo za'o} (= "the cmavo of the same 
selmaho as {za'o}), or {lo selma'o dunli be zo za'o} (= "the things 
equal to {za'o} in their selmaho");
• or use the experimenetal cmavo {ma'oi} 
<http://jbovlaste.lojban.org/dict/ma%27oi>, which belongs to the ZO 
selmaho (thus, same grammatical behavior as {zo}):  {lo cmavo be ma'oi 
za'o} = "the cmavo of selmaho ZAhO".

Here's a nice mindmap that classifies selmaho:
• https://mind42.com/mindmap/03d7fb74-986c-4418-bb14-2dfb86d21dbc

mi'e la .ilmen. mu'o

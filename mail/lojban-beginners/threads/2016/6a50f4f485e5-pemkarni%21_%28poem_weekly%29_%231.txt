Hi. This is pemkarni, a weekly initiative to get all Lojbanists, including
beginners, to write something interesting.

Please send your creations privately to my email address (
ciuak.prog@gmail.com). Also, give the name you'd like to have. There's no
need for your poem to be long or structured or rhyming -- just comply with
the given topic, which is:

    separation ('sepli')

Again, I encourage everyone to try. Cheers!

Now, here are the submissions of the pemkarni number 0, which was about
dark spaces:

i mi surla sarlu.

pe'u ko dragau mi ki'u la lojban.na<http://lojban.na> verclibau mi

So, first of all, «pe'u» eats the «ko», giving a sentence meaning something like "Please do so that someone corrects me". I recommend that you use «.e'o». Then, «la .lojban. na verclibau mi», in order to be contained in «ki'u», has to be made into a sumti with «lo nu». I also prefer «jbebau» instead of «verclibau». So, the sentence would look like:

    .e'o ko dragau mi ki'u lo nu la .lojban. na jbebau mi




Corrigez-moi, s'il vous plaît, parce que le françias n'est pas ma langue maternelle.

coi le karni.

i mi'e la timoteios.i mi xabju le sralygu'e.

i ca'o le cabdei mi pu ctigau la plipe.i la plipe.

Because «plipe» is not a cmevla, you don't need the '.' after it. «la plipe» is all fine.
If you want to stick to using cmevla, you can say «la .plipes.» or «la .plip.».



cu dalpe'o mi.
i la plipe.cu<http://plipe.cu> citka le srasu ni'i la plipe.cu<http://plipe.cu> pipyjalra.

«la plipe cu pipyjalra» is a bridi, and «ni'i» can eat a sumti. So you need to use «lo nu», making it:

    la plipe cu citka le srasu ki'u lo nu la plipe cu pipyjalra

«ni'i» is a word for logical implication, so the sentence would mean 'Plipe is a grasshopper, thus Plipe eats grass'. «ki'u» is a better fit here.



i mi ze'u ciska dei.i mi ba za'u re'u ciska.

The first sentence says you've been writing just *it* for a long time. What about this:

    .i mi ze'u ciska lo dei se karni

«lo dei se karni» meaning 'the magazine-content related to this sentence'.



mu'o

COI take a selbri, sumti, or cmevla. UI are simple attitudinals. «pe'u» is
in COI, so it takes a selbri, sumti, or cmevla. «.e'o» is in UI, so it's a
simple attitudinal.

There's an experimental cmavo for that («dei'ei»), but otherwise you have
to resort to «lo dei .uenzi» or something.

>

>
> mi ze'u ciska ti

«ze'u» has an implication of big longness, e.g. 50 years. I'd say «mi ze'a
lo clani ti ciska».

There are many ways, but the best one is... letters. In a nutshell, if you
put letterals, they will serve as a sumti referring to something from
context. So, you'd say «la plipe», and then just «py.».

Grammatically, a word in the selma'o (cmavo grammatical class) DOI or
COI will consume a sumti after it, unless terminated by {do'u}. That's

There are two ways to use pronouns in Lojban. One uses the dedicated
pronoun sequence {ko'a}, {ko'e}, ..., {ri}, {ra},  etc with {goi}, or
use the fact that a letteral refers to the last sumti starting with that
letter. Thus:

{la plipe goi ko'a citka lo srasu ki'u lo ka ko'a pipyjalra}
or
{la plipe cu citka lo srasu ki'u lo ka py pipyjalra}

(Note I've also altered other parts of that sentence to make it more
correct/idiomatic)

> (1) *.e'o ko dragau mi ki'u le nu mi na filta'a*

Even though this is lojban-beginners, I think it might be good to point 
out that this sentence (and all the previous versions of it) doesn't 
mean what one might expect. {.e'o} (and {ko}) has(have) scope over the 
entire sentence {dragau mi ki'u le nu mi na filta'a}, which includes the 
{ki'u} ("because") clause. Thus, what you request from the listener by 
saying this sentence is that they [correct you because you don't speak 
fluently], rather than that the reason why you make the request is that 
you don't speak fluently (which is the intended meaning, but not the 
meaning of the Lojban).

One way to fix this is:

    (2) .e'o ko dragau mi .i ki'u bo mi na filta'a
        "Please correct me. This is because I don't speak fluently."

{.e'o} is short for {ca'e mi cpedu}, so the {.i ki'u bo} "because" 
actually invisibly attaches to {cpedu} rather than {dragau}. Therefore, 
the underlying meaning of (2) is (3):

    (3) ca'e mi cpedu fi do fe lo ka dragau mi .i ki'u bo mi na filta'a
       "I hereby request that you correct me. This is because I don't 
speak fluently."

While English "Please correct me because I don't speak fluently" is 
ambiguous, Lojban reveals this ambiguity and forces one to choose one of 
the possible readings, which is then reflected in the syntax. So even 
though this particular example might be a bit complicated for 
lojban-beginners, I think it's a good general lesson to take away from this.

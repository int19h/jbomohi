I believe [ka] works here: *remoi fi le ka [ce'u] barda* (second in
bigness/second biggest)


> Also, as x2 is a set am I correct in assuming that I can

I believe so: *ti remoi le'i sedyta'u po mi le ka [ce'u] barda *(This is
second of the hats I own in bigness/This is my second biggest hat).

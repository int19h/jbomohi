On 4/22/08, Vid Sintef <picos.picos@gmail.com> wrote:
> Can a relative phrase be grammatically inserted into a tanru (however
> pointless it may be)?

No, they can only modify a sumti or a vocative.


> Example 1:
>
> le melbi ke pe do [ge'u] mlatu [ke'e] [ku] ;
>
> le melbi pe do [ge'u] bo mlatu [ku]
>
>
> Example 2:
>
> le xamgu ke pe fi'e do [ge'u] cukta [ke'e] [ku] ;
>
> le xamgu pe fi'e do [ge'u] bo cukta [ku]

Those are ungrammatical. Presumably the intended meanings
would be:

le melbi ke me do mlatu
le melbi be do mlatu
le xamgu cukta be fi'e do
le xamgu be fi'e do cukta

mu'o mi'e xorxes

On 4/22/08, Vid Sintef <picos.picos@gmail.com> wrote:
> ¿How different are these:
>
> le melbi mlatu co me do
> le melbi mlatu pe do

Both {pe} and tanru modification are very vague, but
there is a structural difference:

The first one is a sumti built from the selbri {melbi mlatu co
me do}. This selbri can be predicated of something, and {le}
uses this description to get to the intended referent.

The second one is built from a sumti {lo melbi mlatu},
whose referents are restricted in some way by the
referents of {do}.

mu'o mi'e xorxes

Can anybody let me know the shortest way of saying 'a square
 is a rectangle with equal sides'? (Imagine a Grade 1 math lesson
in Lojbanistan).

It seems to me that that is impossible as {lo kurfa} is supposed
to mean 'a two-dimensional right-angled shape/form' but in
common usage has become 'square'.  Using the NORALJV
lujvvo for 'square', I would make it:

{lo kubykurfa cu kurfa poi loi korbi cu dunli simxu le ka clani}

You should either replace {poi} with {gi'e}, or use
{cu me lo kurfa poi ...}. A poi-clause attaches to a sumti.

Also, {simxu lo ka dunli fi lo ka clani}, or {simxu co
dunli fi lo ka clani}, or {dunlysimxu lo ka clani}.
{le ka clani} is not quite right as the x2 of {simlu}.

> For that matter what does vote information 100000 mean?

It means that if you want some other definition to be preferred
over that one, you need to collect 100001 votes. It appears that
official definitions are given a handicap of 100000 votes. Seems
a little excessive, but then I'm not very officially-minded. :)

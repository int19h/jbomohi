=== 2 | 2008-12-16T12:40:49Z | Yoav Nir <yoav.nir@gmail.com> | 71550650812160440q75b6e147p31d2ea07c04cbfe5@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-beginners/cur/1229431249.13cb75a01c8280cc.jbomohi:2,S
For the first question: yes.
For the second: {na} contradicts the entire bridi, while {na'e} contradicts
only the selbri. {mi *na* nelci ro gerku} = it is not true that I like all
dogs.  {mi *na'e* nelci ro gerku} = I other-than-like all dogs.

In the second case, it says that you have some relation to all dogs, but
{nelci} isn't it.

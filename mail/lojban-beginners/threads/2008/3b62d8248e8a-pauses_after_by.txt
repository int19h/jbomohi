On 4/17/08, Brett Williams <mungojelly@gmail.com> wrote:
> If you say more than one BY in a row, like "jy cy by", is a pause required
> after each BY, or only after the last one?

Pauses are not required, not even after the last one.

A pause is required after Cy only if directly preceded by a CV cmavo
and followed by a brivla or CVV cmavo. Otherwise there is no chance
of it blending with anything else.

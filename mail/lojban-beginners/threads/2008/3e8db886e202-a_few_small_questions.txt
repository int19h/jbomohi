> 3) How badly would it screw with my grammar if I were to use a
> one-syllable rafsi in the place of a gismu, for rhythmic purposes in

Depends on the rafsi. If it's a CVC rafsi, it looks like a name; if it's CVV, 
it can look like a cmavo, from any of several selma'o; if it's CCV, it'll get 
stuck to an adjacent word.

I've repeated a syllable, and a whole line, in a song (it's repeated in the 
original French):
ni'o le bi'u cmalu bloti
ni'o le bi'u cmalu bloti
ze'epu na na naku xe klama
ze'epu na na naku xe klama
.a'oi .a'oi
...
la tumlyselselselsruri xamsi
...
.i lei cidjadjadja cu se xaksu
...
le tordu sas sas sastu'u lacpu
...
It parses without the repetitions, but not with them.

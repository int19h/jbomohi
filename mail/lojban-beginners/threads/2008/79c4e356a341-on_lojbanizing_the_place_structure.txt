The current Lojban definition of klama in Jbovlaste is as follows:

x1 muvdu x2 boi x3 boi x4 fi'o se marce x5

(In the HTML and Latex versions, the numbers are written as subscripts. This is a not-quite-canonical shorthand for xy xi pa, xy xi re, etc.)

.i mi ze'i te lisri fo do
I'll tell you a short story.

.i lo rokci pu ki nenri lo condi kabri
A rock was in a deep cup.

.i lo kabri cu culno lo ji'a djacu
The cup was full of water, also.

.i lo smani ba zi ki viska lo bi'u nai kabri goi ky
A monkey soon after saw the aforementioned cup (K).

.i lo bi'u nai smani goi sy ze'a pu taske
The monkey (S) had been thirsty for a while.

.i sy lo lenku .ui dai djacu ky ba zi sai ki pinxe
S drank some cold (oh joy!) water from K, right away.

.i sy pu zi to'e sanji lo za'i lo bi'u nai rokci goi ry cu nenri
S had just been totally unconscious of the rock (R) being in there.

.i ba za ki ku sy pi so'a lo djacu ba'o pinxe .uo ro'o dai
A while later, S had drank almost all of the water (phew! ahhh!).

.i .ue dai sy se spaji lo nu ry lo denci .oi dai .uu cu janli
(wha?!) S is suprised when R collides with a tooth (oy! ow! oh sorry!).

.i lo bi'u nai smani no'u sy ze'a ba cortu .uu lo denci
The aforementioned monkey (yeah, S) for a while longer hurt (aw!) in the
tooth.

.i je sy ze'e ba ro roi kurji loi nu sanji loi nenri be ro da
And forever after S took care to be conscious of what was inside of
everything.

.i fanmo
The end.



mu'o mi'e se ckiku

  coi, Chris.  One small thing.  The second sumti of "ckire" is who
you are appreciative towards.  Since it's rather difficult to show
gratitude to a story, you want that in the X3 place (what you are
grateful about).  So you need to say either "mi ckire do tu'a li
lisri..." or "mi ckire zo'e tu'a lo lisri..." or "mi ckire zi'o tu'a
lo lisri...." or "mi ckire fi tu'a lo lisri....", etc.

On Wed, Aug 27, 2008 at 7:18 PM, chris kerr <letsclimbhigher@gmail.com> wrote:
> In your last example, could the {tu'a} come before the {fi}?

  No.  tu'a is in the same cmavo class as "la'e".  It has to come
immediately before a sumti (or pro-sumti), and in turn the resulting
expression  is still a sumti "tu'a X" -> "something about X".  "fi"
marks a sumti as coming in the third place in a bridi.  "fi X" is not
a sumti, more like a tagged sumti, so can't be further marked.
            --gejyspa

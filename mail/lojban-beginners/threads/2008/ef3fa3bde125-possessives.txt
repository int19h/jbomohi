Yes, they are equivalent. More precise than them
is "le cmene be le pendo be do", which makes use
of the place structures of "cmene" and "pendo".

> and could you also say (something like):
> le le do pendo cmene
> ?

If you add a "ku" after "pendo", yes.

You could even refer to the sound of their name:
le le le do pendo ku cmene ku sance

Or if you wanted to say "your friend's dog's name sounds nice" you could
say:
le le le le do pendo ku gerku ku cmene ku sance cu melbi

There's some (non-human) characters in the Amber game I've been putting
together that talk that way incessantly.

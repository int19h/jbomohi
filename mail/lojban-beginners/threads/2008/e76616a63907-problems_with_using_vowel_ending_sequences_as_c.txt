There's no problem with tanru as names, and the ku with LA is usually
elidable, just as after LE. Similarly, the terminator for DOI/COI is
do'u, also usually elidable. The only issue here is that "fagricipni",
as written, is not a tanru, it's "fa gricipni", cmavo + lujvo. You
need either "fagri cipni" or "fágricipni"/"fAgricipni" to get a tanru.

There are two things wrong with it:
1. All cmevla must end with a consonant.
2. The consonant cluster [sv] is disallowed in Lojban, as it is a
voiced-unvoiced consonant pair.

Given that the IPA for [Sverige] is /ˈsvær.jə/, I suggest [la zveriys.].


> BTW, could some one explain why this is so:
>
> ni'o lo tumla pe ko'a kurfa ki'otre li vo vo so ki'o so xa
> mu
>
> Rather than "ki'otre te'a li re" as it area?
>
[te'a] is ungrammatical in that position.

No; those operators only work within mathematical expressions ([li re pi'i
re] = 2 x 2), not brivla.

[ki'otre xi re] is grammatical, [xi] being an all-purpose subscript to
modify words. But subscripting brivla is unexplored territory, as far as I
know.

> what about ki'otre pi'i ki'otre?

pitki'otre. If you want to use a symbol in a sentence, I think it should go 
where the word would, that is, *before* the number, and should stand for 
{pitki'otre [be] [li]}, with the cmavo understood as needed. As some symbols 
are also well-formed words (there's no one named {km}, but it is valid), they 
should be marked with a hyphen (the dot already being used for a pause).

I'm taking statics, and one quantity (second moment of an area) used in 
statics is expressed in *quartic* millimeters. For that I'd say 
{vontefmiltre}.

Hello everyone, I just finished recording a new song I wrote called  
"coi ro do do'u."  You can hear it here:

http://ixkey.info/lojban/audio/coirododohu.m4a

It's pretty simple, but I hope you'll enjoy it.  It goes like this:


.i .a'o xamgu do gi'e selgleki do

   (I hope it's good for you and it makes you happy)

.i fi'i doi ro do poi cnino lojbo

   (Welcome to all who are new to Lojban)

.i mi'a nelci do .i ba'a prami do

   (We like you; you will be loved)

.i fi'i doi ro do poi cnino lojbo

   (Welcome to all who are new to Lojban)

coi doi ro do coi doi ro do
coi doi ro do coi ro do do'u

   (Hello to all, hello to all, hello to all, hello to all)


mu'o mi'e la bret.

Yes (though "lo birje" would work just as well).

> I'd like also to know cliches for such structures as
> - 'I've got a new car'/'I've got a wife and two children';

mi ponse lo cnino karce .i mi se fetspe gi'e se panzi reda
The car is citno if it was just made; it's cnino if you just got it.
jbovlaste and jbofi'e disagree on the place structure of "fetspe".

> - 'There is a book on the table';

da cukta ga'u le jubme .i lo cukta cu cpana le jubme
"ga'u" means "above"; I didn't find a preposition or tense marker 
meaning "on".

> - 'Let us go for a short walk'.

.e'u mi'o ze'i dzuli'u

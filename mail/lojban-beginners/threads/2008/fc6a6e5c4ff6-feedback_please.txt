and even more certain that I distorted or omitted much of the meaning.
Any suggestions for improvement would be much appreciated.



ni'o mi terve'u ci le me la .reskius. me'u feska'e sfani terkavbu goi
ry .ije punji ry ne'i le sasfoi ti'a le mi zdani .isa'u ry gunma lo
slasi dakli .e lo vorme sekai le nerkla po'o .i ry se nenri lo tolmelbi
panci purmo noi jmina lo djacu ca le nu se bregau .i za'a ry zmadu fi
le ka kavbu be lo sfani .i so'i sfani cu .audai klamu'o .iji'a ne'i le
dakli cu se viska lo terpa se lifri be lo sfani .i se fulta le cpina

cu vruri'a gi'e cpare ve'a ga'u .i mi ganse ca le lamprudo'i le nu lo
panzi cu tolcanci .i la'e di'u sinxa le nu lo sfani cu mo'u gletu ca le
nu pu'o morsi .i la'e di'u sinxa le nu lo .uinaidai se dimna sfani cu
mo'u gletu ca le nu pu'o morsi .i mi kucli le si'o xu se cumki le nu
mau re remyma'utei cu ka'e jmive ne'i le feska'e dakli .i la'u ma zungi
fa mi le terpa tcini



I
bought three Rescue Disposable Fly Traps and hung them up in my
backyard. They are basically a plastic bag with a little funnel
attached on the top. There is some god-awful smelling powder inside to
which you add water when it's time to use.
Well, they work remarkably well. The flies are swarming. And inside is
a vision of fly hell. Floating in the foul liquid are hundreds,
thousands, of dead insects. Their still living cellmates buzz and crawl
around on top of them. I noticed yesterday that maggots had appeared.
This means that some poor doomed fly bothered to coupulate while
waiting to die. I wonder if it is possible to have a second or even a
third generation live their entire lives within the confines of the
disposable bag. To what degree should I feel guilty about having
participated in this nightmare scene?



mu'o mi'e pit

peter hering wrote:
> .i se fulta le cpina litki lo ba'e so'i morsi cinki

This seems to mean, that the liquid floats on the dead flies. While this could
be a neat stylistic device (so many dead flies, that the liquid itself was
swimming in fly corpses), it seems like that wasn't intended. The cause for this
is, that if you put no sumti before the selbri, the first sumti after it is
assigned into the x2 slot, which is the x1 slot of fulta.

I'm not sure, wether the word remyma'utei is appropriate here, as you don't talk
about humans, but flies.

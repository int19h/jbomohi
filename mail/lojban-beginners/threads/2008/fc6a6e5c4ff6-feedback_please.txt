I
bought three Rescue Disposable Fly Traps and hung them up in my
backyard. They are basically a plastic bag with a little funnel
attached on the top. There is some god-awful smelling powder inside to
which you add water when it's time to use.
Well, they work remarkably well. The flies are swarming. And inside is
a vision of fly hell. Floating in the foul liquid are hundreds,
thousands, of dead insects. Their still living cellmates buzz and crawl
around on top of them. I noticed yesterday that maggots had appeared.
This means that some poor doomed fly bothered to coupulate while
waiting to die. I wonder if it is possible to have a second or even a
third generation live their entire lives within the confines of the
disposable bag. To what degree should I feel guilty about having
participated in this nightmare scene?



mu'o mi'e pit

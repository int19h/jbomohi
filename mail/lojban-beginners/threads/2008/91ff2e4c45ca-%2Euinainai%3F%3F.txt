=== 2 | 2008-01-04T07:36:49Z | Penguino <spheniscine@gmail.com> | 97f5058c0801032336p1cabd1cer774074f22151cd95@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-beginners/cur/1199432209.eb689589aa18915d.jbomohi:2,S
*pe'i* Since [nai] is polar negation, [.uinainai] would mean the same as
[.ui], and not equivalent to English "not unhappy", which might be better
expressed as [.uinaicu'i].

I have also been thinking about combinations that might not quite fit the
7-point scale, like [cu'isai] (ex. [a'ucu'isai] strong disinterest), or even
[caicai], which is even stronger than [cai] (this should, of course, be used
sparingly).

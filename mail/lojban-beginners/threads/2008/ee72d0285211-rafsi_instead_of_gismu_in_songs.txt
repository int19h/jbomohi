On the other hand, many songs are actually grammatically incorrect. One
common manifestation of this is in repeating words or parts of words, e.g.
"Mary had a little lamb, little lamb, little lamb". Having a
grammatically-correct song that fits in the proper meter to use in the
rhythm of the tune can be very difficult, and is often not done. Perhaps an
interesting topic of discussion for the lojban list (i.e., not the beginners
list) could be about how to more effectively use lojban in music or metered
poetry-making it fit without loosing understandability.

 

And that's not even considering the song lyrics specifically designed not to
make sense.

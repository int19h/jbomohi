.i ti gerku
        This is a dog.
.i le gerku ku klama
        The dog goes!
.i la dik. catlu le nu le gerku ku klama
        Dick sees the dog go.
.i i'enai la dik. darxi le gerku
        Mean Dick hits the dog.
.i la dik. kusru
        Dick is a Prick.
.i le gerku ku batci la dik. le birka
        The dog bites Dick on the arm.
.i pa'e la dik. cortu le birka
        Dick's arm hurts. Justice is served.

Although I don't see any real problems with the grammar itself, most Lojbanists
use "{cu}" rather than "{ku}" to separate {sumti} from {selbri}.

la'o fy. purpleposeidon .fy. cusku zoi skamyxatra.
> But anyways, the real question is... what does "by standard x" mean?
.skamyxatra

It depends a bit on exactly what {gismu} you're referring to, but it is
generally used to specify the standard or set of qualifications under which an
otherwise subjective statement can be said to be true.

Since the context would almost invariably be "de'i li ..." or "ti'u li ...",
there probably can't be much confusion between dates and times
anyway. More problematic is knowing whether 08-07-06 is a big-endian
or a little-endian date.

I find assigning names to hours and years weird. I would say "li papa" and 
indicate that it's a time by other words:
ca li papa pe le cerni
ca li papa pe le donri (to mintu la'e di'u toi)
ca li papa pe le vanci
ca li papa pe le nicte (to mintu la'e di'u toi)
ca li papa (to mintu li papa pe le cerni toi)
ti'u li papa
.azo'e

If other words indicated that it's a time, I'd have figured it out. As to 
other ways of saying it, Lojban prefers improper fractions to mixed numbers, 
so "li vozefi'uvo".

"pi'e" has other uses. I've been drawing lots on le vobipi'ezevonomoi cartu, 
that is, the map on page 740 of book 48.

The date should be stated in the order year-month-day, e.g. "li pasoxaso pi'e 
ze pi'e repa", unless the month is given as a word, which would be "le 
repamoi be le djunio be le pasoxasomoi".

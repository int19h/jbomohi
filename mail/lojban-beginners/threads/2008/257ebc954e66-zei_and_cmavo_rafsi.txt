He said "you have to do it with the aid of "zei" ..."
This is true of "ka'o'".  It's not true for all cmavo.  Since many cmavo have 
rafsi, it's not usually necessary to use "zei" with them.
It's *possible* to "zei" with any cmavo, whether it has a rafsi or not.  It's 
only required when the cmavo has no rafsi.

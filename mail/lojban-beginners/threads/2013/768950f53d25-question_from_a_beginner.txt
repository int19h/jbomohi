>
> The rafsi for pajni is pai, and for tavla are tav ta'a, so "paitva" is not
a valid lujvo, since tva is not a rafsi for tavla (nor can it be for any
gismu: the "-tv-" is a disallowed consonant combination because of
different voicing). It could be pairta'a or pairtavla (with an r-hyphen) or
pajnyta'a or pajnytavla.
Instead of ta'apai, which falls apart (there's no consonant cluster in the
first 5 letters), you can use ta'arpai (with r-hyphen) or tavypai (voicing
differences again, requiring y-hyphen). Longer lujvo are also possible:
tavlypai, tavlypajni, ta'arpajni.
I don't know whether the choice of lujvo or its definition is any good, but
these are the possible forms it can take.

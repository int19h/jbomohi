=== 4 | 2013-03-27T22:20:33Z | mudri <jammyatjammy@gmail.com> | e664ada0-a917-4d47-ba78-abbeb4c257ef@googlegroups.com | mail/lojban-beginners/cur/1364422833.ae30eed8b5da72ea.jbomohi:2,S
Maybe you've seen {le mi zdani}, or some such. That does indeed mean “my 
house” (or “the house associated with me”). It comes about through a bit of 
syntactic sugar, in which “LE SUMTI SELBRI” is equivalent to “LE SELBRI pe 
SUMTI”. I don't mean to shout, by the way, I'm just using BNF conventions 
;-).

{le mi zdani} = {le zdani pe mi}
{le le jurme ku zdani} = {le zdani pe le jurme [ku]}, though no-one ever 
uses the first form if it requires a {ku}. In fact, I don't know whether 
it's correct with non-KOhA sumti.

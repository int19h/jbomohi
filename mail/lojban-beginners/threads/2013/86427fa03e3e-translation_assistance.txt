If you mean "softly" in the sense of "not loud", then {tolcladu} is more
appropriate than {milxe} (which is present in {mliba'u}). (On a related
note, {mliba'u} really should be {ba'umli}, as if you were to say the full
structure without a tanru, you'd say {milxe lo ni bacru}.)

What you're trying to do with {ku'i} is what's called a connective. The
problem is that {ku'i} is not a connective; {ku'i} is an indicator. It
simply attaches to the previous word to draw our attention to the fact that
it is in contrast with something else. Aside from that, it serves to higher
grammatical purpose. The connective you would use in this case is {gi'e},
"and", as it connects to bridi-tails. ({mliba'u is on e bridi-tail, and
{bevri lo barda grana} is another one. {gi'e} would connect them, applying
the common x1 {ko}.)

In total, you'd get {ko tolcladu bacru gi'e ku'i bevri lo barda grana}.

I hope this cleared things up,
.i mi'e la tsani mu'o

check my grasp of the concepts. Of course, it's hard for me to be sure I am

lanzu.

Thanks for your time/interest!

Perfectly correct!

> I'm obviously trying to play around
> with place structure. I thought this form had a nice ring to it, versus

And this one is also correct and means the same thing.

If you have already learned about {be ... [bei] ... be'o}, you can also 
say {lo lanzu be mi}. This puts {mi} directly in the x2 of {lanzu}, so 
the whole sumti means "The family that I am a member of" = "My family". 
It is slightly more exact, but your version is also easy to understand.

Keep up the good work. :)

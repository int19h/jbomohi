I think {cukla} is fine. How could {cukla} be too specific? As I understand it, what characterize gismu is that they're always vague, not too specific. Their definitions shouldn't be interpreted too literal, but rather approximately.

"Round" is {cukla} in most senses.

cukla. x1 is round/circular [2-dimensional shape/form]; x1 is a 
disk/circle/ring.
Normally used for a filled-in circle/disk, but emphasis on roundness means 
that the concept may include 'ring'. See also djine, ranji, bolci, tarmi.

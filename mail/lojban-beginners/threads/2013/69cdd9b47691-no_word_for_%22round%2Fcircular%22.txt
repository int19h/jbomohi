cukla. x1 is round/circular [2-dimensional shape/form]; x1 is a 
disk/circle/ring.
Normally used for a filled-in circle/disk, but emphasis on roundness means 
that the concept may include 'ring'. See also djine, ranji, bolci, tarmi.


Spheres are 3-dimensional.

ci'ersaptai, saptai, ci'ersmitai, smitai). See alse nejni, te marji for

Which booklets? Lojban for Beginners, Wave lessons?

There are a lot of things that aren't in the beginner guides because they 
would be out of the scope. They're all (except use of experimental words) 
contained in the CLL reference grammar, but that's something to read 
through later.

The main way to improve is, like with any language, to converse with more 
advanced speakers. Twitter works well, and you should soon get the standard 
group of followers (me included). Expect corrections. Others suggest IRC, 
but I've always found it empty and awkward. That might be a time zone 
thing. Find the groups on slower social networks, too. There's a group on 
Facebook and community on Google+.

When you want to learn vocabulary, use either Memrise or Anki (I think it's 
Anki; I remember there being Memrise and something else). A quick search 
should find the Lojban stuff.

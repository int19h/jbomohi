On Thursday, November 07, 2013 20:29:45 liam.m.kane@gmail.com wrote:
> I am almost totally new to lojban and I need something to get me started,
> but in the few conversations I've had I've been told my grammar is
> outdated.  What is the currently accepted learning resource?

You've been told that what about your grammar is outdated? If you say "cu" and 
you've been told to say "ku", there's nothing wrong with either way, and you 
can even say "ku cu". It's a teaching method, not anything about the grammar.

If you've been told you can omit the "ku" in "ku joi", that's a difference 
between two kinds of formal grammars. The PEG can handle "joi" without the 
"ku"; the LALR1 grammar can't.

If it has something to do with names beginning with "la", that's a change 
called dotside.

If it's something else, tell us!

Every so often, someone comes up with the "new" idea that we should update
the Lojbanistan flag. I don't think any of these resurgences ever had any
actual effect, although Bridi Man is one of those flag updates that is
used- just not as the flag.

This is not another call to update.

I just saw a video by Numberphile about the most mathematical flag of any
nation, the flag of the Kingdom of Nepal. It's rather interesting, since
the entire flag is described in purely mathematical terms with absolutely
no units of any sort.

I think it would be interesting to have our flag described in the same
manner.

Anyone want to have a crack at it? Extra points for doing it in Lojban!

Is there a taboo about making up zi'evla-shaped words as (eg personal) 
names, as one would do with me'evla?  I mean, instead of calling oneself, 
say {la .andis.}, one could be {la .andi} (a possible lojbanisation of the 
naljbo name "Andy"), regardless of the meaning of {.andi} (it has acquired 
none so far, I believe). Nobody seems to be doing it. Or at least, I'm 
unaware of it.

O. K., that's all. Thanks.

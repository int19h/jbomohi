Well, the Scientific Method is a series of rules, so that's best translated
as individual, numbered bridi in Lojban, as to the name, my suggestion
would be {lo se saske be zo'e to be zoi.(The category of science, e.g
"Botany"->plants {lo si'o spati}")} ({lo se saske be si'o spati})

"I am learning how to do science"
{mi cilre lo binxo loka kakne lo se saske}

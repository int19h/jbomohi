>
>
> I was wondering how to say "I jump over the wall" and "I jump under the
> bus".
>

mi plipe ga'u lo bitmu
mi plipe lo cnita be lo sorpeka

"Under the bus" is the destination of the jump, so it can go in plipe2.
"Over the wall" is the trajectory of the jump, which does not have a place
in "plipe", so it has to be added with a tag. If you want to be overly
precise you could say "mi plipe fi'o ve pluta lo gapru be lo bitmu".

mu'o mi'e xorxes

Hi all,

While "playing" jboselkei I encountered the following problem:
I wanted to write something like "poi broda .ije brode" but camxes 
complains and splits at ".ije".

How can I express logical connection of bridi within a relative clause?

Within standard Lojban, you need forethought: {poi ge broda gi brode}, or
else you can only use a bridi tail: {poi broda gi'e brode}. With
experimental cmavo, you can replace {.i} by {ju'ei} essentially the way
that you did. For example, {.i lo prenu poi mi tavla ke'a ju'ei ke'a slabu
do cu klama lo zarci}.

I created gismu liste and cmavo liste that include experimental words based 
on the jbovlaste xml dumps in some languages.
http://guskant.github.io/lojbo/gismu-cmavo.html

Languages available for now:
Deutsch, English, Simple English, Esperanto, Français, Magyarul, 日本語, 
lojban, Nederlands, norsk, Polski, Русский, Español, Svenska, Tiếng Việt, 
中文.

I did it because those lists by iocikun are not available these days, I 
don't know why:
https://skami.iocikun.jp/lojban/valsi_tables.html
I wish they would be recovered soon.

There are two main definitions of "cone":
1. (geometry) A surface of revolution formed by rotating a segment of a
line around another line that intersects the first line.
2. (geometry) A solid of revolution formed by rotating a triangle around
one of its altitudes.

I think neither meaning of "cone" reflects {konju}. I think {konju}
describes a *segment* of a line (possibly infinite thus identical to a line
in such case) revolving around another linear segment thus forming a
*surface*. As for konju1 it describes an object (possibly immaterial) with
*a property* of such cone-ness.

This explains why only vertex is specified. but konju2 seems to be
superflous then since it is not always necessary.

  Ah, okay, NOW I understand what your question is --  object vs. property.

  (of course, I've always been very soft on this issue (strong typing of
sumti places) in pretty much all brivla.  I believe that such restrictions
aren't usually useful and just serve to limit the language in ways that it
shouldn't be limited)

  That being said, I think that directions (lo farna) can be thought of as
EITHER objects or properties.  Think of vectors.  I can think of them
either as being fully described by their endpoints (Let's go to my house)
or as an orientation plus distance (Let's go .5 kilometers NNE)  Now, we
are not including a distance when discussing farna or equivalently ve
lamji, so we are actually describing a ray originating from where we are
and passing through that endpoint.  This is equivalent to "Let's head
towards my house" or "Let's head NNE"  The first is an example of Pierre's
construction, and the latter the ka-abstraction.  (i.e. Pierre is saying
"in the direction of the West (of Spain) thing"), and I think both are
equally valid.

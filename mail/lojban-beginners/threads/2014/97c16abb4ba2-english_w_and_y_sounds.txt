> gismu or lujvo cannot contain the diphthongs uV and iV, where the English
"y" and "w" sounds come from.
These sounds occur in cmavo (the attitudinals), in zi'evla (aka fu'ivla,
e.g. {kraui} which has two syllables {kra/ui}), and cmevla (e.g. ".uis.").

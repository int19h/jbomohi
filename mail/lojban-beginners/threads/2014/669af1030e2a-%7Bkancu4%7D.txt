The first one says that there are exactly two of the eyes which you are
using as the units to count people, which barely makes sense. Which two
eyes were they, the left one and the right one? But then say at least "ro
re lo kanla" so we are not left to wonder whether there are other eyes that
were not used. And since you came to the same result using each unit, does
that mean there were twelve people and everyone had two eyes, or just that
there were at leat twelve people and the number of people with no left eye
was equal to the number with no right eye?

As for the second one, yes, you can use steps as the unit, but without
context we don't know what you are counting.

In general, x4 will be the same things that you are counting, but an
example where the unit is something other that what you are counting could
be:

  mi kancu lo prenu li pa re lo re mei
  I counted the people as 12 couples.

"More often" is "zmadu fi lo ka cafne", so perhaps "fi'o cafmau" could be
less cryptic than "fi'o zmaroi", but either of them should work. I haven't
seen "no'o" or "xo'e" used by themselves enough to have a clear idea of
what they mean, but I suppose if they have different meanings then which
one works best would depend on the context of what you want to say.

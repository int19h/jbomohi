My interpretation: 
{ze'e broda jonai brode}
= "broda xor brode is true x time(s) in the whole-of-time interval"
{roroi broda jonai brode}
= "broda xor brode is true at every time (always) in some unspecified time-interval (not necessarily whole of time).

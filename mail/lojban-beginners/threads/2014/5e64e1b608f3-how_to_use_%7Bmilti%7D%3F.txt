> "lo jubme cu milti lo mitre be li bi no no boi lo ka ganra"
> The table is 800 millimeters wide.
>

You could, but also:

  lo jubme cu mitre lo milti be li bi no no lo ka ganra

  lo jubme cu miltre li bi no no lo ka ganra

The last one is closest to what you have in English.
The way you have it is more like "the table is one thousandth of an 800
meter thing in width".


> mi ponse lo re megdo be lo rupnu = I own 2 million dollars.
>
> Is this correct?
>

It is, if you are thinking of 2 (million dollars) but you could also say:

 mi ponse lo rupnu be lo megdo be li re

which is more like (2 million) dollars. Or:

 mi ponse lo megru'u be li re
 "I own 2 megadollars."

mu'o mi'e xorxes

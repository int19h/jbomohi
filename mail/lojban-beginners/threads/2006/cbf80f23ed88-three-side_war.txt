That's {lo remna cu simxu lo nu jamna .ije la protos cu
simxu lo nu jamna .ije la zerg cu simxu lo nu jamna}, i.e.
humans at war with humans, Protoss with Protoss and
Zerg with Zerg.

To make it humans, Protoss and Zerg against each
other you need to make it nondistributive, with {joi}
or {jo'u} instead of {.e}.

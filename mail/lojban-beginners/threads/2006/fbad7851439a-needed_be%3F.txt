{fai} is like {fi} and {fo} (but not {fym}). Since {morsi} has an article, its 
sumti comes after {be}.

.i mi djuno fi le jai ca morsi be fai la djan.
.i mi djuno fi le se morsi be fo la djan.

You probably mean {mrobi'o}, as {la djan. morsi} is true for an indefinitely 
long interval.

Pierre

That's probably the intent, but it contains two common errors:

1- Using {morsi} "is-dead" instead of {mrobi'o} "dies". The time of
someone's death is the time when they die (become dead), not any
time in which they are dead.

2- Using {djuno fi ko'a}, "knowing something about it", when the intent
is {djuno lo du'u ma kau me ko'a}, "knowing what it is". Knowing what
the time of John's death is is more specific than knowing something
unspecified about the time of John's death.

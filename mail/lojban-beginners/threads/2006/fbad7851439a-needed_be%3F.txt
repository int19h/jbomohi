{fai} is like {fi} and {fo} (but not {fym}). Since {morsi} has an article, its 
sumti comes after {be}.

.i mi djuno fi le jai ca morsi be fai la djan.
.i mi djuno fi le se morsi be fo la djan.

You probably mean {mrobi'o}, as {la djan. morsi} is true for an indefinitely 
long interval.

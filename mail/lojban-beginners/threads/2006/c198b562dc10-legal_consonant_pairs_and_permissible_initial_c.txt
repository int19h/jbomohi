CLL: "There are just 48 such permissible initial consonant pairs,     as follows: bl br cf ck cl cm cn cp cr ct dj dr dz fl fr gl gr jb jd       jg jm jv kl kr ml mr pl pr sf sk sl sm sn sp sr st tc tr ts       vl vr xl xr zb zd zg zm zv"

Firstly, I don't see {rs} listed as one of the 48 permissible initial consonant pairs although there are gismu like {jursa} and {darsi} etc.  Also there are lujvo in the lujvo list which also use {rs} as an initial consonant pair: {jaurselylu'i}, {jirsezlu'i}, {ki'orsamsrorau}, {varsenta}.  Not only that, but I am able to also find other lujvo in the "current" lujvo list that appear to violate the initial consonant pair rule, such as {kamsorselmanri}.

Second, for a potential lujvo (one I created) such as {varsetvro} for "airlock", the triple of {tvr} seems permissible because it is not one of the four excluded triples and because {vr} is a permissible initial pair.  But jbofi'e will not accept {varsetvro} although it will accpet {varsetyvro} for some reason. Notice it also accepts the initial consonant pair {rs} as well.  So if {varsetvro} it not a valid lujvo, why not?

"Initial consonant pair" means, most of the time, that it only applies
to the beginning of gismu or lujvo. The consonant pair in a CVC/CV
gismu is not an initial pair, and has a much wider variety of possible
combinations. (CC is an initial consonant pair, and C/C is a
permissible consonant pair. Gismu are CCVCV, CVC/CV.)

> Second, for a potential lujvo (one I created) such as {varsetvro} for
> "airlock", the triple of {tvr} seems permissible because it is not one of
> the four excluded triples and because {vr} is a permissible initial pair.

Nope, consonant triples have to be CC/C, which means that {tv} would
have to be an initial pair, which it isn't. You seem to be thinking
that triples are C/CC.

The CLL 3:6 says:
Pairs of consonants can also appear freely, with the following restrictions:
...
2) It is forbidden for one consonant to be voiced and the other unvoiced.


{t} is unvoiced, and {v} is voiced, hence, *{varsetvro} is not permitted in Lojban.
*{tv} must be diluted with {y}: {varsetyvro}.

That is also the reason why Russians can't have the most direct transcription of their capital to Lojban: *{moskvas.} is prohibited because of *{kv}. {moskov.} or {moskuas.} were suggested, {moskyvas}. is also acceptable.

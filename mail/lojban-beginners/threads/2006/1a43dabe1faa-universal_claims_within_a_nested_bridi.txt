[1] {mi kakne lonu mi citka ro da}

{ro da} here is an example of dropped prenex.
The question is whether the prenex is of the inner bridi ({mi citka
ro da}), or of the outer bridi ({mi kakne lo nu...kei})?

In other words, does [1] mean
"For every X I am able to eat X"  (or simply "I can eat anything")
or
"I am able to (eat every X)"  (or simply "I can eat everything [at
once]")?

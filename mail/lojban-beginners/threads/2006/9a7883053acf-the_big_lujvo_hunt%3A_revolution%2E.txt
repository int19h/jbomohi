I'm continuing my quest to construct new lujvo. This time I tried
"revolution" (fundamental/big change in government). It suprised me that
it hadn't already been created. At least I could not find it in
jbovlaste. If it has, please notify me. I'll also be happy if someone
could come up with a gismu which is closer to "fundamental" than barda.
I'll be happy for any response whatsoever.

This is my notes:

barda (bra): b1 is big/large in property/dimension(s) b2 (ka) as
compared with standard/norm b3
turni (tru): t1 governs/rules/is ruler/governor/sovereign/reigns over
people/territory/domain/subjects t2
galfi (gaf, ga'i): g1 (event)
modifies/alters/changes/transforms/converts g2 into g3

veljvo = turni galfi

g2=t1, t2 dropped.

gaftru: g1 (event) modifies/alters/changes/transforms/converts
governor/goverment g2=t1 into new governor/goverment g3

veljvo = barda gaftru
b1=ga1, b2 dropped

bragaftru: b1=ga1 (event) is a big change in goverment from goverment
ga2, to goverment ga3

Another approach might be to use something like {nu jecta basti} to signify 
the event of replacing a state:

jecta: x1 is a polity/state governing territory/domain x2; 
[government/territory relationship]

basti: x1 replaces/substitutes for/instead of x2 in circumstance x3; x1 is a 
replacement/substitute

I suppose it depends on whether you consider the state to persist but be 
altered by revolution, or whether you consider the state to be wholly 
replaced by it.

property/quality/aspect of x2

{donri} is not "daylight" but "daytime". I'm not sure if the sun can be
said to be its source. Maybe. (And what would the source of the
nighttime be?)

Also possible are {ctefa'o} and {ctekra}. Or {ctefa'o} and {dorfa'o}.

All of these work for "sunrise" and "sunset" as time indicators,
not for the actual event as in "we went to watch the sunset on the
beach".

{cfari} means "x1 starts to happen", {sisti} means
"x1 stops doing x2". You'd need {tolcfa} for "x1 stops happening"
and {tolsti} for "x1 starts doing x2".

I think {lo krasi be lo donri} is best for "the time period that
constitutes the beginning of the daytime period".

We could also say {lo co'a donri} and {lo mo'u donri}.

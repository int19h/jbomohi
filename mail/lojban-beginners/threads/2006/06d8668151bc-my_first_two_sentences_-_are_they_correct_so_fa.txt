On Thursday 16 March 2006 09:46, HeliodoR wrote:
> Neither do I understand ?{zildatni}.

{zildatni} means "information" in the Shannon sense, not necessarily about 
anything.
datni: x1 is information about x2 gathered by method x3
zildatni: x1 is information gathered by method x2
{zil} is the rafsi of {zi'o}, which deletes a place from the place structure.

"predict", "foretell", "anticipate" all use {purci} rather than {balvi}.
I suggest {prujungau}.

(Of course, the prediction comes _before_ the predicted, but the
predicted comes _after_ the prediction, so either {purci} or {balvi}
works depending on how you look at it.)

> for that
> matter, how does one express place structure in lojban?]

{sumti stura}?

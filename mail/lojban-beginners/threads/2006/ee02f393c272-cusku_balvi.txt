I think prujungau works in the sense of

le skami cu prujungau le nu le solri cu spoja

However it makes it look too definite, as in the knowledge is infallible.

Pierre's idea of balvi xusra doesn't require it to be a correct 
prediction, but only claims the person/thing making the assertion 
believes it to be true.  This, in my case, would often be further 
modified by cumki, as in {balvi cumki bo xusra}, as in I have knowledge 
ahead of time that it should be taken with a grain of salt.  Perhaps the 
same could be applied to prujungau, though I still think you need the "bo".

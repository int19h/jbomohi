On 7/21/06, Michael Turniansky <turnip@bcpl.net> wrote:
>
>   Forgive me for switching to English here... but isn't the reason that
> jboski doesn't undestand "cu'u fi'e ko'a" that there needs to be something
> between cu'u and fi'e?

Yes, {ku}, but it's elidable, so it doesn't need to be explicit.
jboski doesn't always handle elidable terminators very well.

> Can a modal directly be followed by a modal?

A term can folow another term. {cu'u [ku]} is a term and {fi'e ko'a} is
another term.

> What
> does it mean?  Doesn't it need an  argument like "cu'u ko'a fi'e ko'a"?

As Robin said, he meant only {cu'u ko'a}, it was a mistake in
editing.

{cu'u ku}  would mean something like "said by someone unspecified".

> Is
> there any kind of logical or non-logical connectives that can go between
> modals?

Yes, they can be connected with {je} or {joi}.

On 1/6/06, Opi Lauma <opi_lauma@yahoo.com> wrote:

> Do sentences with {poi} and {co} have different
> meaning?
> Which ambiguity appears in my sentence with {co}?

{lo zdani co se zbasu be la djan} is a made-by-John type of house.
It can be a house built by John, or a house for things made by John,
or a house similar to ones John usually builds, or a house related
to things made by John in any other way. The most likely meaning,
however, is probably the one you intended, so there is nothing really
wrong with your sentence.

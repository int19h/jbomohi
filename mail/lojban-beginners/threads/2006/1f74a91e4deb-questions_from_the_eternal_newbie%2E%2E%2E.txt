"a only if b" is correct, i.e. "if not b then not a".

> And is this operator the same as "iff" (if and only if)?

No, that's the double implication, {jo}. The corresponding truth tables are:

A   B    A inaja B   A ijo B
T   T           T            T
T   F           F            F
F   T           T            F
F   F           T            T

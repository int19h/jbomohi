Just finished recording a really long sample of the basic sounds of  
Lojban, about 25 minutes in length. It is currently uploading to my  
site (http://www-personal.umich.edu/~alexjm/) linked from the main  
page. This is the same site that has the Lojban Reader I have been  
writing. The file is currently only available in m4a format, because  
I couldn't figure out how to get Garage Band to export to anything  
else; and Audacity is flakey on my machine. Any help with preparing  
an Ogg Vorbis and/or MP3 would be appreciated, by the way.

The transcript, which is also posted, is as follows:

a .e .i .o .u .y

.ai .au .ei .oi .ia .ie .ii .iu .ua .ue .uo .ui .uu

.a'a .a'e .a'i .a'o .a'u
.e'a .e'e .e'i .e'o .e'u
.i'a .i'e .i'i .i'o .i'u
.o'a .o'e .o'i .o'o .o'u
.u'a .u'e .u'i .u'o .u'u

y'y by cy dy fy gy jy ky ly my ny py ry sy ty vy xy zy

[ * is replaced by a consonant from below, in order]
[ bpm vf dtn zs jc gkx rl ]

*a *e *i *o *u *ai *au *ei *oi *ia *ie *iu *ua *ue *uo *ui

*a'a *a'e *a'i *a'o *a'u
*e'a *e'e *e'i *e'o *e'u
*i'a *i'e *i'i *i'o *i'u
*o'a *o'e *o'i *o'o *o'u
*u'a *u'e *u'i *u'o *u'u

I will also try to put together a sort of tutorial/lesson on the  
sounds soon, describing how to make each sound.

Finally!  Questions I feel qualified to answer!

I don't dig the direct Lojbanized transcription name.  I already have a name
that sounds like "Scott."  Much, much groovier to have a name that means
something in Lojban.  An odd thought strikes me: are there people who've
made up Lojbanized transcriptions of entirely new non-Lojban names?  Like if
I decided to call myself la biligot. for some reason.  I'm motivated to
learn Lojban for two reasons: one, you can make relatively simple Lojban
sentences that blow my mind, and two, I cannot stop myself from saying "See,
if we'd been speaking Lojban, this never would've happened." whenever
there's some conversational confusion.

Having a big empty summer between school years helps, too.

mu'omi'e la cuncuxna

> > .i roda poi flecu gi'a rokci cu na'e se cmene
>
> I believe "gi'a" should be ".a.". "gi'a" only connects bridi, and "a"
> only sumti.

No, {gi'a} is right there. {.a} connects sumti, but here we don't
have sumti.

{gi'a} connects bridi-tails, which consist of a selbri and any trailing sumti.
In this case it's just two selbri {flecu} and {rokci}.

> > .i mi ba pensi lo ve ciksi be lo smuni be zo ti be'o bei mi bei do bei
> > ne'i lo xatra
>
> Ack. That sentence makes my head hurt. Also, I think you have an extra
> "bei" before ne'i.

{bei} is correct to add the term {ne'i lo xatra} as an argument to the selbri
{ve ciksi}. Without {bei} the term would be added to the selbri {pensi}.

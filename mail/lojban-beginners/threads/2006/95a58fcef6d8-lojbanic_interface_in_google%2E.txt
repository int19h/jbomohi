Dear Google,
There are versions of your useful Internet sites in many different languages.
Among them are a lot of native languages of different nations and some
constructed languages like Esperanto and Klingon along with some cute
stuff like Elmer Fudd and Hacker. All those improve accessibility and
give Google users an experience of fun, convenience and usability.
I've found out that one may request your team by the e-mail address
that you have supplied (translation-services@google.com) to add a new
language.
I'd propose to add Lojban (www.lojban.org) to this list. I see this
matter as of great importance to the community of Lojban researchers,
enthusiasts and students. Lojban is not just yet another constructed
language - it is designed to express human thoughts with precision and
exactness that is defined by the speaker, with unambiguous grammar and
full isomorphism of text and speech. Those features make this language
available for human-computer communication in a versatile and
convenient way. In future when better voice recognition software will
be available for consumers, this language will be the language of
international sonic user interface in probably every advanced computer
system.
This language is fun, convenient and improves accessibility of Google
nowadays, it will allow even greater advantage in the future. I say,
you should add it.

Thanks for your attention.

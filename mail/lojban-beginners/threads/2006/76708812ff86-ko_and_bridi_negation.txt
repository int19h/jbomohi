On Saturday 22 July 2006 18:47, Dmitry wrote:
> What does "ko na viska me" means?

You mean "ko na viska mi" ("viska me" needs a following sumti to be 
grammatical). "Don't see me." 

> How to say "Do not look at me."?

"ko na catlu mi". The difference is that one told "ko na viska mi" will close 
his eyes or turn the light off, whereas one told "ko na catlu mi" will look 
in another direction. da ka'e viska jenai catlu de

mu'omi'e pier.

On Saturday 15 July 2006 05:10, Earth Engine wrote:
> Shanghai -> canhais.
> Guangzhou -> guandjo,us.
>
> Is these cneme correct?

Neither is a well-formed word. {'} (occasionally written "h") does not occur 
next to a consonant, and {ndj} is a forbidden triple. I'd say {canxais}; for 
Guangzhou you could either insert a pause, {guan.djo,us}, or drop the {d}. 
Not sure whether to drop the {u}, as {ou} is two syllables in Lojban but only 
one in Chinese.

phma

Thanks. I think that {o,u} can be say as one syllables according to The
Complete Lojban Language:

3.3)  .i,iai,ii,iai,ion.

which is technically a different Lojban name. Since the intent with
Lojbanized names is to allow them to be pronounced more like their native
counterparts, the comma is allowed to represent vowel glides or some
non-Lojbanic sound.

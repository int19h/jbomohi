It works for me with dict(1).

arj@gavagai:~$ dict -P - -i "en->jbo" -h lojban.org say
   English to Lojban
1 definition found

>From English to Lojban [en->jbo]:

   {say},        is the gloss word for {cusku}.

Note that the ISO 639-2 code for Lojban is 'jbo', not 'lbo'.

Most of the rafsi are also cmavo.  Rafsi and cmavo are used in totally 
different environments, rafsi always (and only) as elements of a lujvo, and cmavo 
never as elements of a lujvo (unless the rafsi of the cmavo is the same).  Some 
cmavo have rafsi, but the rafsi do not always have the same form as their 
cmavo.

{mi .a do nelci ti .u ta}

What does this bridi mean?

Is it equivalent to this:
{mi nelci ti .u ta  .ija  do nelci ti .u ta}   "I like this indep. of
that,  or   you like this indep. of that",

or this:
{mi .a do nelci ti   .iju   mi .a do nelci ta} "I or you like this,
independent of that I or you like that".

=== 2 | 2006-01-21T07:52:03Z | Yanis Batura <ybatura@mail.ru> | 157539525.20060121135203@mail.ru | mail/lojban-beginners/cur/1137829923.cc735248d28ee81c.jbomohi:2,S
As John Cowan, himself, has answered in the IRC, {mi .a do nelci ti .u ta} is
equivalent to:
{mi nelci ti .iju mi nelci ta .ija tu'e do nelci ti .iju do nelci ta
[tu'u]},
in other words, it equals a cross product of both compound
sumti, where {.a} has greater scope than {.u}.
As John has explained,the left-most sumti connective has wider scope over the next,
following the general rule about predominance of the left.

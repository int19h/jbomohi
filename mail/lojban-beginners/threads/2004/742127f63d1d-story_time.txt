Hmmm...

The standard reading is:

There are zero x's such that there are zero y's such that x sees y.

In other words, no x is such that it sees no y's.

In other words, every x sees at least one y.

Having said that, I do prefer the "nobody saw nothing" reading, which
we called "coordinate scope" in a recent discussion. Here you can
find a lot of talk about this for those of you who are brave enough
and interested:

<http://www.lojban.org/tiki/tiki-index.php?page=Discussion%3A+Three+dogs+attacked+four+men>

The proof is easy. {noda} is equivalent to {naku su'oda}.
Then:
 
noda node viska
= naku su'oda naku su'ode viska
= (by DeMorgan's law) naku naku roda su'ode viska
= roda su'ode viska

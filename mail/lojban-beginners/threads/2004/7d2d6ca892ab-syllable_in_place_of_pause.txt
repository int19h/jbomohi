My husband and I just started studying Lojban.  Because we are nerdy and 
obsessive, we're thinking ahead to the day when Lojban is the interlingua

Lojban speakers.  People just aren't going to do that in real life;

inevitable, it is also anethema to the Lojban philosophy.

references to Loglan speakers who use the syllable "gu" (apparently 
otherwise unassigned) in place of mandatory pauses.

Is there a similar convention among Lojban speakers?

If you like unambiguous forms, use the full form of the gismu and separate 
them with "zei" ('lujvo glue'), which is for lujvo based on words that don't 
have rafsi.  
E.g., lojbau (Lojban + language) = lojbo zei bangu.  This way you don't have 
to remember any rafsi.

A couple of weeks ago, I played a word-building card game called
"Tri-Virsity", which I realized would translate pretty well to
Lojban (as opposed to scrabble, which, umm, not so much).

I've ported the game to the VASSAL online gaming engine, so now
Lojbanists around the world can play this game together.  The
Lojbanic version is called "la cibyska".

The VASSAL module, the English instructions, and my giant 1650 word
Lojban translation of the instructions can all be found on the Wiki
at:

http://www.lojban.org/tiki/tiki-index.php?page=la+cibyska

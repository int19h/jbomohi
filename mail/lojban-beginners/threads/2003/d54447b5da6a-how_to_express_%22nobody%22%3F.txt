That's true, in the sense that there is one person who doesn't love the 
woman, but we aren't concerned with who that person is.

>  Is there a better way to say "Nobody love the woman"?

I don't know about better, but there are alternatives.  For example:

naku su'o da zo'u da prami le ninmu

"It is not true that there is at least one X, such that X loves the woman."

noda zo'u da prami le ninmu

"There is no X, such that X loves the woman."

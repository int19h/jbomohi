geographical area where the rock comes from rather than the actual quarry.]

{zbabu terkunra} as well as a {selzbabu}? Is a salt mine a {silna terkunra}
as well as a {selsilna}? Is an oil well a {ctile terkunra} as well as a
{selctile}? [Similar questions for all these as for {teryrokci} --
"location" and "source" sound rather vague.]

Are diamonds (jarjme? rairjarjme?) found in {terkunra} or only in
{terjemna}?

{mudri terkunra}? ({mudri} has no place for source or location besides the
species of trees used.)

What are the restrictions on the {kunra} ("mineral/ore of type/metal x2")
that come from a {terkunra}?

The Correct Solution is Nick's.

To wit: *always* use four digits for the year.  Always always
always.

That way, as long as you don't use the insane American
month-day-year crap (i.e., whether you go from days to years or
years to days, as long as you do it in order), everything works out.

Samples:

2003-10-01: 1 Oct, 2003.
2003-10: Oct, 2003
2003: Duh.

01-10-2003: 1 Oct, 2003
01-10: 1 Oct
01: Day 1, no particular month or year.

There is no way to have ambiguity as long as you do the year in 4
digits and you don't to mixed-endian (i.e. month-day-year).

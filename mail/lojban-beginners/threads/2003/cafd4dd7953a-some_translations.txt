Remember, boys and girls, vau is your *friend*.  vau can be used for
both kei and ku'o in most cases.

> (What's the difference between a "relative phrase" (terminated by
> {ge'u}) and a "relative clause" (terminated by {ku'o}), then?)

po is a relative phrase.  poi is a relative clause.  There is
essentially no similarity grammatically, because po takes a single sumti
while poi takes a bridi.

ge'u is almost never necessary.

Not sure about meaning, but to get the grammar to hold up, you need a  
{to} and {cu}:

le ninmu cu cadzu teta'i to le xance cu se vasru le kosta daski

The {cu} keeps you from getting {le xance se vasru} as a single  
sumti.  Almost any time the x1 isn't from selma'o KOhA, you will need  
a {cu} or other terminator before the selbri.

The {to} makes {teta'i} take the whole thing as an argument (there's  
a proper term to use instead of "argument", but I'm not sure what it  
is). It's essentially just a parenthesis; without it the parser  
complains.

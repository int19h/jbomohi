As to geodesic dome:

{ta cu xabybolci je gunma be lo so'i sirji be ma'i lo kruvi sefta ku} By
which I intend: that is a half-sphere and composed of many line segments,
which are straight with respect to a curved surface.

It might be better to relax the precision a bit and go with something more
like
{kruvi sirji segunma} as a tanru for "geodesic" with a lujvo like {
ruvsirsergum }

So, {xabybolci ruvsirsergum} ?

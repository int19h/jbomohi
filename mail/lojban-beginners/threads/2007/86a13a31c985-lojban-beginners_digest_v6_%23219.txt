{nai} does not come after brivla. To negate a brivla you either use {na'e}:
{na'e vreji}, or create a lujvo: {nalvreji / nalvei}.

Secondly, cmavo should not be run together with preceding brivla. {vrejinai}
is considered a fu'ivla (loan word) with undefined meaning. In speech this
is distinguished by stress ({VREji nai} vs. {vreJInai}).

Hope this helps.

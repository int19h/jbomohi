On 8/21/07, Vid Sintef <picos.picos@gmail.com> wrote:
>
> {ma} is a place holder for sumti. Can a tense word be an answer to
> such questions? Mightn't the answer be unwantedly the x2 of {cliva}?

Tense words are tags. In {cliva za ma}, {ma} is tagged by {za}, so it is
not filling the x2 of cliva. {za ma} is a separate term. A replacement
answer for {ma} will be a length of time, {za lo mentu be li mu} for
example. Responding {zu [ku]} to the question {za ma} would not be
a strict replacement but it does respond to the sense.

  bacu'e is ugnrammatical... see LRG (CLL), Chapter 10:
----
The only way to combine ``cu'e'' with other tense cmavo is through logical 
connection, which makes a question that pre-specifies some information:

24.9)    do puzi je cu'e sombo le gurni
    You [past] [short] and [when?] sow the grain?
    You sowed the grain a little while ago;
        when else do you sow it?
----

       --gejyspa

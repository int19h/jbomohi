studying Lojban vocabulary. The group has 12 members and 60 sets so far.

These sets include words arranged by classes by Matt Arnold, textbook
lessons by jadenbane, gismu list by Phlogistique and rafsi sets by me.

You're free to join our group by signing up and looking for a group named
"Lojban", and you're invited to create new learning resources for that.

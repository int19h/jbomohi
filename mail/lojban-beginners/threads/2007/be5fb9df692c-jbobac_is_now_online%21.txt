I consider jbobac pretty much usable by now!
And robin also hooked me up with a pretty neat hostname for it:

  http://jbobac.lojban.org/

Please post some stuff! Initiate a conversation! Read out a post you
made on the mailing list and see if it starts a good thread.
If you don't have a microphone you should consider getting one since
they are quite cheap nowadays.

Before you check it out, be sure to get the vorbis codecs from
vorbis.com so that whatever browser plugin you use for all your audio
needs is able to play ogg vorbis and ogg speex without trouble.
You'll find them on

  http://www.vorbis.com/

If you encounter an error, have a question or suggestion please send a
mail my way, reply to this one or visit me (and the others!) in the
#lojban IRC channel on the freenode.

If you find the time and see that the TeamSpeak server is marked as
online in the menu of jbobac, please drop by! Even if there's noone on
the server I have made a little script that pops up a little box on my
desktop whenever someone joins the server. If I notice it and find the
time I will for sure join you then!

Discussion of sounds of lojban speech reminded me that speech synthesis is
built into Mac OS X (actually it has been there since about OS 7, a decade
ago at least) and that it is easily invoked from either Applescript of from
the OS X command line.

Wasted a perfectly good afternoon cobbling together an applescript that
pronounces lojban text. Definitely has rough edges interface-wise and
probably some bugs, but:

http://www.tassos-oak.com/tempp/digits.mp3

is the "Vicki" voice saying {.i no pa re ci...} and

http://www.tassos-oak.com/tempp/tavla.applescript

is the cobbled script for other mac users to play with.

Cheers,

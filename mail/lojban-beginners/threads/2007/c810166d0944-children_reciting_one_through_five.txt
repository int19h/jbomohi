Dear friends,

First of all, I'd like to thank all those who have
contributed mp3 files for my e-learning site very much.
I appreciate your care and help.

I'm still amazed by the variety of these recordings. And
I've decided to publish some of them on my site, at least
temporarily. I've chosen the girl's voice that Matt
provided earlier today (2) and Hans' voice (3). And you
can also compare with my own recordings (1).

Please visit http://www.sondal.net/ljb/ Lesson 1.1

The main purpose of this site is learning/teaching
Lojban. And I would appreciate your views about the
following (views from experienced speakers would be
of particular interest to me):

(1) Do you think the recordings are okay from a
    technical point of view (considering the background
    noise for instance)?

(2) Do you think the variety of the pronunciations is
    an advantage or a disadvantage for a learner, i.e.
    is it interesting or rather confusing to listen to
    three different speakers?

(3) If you had to choose between one of these three
    speakers which one would you choose? And why?

Cheers, Martin

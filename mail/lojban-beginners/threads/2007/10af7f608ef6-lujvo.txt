Reference Grammar Ch.12, Sect.3:
In fact, the relationship will almost always be so close that the predicate
expressing r will be either the seltau or the tertau predicate itself. This
should come as no surprise, given that a word like ``zdani'' in Lojban is a
predicate. Predicates express relations; so when you're looking for a
relation to tie together ``le zdani'' and ``le gerku'', the most obvious
relation to pick is the very relation named by the tertau, ``zdani'': the
relation between a home and its dweller. As a result, the object which fills
the first place of ``gerku'' (the dog) also fills the second place of
``zdani'' (the house-dweller).

On Dec 26, 2007 11:50 AM, <mungojelly@ixkey.info> wrote:

> It's a minor point, but I try to get the denpabu right.


By the way, it's *denpa bu* (pron. *DENpabu*). *denpabu* (pron. *denPAbu*)
is an undefined *fu'ivla*.


> It occurs to me that "mi'e" must require a pause before the name
> unless there's a "doi", is that correct?


Correct. *cmevla* require a pause if they follow anything other than *la*, *
lai*, *la'i* or *doi*.


>
> Also, after "zoi" am I correct that a pause is needed after the first
> instance of the quote, and both before and after the second, as in:
> "zoi gy. something .gy."?
>
> mu'o mi'e .bret.
>
>
A pause is needed after the first delimiter, and before the second. There's
another one after the second *gy.* because it is a letter-word. If a
non-letter-word was used then it won't be needed: "zoi zoi. something .zoi"

*.a'o di'u sidju
mu'o mi'e .djanytyn.

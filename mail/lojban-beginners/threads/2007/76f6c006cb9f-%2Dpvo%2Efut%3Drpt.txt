According to:
<http://www.maestriaenlinguistica.uson.mx/work/resources/LocalContent/55125/10/Tesis%20Ana%20Aurora.pdf>

FUT: futuro (future)
PVO: pasivo (passive)
RPT: reportativo (reportative)

Not sure what the reportative would be in Lojban, perhaps {ti'e}:

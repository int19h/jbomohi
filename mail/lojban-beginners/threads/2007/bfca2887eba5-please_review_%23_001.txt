You want {lenu} after {ki'u} in this case, otherwise {ki'u} governs
only {mi} and then {bilga} is a syntax error.

I like using extra gismu places in some cases rather than the loose
connection with {pe}, so I'd probably change the last bit of your
sentence to {le ckule be fo le panzi be mi tezu'e lenu penmi le ctuca
be ri}.

{ti'u} is normally used with a number, such as {ti'u li so [pe lo cerni]}; I'd 
say {ca lo cerni}.

{cu} is not necessary after {mi}, but is grammatical.

{ki'u} has to be either {iki'ubo} or {ki'u lo nu}.

Instead of {mi bilga lenu klama}, I'd say {ei mi klama}.

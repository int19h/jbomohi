The BPFK is the committee charged with actually finishing the Lojban
baseline (in particular, getting sensible cmavo definitions
finished).  It has had some serious problems with Actually Getting
Shit Done.

We need someone to be our task master.  The job, in brief, consists
of about once a week looking at the current state of affairs and
doing one of the following:

1.  Picking a new section to work on, essentially at random.

2.  Calling a vote on the current section.

3.  Closing a vote on the current section and reporting the results.
This means also doing option #1.

4.  Sending mail to members exhorting them to actually get off their
asses and do some work for a change.

We specifically do *not* want this person to get involved in the
actual BPFK work or discussion!  Everyone who has done that has
gotten bogged down in it and started sucking at the organizational
side.  No Lojban skill whatsoever a *plus*.  :)

If someone can step up to the plate here, it would make a huge
long-term difference to the language.

"this blue thing" is "ti poi blanu", "ti noi blanu" (actually "this, which is 
blue"), or "le vi blanu". "le ti blanu" means "this one's blue thing". "vi" 
is a spatial tense marker; in Lojban, tense indicates both space and time, 
and can even indicate direction of motion. Try figuring out "mo'i ni'a xance 
ko".

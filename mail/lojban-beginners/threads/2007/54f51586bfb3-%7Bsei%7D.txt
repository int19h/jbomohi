  When you say "li'u [is] missed" are you suggesting it's not there in
error?  Remember that li'u is elidble.  It's never necessary at the end
of a bridi.

"sei" takes out of the underlying bridi for the space of one single
bridi (which must not have any sumtis after the brivla).  "cu'u la djan
le zarci" is not a bridi, not is "la djan le zarci".

The original setnence reads like lthis "I am going {John says <-- this
is a note to the reader, a second bridi embedded in the original)} to
the store".

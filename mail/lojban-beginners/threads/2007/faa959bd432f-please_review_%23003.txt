ANDREW PIEKARSKI schrieb:
> Does ku'i always need to be preceded by a pause?
> 
> mu'o mi'e .andrus.

not at all, but ku'i is usually used at the beginning of a new sentence.
new sentences begin with i and i (since it starts with a vowel) needs to
have a pause before it.

mu'o mi'e timos



=== 8 | 2007-04-23T16:59:21Z | "Turniansky, Michael" <MICHAEL.A.TURNIANSKY@saic.com> | 1189a858f8918f43be3f9c7603c73fb4031e7cc0@0456-its-exmp01.us.saic.com | mail/lojban-beginners/cur/1177347561.95cbb91bb9fbf6ff.jbomohi:2,S
  If by "pause" you mean ".i" (sentence marker), the answer is "no, but
ku'i is a discursive.  That means that belongs to the same UI family as
the emotional indicators.  So you can put it anywhere, but the sentence
must still be grammatical with it removed, so for example "mi mlatu ku'i
klama" (My cat, however, came) is perfectly fine."

It changes nothing. {oa} cannot form a diphthong in Lojban,
so in fact it can only be pronounced as two separate syllables.

But even in cases where a diphthong is possible in Lojban,
say {marias}, both pronunciations {ma,ri,as} and {ma,rias}
are valid pronunciations of the same word. You can use the
comma to indicate which one you prefer, but unless it is
your own name and you want to impose your preferred
pronunciations on others, there really is no reason to use
commas. The same goes for capitalization.

1) Can you in line the audio please. It's distracting having the web
browser go to a new page to play audio.

2) Liked the exercises.

3) In the answer section for the exercises you should display the audio
and the word in all cases. This makes it easier to know what the audio
answers are without having to play it. Useful to speed up the exercises
if you just make a simple error. It also emphasises the association
between the oral and the written language.

4) In later section the possible answers in some case should come from
words in previous sections. Not just the section being studied this will
cut down on the isolation of the sections

5) Over all, I liked it. best method I've seen for Lojban training so
far. Excellent for those like myself who don't have time to sit down
evenings to practice, but can grab a few minutes a day at work. It will
also let people get an idea of Lojban without having to dive it the deep
end and see that Lojban is more practical that Kingon.

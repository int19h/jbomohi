What you're thinking of is {ibabo}, but {.e'u ma'a carna fi le berti .ibabo 
ma'a janli le dertu} means that we either actually will or ought to hit the 
ground. What you want is more like {.e'u ma'a carna fi le berti pu lo nu da'i 
janli le dertu} "...before the possibly unrealized event of hitting the 
ground". Or you could say {.e'u ma'a carna fi le berti tezu'e lonu na janli 
le dertu} "...so that we don't hit the ground".

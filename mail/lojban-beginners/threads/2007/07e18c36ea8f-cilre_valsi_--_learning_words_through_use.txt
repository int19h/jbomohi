Using a list of cmavo and gismu in frequency order , I am attempting
to use 8 words a day in example sentences. I will only do 5 days a
week, taking Saturday and Sunday off. There are around 600 cmavo and
1350 gismu for around 2000 basic words. 50weeks*5days*8word is 2000;
so it should take me around a year to get through the list.

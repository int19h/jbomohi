> Are "patlu" also roots, or are tubers different?

A tuber is a horizontal underground stem. The eyes of a potato are buds.

Then there are corms. A corm is also an underground stem, but it is vertical 
and grows leaves on top. Taro and konjaku are corms; both are in the arum 
family.

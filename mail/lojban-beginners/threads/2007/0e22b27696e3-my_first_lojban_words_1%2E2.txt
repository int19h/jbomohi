Are you using kate, kwrite, or gedit to edit it? The one you posted before 
had "日本語" written in UTF-8, and looks right in Konqueror. The charset in the 
meta tag, however, is iso-8859-1, which means that a browser may display nine 
mojibake instead of three kanji. Try changing that to utf-8 and see if it 
works.

Your LANG environment variable should have UTF-8 in it. This tells kate (and 
other programs) to use that encoding by default. I run Ubuntu and that's how 
mine's set.

Pierre

Hi,

I don't think I understand the use of "nu'i." I want to say "I will
return after 10 days." Is it right to say "mi xrukla ba pano lo
djedi"? Also, what does "mi xrukla ba nu'i pano lo djedi" mean?

 To me, "ba pano lo djedi" seems a bit malglico. From what I
understand, the place after ba is supposed to be an event. For
example, "mi xrukla ba lenu do cliva" means "I (will) return after
(the event) you leave" but "pano lo djedi" is not really an event.

Am I right or am I missing something here?

I would say "mi xrukla baza lo djedi be li pano". If you feel that that time 
is short or long, say "zi" or "zu" instead of "za". ("pano lo djedi" 
means "ten days", but doesn't imply that they're consecutive.)

"nu'i" starts a termset, which is not a construct for beginners. The 
afterthought termset conjunction is "ce'e", as in this tonguetwister:
cire cilce carce jarco ce'e reci cilce carce cu jarco
.i cire cilce carce jarco cu jarco reci cilce carce,
.ije'i reci cilce carce cu se jarco cire cilce carce jarco?

As a termset conjunction joins terms, which may na'e sumti, you can make 
nonsense such as "mi fu ku ce'e na ku fe le mlatu cu viska".

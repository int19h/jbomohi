=== 2 | 2012-06-18T01:22:31Z | Jacob Errington <nictytan@gmail.com> | caoq8vdv6jlp7oonqprhgdjknypzfs1jk6odfhb1uc-rdtwi1-a@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-beginners/cur/1339982551.a1c5a78703bf95f8.jbomohi:2,S
{.ije ko'a tirna lo voksa be ko'e noi krixa cusku lo cmene be ko'a mo'ito'o
lo foldi mo'ifa'a lo drata foldi zi'e noi simxu co jungau fi lo nu se
klamu'o lo bloti}

is my attempt. The spatial tense might be weird; I'm no expert on them, not
to mention that it simply may not be smudra.
Also, the {noi} attaching to {ko'e} is intentional, as voices cannot cusku
as they are ve cusku. However, a se voksa can be a cusku. The usage in
English is metaphorical, I'd say.

mu'o mi'e la tsani

=== 5 | 2012-06-23T23:14:50Z | Jonathan Jones <eyeonus@gmail.com> | camkwhj0f-kvg+rubnahbg5jsekcn3etwudnhvgwlygnvxoxr6g@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-beginners/cur/1340493290.60463fba2d462851.jbomohi:2,S
For the first sentence, The Borg are identifying themselves by name. This
means that the gismu {cmene} is needed: "x1 is the name of x2...". Since
the name is first, in order to keep the order of the English intact we have
to flip {cmene} using {se}, i.e. {se cmene} or {selcme}. The x1 of {cmene}
(or the x2 of {selcme}) is a name, and so is a quoted word, which means we
use {zo}, at least for single word names. Finally, The Borg are announcing
that /they/ are the Borg, the x2 of {cmene}. We can use either {mi} or
{mi'a}, the difference being that {mi'a} explicitly excludes the
addressee(s), whereas {mi} does not. So, we have:

{mi'a selcme zo.borg.}

For "We are the Borg".

the Starfleet vessels are not boats.

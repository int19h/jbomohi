StackExchange provides a nice framework for language related questions for 
Lojban and it already has a lot of useful capabilities in place.
There are other languages doing fairly well on the platform and Lojban 
would be a good addition.

Show your support by going over to:
http://area51.stackexchange.com/proposals/46646/lojban-language-usage?phase=definition&conf=1
and checking it out.

I don't know if it will catch on since the Lojban community is fairly small 
but it is worth a shot

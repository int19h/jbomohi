We had a discussion yesterday with one Russian guy interested in lojban.
What we decided is that {kibypapri} is not what it's intended for.
Our eye scans some surface and reads information from it. This is the most 
general definition of any written text or media embedded into it.
A web-page is what can be called {kibro seltidysfe} as it may contain not 
only text and it can be not only two-dimensional (in future, perhaps).
On the other hand {pelji seltidysfe} might be a good term for a sheet of 
paper with a written text.

Note that {kibypapri} has two noticeable bugs.
1. it references to {papri} that can be only physical
2. we can say either {papri} or {kibypapri} but not "page" in general

One more note.
{ckupau} is a page of a book and therefore doesn't apply to web-pages which 
can be single.

Hey Lojbananas, we're attempting to develop a standardised test for rating 
proficiency in Lojban. We would like to have a rating system, much like the 
Japanese Language Proficiency Test and similar nationally sponsored 
language standards, to provide specific goals and targeted study for 
learners of the language. We hope that people will study together for 
specific test levels and this will provide more in-language communication 
with similar-level speakers.

However, we need a bit of community assistance. We need to discuss ideas 
for the formatting of these tests, including what sorts of grammar, 
vocabulary, and types of tests are included and for what levels they are 
included. Additionally, we need web designers and database engineers to 
help design the back-end of the system.

Suggestions are welcome in the comment box as always and people who are 
more interested can join the LBCK mailing list.

https://groups.google.com/forum/?fromgroups#!forum/lojban-lbck

Thank you so much for your suggestions and assistance!

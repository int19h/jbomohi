This is an audience participation exercise.

The point of this exercise is for nintadni to get some practice writing
Lojban, while at the same time having some amusement and/or enjoyment in
the posts put here.

I don't know how prevalent "Yo Mamma" jokes are around the world, but at
least in the areas I've lived, especially where a large number of
African-Americans live, they're an enjoyable, cheap pastime that produce
lots of laughs- as long as no one takes them seriously, of course.

"Yo Mamma" jokes always start with "Yo Mamma so fat", or "Yo Mamma so
stupid", or "Yo Mamma so poor", or some such other insulting thing,
followed by an obviously impossible reason for why the mother is such.

Like so:
"Yo Mamma so fat she had a dream about marshmallows and when she woke up
her pillows were gone."

Let's make some "Yo Mamma" jokes in Lojban!

I'll be the first, since I already made one:

{lo do mamta cu traji plana lodu'u lo cnita be ri cu se xabju lo zdacau
lanzu}

I really want to see some funny stuff, people! Don't be shy!

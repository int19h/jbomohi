The description of "kakne" is "x1 is able to do/be/capable of doing/being 
x2 (event/state) under conditions x3 (event/state)". Does "being" imply 
that "kakne" can be used without "nu" after it, and instead using a simple 
sumti (as I did above).

Probably a simpler example is {mi kakne lo tavla be fo la lojban.} - "I 
can-be a talker in-language Lojban" .i xu di'u xagytadji lo nu pilno zo

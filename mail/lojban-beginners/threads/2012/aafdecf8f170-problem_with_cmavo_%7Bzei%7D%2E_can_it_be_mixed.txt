=== 2 | 2012-04-15T11:42:35Z | Jonathan Jones <eyeonus@gmail.com> | camkwhj3sjiuhzk53m5dcx9gem3-zqe_cjzad2mcyybaedscudg@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-beginners/cur/1334490155.886a62003d3c6206.jbomohi:2,S
"zeipagbu" can't be a lujvo. It will /always/ break into "zei pagbu",
because a lujvo must have a consonant cluster within the first 5 letters.
"zeipagbu" doesn't have a cluster until the 6th letter. In order to make a
lujvo of "zekri pagbu", you have to use the /other/ rafsi of xekri, "zer".

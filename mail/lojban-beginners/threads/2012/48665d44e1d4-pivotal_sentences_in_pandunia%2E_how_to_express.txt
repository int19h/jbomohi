Here is an example if a sentence in Pandunia 
<http://www.kupsala.net/risto/pandunia/english/lesson02.html>language.
mi peti ti tshau ta. = I ask you to say hello to him. 
parsing: [I ask you greet he/she/it]

So the second "sumti" *ti *in *mi peti ti *becomes the first "sumti" of the 
next construction "ti tshau ta".

I wonder whether it's possible to do so in Lojban. May {be} or {.i ri} can 
do the trick

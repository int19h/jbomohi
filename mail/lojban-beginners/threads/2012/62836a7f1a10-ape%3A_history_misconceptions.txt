This week's APE is all about commonly believed historical facts which are
/completely untrue/.

There are no rules for this one, other than it has to be something that a
great many people /think/ is true, but actually is completely wrong.

For example:

{la.calumbys. pu na troci lonu je'urja'o lodu'u la terdi cu bolci .i lo
sego'i cu se djuno .i cy. troci lonu tolyli'a la.indias. lo stici}
"Columbus wasn't trying to prove the world was round. This was already
known. He was trying to get to India by going West."

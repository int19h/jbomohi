That said, here's the latest APE:

Come up with words of wisdom for Lojban. There's a specific word for this,
somewhat related to clichè, idiom, psalm, koan, etc., but I can't remember
what it is. Whoever tells me first gets bonus points. :)

You can make something up, you can translate something from another
language, whatever. The only requirement is that it be one or two jufra of
wisdom an old jbopre would believably impart to grandkids.

To start things off:

{lonu lo srera na ve cilre cu se jalge lonu rapli li ci'i}
"A mistake not learned from is repeated endlessly."

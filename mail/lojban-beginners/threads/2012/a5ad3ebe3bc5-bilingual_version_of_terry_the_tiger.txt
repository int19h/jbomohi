You have a typesetting error on page 5:

tu'a
The bridi implied by. tu’a can be translated as “some abstraction
associated with...”, or more colloquially, “some
stuff about...”. tu’a is easily the most popular way of dealing with
abstractions you wish weren’t there in Lojban;
Lojban sentences using it come out fairly similar to the natural language
sentences without abstractions that
we’re used to seeing. So the usual Lojban for “Jyoti is interesting” is
*tu’a la djiotis. cinri*

"*tu’a la djiotis. cinri*" should be on the same line as the rest of the
sentence it is a part of. This would have the additional benefit of
removing the one line 7th page.

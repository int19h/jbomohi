Should {ko} be used in sentences marked with {.e'o}? It's something I see 
often (and usually do myself), but {do} is generally used with {.e'u} in 
particular. My thought was that, because attitudinals beginning with "a" 
and "e" (and some "i") show that the marked text is a hypothetical 
assertion, not a real one, using {do} would be the correct thing for the 
hypothetical world (in which la'e {do} is actually doing what is said in 
the sentence). .iepei

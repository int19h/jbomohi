I have the same problem. I try to pronounce my r's more frontally (as

course, the insertion of a buffer vowel between the r and the x.

it extraordinarily hard to differentiate [i], [@], [I], [Y].)

Hi Alan,

I am learning several languages, but am no professional on lojban.
However I would suggest that you differentiate by the level of stress
on the "throat sound". As I recall French stresses the "r" intensely,
so for lojban you might want to pronounce it softer, as has been
suggested. No need to go for the English, but the German "r" is kind
of soft. The "x" I remember as being pronounced as in Arabic, where it
should sound like, how can I put this, you were gathering salive in
order to spit. It is not a nice way of explaining the sound, but it´s
something we can all imagine :) Only that you open the throat like
saying the "r" in "rouge" without "rolling" it too much.

For the "xr" it is "saliva" plus "rolling" the "r". Serious throat
clearing, if you may :)
J`espére que ce s´entend raisonable pour toi :)

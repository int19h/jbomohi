mi'e la fagri cipni


mi djica lo nu mi klama lo zarci be lo cukta
I want the event of my going to a store that sells books.
I want to go to a book store.

If I wanted to get to the third place of "djica" here I believe that
I would need two terminators here: either "ku ku" or "be'o ku".  The
first terminator, either "ku" or "be'o", terminates "lo cukta"; the
second, "ku" in both cases, terminates "lo zarci ...".

mu'o mi'e la fagri cipni

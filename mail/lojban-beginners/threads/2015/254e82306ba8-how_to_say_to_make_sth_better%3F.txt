> Hi friends. I am new in lojban.
> I wonder how to say "to make something better" in lojban.
>

If you need literally a verb then {jai gau zenba fai SOMETHING lo ni xamgu}

E.g.
mi jai gau zenba fai lo dinju lo ni xamgu
I make the house better.

Of course we can have other rephrasings not necessarily imitating English
word order:
gau mi lo dinju cu zenba lo ni xamgu
which would mean the same, although it sounds (although doesn't mean)
something like "due to me the house increases in being good"

There is also a verb
xagzengau = xa (agent) improves x2, makes x2 better ...

mi xagzengau lo dinju = I improve the house.

although it requires learning one more word where is in the previous
examples I used only core vocabulary.


Can anybody tell me?

There are at least six ways to say this, of which gleki only showed two:

   (1) mi gasnu lo nu X zenba lo ka xamgu
       "I bring about that X increases in goodness."

   (2) mi gasnu lo nu X xagze'a
       "I bring about that X improves."

   (3) mi xagzengau X
       "I improve X."

   (4) gau mi X xagze'a
       "Brought about by me, X improves."

   (5) mi jai gau xagze'a fai X
       "I am the cause of improvement of X."

   (6) mi jai gau zenba fai X lo ka xamgu
       "I am the cause of increase of X in goodness."

They all mean the same, and only differ in structure. It is a matter of 
personal preference, but (1-6) are all correct.

mi'e la selpa'i mu'o

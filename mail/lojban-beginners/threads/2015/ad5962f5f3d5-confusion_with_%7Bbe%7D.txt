In short in corresponds to English "of" that is attached to nouns.

{sazri} = to drive
{sazri lo karce} = to drive a car
{lo sazri} = driver
{lo sazri be lo karce} = driver of a car

http://mw.lojban.org/lmw/The_Crash_Course_(a_draft)#Places_for_nouns

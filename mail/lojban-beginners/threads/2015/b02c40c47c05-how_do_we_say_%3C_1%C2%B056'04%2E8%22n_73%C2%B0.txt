The final version 'must' have the digits 1, 56, 4.8, then 73, 32, 23.2, and
some direct indications of N and E. No conversion to decimal or whatever
the second version xorxes gave is acceptable. The only questions are what
the separators are and the actual form used for the indication of degrees,
minutes, and second, and the direction indicators.

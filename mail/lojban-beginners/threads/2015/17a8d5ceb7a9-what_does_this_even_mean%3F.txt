coi ro do

I just realised I forgot {lo nu} when writing the text for a song (which 
has already been partly recorded and I don't want to redo it) which leaves 
me with these two sentences:
.i co'u piro lo munje cu culno lo melbi xrula
.i co'u piro lo munje cu sasfoi lo melbi se peisku

(what I meant was ".i co'u lo nu piro lo munje cu culno lo melbi xrula" etc 
with the intended meaning "Until the whole world is full of beautiful 
flowers")

According to camxes this parses as something like {co'u (piro lo munje)} cu 
{culno (lo melbi xrula)}
Is it possible to get a meaningful English translation of this sentence as 
it is? Like "At the end of the whole world it is filled with beautiful 
flowers" or "The endpoint of the whole world is filled with beautiful 
flowers"?

Cheers,
Erik

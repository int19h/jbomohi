I have just read through the CLL version of Terry the Tiger 
(http://mw.lojban.org/papri/la_.teris._po%27u_lo_tirxu_cu_vitke_zi%27o_le_barda_tcadu) 
having had to look up a lot of words. It left me with some questions that 
la sutsis or la jbovlaste couldn't answer:

1. Why all the "bo"s with cmavo in the beginning of bridis? For example 
.isemu'ibo/.ibabo/.iki'ubo. Don't these cmavo take the following sumti as 
an argument anyways? What have I missed?
2. jinvi - Is jinvi used for both having an opinion about something and 
believing something? Coming from Swedish where these concepts are quite 
separate, it seems like malglico, but perhaps it isn't.
3. When looking up pi'egre in la sutsis it has 6 places. How do we deal 
with this?

di'ai co'o

Not directly related to the post, but rather to la sutsis. How can changes
be made to it? I entered my standard look-up word (whiskey) in the Eng-Loj
section, and got only whiskey coffees. It turns out that the word "uiski"
has as its definition "x1 is whisk(e)y distilled from x2." That "(e)" makes
the word unfindable by normal search, and it makes "whisk" a separate
'word'.

stevo

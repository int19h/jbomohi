Dear Sirs:

		I am very new to languages and lojban, in particular.

		In reading different pages on various websites I saw that lojban has a self-segregating morphology.

		But I have a question concerning how this would work.

		If I have a fragment- cvccvcvcv-, Which way will this break apart and why?

		Will it be -cvccv-cv-cv  or  -cv-ccvcv-cv ?


		Thanks,

It depends on the stress. If there is a single stress on the penultimate
syllable of your fragment, then the first cv falls off and everything else
stays together.

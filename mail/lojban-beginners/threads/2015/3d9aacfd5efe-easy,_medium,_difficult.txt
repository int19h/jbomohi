it's a scale. {no'e frili} or {no'e nandu} would mean "medium in
difficulty".
{cnano} means "to be normal, average" not necessarily in difficulty but you
can also say
{cnano lo ka nandu} - "normal in its difficulty" or {cnano lo ka frili} -
"normal in its easiness", not exactly what "medium" means.

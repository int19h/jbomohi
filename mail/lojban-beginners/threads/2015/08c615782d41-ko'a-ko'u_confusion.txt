  To amplify, just think of it as the word "she (or he, or it)".  That
causes all kinds of trouble in English like -- "Tony was Jack's father.  He
wears bright red pants.  Yesterday went with Mary to the store"  Care to
guess which person the "he" is talking about in sentence two?  How about
sentence three?  The same?  Different?  Well, lojban offers you a way to
disambiguate those references with 10 different flavors of he/she/it
(really many more, since you can use letterals or any arbitrary label at
all).

or you jsut want to know how to say "and" in Lojban?
The first question is a well known limitation of jbovlaste in being mostly
a Lojban to "another language" dictionary interface.
I'm solving this issue by providing an English to Lojban dictionary:
http://mw.lojban.org/papri/L17-04

As for "and" most often words for it are {ce} and {e}.

mi ce do casnu lo mlatu = I and you discuss cats (i.e. we discuss it with
each other)

mi e do casnu lo mlatu = I and you discuss cats. I  other words, I discuss
cats, and you discuss cats. Maybe, separately, i.e. with different people.

In some dialects instead of {ce} you may use {jo'u}, instead of {e} you may
use {je}.

As for the most standard dialect of Lojban the CLL, chapter 14
<http://lojban.github.io/cll/> logical connectives are explained in full.

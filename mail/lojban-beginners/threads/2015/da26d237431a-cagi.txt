I suggest "contracts" rather than "is compressed" for "tolba'o", because
"is compressed" suggests an agent.

The Lojban doesn't strictly require that ko'a na du ko'e, although it can
be expected. You could instead say "ca gi ko'a tolba'o gi na'e bo ko'a
banro".

mu'o mi'e xorxes

There are lots of options. Expressing the relationship as merely being
simultaneity isn't really enough, and using definite (if unspecified) sumti
is somewhat off, in my opinion. One option is:

ro nu da banro cu rinka de poi nu di tolba'o

If you don't know how to write the prenex expansion for this, ask.

mi'e la latro'a mu'o

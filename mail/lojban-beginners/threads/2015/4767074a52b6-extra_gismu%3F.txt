There have been no gismu officially added, but some people have proposed 
ones for experimental purposes.  While the gismu and cmavo lists were 
baselined, they are on wiki pages and this has meant that people have 
modified the baseline list without official authorization, but there is 
relatively little that we can do about it at this point (we could put up 
the old baseline list, but people would likely still modify it).  It is 
possible that experimental gismu that have been added are marked as 
experimental in their definitions; I know that this has been done in the 
cmavo list.

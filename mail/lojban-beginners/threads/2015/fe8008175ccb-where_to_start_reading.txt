For the dotside point, in print we can specify word boundaries with spaces.
Thus the dots can be safely omitted, but they are still included in the
correct pronunciation. In speech you need the dots, period, because without
them {la .latros. mo} (What does Latros do?) can become {la la .tros. mo}
(Mo, who is associated with Tros, ...)

mi'e la latro'a mu'o

  Speaking for myself, as author of Terry the Tiger, if there is a dot
before the T in the title, that's because someone added it (probably in the
migration from the old wiki to the new).  I certainly never had it.
Dotside has never been considered official, and until it is, I won't use
it.  All that being said, as latro'a said, it doesn't make a difference in
print since the periods are always optional if there is a space in place of
them.

  I happen to immodestly think my story is a great beginning text...

For the dotside point, in print we can specify word boundaries with spaces.
Thus the dots can be safely omitted, but they are still included in the
correct pronunciation. In speech you need the dots, period, because without
them {la .latros. mo} (What does Latros do?) can become {la la .tros. mo}
(Mo, who is associated with Tros, ...)

mi'e la latro'a mu'o

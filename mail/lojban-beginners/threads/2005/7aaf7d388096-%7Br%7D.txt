lojbo {r} can be rendered as any recognisably rhotic sound. Thus, IPA 
[ɾ] (Maori, Greek), [ʀ] (French, Northumberland English), [ɹ] (Standard 
British, New Zealand, Australian, United States and Canadian English) 
and [r] (Russian) can can all represent the lojbo {r} sound.

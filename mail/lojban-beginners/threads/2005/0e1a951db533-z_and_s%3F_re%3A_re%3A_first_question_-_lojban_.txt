The "j" sound, ZH, is voiced and the "c" sound, SH, is not. The difference is the same as the difference between S and Z. Or between F and V. Your vocal cords move with ZH, Z or V but with SH, S or F the sound is just the friction of blown air.
- epkat

coi .adam. .e cindras

Rather than wait for Lojban sound files, it might be faster to look on
the web for pages on How to Pronounce English (or French, or Russian).
The Lojban {c} and {j} are found in all those languages as well as
others. There are probably sound files for those already on the web.

The IPA symbols are (& I hope these encode):
Lojban {c} IPA / ʃ /
Lojban {j} IPA / ʒ /

Good luck!

a long fall sounds better than a big fall in english (despite the original being "Humpty Dumpty had a big fall"), however looking up the definitions they are extremely similar:

 clani       cla      
 long                                      
  x1 is long in dimension/direction x2 (default longest dimension) by measurement standard x3 

 barda       bra      
 big                   'large'             
  x1 is big/large in property/dimension(s) x2 (ka) as compared with standard/norm x3  

Seems that the difference is that clani is more about a measured dimension of a physical object, whereas barda is more about describing things (not necessarily objects - 'property/dimension(s)' )that are relatively large, so i think perhaps barda is the better choice for this?

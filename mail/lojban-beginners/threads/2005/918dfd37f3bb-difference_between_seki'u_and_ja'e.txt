{seki'u} deals with reasons and justifications specifically. {ja'e}, based 
on {jalge}, deals with any kind of general result from a process or action, 
including physical causes, motivations, what have you. Or, rather, {seki'u} 
says that whatever happened was a justification for its sumti, while {ja'e} 
merely says that whatever happened was a result of its sumti.

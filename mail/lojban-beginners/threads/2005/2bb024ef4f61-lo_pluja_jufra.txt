That's not a lot of context.  It doesn't give us any idea who "lo
finti" is.  Let's try the actual paragraph:

> ni'o sy te preti lo nu terpa lo tilju ratni festi 

S asks about fearing the heavy atomic waste ("nuclear waste" was my
goal there).

> .i py cmila gi'e cusku lu na zasti 

P smiles and says, "Doesn't exist.

> .i lo cmima pu zu finti lo vreji poi skicu ra li'u ju'e 

The members long ago invented the records which describe it."

> lu .ue sai y xu sfasa su'o da lo nu punji ra ti li'u ju'e 

"Holy shit. Umm.  Was there a punisher (should be "nu sfasa", I
think) of someone/thing for putting it here?"

i.e. "Did someone get in trouble for it anyways?".

This is important, because now we know who "lo finti" is: the
creator of the records that showed nuclear waste at Hunter's Point.

> lu na go'i 

"Nope.

> .i .io lo finti cu jai gau simlu lo ka ba lo nu punji
> cu galfi fi lo te vimcu be lo gasnu li'u ju'e 

"It's really cool; the inventors made it seem that after the
putting, there was modifying into something from which the doers
were removed."

i.e. "The people who made up the records also made it look like the
records were altered to hide who put the nuclear waste here".  I do
remember having some trouble with that sentence, so if it's really
totally incomprehensible, I'll try to re-work it again.

> lu .ua mutce certu li'u ju'e 

"Wow.  Well done."

> lu mi tugni .i ku'i pu ku mi cmibi'o li'u ju'e 

"Yep.  But that was long before my time.

> lu .i ku'i ma mukti lo nu tcica gi'e nai fu'i terve'u lo dinju
> li'u ju'e 

"But why the deception, instead of just the simple move of buying
the building?"

> lu mi'a mutce djica lo nu no da vitke li'u ju'e 

"We very much prefer that no-one visit us."

> lu .u'i ru'e li'a sai li'u

"Heh.  No shit."

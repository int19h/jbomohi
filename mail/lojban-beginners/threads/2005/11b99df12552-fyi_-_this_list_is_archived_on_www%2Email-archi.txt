Hi all,

as of know - probably starting with this email - the lojban-beginners 
mailing list will be archived at http://www.mail-archive.com/.

The Mail Archive is a free service that has quite convenient search 
capabilities and is used by more than 3400 lists. Many of them are from 
the Open Source Software (OSS) community, but there are lots of others 
as well.

Unfortunately, they cannot import old archives, so only new messages 
will get archived there.

Maybe this will be helpful for anyone.

Regards,

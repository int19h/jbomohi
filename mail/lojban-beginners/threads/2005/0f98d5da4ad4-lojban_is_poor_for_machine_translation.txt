"Loglans are poor MT interlinguas" - 
http://www.rickharrison.com/language/mtil.html

Most of you would probably know of this article, which argues that the 
syntax of Lojban is overly complex; sumti place structures are too rigid and 
counter-intuitive; that an artificial language would be more difficult to 
translate into natural languages as opposed to natural language to natural 
language translation; that required pauses are unnatural and impractical; 
and that the compound words are difficult for ML to translate. 

What is your view of this argument?

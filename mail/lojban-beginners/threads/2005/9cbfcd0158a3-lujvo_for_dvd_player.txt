I would suggest dropping {kelci} from your plans completely.

The lujvo list has this to offer:

snaku'ocukca'a
sance+skuro+cukla+cabra:  record player:   x1 = cabra1 (apparatus), x2
= cabra3 (apparatus operator)

So that might be a good starting point.

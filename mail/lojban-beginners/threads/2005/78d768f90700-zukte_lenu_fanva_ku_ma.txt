now, and I'm beginning to wonder what people generally go for when they 
translate into Lojban. So far, my attempts have been pretty straightforward 
renderings that, when translated, yield something that comes very close to 
the original English (including word order, etc.) But I've seen Lojban 
translations that barely resemble the source text at all, and magically 
become very terse (for Lojban). So do people go for closeness to English, or 
throw their translations into the furthest reaches of Lojban? Just 
wondering.

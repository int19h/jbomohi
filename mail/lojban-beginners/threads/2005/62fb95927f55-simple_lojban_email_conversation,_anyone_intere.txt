Hi,

Let's try to have a nice simple bilingual conversation, i.e. we give the 
lojban and (litteral) english versions of everything we say, which we'll 
try to keep short, so as not to get discouraged. Attitudinals are 
indicated by [] and inlined comments in (). I chose an ASSIMIL kind of 
fictive starting point, two persons on a phone or something. Corrections 
are welcome !

.a'u do mo

[interest] What about you ?

.ui mi bazi cliva le mi briju .i .ei baziku mi ze'a denpa .oi vi lo 
sorprekarcytcana

[happy] I shall soon leave the office of mine. [obliged] Soon afterwards 
I  wait a while [complaint] at the the many-person-car-station (i.e. bus 
station).

N.B. I found the "bus station" lujvo in
http://www.lojban.org/tiki//tiki-index.php?page=Getting+Around&highlight=bus

Notice how I spell your name. You spelled it koe-leen. (Actually, you might 
be correct, because I've heard of one or two Colins pronounced koelin.)
You misunderstood what you have read. "Topic", "Colin", "hot", make an 
"ahhh" sound. In Lojban this is always represented by the character "a". 
(Lojban has no other "a" sounds such as the "a" in "Matt" or "cat.") English 
speakers usually slur the "o" sound in "low", "crow", "toe", to blend into 
the "oooo" sound (from flute, root, crude) on the end of the vowel, and that 
is why "o" is almost always pronounce impurely by English speakers. Listen 
in the broadcast to how I try to pronouce "o" with what appears (to English 
speakers) to be a foreign accent. This is a pure "o". Pronounce Lojban 
"Lowzhbahn" except without the "oo" as in "you" creeping into the end of the 
letter "o".

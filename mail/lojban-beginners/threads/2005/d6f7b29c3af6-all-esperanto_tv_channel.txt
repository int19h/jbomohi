http://internacia.tv/ is apparently the first-ever TV channel with
programming all in Esperanto. It launched online earlier this month.
Their English language info page is at
http://internacia.tv/modules/sections/index.php?op=viewarticle&artid=22.
According to a wikinews article, there was a previous attempt at
Esperanto web-broadcasting that never got off the ground. I learned of
it from this post on Slashdot's Spanish sister site. Also interesting,
though their video stream is in Windows media format, they have
instructions for Linux users to watch with MPlayer (apparently Linux
is "Linukso" in Esperanto).

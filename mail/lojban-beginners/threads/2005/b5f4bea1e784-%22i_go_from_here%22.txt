Many cmavo in Lojban are used for conversion - sumti to selbri, selbri to modals and so on.
{vi} is a tense and cannot perform as a standard sumti by itself.
So I cannot say {*mi klama fi vi} for "I go from here".

Is there a cmavo in Lojban converting {vi} in the way it can take place as a sumti?
In other words, is there a "tense to sumti" conversion in Lojban?

I'm interested in this question exclusively, not in how to say "I go from here" in Lojban by other means.

Hi and welcome!

Remember that "." in Lojban marks pauses in speech but doesn't have any
syntactic function -- specifically, it doesn't separate sentences or other
utterances, so you'll probably want an ".i" in between your sentences: {mi
rinsa do .i mi ninpe'i do .i mi gleki}.

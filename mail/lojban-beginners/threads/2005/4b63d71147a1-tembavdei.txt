Has anyone ever tried to use the lujvo { tembavdei }?

tembavdei
temci+balvi+djedi: anniversary day: x1 = djedi1 (full day) = temci3 (end 
time), x2 = djedi3 (full day standard), x3 = balvi2 (before), x4 = temci2 
(start time)
 
I'm trying to write "It will soon be the sixtieth anniversary [day] of the 
attack on Hiroshima."
{ ba zi tembavdei fi lo nu gunta la xirocymas kei lo nanca be li xa no }

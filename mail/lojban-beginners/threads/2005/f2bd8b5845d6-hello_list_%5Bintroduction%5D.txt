Short version: hi! I'm new. I'm gonna learn Lojban and ask questions about it, so I hope you'll be a nice list :p

Long version:
It's long since I came upon the thought that natural languages are clumsy and ineffecient and that something should be done about it, and I soon heard a bit about Esparanto.

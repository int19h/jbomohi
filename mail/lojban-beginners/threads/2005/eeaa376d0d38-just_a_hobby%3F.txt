I don't know what the future holds for widespread adoption of Lojban. 
Neither do I know if it will yeild measurable scientific insights. I enjoy 
speaking a language that has so much functional elegance. In the real world, 
we have to be compatible with everybody else even if the existing monopoly 
is something defective. For me I like to imagine what native Lojban speakers 
would be like in a world where longstanding traditions are discarded for 
that which has the most intrinsic merit. I go into more detail on my page 
about why I am learning Lojban:
http://www.nemorathwald.com/Why_Learning_Lojban.htm

Minor point: {ca lo ba nanca be li so'i} is
"at some point in many future years". {ca} only indicates
that two events have some time-point in common.

For durations, use ze'a: {ze'a lo ba nanca be li so'i}
is "during many future years", or "for many years to come".

.i mi'a pacna (lo nu (do (gleki lo se jinga) gi'e (nelci ri ca lo ba

Pacna ("hope"):
    x1 is "mi'a", "we"
    x2 is the rest of the sentence ("lo nu do gleki..."), an event.

gleki ("happy"):
    x1 is "do", "you"

    x1 is "do", because the "gi'e" is scoped back to only just before

Something just occurred to me about Lojban's variegated question words. I 
mean, I've thought about it for some time, but a possible use for what I've 
been thinking of just struck me. Anyway. Ever since I read the Lojban for 
Beginners lesson on question words, {ma mo ma} has been in my head, just 
kind of floating around. The only use I could think of for it was a general 
"HUH?!?" kind of expression. But it just occurred to me that that'd be a 
great way to say, "What's up?" "What's going on?" and the like. Does anyone

ways to say "How goes it?" I'll be glad to hear what you think.

Well, more specifically, it means something like "You are what/are doing
what?" So it can mean "Who are you?" or "What are you doing?" or
anything in between. Lojban makes little distinction between doing and
being unless you intentionally add that meaning.

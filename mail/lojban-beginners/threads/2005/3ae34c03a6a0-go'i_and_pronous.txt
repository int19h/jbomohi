Yes.

>or will I be saying "do prami mi" ?
> I mean, when I say go'i, are mi and do mapped to the meaning they have when said by me or they still function as if said by the person who is speaking to me?

{go'i} retains the meaning, not the words.

If you want to retain the words, then you can do so with {ra'o}:

A: mi do prami
B: go'i ra'o

A: I love you
B: Me too (i.e. and I love you)

If B didn't use {ra'o} there they would be saying "yes, you do".

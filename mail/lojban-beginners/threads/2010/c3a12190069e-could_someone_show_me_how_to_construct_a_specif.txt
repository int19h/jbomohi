On Sat, Sep 25, 2010 at 01:15:12AM -0700, Sage wrote:
> I want to make "bartu jdice zgana tadji" into a luvjo. I read the
> Reference Grammar but I can't figure it out. Could someone go
> through a step-by-step process of making it into a lujvo?

Step 1: Get jvocuhadju

  It's part of jbofihe; there's a Debian package.

Step 2: Run jvocuhadju

  $ jvocuhadju bartu jdice zgana tadji
    Possible rafsi for input words :
    bar
    jdi
    zga
    tadji
    --------------------
    Score  Lujvo
    --------------------
    13795 barjdizgatadji

Step 3: Coin a fu'ivla instead :)

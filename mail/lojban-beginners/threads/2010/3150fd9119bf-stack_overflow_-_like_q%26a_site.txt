I just discovered that someone porposed to open a StackOverflow-like
area dedicated to Lojban.

I just endorsed it. Of course we are very very short of votes to have
it created. In case you feel it's a good idea, just head to:
http://area51.stackexchange.com/proposals/21080/lojban and follow the
initiative.

There's also another initiative for Planned Languages in general, so
it might have sense to join the forces with others to have the
visibility that a SO-like site can give:
http://area51.stackexchange.com/proposals/12409/planned-languages

For those unaware of Stack Overflow, it's one of the most successful
site of Q&A about programming. Usually the quality of the answer is
extremely good and questions get answered in minutes (if not seconds!)

Similar sites exist for hardware questions and many other topics.

> I have just discovered an error in the Smart.fm gismu lesson. This error
> did not originally exist, and was placed there at some later date.
>
> The definition for {cusku} on Smart.fm is currently
>
> "x1 says x2 for audience x3 via medium x4"
>
> This is incorrect. The English word "says" carries with it the implied
> meaning that it was verbally spoken. {cusku} is any form of expression, not
> merely speech, which is {ba'usku}.
>
> Originally, and correctly, {cusku} was
>
> "x1 expresses x2 for audience x3 via medium x4"
>
> Unfortunately, I am currently unable to fix this problem.
>
> It is my hope that Robin may be able to determine the method neccessary to
> fix this, when he has the time and inclination to do so.
>
> It is possible that the only way in which to fix it is to delete the item
> and re-add it with the correct definition.
>

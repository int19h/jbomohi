Your best bet is to drop by IRC http://www.lojban.org/tiki/Lojban%20IRC and
ask for a lesson.

There's also a lesson that was created based off of several teaching
sessions on IRC.  You can find it here:
https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mjMlyQfAd_yVLv6Npp_cqshQpa9hxksqohrZtLoSp6o/edit?hl=en&authkey=CIKOt4IH&pli=1#

Also, it's a little out-dated, but
http://www.lojban.org/tiki/tiki-index.php?page=Learning has some links to
some good material.

And of course, there's also this mailing list/google group.
http://groups.google.com/group/lojban-beginners/?pli=1

semester of Latin. I got funny associations when I got to words like "verba"

recommendation to people learning multiple languages at a time: Make
flashcards with words in one on one side and the other in the other. So for
you, Russian on one side and Lojban on the other. I didn't try it, but I've
been told it works very well.

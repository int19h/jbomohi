On Thu, Dec 9, 2010 at 8:02 AM, tijlan <jbotijlan@gmail.com> wrote:
> In zasni gerna
> (http://www.lojban.org/tiki/tiki-index.php?page=zasni+gerna+cenba+vreji),
> NOI can directly modify a selbri. I'd like to learn more about it.
>
> In "mi pu dansu noi ke'a nandu", what does "ke'a" refer to? "lo nu dansu" or
> "lo nu mi pu dansu" or something else?

Something else. It's the x1 of dansu, the same as in its existing use:

    lo dansu poi melbi
    "The dancer who is pretty."

    ta dansu poi melbi
    "That's the dancer who is pretty."

For the sense you mean, you'd have to say:

    mi pu dansu noi nandu ke'a

mu'o mi'e xorxes

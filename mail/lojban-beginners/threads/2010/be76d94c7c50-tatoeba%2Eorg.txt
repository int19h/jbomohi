This is really cool! I've been thinking for a while about a site with this
format to make lojban example sentences... but this is even better!

Can someone translate five sentences into lojban so we can get it added to
the site?
 I'd like to request a new language. What do I have to
do?<http://tatoeba.org/eng/faq#new-language>

*1)* Send us an email (team@tatoeba.fr) and indicate in the title the
language(s) that you would like us to add.

*2)* In your email, tell us what icon we can use for each requested
language. It does not necessarily have to be the flag of a country. We just
want a picture that people can easily associate to the language. Keep in
mind that our icons are only 30x20 pixels.

You do not have create the icon yourself. For graphical consistency, it's
better that we do it. Simply send us (or link us) an image from which we
will create the icon.

*3)* Translate five sentences into your language(s), and indicate in your
email the ID's of your sentences. The language of your sentences will either
not be detected, or will be mis-detected, but this is not an issue. You will
be able to set the correct language once it's available.

*IMPORTANT:* We will only add your language(s) if you have done all of this.

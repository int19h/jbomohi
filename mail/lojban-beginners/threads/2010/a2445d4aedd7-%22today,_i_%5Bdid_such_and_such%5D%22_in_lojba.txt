On Tuesday 28 September 2010 23:20:04 Jonathan Jones wrote:
> Ah, I see. But the {cabdei lonu} bit works for "Today, ....", yes?

No. "cabdei lo nu mi co'e" means "It was the same day that I did so." I would 
say "ca le cabdei mi te vecnu lo sipydakli gi'e kansa le mi pendo po'u la tom 
gi'e gunka le mi garna prenu skina".

Btw, "mi kansa lo mi pendo me'e la tom" means that Tom, not "Tom", is the name 
of our being together, not of my friend. If you want to say it that way, 
it's "mi kansa lo mi pendo beme'e zo tom".

And please don't put me with Jeeg and Talen. I'm only a man.

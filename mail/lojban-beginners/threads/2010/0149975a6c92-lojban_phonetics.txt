It's not exactly technically correct (their is a little more variety in how
each letter can be pronounced).  But for starting out the guide for vowels
in "Lojban for Beginners" works well for all English speakers.
http://www.tlg.uci.edu/~opoudjis/lojbanbrochure/lessons/less1.html

You should be able to pronounce the s and t as you normally do, I don't
think that is much of a problem.
You have the option of making your normal r sound, or rolling it if you
like.
x is difficult for English speakers.  If you find a video in lojban with
lojban subtitles so you can pick out the pronunciation that might help you
understand it.

I think reading the section on pronunciation out of The Complete Lojban
Language is very useful.
http://www.lojban.org/publications/reference_grammar/chapter3.html

The official guide (strait out of The Complete Lojban Language) is this, but
unless you fluent in this technical jargon it's not very helpful, unless you
want to try to look it up online.

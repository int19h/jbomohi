*Q.*
*Many people think that all you need for a new language is some new words.
We all spend years learning the grammar of our native languages, but most
people never really consider all the structure needed to hold all those
words together. Which constructed languages do you think have the most
complete grammars? Na’vi was created by a linguist who knows these things.
But did Klingon originally have grammar or was it just a bunch of loudly
grunted words and oddly placed apostrophes? — Janet
V<http://schott.blogs.nytimes.com/2010/01/12/q-and-a-sprechen-sie-elvish-esperanto-klingon-navi/?apage=1#comment-61963>
*
A.

*Arika Okrent*: Klingon was invented by a linguist too. (Marc Okrand, who
wrote a dissertation on Mutsun, a Native American language). Like Na’vi,
Klingon stands apart from most invented languages created for films in that
it’s not just a bunch of vocabulary or nonsense sounds, but a grammar with
explicit rules for forming words and for forming sentences. The constructed
language with the most complete grammar is probably Lojban – a language
created to reflect the principles of logic. The grammar comes to over 600
pages.

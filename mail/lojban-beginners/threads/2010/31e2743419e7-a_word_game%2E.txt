I recently came up with a very simple game that my friends find highly
addictive - we've been playing it in English, but I'm willing to bet it'd be
just as fun in Lojban too.

The rules of the game are simple: Get a bunch of people together, the more
the better, and make a sentence. Each person gets one word, going in a
circle, for as long as everyone is able to keep straight faces. It'd
probably be a good idea to not count gadri as a word when playing this in
Lojban.

The best part is, it's nearly guaranteed that the resulting sentence will be
hilarious, without any effort to make it so.

I recently came up with a very simple game that my friends find highly
addictive - we've been playing it in English, but I'm willing to bet it'd be
just as fun in Lojban too.

The rules of the game are simple: Get a bunch of people together, the more
the better, and make a sentence. Each person gets one word, going in a
circle, for as long as everyone is able to keep straight faces. It'd
probably be a good idea to not count gadri as a word when playing this in
Lojban.

The best part is, it's nearly guaranteed that the resulting sentence will be
hilarious, without any effort to make it so.

In the improve community, I'm familiar with two variations of this
game used as warm-up tools.

One I know as word popcorn, where you say the first word that comes
to mind, no matter what it is.  It is designed to break down self-
censorship.

The other I know as Word at a time, where each person says one word
around the circle trying to tell a cohesive story.

A variation of that game is a counting game, where as a group, with
no pattern or clues, you try as a group to monotonically count to 20.
If a number is repeated (say two people say '2') or a pattern developers
(like the count going clockwise around the circle) you start over at
1 and try again.

For your game, sufficiently clever players would need to ban use of
the word 'si'!

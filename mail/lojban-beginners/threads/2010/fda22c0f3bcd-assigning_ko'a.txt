How do I assign KOhA to refer to sumti joined with a JOI ?

For example in:

  le plise ku jo'u le najnimre ku goi ko'a casnu .i ko'a sanga

the orange is the one singing. How can I make "ko'a" to refer to both the
orange and the apple? In other words, how to render "them" ?

NOTE: this is just an example, I could use "gi'e" here with no need of
"ko'a", the question is about assigning "ko'a", not how to translate the
example.


Thanks.

On Sun, Sep 12, 2010 at 5:09 AM, Remo Dentato <rdentato@gmail.com> wrote:
> How do I assign KOhA to refer to sumti joined with a JOI ?
>
> For example in:
>
>   le plise ku jo'u le najnimre ku goi ko'a casnu .i ko'a sanga
>
> the orange is the one singing. How can I make "ko'a" to refer to both the
> orange and the apple? In other words, how to render "them" ?

One way is to use "vu'o", as Lindar said. Another trick, is to put "ko'a" first:

  ko'a goi le plise jo'u le najnimre cu casnu .i ko'a sanga

In this case, if you wanted ko'a to be just le plise, you would need
to use "ge'u", the terminator for PE:

  ko'a goi le plise ge'u jo'u le najnimre cu casnu .i ko'a sanga

The assignable variable can go on either side of the assignment
phrase, so if you want conciseness, put it first for multiple sumti
and last for single sumti.

mu'o mi'e xorxes

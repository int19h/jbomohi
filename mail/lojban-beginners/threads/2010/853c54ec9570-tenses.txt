I personally would recommend not reading L4B at all.
(this is my personal opinion)

{pu} is a tcita. It adds an un-numbered place indicating the tagged
sumti happens after the selbri. {ba} is adding a sumti which happens
before the selbri, {ca} something that happens during the selbri.

Try http://jbotcan.org/wave_lesson/ instead.

Can you be more specific with the question? "pu", "ca", "ba" take only
one sumti when used as tags.

Maybe you are thinking of their use as connectives, as in ".e ba bo"?
In such constructions it is the connective ".e" that takes two sumti,
and "ba" is just being added to the meaning of ".e".

("digraph", not "diphthong".)

The problem is that writing it as two words will break the stress
pattern, which the Polish voices do perfectly for Lojban. After some
experimenting, I think the best is to write it "girtzu".

The diphthongs are also not a problem, although typing "ł" can be
something of a pain without a Polish keyboard:

http://say.expressivo.com/ewa/aj,_ej,_oj,_ał,_ja,_je,_ji,_jo,_ju,_ła,_łe,_łi,_ło,_łu,_girtzu

http://say.expressivo.com/jacek/aj,_ej,_oj,_ał,_ja,_je,_ji,_jo,_ju,_ła,_łe,_łi,_ło,_łu,_girtzu

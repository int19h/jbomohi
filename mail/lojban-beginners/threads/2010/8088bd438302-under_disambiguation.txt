I thought of an interesting conumdrum recently.

Let's say you have a piece of text, that spans multiple lines. Part of this
text is overwritten by a different text. The overtext has text both under
it, as in what it is overwriting, and under it as in lower on the page.

How would one unambiguously, as simply as possible, distinguish between the
two kinds of "under" in Lojban? (Score 3,000 points for doing it using only
spatial cmavo. :) )

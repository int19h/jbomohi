From the GoldenDict jbovlaste export:

bavykri

x1 expects/anticipates x2 to happen. (Cf. balvi, krici, ba'a.)

sojypa'a

p1 expects p2 (event); p1 expects p2 to happen. (p2 is likely. It has high
probability. Cf.pacna, sorpa'a, sotpa'a.)

kanpe

*Experimental valsi*

x1 expects/looks for the occurence of x2 (event), expected likelihood x3 (0-1,
default li so'a (i.e. near 1));....



That said, I don't think any of those are good choices absent content or
more description. My take on pregnant, {nerve'a}, and on "expecting", {ba
rorci}.

I find that deriving the Lojban from "expecting" feels
malglico to me.  Oddly The Draft Reference Grammar already
suggests a lujvo -- and it actually seems right to me -- "pazvau"
derived from the tanru "panzi vasru" because (s)he contains her
offspring at this point in time. Though perhaps "tarbyvau" derived
from "tarbi vasru" might be better as it is more general, inclusive
of surrogate mothers or women who can not produce their own ova and
must use donor ova; ie, the "tarbi" contained within the woman is
not her "panzi".

That needs to be "{do cusku lu coi latros. .ui li'u}."  "{du'u}," like other
abstractions, takes a full {bridi}, which "{coi latros. .ui}" is not.  "{lo se
du'u [bridi]}" refers to the words that would express the {bridi}, while just
"{lo du'u [bridi]}" would be the abstract statement expressed by the {bridi}.
An example of the proper use of "{se du'u}" would be "{do cusku lo se du'u do
cnino}" -- "You say that you are new" -- which is practically equivalent to
"{do cusku lu mi cnino li'u}" -- "You say, 'I am new.'"

mu'omi'e .kamymecraijun.

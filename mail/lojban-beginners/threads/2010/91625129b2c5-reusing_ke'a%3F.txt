On Fri, Sep 17, 2010 at 10:14 AM,  <kfa@gmx.net> wrote:
>
> Can I reuse ke'a in a subsequent bridi to avoid a longer or repetitive
> pro-sumti construction?

Nope.  If you don't want to bother with a "goi", the shortest ways to
grab something a little ways back are the alphabet method, or ri/ra,
or try just a blank space!!  If you're in a hurry, a blank space is
almost always the way to go.  Don't underestimate what people can
figure out, in English it's always just "it" or "that" which is
basically a freaking blank space-- those could mean anything!

OR, another idea occurs to me: Don't even use another bridi: "zo'e
co'e zo'e noi co'e zo'e fa ke'a noi zo'e co'e lo nu co'e ke'a" then
you CAN reuse "ke'a", because you've started a new "noi"!! :D  I gave
you just want you wanted, I deserve a prize, I will now reward myself
with breakfast.

mi'e la stela selckiku

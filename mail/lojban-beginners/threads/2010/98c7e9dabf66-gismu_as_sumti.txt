It it always what fits what sorts of things go in the x1, not the
concept itself.
lo xebni ku - a hater
lo si'o xebni kei ku - hate (as a concept)
lo nu xebni kei ku - hating (as an action-concept/event)

As Lindar said, "lo <brivla>" always means something that fits x1 of <brivla>. 
So:
lo xebni = a hater
lo rupnu = something whose value is measured in a monetary unit
lo nenri = something that is inside
To refer to a unit as such, I use "si'o": lo si'o snidu cu gradu tu'a lo 
sodnrcezi lo temci.

Also, I think that "converting" a gismu to a sumti is a misleading way of
phrasing (like "creating" energy when "generating" would be more
appropriate).

gismu describes a type of word; sumti describes its use in a sentence (okay,
a bridi).

I think of it more as "using" a gismu as a sumti

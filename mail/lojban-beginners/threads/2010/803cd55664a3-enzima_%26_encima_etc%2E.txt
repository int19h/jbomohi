Related question: if you're Lojbanizing words in languages where the same
spelling leads to different pronunciations in different regions, which
pronunciation should you base it off of? For example, the soft "c" in
Spanish has rather different pronunciations in Spain and Latin America, with
Spain using a sound similar to a soft "th" and Latin America using a sound
more like English's soft c (this is from my HS Spanish classes; the actual
situation is probably more complicated than this). Which is preferred? In
tijlan's example, if you chose the Spanish (that is, European) pronunciation
you could distinguish between words with a z and a c; is that a good thing?
etc.

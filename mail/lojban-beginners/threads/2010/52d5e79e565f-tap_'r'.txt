I always pronounce "r" as a tap, flap, or trill, except when it's syllabic or 
preceded by 'x' ("xr" comes out as a voiceless simultaneous trill and gargle, 
so it's still trilled, and "xrx" - well that would take too long to explain 
and ...) or in a foreign name. It is quite okay to pronounce it as a tap.

Any rhotic sound is okay. Honestly, I think most agree that the
American R (whatever you want to call it) is really bad form, but any
other rhotic is acceptable. I tend to trill except when L proceeds R,
then I use the French R.

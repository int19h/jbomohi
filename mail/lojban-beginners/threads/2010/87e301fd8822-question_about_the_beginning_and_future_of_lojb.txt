Lojban is noted to have 1,300 root words or gismu. The rest of the
words, if not cmavo (structure words), are various combinations of
gismu to describe more advanced objects.

My question is, will the number of gismu ever expand from 1,300? Who
created these root words? Possibly more fundamental root words can be
created? How was it decided, which would should be root, which should
not?

Here is an example of something that really gets to me:
The word for mouse: smacu. (gismu)
The word for rat: ratcu. (gismu)
The word for horse: xirma. (gismu)
The word for pony: cmaxi'a. (lujvo)

Why is rat allowed to have its own root word but pony isn't? Who
decides the importance of words?

Thank you,

I can't say with certainty, but I believe the gismu were selected for their
frequency of use.  That's why there are sometimes very similar concepts that
still end up with their own root word.

If you think about it, pony really shouldn't be a root word though.  It's a
baby horse.  Should there is a special root word for "baby kangaroo" as
well?  Just because english decides that a word is important enough to
warrant a two syllable word doesn't mean that it's logical.

Ponies are not baby horses, they are a special breed of horses, actually a
subspecies of what we generally refer to as "horses". The relation between
"pony" and "horse" is about the same as between "terrier" and "dog". But
rats and mice are scientifically speaking not the same species - mice are
members of genus *Mus* (with 30 known subspecies), rats are members of genus
*Rattus* (with 64 known subspecies), while ponies *are* the same species as
other horses.

The way the gismu were selected is described in CLL :
http://www.lojban.org/publications/reference_grammar/chapter4.html#s14

I thimk it is very unlikely that the number will increase. Maybe one
or two (look at "experimental gismu" in jbovlaste) but not a
significant number. I think it would be more likely that some gismu
will be forgot for being largely unused.

My understanding is that the BPFK is in charge of everything
pertaining the language itself:
http://www.lojban.org/tiki/tiki-index.php?page=baupla+fuzykamni.

Just as a side note, in case the implicit request was to add or remove gismu.

I'm sure that everybody, including those that had a part in the
creation of lojban, have their pain point about the language. The
(in)famous cultural gismu for example (glico, fraso, ...) or the fact
that in the last rafsi reallocation {tel} has not been assigned to
{te}.

The BPFK has its plan to progress the language and the progresses are
strongly dependent on the time they can spend on working on Lojban. I
think that any help they can get will be welcome. Of course I'm
talking of help to make their plan moving forward, not to change their
plan completely :).  I'm sure that any proposal to improve the
language will be considered in the due time.

My personal priority (i.e. the area where I think I can help) is to
try attract more speakers and get more lojbanic material available to
them. Others are working to the same goal by teaching newbies. Other
are writing lojbanic software and so on.

My personal suggestion is to really try using lojban, you'll discover,
I guess, that while annoying there's no real harm in having both
{ratcu} and {ractu}.

There are also fu'ivla. A type-3 fu'ivla is a combination of a gismu and a 
foreign part; a type-4 fu'ivla is a bare foreign word (or an imitative word 
or an irregular combination such as "vonpaso").

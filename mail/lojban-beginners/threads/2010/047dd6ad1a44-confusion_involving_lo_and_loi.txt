Consider the statement "ci lo ninmu cu nelci la deivyd.bo,is". The number of 
women who like David Bowie is 3. If "pafi'uci lo ninmu cu co'e", the number 
of women who like him is 1/3. Which third of her likes him? The brain and 
arms, the torso, or the legs?

"ci loi ninmu cu nelci la deivyd.bo,is" means that three groups of women like 
him. So "pafi'uci loi ninmu cu co'e" means that one-third of a group of women 
does. This is the meaning you want: one in three women is one-third of a 
group of women.

=== 3 | 2010-07-15T05:01:46Z | Jonathan Jones <eyeonus@gmail.com> | aanlktilx3pn5mrojth1ymecmayiifemoru7wwrgv7e66@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-beginners/cur/1279170106.a32bc92b0739ad79.jbomohi:2,S
Even if {lo ninmu} refers to multiple women, they are each considered
individually. {pa fi'u ci lo ninmu} therefore means "one third of each
woman".

{loi ninmu} considers the entirety of what is being specified as one unit, a
group. {pa fi'u ci loi ninmu} means "one-third of a group of women".

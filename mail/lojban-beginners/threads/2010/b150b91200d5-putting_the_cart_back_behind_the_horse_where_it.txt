While I would like to take some credit, I really should redirect the
spotlight to xalbo and kribacr. I think they've been the drive behind
the new teaching methods this whole time, and they've certainly made a
dent in both modern vernacular and improving teaching methods. When
teaching, I tend to loosely follow the methods that were outlined by
these two, blended in a bit with my own preference and the wisdom of
the elder Lojbanists.

Also, thank you to everybody that got SmartFM up and running. I wish I
had the patience to finish those audio files! It's a really great
resource, and helps me with my vocabulary.

By the way, I know there are a lot more people out there that don't
know about IRC or the Wave lessons. Let's spread the word, or create
some kind of medium to allow a wider audience to reach these updated
lessons.

Good job to all of you, and keep up the good work.

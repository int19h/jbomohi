There's a kind of question I've seen on some tests. You have two lists of 
words or phrases and you have to match them up. For instance:

1. nargile
2. ractu
3. bratu
4. ma'arjipci
5. vombatu
6. xanto
7. tolzi'o
8. tarbi
9. lerfu
10. fanva
11. cakcinki

'. nenri lo gutra
a. damva'u tutci
b. ponjo cipni
c. sralo mabru
d. clani se nazbi
e vofli lo trixe nalci
f. plipe mabru
g. tceberti
i. farlu bisli
j. selci'a selci
k. cusku lo selsmu be lo mintu bau lo drata

Is this a good kind of question for us to put on the test?

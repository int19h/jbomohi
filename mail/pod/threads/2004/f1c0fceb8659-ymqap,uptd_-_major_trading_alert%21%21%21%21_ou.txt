AUSTIN, Texas, Jun 16, 2004 (BUSINESS WIRE) -- UPTREND Corp. (Pink Sheets:UPTD) announced today that it has received an order for 600 AirVests, representing half of its initial 1,200 unit order. 

The Company received an order for its patented AirVests from a Dallas-based online and mobile retailer. Product is expected to be delivered in the third quarter of 2004 and the Company believes that potential exists for additional orders from this customer later in the year. 

Robert Bitterli, CEO of UPTREND, said, "This initial order speaks to the excitement surrounding the AirVest product. While the AirVest represents a new safety product for the motorcycle industry, we are extremely encouraged by the progress of our business development executives. We have developed a backlog of diverse sales opportunities. We believe that word-of-mouth advertising, in conjunction with our aggressive direct sales and marketing efforts and those of our retailers and marketing partners, will allow us to continue growing our business rapidly." 

The "AirVest" is the most revolutionary product to hit the motorsports industry since the helmet. An innovation in high-quality safety gear, the AirVest acts as a wearable airbag in the event of an accident, helping to protect the rider's chest and back from impact. AirVest is light weight, inflates in milliseconds and is reusable.

Okay, Nick, here's the decision I've come to (I reserve the right
to change my mind of course).

If the BF tries to tread a fine line between fundamentalism and
revisionism it will be too painful, because there'll be too much
arguing. I'll participate on either of two premises: 

(I) The BF is nonnegotiably fundamentalist, and the CLL is right 
in every respect where it cannot be shown to unsalvageably
contradictory or in conflict with basic Loglan principles. The
BF will work to complete the documentation of Standard Lojban
(Catnyjbo).

(II) I participate in the BF with the goal of defining Academic
Lojban (Skejbo), not with the goal of seeking consensus with 
fundamentalists. BF decisions can be decided not by consensus 
but by majority vote between a fundamentalist consensus and a 
progressivist consensus (Academic Lojban).

In addition I will try to work in collaboration with others to
develop and document Academic Lojban, conceived as a nonradical
Lojban Mark 2 designed to have continuity with Lojban Mark
1 in terms of the effort required to learn it (so not redoing
vocab from scratch, say). It will be impossible to develop 
Academic Lojban on the basis of consensus across the community. 
Instead, it will have to be developed by consensus among the 
Progressivists, if it is to be developed at all. My hope is that 
it would then recruit from those disillusioned with Standard Lojban. 
I don't want to wait 50 years to begin on Academic Lojban; I
am interested in Lojban only because it holds out the promise
of something that Academic Lojban would strive to realize.

The BF can choose to try to emulate in SL bits of AL in the 
hope that SL will become good enough to stop people preferring AL. 

I want to stop wrangling over Lojban, given that no satisfactory
consensus or compromise over monolithic Lojban is possible. So
instead I will push for the Three Dialects solution. If anybody
harangues me over that, I shall invite them to raise it at the
AGM. 

--And.

It's not OT, though. I mentioned these examples to show that it is
possibly incorrect to be treating 'fractional quantifiers' in terms of ordinary
fractions. The point is that it's normal English, because
the way English fractional quantifiers work is not by calculating
the total number of members of the set but by calculating how
many per randomly selected 100 have the property. That is, "50%
of integers are even" is taken to mean "one in every two integers
is even".

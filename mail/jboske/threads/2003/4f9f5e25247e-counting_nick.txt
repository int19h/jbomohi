No -- For Kind as defined by me -- &-Kind -- Mr Nick does write words and 
eat specific fish. However, he does so in infinitely many possible
worlds. One of the special things about &-kinds is that they inhabit
many worlds, occurring wherever their manifestations occur in those
worlds.

We can contrast &-Kind with the notion of "that which is whatever
manifests Mr Broda", i.e. the portion of a world that manifests
Mr Broda separated from the portion of the world that doesn't
manifest Mr Broda. E.g. the portion that is gold, separated from
the portion that is not gold.

Lojbab referred in a recent message to my despair at the mess that
is SL. There are two reasons for the despair. The first is that
until recently, the prescription was deemed complete, even though
the language was far from complete. The second reason is that
if the text of prescription is the only source of authority, then
it becomes possible to cast doubt on every aspect of the prescription,
greatly diminishing its value.

But there are also reasons for hope. It is now generally recognized
that the prescription needs to be extended by the BF. And the BF
is led by a dynamo who believes Lojban should realize the guiding 
logical principles of Loglan.

But in the medium term, which I define as beginning when the 
BF's current mission is discharged, and ending when all fluent
Lojbanists agree with one another on all points of Lojban
grammar, the problems will persist. The prescription may still
be incomplete, sometimes in crucial ways, and if the prescription
is solely textual it will always be susceptible to sophistry.

The solution would be to keep the BF in session in the medium
term. Issues unresolved by the prescription and issues of
how to interpret the prescription could be referred to the
BF. The BF could propose answers and put them forward to the
community for ratification. As time went on, the workload
of the BF would drastically diminish (because unsettled
isses breed unsettled issues and settled issues breed settled
issues).

In that scenario the outcome at the worst would be that due
to backwards compatibility Lojban would be somewhat kludgey,
somewhat ugly, and somewhat unergonomic. But it would at 
least not be a mess. It might still be Microsoft Lojban, but
at least it wouldn't be McLojban.

--And.

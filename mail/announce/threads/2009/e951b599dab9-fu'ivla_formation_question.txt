Here's a proposed question for testing fu'ivla formation:

ma na taidra fu'ivla
A. zo .aierne
B. zo .urartu
C. zo kartuli
D. zo kafkaso
E. zo tirkce

Slight problem, though: "zo .urartu na taidra fu'ivla" is ungrammatical, 
because "urartu" breaks up and "zo" grabs only the word "u". How do you 
suggest fixing that?

Another version: "ma na fu'ivla", and substitute "xrazdani" for ".urartu". zo 
xrazdani has nothing to do with the rest of the words, but happens to sound 
like the name of a river in the area.

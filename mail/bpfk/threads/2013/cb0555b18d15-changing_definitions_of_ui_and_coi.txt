Currently:

valsi: iu
Definition: attitudinal: love - no love lost - hatred.
Notes: See also {prami}.

 
Proposed:

valsi: iu
Definition: attitudinal: love.
Notes: The full scale is: {iu} ('love') - {iucu'i} ('no love lost') - 
{iunai} ('hatred'). See also {prami}.


Reason:

Newbies are puzzled why every UI and COI has two or three meanings. The 
proposal changes nothing in the meaning but fixes the format of definitions 
making it more usable.

 
Additional notes:

The same change is to be done with all other UI and COI

CLL:

It is grammatically permitted to tag more than one sumti with the same FA
cmavo. The effect is that of making more than one claim:

3.9)   [fa] la rik. fa la djein. klama [fe] le skina fe le zdani fe le zarci
       [x1=] Rick x1= Jane goes-to x2= the movie x2= the house x2= the office

may be taken to say that both Rick and Jane go to the movie, the house, and
the office, merging six claims into one. More likely, however, it will
simply confuse the listener.

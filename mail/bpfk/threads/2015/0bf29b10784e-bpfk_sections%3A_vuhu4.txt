On Mon, Jul 20, 2015 at 5:06 AM, Alex Burka <durka42@gmail.com> wrote:

> Actually, I have no idea how >2-ary mekso operators work. Is {pe'o} or
> {ge'a} required to make this example work?
>

"pe'o" is always optional, never required.

"ge'a" is not required in forethough (Polish notation), where operators can
take any number of operands (greater than zero). Only in infix or in
afterthought (reverse Polish), which are always binary, is "ge'a" required
as a dummy operator.

mu'o mi'e xorxes

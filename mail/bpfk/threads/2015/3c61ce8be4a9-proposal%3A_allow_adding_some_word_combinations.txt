It's a well known problem that JVS is meant to be a Lojban2English (or to 
any other language) dictionary, not vice versa.

Some English words that people are searching for can't appear in JVs since 
they are expressed using word combinations in Lojban.
Adding new lujvo is not an option: why should we relex English when already 
existing words can do the trick?

La Muplis <http://mw.lojban.org/extensions/ilmentufa/i/muplis/index.html> (or 
tatoeba.org, its database) isn't exactly what needed since people are used 
to dictionaries with isolated words.

So I propose to allow adding these word combinations to JVS:

"TAG brivla", "NAhE brivla".

You may propose allowing other combinations as well.

selbri. For example, {lo bercribe} means "a polar bear", but {la bercribe} 
means "Ursa Minor". (What to call Ursa Major I'm not sure. Ursa Minor's tail 
is at the North Pole.)

Also words and phrases can be entered in the Lojban Wiktionary.

I think the simplest solution is to just allow any string of Lojban words 
to be entered as a new category, "phrase". With perhaps an additional 
category, "name-phrases", for anything beginning with {la}, and possibly 
also {la'au} and {li'ai}. This will greatly increase flexibility in the 
handling of non-cmevla names, which might run the whole gamut from being 
single brivla, to containing sumtcita and numbers, as well as allowing 
translations for entire book titles.

I notice that a lot of combinations are "hardcoded" in PEG.
E.g. a lot of constructs are followed by "free*" several times in the
grammar where the first part of the construct remains the same.

Here is an experimental grammar:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Ilmen-vodhr/ilmentufa/master/camxes-exp.js.peg

And here are the stats:
http://pastebin.com/raw.php?i=ADTJ0KdT

Here, "KE_clause free*" is used 11 times in PEG and even when "free*"
doesn't follow KE_clause it could.
Why not move "free*" into KE_clause itself ? This would make the grammar
easier.

"free*" is used ~200 times in the grammar. I'm sure this number can be
lowered significantly.

Anyone is interested in joining such constructs into new variables?

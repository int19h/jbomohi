Now: {cifnu} = x1 is an infant/baby [helpless through youth/incomplete 
development] of species x2. |>>> Also infantile.  See also makcu, verba.

Proposed: {cifnu} = x1 is an infant, baby, young offspring of species x2. 
|>>> Not necessarily helpless. See also makcu, verba, panzi.

Reason: a discussion 
<https://groups.google.com/forum/#!topic/lojban/N5bA5JlhroQ> shows that one 
shouldn't paid attention to "infant", "helpless" and "infantile" words. 
Separate words for "altricial/precocial", stages of development like 
larvae, imago etc. and for immature/mature are to be invented instead.

I can agree that it can be a pain so this proposal may sound reasonable.

I can't agree with changing anything within {li}, however. {li} is for
mekso which is a special subset of Lojban with its own grammar.

{by} as a anaphorical marker and {by} within {li} are two different
entities.
Terminating {li} with {boi} or {lo'o} isn't that hard IMO.

But how would "(n+1.3)^(3.15*r^2)" be expressed under this new xorxe's
proposal then?

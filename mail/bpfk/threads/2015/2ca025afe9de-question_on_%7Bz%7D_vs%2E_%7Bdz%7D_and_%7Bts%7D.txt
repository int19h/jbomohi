Today I was reminded of one "flaw" in phonology namely, the presense of [z]
in Lojban.

Mandarin doesn't have it/has it as an allophone of t͡s.

https://docs.google.com/document/d/1_w-c7yM6beFUc_G-XCSLlRfrCewhQosFdQuPD1DwhuU/edit?usp=drive_web

So my question is: what would be the minimally destroying changes to remove
[z] from the language or to remove {dz} instead so that Mandarin speakers
can use t͡s to mean [z]. I know that replacing "voiced/voiceless"
distinction with "non-aspirated-semi-voiced/aspirated-voiceless" might not
be embraced easily but since Chinese is one of the source languages may be
something could be done to make their life happier?

I suppose to do that one needs to always insert buffer between {dz}, never
pronounce {dz} as an affricate and may be even always insert {y} in
{radzu'e}.

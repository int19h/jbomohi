Two bridi can be connected by {i SUMTCITA bo}. {bo} has two effects: it 
prevents an immediately following sumti from being taken as the object of the 
preposition, and it groups the bridi more tightly and right-associatively than 
surrounding bridi connected by just {i}. One may want left-association:

lo filso cu lacpu lo mamta bakni lo carce .icabo lo cifybakni cu pinfu 
.itezu'ebo facki lo jei la .iaves. kau gasnu lo nunbilma

(This is an abbreviated version of 1 Samuel 6:7-9.) Is there a way to connect 
three or more bridi with prepositions without forcing right association?

Pierre

First, left-grouping is the same as most of other grouping rules of Lojban.
For example, a string of tanru units is read as left-grouping:
((broda brode) brodi)
((broda ja brode) ja brodi)
I rather don't understand why only BO and CO should support right-grouping 
among many other connectives.

Secondly, just the same problem as la piier raised. When I first wrote a 
logical proof on "individuals" in Lojban, I should first mention "these 
BO-s are regarded as left-grouping":
broda
iseni'ibo
brode
iseni'ibo
brodi
....
Right grouping of BO is very annoying for me.

We see no grouping at all on the parsing tree. It's the same thing for 
JA-connection or simple string of tanru units:
http://mw.lojban.org/extensions/ilmentufa/camxes.html
([broda {ja brode} {ja brodi}] VAU) 

I guess it is because of PEG's characteristic (na'e birti). I heard about 
left-recursion problem of PEG. PEG first parses a string like

broda brode brodi brodo
 as 
(broda (brode (brodi brodo))).

We should therefore modify the grouping after parsing.

Grouping of JA-connectives is ugly. Not [only] that they group to the right
but this
sumti_3 = sumti_4 (joik_ek stag? BO_clause free* sumti_3)?)

is what I completely disagree with. Node must couple either with another
node of the same level or with newly created constructs. Thus sumti_4
mustn't be linked with its parent node sumti_3 as its sibling node.
Scoping, precedence and 'embedding clauses into clauses' are all smashed
into one type of brackets in PEG which makes the real structure somewhat
unclear.

A solution without providing any technical mechanism was proposed here:
https://github.com/Ilmen-vodhr/ilmentufa/issues/116

We have {ke} or {tu'e} for any grouping (la jbofi'e has limitation in {ke}, 
and la camxes and la ilmentufa inherited it, but it is resolved in la 
zantufa_0.16 or later). 

Besides right-grouping, {bo} has un-substitutable role in the grammar as 
short scope joiner, and we have no short scope joiner in left-grouping 
(similarly, we have no long scope joiner in right-grouping other than 
{co}). This lack brings problems in practical use.

In order to make the grammar more useful, we would have two alternatives.

1. all joiners do grouping to the same direction, and use {ke} or {tu'e} 
for different grouping.
2. create two cmavo for each scope of joiner, one for left-grouping, and 
another for right-grouping.

I prefer 1 for simplicity, and did it in my parser la zantufa.

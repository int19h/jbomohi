Hello ladies and gentlemen.

So here's the deal. I'd be fine with finishing the entire thing on my 
own (though helpers are allowed :P ), it's a very easy but boring task, 
*but* I have a few comments first.

Firstly, I find it weird that so many of the blue sections haven't been 
voted on even though blue indicates that a page is ready for a vote. 
Then again, many of the allegedly complete sections (marked blue) 
contain many mistakes. Should I care to fix every incorrect example 
sentence? What about strange statements in the notes? What about all the 
sections with irregular formatting that don't follow the standard format 
of a cmavo definition (as defined by the template)? Almost all of the 
time is spent on waiting for the tiki to update all those small 
formatting changes. If it weren't for that, I'd be able to define all 
the cmavo within a very short time (let's say 1 or 2 days).

Secondly, there are some pages which have an ongoing vote, with four or 
five votes recorded which are several years old. The current BPFK has no 
actual members right now, as Robin not only called the BPFK dead, but 
also said that anyone could simply declare themselves a member (in other 
words, anyone is the BPFK) This means it's very hard to judge what the 
different votes are worth. Therefore, I would suggest declaring all 
previous votes null and void and to do a new, proper vote with members 
who are *currently* active in the community.

So let's please pick new voting members (otherwise we don't know what 
constitutes a majority) and then vote on the blue sections. If there are 
problems in them, then people can vote no with an explanation of what's 
wrong.

mi'e la selpa'i mu'o

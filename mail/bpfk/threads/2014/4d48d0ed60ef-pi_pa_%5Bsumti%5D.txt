*cmavo: si'e (MOI)**
**Proposed Definition*

Converts a number, lerfu-string or ME-converted sumti into a tanru-unit. 
The place structure in the case of a number is /"x1 is/are a 
/[number]/th of x2"/. In general for any sumti the place structure is 
/"x1 is a part/fraction of x2, quantified by /[number/lerfu/sumti]/"/. //

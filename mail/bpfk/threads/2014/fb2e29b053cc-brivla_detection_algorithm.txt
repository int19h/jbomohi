No, because of the slinku'i rule. The slinku'i rule means you need to
examine the rafsi structure of your candidate form anyway, so determining
whether it's a gismu, lujvo or fu'ivla is not much more complicated than
determining whether it's a valid brivla. You can't avoid rafsi
unfortunately.

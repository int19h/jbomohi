"jei" is in a sense easier than "ni", although the issues ar
essentially the same.

There are two competing definitions for "jei":

(1) jei = du'u xu kau

(2) x1 jei (bridi) kei x2 = x1 se jetlai lo du'u (bridi) kei x2

(1) is how everybody uses it, (2) is a literal reading of the cmavo
list definition. I went with (2) in the Lojban-Lojban for jbovlaste,
but I don't really care one way or the other. I just don't need "jei".
See:

http://jbovlaste.lojban.org//dict/jei
http://jbovlaste.lojban.org//dict/jetlai

number.

djuno, facki, jdice, senpi, etc.

Log Message:
-----------
Mostly finished chapter 3, not including index work. I put "<!--zort
mark:[comment]-->" in several iffy places, which must be checked. Also,
added what I did to TODO.

Here is a complete (I hope) list of the types of changes:

 * Converted the tables/lists from <programlisting> to either
   <informaltable> or <simplelist>
 * Put <jbophrase> where appropriate, with "role" and "valid"
   categorization
 * Designated X-SAMPA and non-English languages (except Quenya)
 * Fixed up examples (<programlisting>, <interlinear-gloss>, <gloss>...)
 * Changed section and chapter numbers to names

 Several tags and attributes were invented, specifically:

 * <example role="pronunciation-example">, for all the examples in this
   chapter
 * <pronunciation>, replacing <interlinear-gloss>
 * <ipa> meaning the IPA part of a pronunciation example
 * <jbophrase role="letteral"> for lojban letters EXCEPT for
   multi-letter letterals. That is, I decided not to use role="letteral"
   for {.y'y.}, because it's treated as a vowel pair, not a letteral.
 * <jbophrase role="morphology"> for consonant pairs.
 * <jbophrase role="diphthong"> for, well, diphthongs.
 * <phrase role="X-SAMPA">
 * <jbo role="pronunciation"> for those with redundant explicit stress
   and/or syllabication, i.e. most of the ones with capital letters
   and/or commas

Unfinished stuff in chapter 3 (chapter phonology?):

 * A <variablelist> that needs to be changed to an <orderedlist>
 * Index stuff

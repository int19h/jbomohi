1) fagzda (hearth, fireplace)

z1 is a fireplace/hearth for fuel f2 with oxidizer f3 (default air/oxygen)
 
from
z1 zdani lo fagri be f2 bei f3

2) fagju'e(fender)

j1 fagju'e f2 f3
j1 is a fender/firescreen for a fireplace using fuel f2 burning in f3
 
from
j1 julne fi lo fagri be f2 bei f3

3) bifpra (fan)

c1 bifpra b3 c3
c1 is a fan/blower/punkah blowing air/gas with speed b3 by process c3
 
from
c1 cupra lo brife be cy bei b3 be'o c3

4) tolpa'a (dread/apprehension)

p1 tolpa'a p2 p3
p1 dreads/is apprehensive about event p2, expected likelyhood being p3 (0-1)

5) nonpa'a (hopeless/desperate)

p1 nonpa'a p2 
p1 feels neutral about event p2, expected likelyhood being p3 (0-1)

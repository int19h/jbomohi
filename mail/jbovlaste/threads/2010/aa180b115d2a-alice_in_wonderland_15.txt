Please comment:


carcylai (cartload)

x1=k1 is x2=k2 cartloads (default: one) each of size x3

based on Arnt's suggestion for muclai in:

http://lojban.com/tiki/tiki-view_forum_thread.php?comments_parentId=7708&topics_offset=324&topics_sort_mode=lastPost_desc&forumId=5&display=print_all


Likwise:

kabrylai (cupful), baktylai {bucketful}, bevma'elai (truck load), botlai 
(bottleful) etc

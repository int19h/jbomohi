Considering that jbovlaste has {sorgugje'a} for "empire" and in the
translation {lo jirnyde'i be la .uisterim.} (

translated "emperor" with {sorgu'etru} at page 6..

So, if an "empire" is a "domain of many countries", an "emperor" should be a
"governor of many countries".

I've no particular attachment to {sorg'etru} but I feel that {balnoltru} is
too restrictive, a "big kingdom" is not an empire since an empire is made by
more nations (that might have different languages and cultures and even have
a king that is subject to the emperor).

No. The 100,000 (not 1,000) votes displayed in jbovlaste are just a  
placeholder for “this is an official definition, it should rank above  
any other”. Official definitions are determined by the BPFK[1], not by  
jbovlaste users (though if there were 100,000 jbovlaste users that  
agreed on something, that would be -- significant).

[1] http://www.lojban.org/tiki/baupla+fuzykamni

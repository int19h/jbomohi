bikspu (snap)

s1 bikspu s2 s3
s1 snaps in response to person/object/event/situation/stimulus s2 with response s3

gloss: 
snap; answer abruptly
respond abruptly
answer abruptly

biksku (snap)

c1 biksku c2 c3 c4
c1 (agent) snaps c2 (sedu'u/text/lu'e concept) at audience c3 via expressive medium c4 
 
gloss: 
snap; say abruptly
say abruptly
say sharply

capsidba'u(snarl/growl)

b1 capsidba'u s2 s3
b1 snarls/growls the threat of idea/action s2 at audience s3

pipkla(skip)

k1=p1 pipkla k2=p2 k3=p3 k4
k1=p1 skips/hops to k2=p2 from k3=p3 via route k4

gloss:
skip; hop
hop; skip
bound: skip

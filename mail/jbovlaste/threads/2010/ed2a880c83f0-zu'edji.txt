But could somebody remind me why "intend" is a gloss for zu'edji.  When tax
season comes around I "intend" to get my taxes done but I really don't
desire to do them.  People intend to do all kinds of things that they really
don't djica/desire/want/wish to have happen.  Also the "for z3" is kind of
ambiguous.  I'm assuming that it means "for purpose/towards end z3" but it's
not clear.

I propose changing the definition of {zu'edji} just a bit so that instead
of:
Definition: z1 intends/wishes to do z2 for z3
we have:
Definition: z1 wishes/desires to do z2 for purpose z3

Definition: z1 intends/plans to do z2 (event) for purpose z3

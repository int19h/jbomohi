Agreed. I think one of the points of Lojban is to give as many words logical
roots as possible. Why? We want to have a clue as to the meaning without
having to look the word up in the dictionary. It can't be perfect, but if
this aesthetic is ignored for a non-cultural word such as 'crab,' then
Lojban will simply lose one of its advantages over many natural languages. I
think most of us would rather not see this happen.

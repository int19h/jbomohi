Some weeks ago I entered the Lojbanized Latin names of the months (just the 
currently used names). "martio", "prilio", "madjio", "djunio", and "djulio" 
were rejected. There's a bug in vlatai which calls a fu'ivla invalid if 
changing a diphthong that can occur in fu'ivla but not lujvo to one that can 
occur in lujvo turns it into a lujvo (such as "marti'o"). Vlatai 
considers "mercedonio", which also ends in the diphthong "io", to be valid. 
(lo mercedonio cu se setca be fi le latmo veldetri be'o masti pu la .iulius.) 
Is anyone planning to fix vlatai or change the way jbovlaste decides if a 
word is valid?

zacyci'e (market; economic)
c1 is a market interrelated by structure c2 among components c3
displaying c4 (ka)

pracmu (capital; economic)
j1 is capital (factors of production) for goods/services j2=p2

zukselpla (policy; political)
p2=z2 is a policy of p3=z3 (nu) prescribed by p1

nuntumcrepu (agriculture)
n1 is agriculture of product c2 from source/area c3=t1 by farmers c1

nunfipcrepu (fishery)
n1 is fishery of product c2 from source/area c3=t1 by fishermen c1

tadjycpa (adopt; embrace)
c1 adopts c2=t1 for purpose t2

gugja'anunsnu (summit; political)
n1 is a summit between j1=c1 about topic/subject c2

flari'a (enact; legal)
r1=f5 enacts law r2=f1 for community f3 under condition r3=f4

gugbancu (foreign; territorial)
b1 is territorially foreign to b3=g3


I realized that having "vudypai", from which "ka vudypai" would be
easily formed, is better than having "kamvudypai":

vudypai (justice)
v1=p1 does justice to p2 by standard v2

=== 7 | 2009-07-04T13:57:55Z | tijlan <jbotijlan@gmail.com> | 4de8c3930907040657n28692c45xf8e49452eecf9a0f@mail.gmail.com | mail/jbovlaste/cur/1246715875.0283258662ee8514.jbomohi:2,S
Next ones:


narpanra (unique)
p1 is unique/unequaled/unparalleled/unmatched / the only one of its kind.


jectru (government)
j1=t1 is a government of people/country j2=t2; j1=t1 is governmental.

* This is different from "jecta" in that it doesn't have the
adjectival meaning of "x1 is civil/political".


jectrubi'i (intergovernmental)
jb1 is intergovernmental / takes place where the decision-making power
is shared among je1=t1 (governments).

* http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intergovernmentalism


jiktru (institution; established organisation)
t1 is an institution governing social/cooperative human behaviour t2.
Note: Use x2 tu'a for e.g. education.

* http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institution
* Other possibilities: tratru, tertratru.


multertu'i (unanimous; based on complete agreement)
m1=t3 (du'u) is unanimous / based on complete agreement among t1=t2.

tugysi'u (unanimous; sharing the same view)
t1=t2=s1 are unanimous / sharing the same view on t3=s2 (du'u).

* http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/unanimous
* "tugysi'u" is different from "sarxe", which has no du'u place.


ja'arcu'a (elect; to choose in election)
c1 elects c2=j1 (candidate) from alternatives c3.

* There is "gubycu'a" for "vote".


selgu'e (citizen; member of a state)
g2 is a citizen, a legally recognized member of g1 (country/state) in
g3 (land/territory).

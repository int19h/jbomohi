I think the difference between {sunla} and {kerfa} is similar to the
difference between {skapi} and {pilka}.

{kerfa} and {pilka} are body parts, like {xance} or {tuple} whereas {sunla}
and {skapi} are types of material, like {mapni} or {silka}.

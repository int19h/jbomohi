Strictly speaking, the second place structure is for {selmifsle}, while the
first is for {mifsle}. I don't think it would hurt for {mifsle} to have
both meanings, do you? That said, it seems easy enough to coin both words:
{mifsle} for block of a cipher, {selmifsle} for block of some plaintext.
That's what I would do.

I'm pretty sure that in the genetic code, the amino acid is the plaintext,
because amino acids do all the useful protein magic while codons just sit
there in an easy-to-manipulate format.

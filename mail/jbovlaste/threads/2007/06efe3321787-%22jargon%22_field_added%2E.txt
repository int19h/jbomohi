Because that's entirely orthogonal.  The point here is that
"noljedai" was made up for my RPG campaign, and no-one should ever
think that it's OK to randomly use that for "Jedi" unless they're
playing with me.  Hence, either I don't put it in jbovlaste or if I
do, it needs to make it clear that that's a special word.

I think jbovlaste should be used for any words Lojbanists actually
use, but that doesn't mean all of them are equally general-use.

Can you explain why you don't like this idea?

Because that's entirely orthogonal.  The point here is that
"noljedai" was made up for my RPG campaign, and no-one should ever
think that it's OK to randomly use that for "Jedi" unless they're
playing with me.  Hence, either I don't put it in jbovlaste or if I
do, it needs to make it clear that that's a special word.

I think jbovlaste should be used for any words Lojbanists actually
use, but that doesn't mean all of them are equally general-use.

Are you saying that "noljedai" could conceivably have a different meaning when being used in your RPG campaign from when it is used elsewhere? If so, this is a break with unambiguousness (which is an express design criterion of Lojban), and should not be endorsed by an official LLG project.

Or are you saying that most of the words that are coined for the project are likely to be of poor quality, and should hence be avoided? If so, I think a more appropriate solution is downvoting, or a checkbox in the definition editing page which says "do not vote after adding".

In any case, it's good to see that you are doing stuff with the Jbovlaste infrastructure. Don't let my whining put you off!

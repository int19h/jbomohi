"Be a  Washington Loglan Worker", a newsletter published by Bob LeChevalier in July and August 1986 as an attempt to create a Washington D.C. area ((Loglan)) special interest group (SIG). The newsletter was renamed to "((hoi loglypli))" in April 1987, and finally, in February 1988, to "((ju'i lobypli))".

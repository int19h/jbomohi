"Hey, Loglan User", a newsletter published by Bob LeChevalier in June 1987. As successor to ((me la uacintyn loglytuan)), it was issued as number 3, and aimed to represent a broader "((Loglan)) Users' Group", rather than just serving the Washington D.C. SIG. Although dated April 1987, it was not published until June 1987 due to complications from strained relations with ((The Loglan Institute)). After one issue, it was renamed, in February 1988, to "((ju'i lobypli))".

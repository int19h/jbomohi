This page links to the official word lists created by the LLG, which are references and tend to be of limited use in learning the language.



They also are limited in scope, being only those words officially endorsed; nothing has been added to these lists in quite some time.



There are also ((Vocabulary|community edited vocabulary lists)), such as those sorted by topic or devoted to particular situations.



For tools to use with these lists, and for much larger lists, see ((Dictionaries, Glossers and parsers)).



For flashcard software that can help you learn the words, such as [http://memrise.com|Memrise] and [http://quizlet.com|Quizlet], see ((Software assisted learning)).



A separate page for ((jbo_cheat_sheet|cheat sheets)) has been set.



((Word frequency lists|Word lists sorted by usage)) are also available.

Women-specific 'artlang', invented by Suzette Haden Elgin. Lojban's ((evidentials)) are inherited from it (although of course, L�adan in turn got them from Amerindian human languages.)

What does it mean for a language to have a gender? - la ((kreig.daniyl.))

Check out [http://www.interlog.com/~kms/Laadan/,] and you tell me! :-)

Another "test of the ((Sapir-Whorf Hypothesis))" language.

''It has words for womanly things. That doesn't make it womanly, just gender-neutral. English may be "male" in some way, but laadan strikes me as neither.''

L�adan is clearly feminine; see the pretty purple they used for the web page? It looks like a breast cancer site.

''But is the vocabulary purple also?'' (The vocabulary is indeed worth examining; it has specific terms for things like "discord in the home", "anger, for good and not futile reasons", "pain or loss which comes as a relief by virtue of ending the anticipation of its coming", "contentment despite negative circumstances", "the female sexual act", and "the act of relinquishing a cherished/ comforting/ familiar perception".)

I despair of this ThreadMode discourse ever congealing into DocumentMode :-) , but the foundational assumption of L�adan is that women's perception of the word is fundamentally incommensurable with men's. So if purple meta-means what I think it meta-means, the answer is yes.

It utilises two distinct tones:</div>
<ul style="line-height: 19px; list-style-type: square; margin-top: 0.3em; margin-right: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; margin-left: 1.5em; padding-top: 0px; padding-right: 0px; padding-bottom: 0px; padding-left: 0px; list-style-image: url(http://127.0.0.1/w/extensions/FCKeditor/css/bullet.gif); color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: sans-serif; text-align: -webkit-auto; "><li style="margin-bottom: 0.1em; "><i>lo</i>&nbsp;–&nbsp;<span title="Representation in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)">/lō/</span>&nbsp;or&nbsp;<span title="Representation in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)">/lò/</span>, a short, medium or low tone, represented by a single unmarked vowel</li>

<div style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: sans-serif; line-height: 19px; text-align: -webkit-auto; ">The word "Láadan" has three syllables: "lá-" with the short vowel /a/ plus high tone; "-a" with the short vowel /a/ and no tone; and "-dan".</div>
<div style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: sans-serif; line-height: 19px; text-align: -webkit-auto; ">Láadan does not allow any double [i.e., long] phonemes. Whenever two identical short vowels would occur side by side in a single morpheme, one of them has to be marked for high tone. When adding an affix would result in two identical vowels side by side, an epenthetic /h/ is inserted to prevent the forbidden sequence. The language will allow either "máa" or "maá," but not "maa". These combinations can be described as:</div>

<div style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: sans-serif; line-height: 19px; text-align: -webkit-auto; ">Láadan lacks the&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consonant" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consonant" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">consonants</a>&nbsp;<span title="Representation in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)">/p, t, k, ɡ, s/</span>. However, it uses&nbsp;<i>b</i>,&nbsp;<i>d</i>,&nbsp;<i>sh</i>&nbsp;(<span title="Representation in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)">/ʃ/</span>),&nbsp;<i>m</i>,&nbsp;<i>n</i>,&nbsp;<i>l</i>,&nbsp;<i>r</i>,&nbsp;<i>w</i>,&nbsp;<i>y</i>&nbsp;(<span title="Representation in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)">/j/</span>),&nbsp;<i>h</i>&nbsp;with the same phonetic value as English. In addition to these, three&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digraph_(orthography)" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digraph_(orthography)" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">digraphs</a>&nbsp;require further explanation:</div>
<ul style="line-height: 19px; list-style-type: square; margin-top: 0.3em; margin-right: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; margin-left: 1.5em; padding-top: 0px; padding-right: 0px; padding-bottom: 0px; padding-left: 0px; list-style-image: url(http://127.0.0.1/w/extensions/FCKeditor/css/bullet.gif); color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: sans-serif; text-align: -webkit-auto; "><li style="margin-bottom: 0.1em; "><i>th</i>&nbsp;–&nbsp;<span title="Representation in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)">/θ/</span>, a&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiceless_dental_fricative" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiceless_dental_fricative" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">voiceless dental fricative</a>&nbsp;(always as in English&nbsp;<i>think</i>, never as&nbsp;<i>then</i>),</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.1em; "><i>zh</i>&nbsp;–&nbsp;<span title="Representation in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)">/ʒ/</span>, a&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiced_postalveolar_fricative" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiced_postalveolar_fricative" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">voiced postalveolar fricative</a>&nbsp;(as English&nbsp;<i>plea<b>s</b>ure</i>),</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.1em; "><i>lh</i>&nbsp;–&nbsp;<span title="Representation in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)">/ɬ/</span>, a&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiceless_alveolar_lateral_fricative" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiceless_alveolar_lateral_fricative" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">voiceless alveolar lateral fricative</a>&nbsp;(as&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_language" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_language" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">Welsh</a>&nbsp;<i>llan</i>).</li></ul>
<div style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: sans-serif; line-height: 19px; text-align: -webkit-auto; "><b><font size="5">Grammar</font></b></div>
<div style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: sans-serif; line-height: 19px; text-align: -webkit-auto; ">Most Láadan sentences contain three&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammatical_particle" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammatical_particle" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">particles</a>:</div>
<ul style="line-height: 19px; list-style-type: square; margin-top: 0.3em; margin-right: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; margin-left: 1.5em; padding-top: 0px; padding-right: 0px; padding-bottom: 0px; padding-left: 0px; list-style-image: url(http://127.0.0.1/w/extensions/FCKeditor/css/bullet.gif); color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: sans-serif; text-align: -webkit-auto; "><li style="margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">The&nbsp;<i>speech-act</i>&nbsp;particle&nbsp;– this occurs at the beginning of the sentence and marks it as either a statement (<i>bíi</i>), a question (<i>báa</i>),&nbsp;<i>et cetera</i>; in connected speech or writing, this particle is often omitted. They are:
<dl style="margin-top: 0.2em; margin-bottom: 0.5em; "><dt style="font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Bíi&nbsp;</dt>
<dd style="line-height: 1.5em; margin-left: 2em; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Indicates a declarative sentence (usually optional)</dd>
<dt style="font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Báa&nbsp;</dt>
<dd style="line-height: 1.5em; margin-left: 2em; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Indicates a question</dd>
<dt style="font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Bó&nbsp;</dt>
<dd style="line-height: 1.5em; margin-left: 2em; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Indicates a command; very rare, except to small children</dd>
<dt style="font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Bóo&nbsp;</dt>
<dd style="line-height: 1.5em; margin-left: 2em; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Indicates a request; this is the usual imperative/"command" form</dd>
<dt style="font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Bé&nbsp;</dt>
<dd style="line-height: 1.5em; margin-left: 2em; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Indicates a promise</dd>
<dt style="font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Bée&nbsp;</dt>
<dd style="line-height: 1.5em; margin-left: 2em; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Indicates a warning</dd>
</dl>
</li></ul>
<ul style="line-height: 19px; list-style-type: square; margin-top: 0.3em; margin-right: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; margin-left: 1.5em; padding-top: 0px; padding-right: 0px; padding-bottom: 0px; padding-left: 0px; list-style-image: url(http://127.0.0.1/w/extensions/FCKeditor/css/bullet.gif); color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: sans-serif; text-align: -webkit-auto; "><li style="margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">The&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammatical_tense" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammatical_tense" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">grammatical tense</a>&nbsp;particle&nbsp;– this occurs second in the sentence and marks it as either&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Present_tense" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Present_tense" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">present tense</a>&nbsp;(<i>ril</i>),&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Past_tense" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Past_tense" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">past tense</a>&nbsp;(<i>eril</i>),&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_tense" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_tense" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">future tense</a>&nbsp;(<i>aril</i>) or hypothetical (<i>wil</i>); without the tense particle, the sentence is assumed to have the same tense as the previous sentence.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">The&nbsp;<i>evidence</i>&nbsp;particle&nbsp;– this occurs at the end of statements and indicates the trustworthiness of the statement. They are:
<dl style="margin-top: 0.2em; margin-bottom: 0.5em; "><dt style="font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">wa&nbsp;</dt>
<dd style="line-height: 1.5em; margin-left: 2em; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Known to speaker because perceived by speaker, externally or internally</dd>
<dt style="font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">wi&nbsp;</dt>
<dd style="line-height: 1.5em; margin-left: 2em; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Known to speaker because self-evident</dd>
<dt style="font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">we&nbsp;</dt>
<dd style="line-height: 1.5em; margin-left: 2em; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Perceived by speaker in a dream</dd>
<dt style="font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">wáa&nbsp;</dt>
<dd style="line-height: 1.5em; margin-left: 2em; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Assumed true by speaker because speaker trusts source</dd>
<dt style="font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">waá&nbsp;</dt>
<dd style="line-height: 1.5em; margin-left: 2em; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Assumed false by speaker because speaker distrusts source; if evil intent by the source is also assumed, the form is "waálh"</dd>
<dt style="font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">wo&nbsp;</dt>
<dd style="line-height: 1.5em; margin-left: 2em; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Imagined or invented by speaker, hypothetical</dd>
<dt style="font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">wóo&nbsp;</dt>
<dd style="line-height: 1.5em; margin-left: 2em; margin-bottom: 0.1em; ">Used to indicate that the speaker states a total lack of knowledge as to the validity of the matter</dd>
</dl>
</li></ul>
<div style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: sans-serif; line-height: 19px; text-align: -webkit-auto; ">Láadan is a&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verb%E2%80%93subject%E2%80%93object" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verb%E2%80%93subject%E2%80%93object" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">verb–subject–object</a>&nbsp;(VSO) language.&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verb" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verb" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">Verbs</a>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjective" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjective" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">adjectives</a>&nbsp;are interchangeable. There are no&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Article_(grammar)" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Article_(grammar)" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">articles</a>, and the object is marked by the&nbsp;<i>-th</i>&nbsp;or&nbsp;<i>-eth</i>&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffix" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffix" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">suffix</a>. The&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plural" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plural" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial;!
  background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">plural</a>&nbsp;number is shown only by the&nbsp;<i>me-</i>&nbsp;prefix to the verb (<i>wo-</i>&nbsp;is used in some versions of the language). The particle&nbsp;<i>ra</i>&nbsp;following a verb makes it negative.</div>

<div style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: sans-serif; line-height: 19px; text-align: -webkit-auto; ">The&nbsp;<i>speech act</i>&nbsp;particle, at the beginning of a sentence, can also carry several suffixes, which expand on the overall state of the sentence. For example,&nbsp;<i>bíi</i>&nbsp;begins a statement, but&nbsp;<i>bíide</i>begins a statement that is part of a narrative;&nbsp;<i>bóoth</i>&nbsp;begins a request made in pain;&nbsp;<i>báada</i>&nbsp;begins a question that is meant in jest.</div>
<div style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: sans-serif; line-height: 19px; text-align: -webkit-auto; "><b><font size="4">Pronouns</font></b></div>
<div style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: sans-serif; line-height: 19px; text-align: -webkit-auto; "><a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pronoun" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pronoun" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">Pronouns</a>&nbsp;in Láadan are built up from a number of constituent parts. The consonant&nbsp;<i>l</i>&nbsp;marks the&nbsp;<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammatical_person" _fcksavedurl="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammatical_person" style="color: rgb(0, 43, 184) !important; background-image: none; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: initial; ">first person</a>,&nbsp;<i>n</i>&nbsp;the second person and&nbsp;<i>b</i>&nbsp;the third person. Usually, these are followed by the vowel&nbsp;<i>e</i>. However, the vowel&nbsp;<i>a</i>&nbsp;is used to designate someone who is loved (<i>lhe-</i>&nbsp;is prefixed to describe someone who is despised). The suffix&nbsp;<i>-zh</i>&nbsp;is used to mark a plural pronoun for numbers up to four, and&nbsp;<i>-n</i>&nbsp;for numbers beyond that. Therefore,&nbsp;<i>lazh</i>&nbsp;means "we, several beloved", and&nbsp;<i>lheben</i>&nbsp;means "they, many despised".</div>

;tai'i:((Phenomimes and psychomimes|Phenomemes)) (proposed by ((tijlan)).) 

Converts following cmevla or zoi-quote into phenomeme. Broader term than ((ci'oi)) and ((sa'ei)). See also ((BPFK Section: Epistemology sumtcita|tai)), ((ki'ai)).



* Examples in some languages. 

** jpn: しーん (shiin): soundlessly.

** kor: 슬슬 (seul-seul): gently. 

** nep: हत्तपत्त (hattapatta): immediately.

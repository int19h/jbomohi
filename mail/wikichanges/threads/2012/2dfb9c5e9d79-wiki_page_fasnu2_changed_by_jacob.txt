According to the gimste, 

''fasnu = x1 (event) is an event that happens/occurs/takes place; x1 is an incident/happening/occurrence.'' 

However, ((la tsani|I)) believe that ''fasnu'' should have a second place, in order to better comply with my ((Tsani's Interpretations: Abstractors|ideas about abstractors)). ''fasnu'''s x2 would be the predication of which the event (fasnu1) is an instance. Adding this second place to fasnu turns ''lo nu broda'' into sugar for ''lo fasnu be lo du'u broda''.

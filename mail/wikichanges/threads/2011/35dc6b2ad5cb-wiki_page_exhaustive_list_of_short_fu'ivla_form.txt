When worrying about whether or not a word is legal, please recall that all brivla must be stressed on the second to last syllable.

"__((nice fu'ivla forms))__" are those forms that don't contain VVV, CC/C or CCCC groups, which can never appear in lujvo, and start with one or two consonants. These nice forms are the ones that sit most comfortably among native Lojban forms.

4-letter fu'ivla
*VCCV  iglu alga (in all cases where CC is allowed non-initially, C/C is also allowed. Those are not listed separately.

5-letter fu'ivla
*CCVVV praia
*CCCVV spra'i (VV can't be a diphthong)
*VCCVV ostoi
*VCCCV astro
*VVCCV uiski

Notably, CCVCV and CVCCV aren't present in the above list, as those are the forms used by gismu.

6-letter fu'ivla
*__CVVC/CV__ ma'agni
*CVCC/CV saprka
*CCVVVV brai'oi  
*__CCVVCV__ tce'exo
*CCCVVV stroia
*CCCCVV tsmla'i (VV can't be a diphthong) (camxes won't like this form because of the initial cluster)
*VC/CVVV iksoia
*VC/CVCV odbenu
*VCCCVV astroi (VV must be a diphthong)
*VC/CCVV aksroi
*VCC/CVV asrkoi
*VCCCCV astrgo
*VVCCVV iandau
*VVCCCV iastro
*VVVCCV uaisto

  Yes, I can make sense of this.  He is saying that the one thing that
confounds translation software is the heavy use of synonyms of words, but
use of the most basic words would make translation much easier.  Therefore

as captioning for videos.

  That being said, the problem is the exact oposite. Words that are ore
unusual are less likely to have multiple and/or ambiguous definitions, and
therefore the easiest to translate automatically.  That's why the earliest
successful translators worked on specialized domains like technical
journals.

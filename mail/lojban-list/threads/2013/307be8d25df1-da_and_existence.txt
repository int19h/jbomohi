In another thread there were comments that led me to believe that {da} must
refer to a thing that actually exists.  The definition also sounds like
that is the case:
>
> logically quantified existential pro-sumti: there exists something 1
> (usually restricted).


That seems kind of strange to me.  So, does {da poi na zasti} basically
mean, by definition, the empty set?

And does that mean that I have to be very careful when using {da} to make
sure that we're talking about existent things?

i.e. if I'm thinking about unicorns what does it mean for me to say {da poi
mi pensi ke'a cu cinri}.  I guess in that example it might be ok since my
(thoughts about unicorns) do exist but... still, seems weird for {da} to
require existence (if in fact I'm understanding the definition correctly).

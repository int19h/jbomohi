{li pavovo} is the Lojban for "The number 144", so "A gross of (chicken)
eggs" could be {pavovo jipcyso'a}.

For "one part in 144", the Lojban {li pa pa'i pavovo} means "the number
1:144", or "The number ratio one in 144", and for "1/144", {li pa fi'u
pavovo}. or "The number 1-divided-by-144", "The numerical fraction 1 over
144".

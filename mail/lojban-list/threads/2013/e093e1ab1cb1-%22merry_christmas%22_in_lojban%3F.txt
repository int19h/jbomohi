I'd say "xisnunsla", as "xisysla" means someone who celebrates it, but drop 
"jbe", as Jesus was most likely born in Tishri, not Tevet. So we could say:
gleki xisnunsla
gleki .a'o xisnunsla
lo se gleki ko li'i xisnunsla

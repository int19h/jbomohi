coi rodo

It's my first post here, but I'm not a complete nintadni, and I'd like to 
take part in improving and popularizing lojban.

There are many things that may be very useful for me or everyone who is 
interested in lojban. We'd love to read about current state of affairs, 
news, proposals; we want to access all lojban-related programs; we want to 
add new lujvo to the jbovlaste; we want the information to be *organized 
and easily accessible*.

And what do we have instead? The great *mess*. I do not want to offend 
anyone, but the main web-site is ugly and unhandy. Someone will say that it 
does not matter, but it does! The usability must be one the primary 
concerns in any enterprise. Just look at the interface of jbovalste from 
the very first page http://jbovlaste.lojban.org/newaccount.html to the page 
where we add new words with its loooong and booooring instructions. The 
parser is broken (try "ti nanca li xa" or any other phrase with "li"). 
Mailing lists as the main way of communication are great if you do not want 
to find some particular info, because in this case the task becomes 
unbearable. And this list is in no way complete.

The lojban project is stagnated. And we must start its reanimation with 
reanimation of its *interface*. There's a plenty of work to do. We should 
start the "Usability Project" as soon as possible. To accomplish it, we 
need 1) experienced lojbanists who can organize and filter information, 2) 
programmers and designers who can create handy and featured software and a 
web-site.

mi'e remselgin

Surely. BTW, I'm a programmer. But obviously I cannot do all this work by 
myself. It is not an issue of some minor fixes or improvements such as 
fixing the jbovlaste web-pages -* it is an issue of a global change of the 
way the information is presented and processed.*

The way information about lojban is presented on its official resources may 
be appropriate for a small group of men working together, but it is not if 
we take into account newbies spread all over the world and even active 
"lojban developers". The current lojban project interface (i.e. its site, 
textbooks, media, reference etc.) looks like "Hey, guy, there's some 
awesome stuff inside, but you must read this big grammar with plain black 
text on the background, crunch a heap of mailing lists, and then you'll 
certainly appreciate its awesomeness". Lojban is really awesome, and it 
must look beautiful and attractive for everyone.

We can add interactive elements, browser applications, games, consistent 
learning programs for different kinds of users, we can form translator 
teams, add special features for "lojban developers" and even for BFPK if it 
will countinue its work... Also we have to remove outdated resources from 
main site and store them as history in the wiki, for example. I have a lot 
of ideas, and *later I will make a detailed roadmap *about this update. I 
hope most of lojbanists will support it, but still it is a team work, and 
any help will be appreciated, especially from those who have been with 
lojban for a long time.

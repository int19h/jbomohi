The current Lojban alphabet right consists of 23 letters, 5 capital or
accented vowels, the ' . and , as below:

aä b c d eë f g iï j k l m n oö p r s t uü v x y z ' . ,

The practice of capitalizing the entire emphasized syllable is still
practiced but has fallen out of favor, with accenting becoming more
widespread in use. (The original {la.paRIS.} became {la.parIs.} and is now
{la.parïs.})

The minimum Lojban alphabet would therefore require 31 characters.

Hello everybody,

I was trying to figure out how to express relative angular direction in 
Lojban, but I've been hindered by the unfathomable definition of {jganu}.

I'd like to translate « From me [vertex], there is a 1/8 leftwise turn 
angle between East and the mountain » (note: a turn is a complete 
revolution, otherwordly 360° or 2? rad).
How can I say this in Lojban?

Any suggestion would be greatly appreciated.
Best regards, Ilmen.

=== 2 | 2013-06-27T22:47:16Z | Jonathan Jones <eyeonus@gmail.com> | camkwhj2tqpgun1d553d_gh6r0g+s6waclbcjr3dj1ww13aoqqq@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-list/cur/1372373236.fb767054f4a22859.jbomohi:2,S
{jganu} defines the angle by a point (x2) and a line (x3).

In this picture:
{la'o my.∠ACB.my jganu cy. lo linji be .abu ce by.}
 [image: http://mathworld.wolfram.com/images/eps-gif/Subtend_700.gif]
I recommend using {radno <http://vlasisku.lojban.org/vlasisku/radno>} or {
carna <http://vlasisku.lojban.org/vlasisku/carna>}. You can easily specify
"1/8 turn", with {pifi'ubi carna}

And so you know, the direction place in {carna} defaults to anti-clockwise,
per the right-hand rule, and can be explicitly stated simply with {li ma'u}
(acw} and {li ni'u} (cw), as on a polar coordinate grid.

I'm afraid this wouldn't be compatible with the forethought sentence 
connective + BAI bo (which isn't currently grammatical, but which I 
proposed a while back):

      (1) .i je gi broda gi bai bo brode

should be the forethought version of:

      (2) .i broda .i je bai bo brode

I don't know why (1) is not grammatical even under current rules (with 
{ge} instead of {je gi} of course), and I feel it should be.

With {gi bai bo}, there'd be "ambiguity" (sa'e, a problem):

      (3) .i je gi broda [gi bai bo] brode [gi brodi]

Is the [gi bai bo] a bridi-tail connective, or does it act as the 
seperator for the sentence-jeks as in (1)? If the former, then [gi 
brodi] must be added to finish the jeks and the option for BAI+bo as in 
(1) is lost entirely.

mu'o mi'e la selpa'i

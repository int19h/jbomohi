Today we found that you can't use MEX with ROI, MOI, or MAI, even in the
PEG. camxes treats a closed MEX (e.g. {vei mo'e ko'a [te'u ve'o]} as a
"quantifier", distinct from PA* (i.e. PA* = PA | PA PA*), which it calls a
"number". With MOI we have the YACC-friendly hack "ME LI MEX MOI", which
still cooperates with the PEG, but as best I can tell there is no such hack
for ROI and MAI.

Would anything go wrong if MEX ROI, MEX MOI, and MEX MAI were made allowed
in the PEG? If not, can we get this into camxes?

Several of these have more than one translation, depending on how they are 
used in a sentence, or which homonym you mean, and "who" and "whom" are just 
different cases of the same word. Also some we haven't invented words for in 
Lojban.

> what gloss word for: -I know there is general terminology for group of
> words but here I ask about the special one gloss word for the word?
> 
> artery:      , vein:            , perfume:         , light:            ,

blutu'u (for both); panta'u; gusni, linto

> suit:          , mirror:         , rectangle:              ,

mapti; minra; clakurfa

> where:            , which:        , who:         , whom:         ,

bu'u ma; ma poi, lo mo; ma (poi prenu)

> whose:             , how "in the sense of an ask about the
> method":                       , how "in the sense of an ask about
> quantity":                 , what:            , yes:             ,

pe ma; ta'i ma, tai ma; la'u ma; ma; go'i

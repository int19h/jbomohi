I'm not discovering something new. It's rather a banality. And you can say 
it's just a small note about lojban. A small note but a big problem imo.

The biggest problem of this language is that it has not less than 4 
independent lists of words.

Let's take {zgana} for example.

gismu: zgana
short rafsi: zga
attitudinal: za'a
BAI-cmavo: ga'a

In some cases there can be more than one short cmavo.
The result is that many people who wish to learn this awesome language  are 
stuck at the level of memorizing those independent, *phonologically underivable 
from each other *sets of words.
It could have been done another way (see gua\spi) but it's a pity that this 
is a drawback of the most complete and (still !) most popular loglang on 
the planet.

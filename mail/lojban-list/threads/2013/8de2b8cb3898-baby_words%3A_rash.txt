Chances are that there will eventually be multiple different lujvo (and 
possibly some medical fu'ivla) for all the many different types of 
rashes there are. As a starting point, how about something like:

pilba'abi'a :
      x1 bilma lo ka lo pilka be ce'u cu se barna kei x2

"x1 has the symptom that their skin has spots from disease x2"

This would be a general term that works for any rash, but is still more 
specific than just {barna}.

For just "skin spots", there is {pilba'a}: "x1 are spots on x2's skin", 
which probably has its uses, too.

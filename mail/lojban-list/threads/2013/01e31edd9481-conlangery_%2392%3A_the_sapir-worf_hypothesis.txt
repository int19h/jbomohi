Yeah, I felt that was quite biased. I mean, sure, it's a controversial 
topic and there isn't much support for strong Sapir-Whorfianism today. But,

memorize an describe experiences or knowing which way is north), but they 
are very clear with that everything related to Sapir-Whorf is stupid and 
ridiculous. It seems more of a bashing than a discussion, but that's my 
five cents. I don't believe in Sapir-Whorf as originally hypothesized 
though.

positive, and somewhat thinking that it's an impossible and ridiculous

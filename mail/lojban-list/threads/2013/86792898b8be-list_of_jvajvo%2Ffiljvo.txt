After an email exchange on the lojban-beginners list (sorry I didn't notice
we were there!) I've made another attempt to create a list of "easy lujvo"

The idea is to create a class of lujvo, rather than defininig each single
lujvo, for some specific rafsi.

This is to expand the ideas in section 12 of CLL where -gau, -ma'u, -rai
and few others are explained.

I took the list that stela.selckiku proposed some time ago and I should
have captured the ones in section 12  that Jhonatan pointed to yesterday.

I'd like to take any comment/suggestion on this and, if deemed useful by
enough people, I'll try to condense them in a couple of pages to add to the
wiki.

Here's the current list: https://dl.dropbox.com/u/7681579/easylujvo.pdf

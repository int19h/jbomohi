{cpifratercula} is fine morphologically, but I think you'd want 
{cpifraterkula} instead, since {c} is pronounced [S] in Lojban and you 
want [k].

{cipnyfratercula} is not a fu'ivla, as fu'ivla cannot contain -y-. It is 
a lujvo from the veljvo {cipni fratercula} (gismu + zi'evla}. It would 
also be fine to omit the cipni-prefix altogether and just say {fraterkula}.

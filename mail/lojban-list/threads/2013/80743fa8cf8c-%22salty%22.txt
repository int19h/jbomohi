Now we have a problem. It would allow us {salni} if we hadn't already
{sarni} for triangle. This possible conflict is described in
http://dag.github.io/cll/4/14/
A quite reasonable rule I'd say. It's a pity it's not used when creating
lujvo with short rafsi.

So I suggest removing {sarni}. It won't make {salni} a winner but at least
would let it in.

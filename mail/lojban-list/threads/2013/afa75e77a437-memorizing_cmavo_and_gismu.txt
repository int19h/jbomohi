The two flash card programs most used are Anki and Memrise.

Anki is offline, Memrise online, and you can find all the various Lojban
sets by searching for them within their respective search features.

least this set- http://www.memrise.com/course/37344/simplified-gismu/ -

audio. I know the Memrise version does because I put it there myself, but
Robin did the Anki version.

=== 4 | 2013-11-10T14:28:10Z | Jacob Errington <nictytan@gmail.com> | caoq8vdvck3b3wvrvkk4lb3vskmrpuq3quc5kpbfuwtmgmz-04q@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-list/cur/1384093690.352766ec1db5f8ed.jbomohi:2,S
The only advice I can offer is to guarantee that you *know* how the cmavo
works before memorizing it. Far too many times do nintadni come into IRC
saying that the cmavo definitions are opaque and impossible to learn from,
at which point the most sensible answer we can provide is "yes."
The cmavo definitions are not meant to be learned from, really. They're
best used as a reference for someone who already knows how the work, having
been taught how to use them in the CLL or a guide such as the Waves.

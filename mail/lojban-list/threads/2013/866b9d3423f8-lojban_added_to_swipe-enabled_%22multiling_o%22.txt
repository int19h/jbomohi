The technology has been made available due to the frequency dictionary that 
i provided the author with (it's attached to this message, please study it).

Probably dots should be optionally removed from words that start from 
vowels. 
*va'i* such words should be duplicated *pe'i*.

to make the application work proprely.

Technology similar to swipe (called Swype) is described here 
http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swype

And here is the video.
http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mRUoWUhcRlE

Note that Multiling just uses a similar swipe technology, it's not Swype.

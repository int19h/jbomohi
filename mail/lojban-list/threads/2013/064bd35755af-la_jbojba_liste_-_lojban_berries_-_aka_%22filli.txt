The main idea is to fill gaps in lojbanic lexicon.
1. Into jvs.
2. Into tatoeba.
3. There is at least one tab  that is called "semantic list". Grouping 
words into a thesaurus or a dictionary a la CycL/Upper ontology/Roget etc. 
would also be nice.


Note that i placed some other projects like translating books, stories and 
poems there. I don't think there serioud limitations in Google docs for 
spreadsheets with large number of tabs.



> What is LFN?
>

It's a list taken from Lingua Franca Nova dictionary. If you don't like it 
don't fill it.
la jbojba has many tabs. Choose the one that you like.
If you don't like any please create another tab and put your favorite lists 
there.

If you wanna other people to join your translation project you can import 
the source text of the book/story/song/poem there and inform us here about 
your project.
So it's a crowd-sourcing project not only for filling the vocabulary.

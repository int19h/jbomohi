We are building a MOOC platform (MOOC = Massive Open Online Course).

The USP of the platform is that it automatically and instantaneously 
applies errata back into the course materials.  All examinations are by 
peer review. We believe that action learning is the best way of learning. 
There are no 'teachers' on our Internet courses, there are just 
participants with different capabilities in the problem domain. 

We are looking for a closed room alpha test before the Internet launch in 
October.

It occurs to me that the Lojban language would be an excellent knowledge 
domain to test our system. And the Lojban culture seems very much aligned 
with our non-hierarchical crowd-based technology.

All participant created learning materials (which of course would be 
practically speaking nearly  error free) and tests and examinations would 
be donated to humanity in perpetuity via a Creative Commons license.

We are based in London, but we could offer to carry out the closed room 
test wherever the greatest number of enthusiastic Lojban people are – 
London? Washington? NY? Paris?

As I have just noticed today, Slack does one other thing.  If your name 
is mentioned in the discussion, Slack sends you a notice with the text 
in context.

If you use slack, gleki, I'm surprised that you don't get flooded with 
such notices, since your "name" is a common Lojban word.

For everyone else, it means that it should be easy to ask me (lojbab) a 
question on IRC without my actually being on.  I likely won't answer 
right away, and I am not sure how best to answer (if I answer on IRC 
when the questioner is not on, s/he might not see the answer, so I 
suspect that I would answer on Lojban List citing the question.).

Someone should try this sometime.

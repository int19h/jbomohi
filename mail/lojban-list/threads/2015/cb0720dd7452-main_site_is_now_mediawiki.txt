Hi Gleki,

Robin said I should talk to you about the MediaWiki presentation. Feel free
to forward this to other relevant parties...

I have not learnt much Lojban yet but I do also want to help attract more
learners. I've been liking the recent changes made and I found myself
spending an afternoon mocking up some suggestions for even further
improvement.

I've designed some new themed icons if you wanted to use them for the
MediaWiki, attached as SVG. You don't need to use them but I thought these
might match the existing colours and layout. Since making them, I realised
that "?" and "!" should probably be avoided as they are not Lojban symbols,
but perhaps that can be improved in a later iteration.

I would love to see the Lojban website and content translated into many
languages; I cannot help with the translation but I thought I'd make
mockups to see how having many more languages could work with the design.
So I have also attached that mockup as a PNG (which includes my icons
above).

Wow, I just reloaded the site and see that it has already been updated with
a similar idea to mine. I like the new content. Oh well, perhaps my icons
might still be useful?

Any feedback is more than welcome: I can make adjustments, of course.

Many thanks,
Timothy

The experimental gismu {datru} was invented to fix some of the shortcomings of {detri}, and unfortunately it was used for a while before anyone noticed that it conflicts with the official {tatru}. Disrupting usage is bad, but leaving a flagrant violation of our own rules in the lexicon is worse, so I've just added {datro} to la jbovlaste as a replacement.

I left a note in the notes of both words. Since there is usage of {datru}, we shouldn't necessarily downvote it so hard that it disappears from dictionaries... but on the other hand, dictionaries with working full-text search will come up with {datro} anyway.

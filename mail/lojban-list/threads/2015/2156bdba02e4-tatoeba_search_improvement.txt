Previously when you search for {.i} on Tatoeba it returned {go'i} and other
words with /'i/.

A change has been pushed to production
https://github.com/Tatoeba/tatoeba2/commit/bfd62f93bc1882b9466ad0b86a29e00450587a6d

It makes apostrophe a letter, not a separator thus it allows to search more
correctly. Searching for "i" no longer returns sentences with {go'i}.

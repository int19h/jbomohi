Although I already posted this to in Lojban to commited members here is a
crosspost in English.

The reference grammar of Lojban is now almost ready to be published. It's
technically a legacy edition meaning that nothing but formatting and
obvious mistypes are fixed there compared to the first and only paper
edition of it.

We are now ready to accept proposals to be included into future editions of
The Lojban Reference grammar (CLL).

Definitely, higher priority will have those proposals that
1. fix more mistypes
2. explain things better for newcomers, as well as linguists, logicians,
programmers whatever.
3. Rearranging materials like changing the order of chapters.
4. Applying corrections to the language approved by BPFK committee changing
the text of the grammar only if absolutely necessary.

Less priority will have more serious proposals like:
4. Adding new sections and chapters to CLL
5. Additions to the language
6. Non approved changes to the language (which apparently will never be
included before BPFK committee approves of them)

Proposals are accepted in any form but two suggestions:
A. The development goes on at https://github.com/lojban/cll/, you may add
issues at https://github.com/lojban/cll/issues . The current branch we'll
be experimenting in is
https://github.com/lojban/cll/tree/docbook-prince-cll-2.0 but more branches
may appear in future when deadlines of publishing new versions will be
coming.

As of now the closest milestone is
https://github.com/lojban/cll/milestones/lo%20nu%20gau%20ku%20la%20xuncku%20co'a%20jai%20nu%20ciksi%20lo%20jbobau%20pe%20lo%20cabna
which means issues in it are the first candidates for inclusion into CLL.

B. Aso you may use this mailing list for sending your proposals. I suggest
starting topics of your messages with [cll 2.0] or maybe [cll 3.0] (the
latter for proposals that you feel are more distant in future or more
hypothetical).

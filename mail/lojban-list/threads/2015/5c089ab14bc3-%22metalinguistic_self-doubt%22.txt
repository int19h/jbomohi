For metalinguistic concerns we have the {jo'a}-{na'i} scale; {ju'o} is 
different. For "did I word this correctly" I'd probably say {jo'a nai toi'e 
ju'o cu'i} or if experimental cmavo are not allowed, just resort to {sei 
.oise'i xu smudra}. Unless there's a lot of usage of {ju'o} in conflict 
with the dictionary definition, I'd say we should leave it alone.

With some experimental grammar you can get the following which I reckon 
is a pretty spot on translation (minus the gender implications of 
"husband" and "wife").

.i .ai ca lo cabdei mi speni gi'e voi lo mi se speni ki pambi'o

Without any experimental stuff:

.i .ai ca lo cabdei mi me lo speni noi lo se speni (be ke'a ke'a/cu) pambi'o

In the parentheses, you can choose one or the other depending on how 
precise you want to be.

.i mi'e la tsani mu'o

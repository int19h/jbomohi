Right, this word is very common in discussions that occur in Lojban 
about Lojban's logic. We basically have been using it without ever 
defining its place structure.

To me the two main contenders are:

a) "x1 has scope over x2"

b) "x1 is the scope of operator x2"

We just never decided. And we failed at any attempts to formulate a 
convincing Lojban definition.

Definition a) sounds more "active". I *think* I have a slight preference 
for a). A third place might be needed (an "in context/bridi x3" place).

Btw, I think to get the kind of meaning that you entered into JVS one 
can use {lo si'o sko'opu}.

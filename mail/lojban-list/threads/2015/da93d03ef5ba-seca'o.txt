On Monday, August 31, 2015 15:56:01 Gleki Arxokuna wrote:

TAhE are tenses, {ca ca'o} is a tense or a construction of two tenses, {PA 
roi} I think is a tense but I'm not sure, but {fi'o fadni} is not a tense. BAI 
is equivalent to {fi'o <brivla>} (except {do'e}, which is {fi'o co'e}) and can 
be converted with SE.

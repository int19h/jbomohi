I see that Duolingo has been mentioned a couple times here already, but 
those topics didn't get much response, and things have changed a bit in the 
past couple years.

With Duolingo <https://www.duolingo.com/>, you start out learning and 
translating simple phrases and sentences, then work your way up to longer 
texts. The process is kind of "gamified" in that you earn points and things 
as you go along. They also can send you reminders to practice a little bit 
every day. I think Duolingo would be a good way to learn Lojban – 
certainly, the interactive courses are better and easier than studying 
flash cards by yourself.

Duolingo is free, and the content for new language courses is generated by 
the community. They've recently added other constructed languages -- 
Esperanto and even Klingon -- so if enough of us express interest, there's 
no reason we can't have Lojban be part of the list. You can go to the Duolingo 
forum <https://www.duolingo.com/discussion> to talk about adding Lojban and 
if you know enough to help, apply here 
<https://incubator.duolingo.com/apply>.


I really think this would be a good idea because it would give Lojban more 
exposure, and help new people learn in a more interactive way.

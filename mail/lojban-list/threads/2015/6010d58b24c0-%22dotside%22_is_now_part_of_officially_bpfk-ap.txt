The membership of the re-authorized BPFK has taken a vote on the rule 
commonly referred to as "Dotside",

> All cmevla must begin and end with a glottal stop or pause.
> {la}, {lai}, {la'i} and {doi} are allowed in cmevla.

with 7 votes in favor and 1 abstention.[1]

(The 1 abstention is probably because the person in question doesn't 
read Lojban anymore, and BPFK is conducting all its business mainly or 
exclusively in Lojban now.)

The rule is thereby considered part of officially BPFK-approved standard 
Lojban.

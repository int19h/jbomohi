mumble のインストール方法はこちらで大部分日本語訳されています：
http://sourceforge.jp/projects/sfnet_mumble/howto/install

そこには gengoo 系について書かれていませんが、 distro に入っているので
http://gpo.zugaina.org/media-sound/mumble
すぐに emerge できます。
# emerge =media-sound/mumble-1.2.8 --autounmask-write
# etc-update
# emerge =media-sound/mumble-1.2.8

mumble の使い方はこちらに日本語で書かれています：
http://sourceforge.jp/magazine/10/03/11/093256

GUIでのサーバへの接続方法は私にはよくわからなかったのですが、GUIで接続の設定をしなくても、以下のコマンドで自動的にロジバンの部屋に入れます。

Linux （多分 Mac も）の場合、 terminal で以下のように入力します：

mumble 
mumble://mumble.metal-bunker.info:64738/lojban?title=Metal%20Bunker&version=1.2.0

Windows の場合はコマンド・プロンプトで多分こうです：

mumble.exe 
mumble://mumble.metal-bunker.info:64738/lojban?title=Metal%20Bunker&version=1.2.0

> I noticed that some people have trouble operating Skype.
> So here are the basic rules of a successful voice chat for Skype
>
> 1. Use a group where all potential speakers share their interest in 
> the forthcoming conference in text form only. This group already 
> exists. It's called {ko ciska po'o tavla ...} (meaning "Talk by 
> writing only...") This group is only for planning, not for actual 
> talking. Ask in this mail thread to add you there.
> 2. One person runs a desktop version of Skype and has good incoming 
> and outcoming internet connection (~1Mbit/sec in both directions is fine).
> 3. This person must add all interested people to his/her friend list 
> and those people must confirm "friendship".
> 4. This person calls the first person and after successful connection 
> drops other people into the chat window.
> 5. Please, everyone switch off your video by pressing on the video 
> button so that it becomes crossed out.
> 6. Before any conference check your microphone and audio settings of 
> Skype. Add "echo123" user (it's a bot) to your friend list, call it 
> and follow the instruction. Change settings of your Skype client if 
> necessary.
> 7. If you have noise around you during the conference or if other 
> members notice a noise from your side switch off your microphone by 
> pressing on the Microphone button so that it becomes crossed out.
>
> I think that as of now Skype is preferable since it has better voice 
> compression and buffering mechanisms preventing clicks/pauses during 
> conversation due to unstable internet connection. As of now it's

> connection. Many even change to mobile connection dropping the level

>

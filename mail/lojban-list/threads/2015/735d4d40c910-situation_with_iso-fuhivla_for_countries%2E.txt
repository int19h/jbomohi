Compared to the original xorxe's list around 15 countries don't have ISO
now.

E.g. St. Barthélemy was about to get .bl code however it didn't.

Countrees disappear, ISO codes get reassigned. So ISO-fuhivla are drifting
apart from Lojban system.
I propose
{gugdebulu} for St. Barthélemy
{gugdebuke} for Caribbean Netherlands
{gugdecupu} for Clipperton Island
{gugdecuve} Curaçao
{gugdedugu} Diego Garcia
{gugde'e'a} Ceuta & Melilla
{gugde'e'u} European Union
{gugde'icu} Canary Islands
{gugdetu'a} Tristan da Cunha
{gugdeke'o} Outlying Oceania
{gugdesusu} South Sudan
{gugdesuxe} Sint Maarten
{gugde'umu} U.S. Outlying Islands
{gugdevu'a} Vatican City
{gugdexuku} Kosovo
{gugdesuxe} St. Helena

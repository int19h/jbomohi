Here is a short peg.js parser of morphology of Lojban words.

Features:
1. only checks for morphology of words, the rest is thrown away. Hence, you
don't need much prettification, a simple
'[["cmavo","coi"],["cmavo","do"],["cmavo","mi"],["gismu","tavla"],["cmavo","do"]]'
is returned.
2. when you need a parser of minimal size. morfologi.js
<http://mw.lojban.org/extensions/ilmentufa/morfologi.js> file, the compiled
parser ready to use by javascript-compatible apps is under 30 kilobytes of
uncompressed (but minified) javascript.
3. can help you study lojban morphology from PEG
<http://mw.lojban.org/extensions/ilmentufa/morfologi.js.peg>, which is
easier to grasp when everything else is removed.
4. can help restore omitted spaces within compound cmavo and similar (so
that you can apply your writing conventions)
5. somewhat faster than the full grammar parser when you run numerous
queries. E.g. this parser is now used in la sutysisku
<https://mw.lojban.org/extensions/ilmentufa/i/en/index.html#sisku/ni'u> app
to automatically determine to which word class a given dictionary entry
belongs.
6. doesn't support zoi ... zoi quotations (a separate preprocessor needed).
7. http://mw.lojban.org/extensions/ilmentufa/morfologi.html

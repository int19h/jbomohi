Deuteronomy 23 (va is the Hebrew numbering, v the Gentile numbering):
\va 11 \v 10 .i lo da'i nanmu poi naljinsa ri'a lo nicte fasnu cu .ei barkla 
lo ginka gi'enai nerkla lo ginka
\va 12 \v 11 .i ca lo vanci ra sezlu'i fi lo djacu .i ca lo solnuncanci ra 
nerkla lo ginka

According to jbovlaste, {sezlu'i} has x1, which is both x1 and x2 of {lumci}, 
and x2, which is x3 of {lumci}. x4 of {lumci} is missing. In this verse, a man 
washes himself with water (lo ve lumci, lo fi'a sezlu'i?). What he washes off is 
not specified (presumably whatever he got on himself during the night). 
Shouldn't {sezlu'i} have a place for the stuff (water) that he uses to wash 
himself?

It's a long standing issue that IRC chat that most Lojbanists use is out of
fashion in the modern days internet.

We now have a new gateway to it from another popular app, Telegram
<http://telegram.org> instant messenger.

So to get connected to Lojban chat and to be able to read the history of
the chat from the day you joined like pros in Lojban do
you now have the following options:

1. In Telegram search find "lojban" user (@lojban) and ask 'em to add you
to the chat.

Alternatively,
2. http://vrici.lojban.org:3001 Submit your email and get an invite to
http://lojban.slack.com in your e-mail inbox.

Alternatively,
3. Use IRC chat directly via
http://kiwiirc.com/client/irc.freenode.net/?#jbosnu,#ckule,#lojban

The Era of Lojban IRC Detachment is over. Congrats!

P.S. Direct links to join Telegram Lojban chat won't be posted here to
prevent spammers from infiltrating. As of now there is no technical means
of making gateways to other instant messengers like WhatsApp and LINE.

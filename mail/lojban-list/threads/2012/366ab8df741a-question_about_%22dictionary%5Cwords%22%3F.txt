No language can ever be 'complete' - new words are always needed as
the world continues to change. I'm sure you've found that there is
vocabulary missing from the dictionary (I have missed a lujvo for
'bacon', for example). New lujvo will inevitably be added, and I'd be
surprised if no new gismu are ever created although this should be
rare. There are also proposals for new cmavo, and some of those will
inevitably be formally added to the language.

I do not speak officially of course, but this is the general
impression I've got while I've been studying Lojban.

=== 6 | 2012-03-07T18:45:25Z | vruxir <kextrii@gmail.com> | 17025575.2830.1331145925583.javamail.geo-discussion-forums@vbiz13 | mail/lojban-list/cur/1331145925.d6ad5c4f650cabb6.jbomohi:2,S
I saw the same thing yesterday (on Chrome), so I asked the creator what the 
problem was. He said the font didn't have a specified character for "space" 
-- which wasn't a problem in some cases (Firefox didn't show boxes).

Anyway, he added a character for space, and reuploaded the font. If you 
clear your recent cache (and delete the font from your fonts folder, if you 
downloaded and installed it) and reload the page, it should look okay now. 
(And the bookmarklet should work fine too.)

I'm having fun reading English in Dotsies -- certain words create very 
memorable and aesthetically pleasing shapes. I'm going to practice some 
more and see if I eventually benefit from it.

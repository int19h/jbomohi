So, y'all may have noticed that a lot of work around here is waiting
on me specifically, and y'all may also have noticed, via my
plentiful rants on the subject, that I've *tried* to get other
people to take this stuff on and it hasn't really worked.

So, here's another option: what if y'all paid for some of my time?

It turns out that my work will let me do 32 hours a week and still
get benefits.  It also turns out that my before-tax income is about
$550 per work day (I would prefer not to take a pay cut).

So I find myself wondering: if people in the community pledged
monthly money for this purpose, could Lojban afford to have me doing
a day or two each month of nothing but Lojban-related work?

Would people even want to do that?

Is my arrogance astounding or *what*?  :D

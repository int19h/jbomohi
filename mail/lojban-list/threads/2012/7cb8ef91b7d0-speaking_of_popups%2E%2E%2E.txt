Firefox users, today's your lucky day. I think I've heard people ask for a 
browser popup extension before... So I made one. It's called Lojban Popup:

https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/lojban-popup/

(Just published, so it's not in the search results yet.)

It's based on the code for Mandarin Popup, my favorite popup add-on for 
Firefox, and has more or less the same functionality. When you hover over 
text, it pulls up possible matches from a JSON-ized local copy of 
jbovlaste. It ignores spaces, dots, commas, and punctuation in order to 
maximize useful matches (ignoring spaces isn't ideal, but better to catch 
extras than to miss something).

The popups match on words (gismu, cmavo, cmene, lujvo, fu'ivla, etc.) or 
rafsi.

You can right click and bring up a dictionary browser by which you can 
search the local dictionary by word, rafsi, or selma'o (search in all caps 
to match selma'o; otherwise, the search is case-insensitive).

The local dictionary is up to date as of May 24, 2012. My main concern in 
terms of upkeep (other than serious bug fixes) is updating the dictionary 
file, since I had to do a lot of tweaking to set it up. (In other words, 
grumble grumble don't ask for frequent updates :)

Take it for a spin, let me know if there are serious problems, and TRY not 
to become overdependent and get tunnel vision :)

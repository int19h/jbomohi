coi rodo,

         I'm relatively new to lojban and have a question about lujvo.  
Since there are so many rafsi, how is it reasonable to assume that others, 
even those who are "fluent", would be able to mentally parse the lujvo into 
its rafsi parts and understand which brivla the rafsi is short for in 
normal conversation?  I do not think that the human brain's language 
centers are capable of this type of language processing, at least not at a 
reasonable speed.  Sure, you can figure out lujvo, but not quickly enough 
to have a conversation using lujvo made up on the spot, only lujvo that are 
commonly used.

co'o rodo

coi .adam. mi'e .alyn.

I'd like to participate in a Lojban hackathon; I wasn't able to get
to LogFest this year and I think it would be a good substitute for
that event for me.

I'd specifically like a day/weekend where we all meet on IRC and
support each other's personal work, find problems in common and
fix the ones that come in bite-sized pieces.

I'm working on the PEG parser, and Robin is working on the CLL.
There is a lot of social network activity of late as well as all
manner of projects pushing in multiple directions.  Translation,
audio recording, writing, &c.

I consider our software infrastructure a bit of a mess, but I
consider the organization of Lojbanistan generally a bit of a mess,
so take that for what it's worth!

If we were going to have anything to specifically focus on, it would
be working with Robin on the CLL, hands down.  He has a list of tasks
to get done, some of which he's burned out/blocked on.

Left to my own devices, I would continue my work of extracting
Lojbanic sentences out of the CLL and adding them to the test suite
for my parser.  It's fairly rote and a good way to uncover bugs.

If I were going to point anywhere else, it would be dictionary work.
I don't know if anyone could succinctly summarize what needs doing
there, at least not in a manner that won't engenders a lot of
follow-up conversation.  It's a project with a lot of history/
voices/needs, with just enough infrastructure to work well enough.

mu'o

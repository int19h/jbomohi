I need your feedback. I have been thinking on naming conventions for a
fictional story; more specifically on what would be family names. On
my fictional story, boys take the name of their fathers as family name
and girls take the name of their mothers. The name is built by putting
them together by using {pe} thus: {la zmabaz nitoris pe la nolzu'e zei
ioxnaris}. In this example the character name is {la zmabaz nitoris}
while eir family name is {la nolzu'e zei ioxnaris}. But I have been
thinking that this might not be actually a full name, but two names
bound together by {pe}. Is that how is it interpreted? Would this
naming conventions be a possibility in the real world?

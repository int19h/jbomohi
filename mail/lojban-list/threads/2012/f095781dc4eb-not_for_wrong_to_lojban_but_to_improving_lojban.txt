Hey ki'es.di'es

With regards to point (5), this has been discussed. A scheme to use
punctuation (characters like ? and ") to aid clarity in texts can be found
here
http://www.lojban.org/tiki/typography
This page also links to a discussion about using punctuation and also to a
table of listing what characters have what use.

The first two points, (1) and (2), lojban can be written in many different
ways. Each different way is called a "mode" or "orthography". Normally we
use the 'latin mode', but some people have developed others. Lots of them
are posted on jboctan.org . You may find the arabic orthography interesting
http://jbotcan.org/ideas/#629 . It can be fun to play around with different
scripts, but the latin one is very dominant. On the other hand, they should
all be bijective mappings of each other, so easy to display it however you
want.

Your other points I do not understand.

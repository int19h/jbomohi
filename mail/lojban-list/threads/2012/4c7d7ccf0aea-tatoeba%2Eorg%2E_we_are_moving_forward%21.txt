Thanks everyone for translating more sentences in tatoeba.org!

After my last appeal in Facebook and G+ we moved from place No 35 to No 33 
with 3779 sentences (btw, leaving Interlingua and Ido behind).

Currenly I dream of reaching 5200 sentences therefore I'm placing this 
updated message, .a'o relevant for every jbopre.

*Leave Berber language behind in 10 days!*
See how few sentences has been translated to Lojban in tatoeba.org?
http://tatoeba.org/eng/stats/sentences_by_language. Lojban is currently No 
33.

If there are 30 Lojbanists around and each translates only 5 sentences a 
day we can reach Berber by the number of sentences just in 10 days!

Yes, quality says more than quantity so I suggest translating the most 
important sets of sentences like
*Tourist sentences* - http://tatoeba.org/eng/sentences_lists/show/6 
*Colloquial* - 
http://tatoeba.org/eng/tags/show_sentences_with_tag/Colloquial
*Everyday life* - 
http://tatoeba.org/eng/tags/show_sentences_with_tag/everyday_life

They are very important for nintadni.

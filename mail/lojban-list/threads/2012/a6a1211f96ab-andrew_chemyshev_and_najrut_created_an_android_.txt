I downloaded one of the recent GoldenDicts presented by najrut earlier in 
this group.
And immediately contacted Andrew Chemyshev who used to create plugins for 
Android version of Goldendict.

So now we have on Android marker
English - Lojban dictionary<https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=udm.udmurtlyk.dictdata.engjbo&feature=search_result#?t=W251bGwsMSwxLDEsInVkbS51ZG11cnRseWsuZGljdGRhdGEuZW5namJvIl0>
Russian - Lojban dictionary<https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=udm.udmurtlyk.dictdata.rusjbo&feature=search_result#?t=W251bGwsMSwyLDEsInVkbS51ZG11cnRseWsuZGljdGRhdGEucnVzamJvIl0>

Unfortunately they mentioned my name as the author of the app which is 
obviously not true.

However, now we have nice dictionaries for Android better than offered here 
previously.

Let's test the app.

I should mention that if you don't have a free version of Goldendict 
installed on your device the app will offer you to do so.
And secondly, free version of GoldenDict allows you to use not more than 5 
dictionaries (so if you will use it for Lojban only it's ok but otherwise 
you have to deactivate other dictionaries).

Anyway, thanks, najrut and Andrew for your great work!!!

A zombie is a specific kind of un-dead. In the Voodoo myth, a zombie would  
not only be called back from the death but also be controlled by the the  
one performing the ritual

This last characteristic is reflected in the usage of "zombie" to designate  
computers that have been compromised and can be controlled from outstide.

My proposal is to import the word as a fu'ivla as it is a very specific  
word, used more or less in all languages:

{dzombii} -> x1 is a zombie summoned/controlled by x2

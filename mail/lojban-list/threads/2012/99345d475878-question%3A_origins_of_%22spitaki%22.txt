> Can anyone explain me the etymology of "spitaki"? From which language

Parrots are from the order of Psittaciformes. Using part before -formes, 
we are left with "psittaci". To turn this into a stage-4 fu'ivla, we 
can't use *{psitaki}, because {ps} is not valid. Therefore, we switch ps 
to sp and get {spitaki} = parrot.

Exactly, and to the grammatical unit bounded by that word (I think I 
didn't make that up).  Just as attaching it to the .i would attach it to 
the whole sentence, putting it after {lo} attaches it to the entire 
sumti-phrase.

Otherwise, you could put it after the {be'o} that was elided.

There are established hexadecimal numbers:
A=dau
B=fei
C=gai
D=jau
E=rei
F=vai

So you could use {dau} and {fei}, though making clear which base is
being used is left as an exercise for the reader.

Lojban has numbers up to base 16.
I'm sure you're familiar with the digits from 0 to 9. Next:
dau = A
fei = B
Those are enough to express any number in base 12.

    12     =      1A    [base 12]

>> functions, du'u (which should probably be nu) and ka (propositions with

>> hallucinations, or the representational ones.  The notion of a general
>> abstraction is basically unintelligible and seems to be there for
>> "completeness". Most abstractions abstract in a particular way (see the
>> functions on worlds reading for some) and most intensional contexts are
>> generated by predicates that allow such contexts (and occasionally require
>> them).

>> As for "su'u" as general subordinator, it was never used that way,

>> mainly with propositional attitude predicates. It's a relatively short
>> list, maybe twenty or so gismu. In most other cases you can use "nu".

>> by the subordinate bridi itself.

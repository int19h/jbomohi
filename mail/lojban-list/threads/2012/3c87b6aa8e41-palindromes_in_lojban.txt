I did some automatic mining for palindromes in Lojban. I looked for
those containing only words listed in jbovlaste and of course
parseable (I checked with jbofihe, I hope the only bug is accepting
texts ending with ZOI).
Results:
all 1-word palindromes: a, e, i, o, u, y, a'a, e'e, i'i, o'o. u'u,
y'y, ii, uu, iklki, mam (all of them parse)
all grammatical 2-word palindromes: https://dl.dropbox.com/u/17805197/lojban-palindromes/lojban2-parsed
all grammatical 3-word palindromes: https://dl.dropbox.com/u/17805197/lojban-palindromes/lojban3-parsed
I filtered palindomes containing brivla, as they are usually more
interesting:
2-word: ermine nimre, ernace canre, iklki iklki [well...], i ctitci, i
jmatamji
3-word: https://dl.dropbox.com/u/17805197/lojban-palindromes/lojban3-brivla

Enjoy! I haven't read them all, if you find something funny, please
share!

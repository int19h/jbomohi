Hi all,

I have a conscript project, a rather large one. So far it has stayed 
completely alphabetical and adaptable to any alphabet or language. It has 
developed enough that I am musing paring it up with a lang(con or nat) and 
developing a more advanced version, tailored for application. I'll spare 
you the history/backstory, started as binary represenation, evolved as I 
learned Chinese, blah blah...

I am very drawn to lojban, being a programmer, and am considering investing 
the time to learn, and hopefully talior a Dscript version for it.

Dscript, dimensional script as I call it, started as a "directional 
script"( could be written both vertically and horizontally in cursive with 
a single design ). Dimensionality come up when these "letter strings" are 
allowed to fork and letters be placed inside of letters.

The "letter strings" can be curled up and contorted to taste, providing 
more flexibility and options than traditional scripts. A user can often 
even achieve a "logo graphic" version that is still legible alphabetical 
text, this is mostly just a product of there being so much selection in 
word forms that something will likely fit. one could call it "alphabetical 
glyphs" I suppose.

There are also number systems, base 10/16, 12, 32, and my fav base 100. 
There is even a chemistry notation system that basically sacrifices stereo 
chemistry in order to allow fundamental structure to be drawn much 
"simpler" with less "noise". poor for precision, excellent for large 
bio-chem stuff.

The "Dscript hole" just seem to keep going, I just seem to keep digging 
myself a deeper hole.

Anyways, my point is do you guys think Dscript would pair up well with 
lojban? Do there appear to be any natural synergies or development paths?

I have attached an example text and the word "lojban" in Dscript, I just 
used English version Dscript to write the lojban.
You would probably want to revamp the alphabet based on common letter 
combos of lojban, here is a mini quick start guide to re-index the alphabet

There are also some sample of various Dscript elements attached.
All is available creative commons, free to copy/edit/sell, materials at:
http://dscript.ca
http://dscript.org

key question: anyone interested in discussing applying Dscript to Lojban?

I love feedback, and am willing to help apply Dscript, I just love seeing 
it used :)

Strictly speaking, there is no official writing system. Any writing system
that can unambiguously refer to each of lojban's sounds is valid, but none
is official. The Roman writing system, albeit the most widespread and de
facto official script, is not *technically* official.

Sorry to pick nits here, but I don't like seeing anyone being mislead, even
those who are unlikely to even really learn much lojban.

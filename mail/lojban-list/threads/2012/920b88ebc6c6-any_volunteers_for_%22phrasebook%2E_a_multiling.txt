Hi,

Anything that helps learning Lojban is welcome. This is useful in my 
opinion. I think missing translations should be blank, so that it is easier 
to focus attention on them. 

Another issue is what to do for controlling the quality of translations? It 
would not be enough if someone checks/approves them, since they could be 
latter changed again. Once this grows substantially and there are many 
users, we will face the issue of quality control. We need a way to lock 
phrases once we know they are ok.

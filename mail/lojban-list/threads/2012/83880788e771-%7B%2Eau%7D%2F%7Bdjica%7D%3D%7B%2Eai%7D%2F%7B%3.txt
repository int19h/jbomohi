.ai = zukte
Someone does something (zukte2) for some purpose (zukte3), all of which 
is intentional.
The purpose (zukte3) of action z2 is what their intention is in doing 
zukte2.  (Wow, that is horribly phrased.)
Maybe an example will be helpful.

.ai mi na za'u re'u citka lo rectu
~=
mi zukte fi lo nu na ze'u re'u citka lo rectu

You could ask what the zukte2 would be in such cases. I think, often 
zukte2 and zukte3 can be identical. The action is also the intention, 
that is, the action is 'intentional'.

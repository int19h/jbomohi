I don't think one exists. There is not yet a single coherent naming scheme
in lojban culture.

Some people take words they like (e.g. la gleki arxokuna, la gejyspa), some
people lojbanize their names, possibly with some modifications (la lojbab,
la xorxes), some people make cmevla out of rafsi they like (I can't think
of any examples on the list), and some people just do random stuff (my name
is derived from my handle).

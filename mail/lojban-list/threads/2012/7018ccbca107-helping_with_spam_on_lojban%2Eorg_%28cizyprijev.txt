If you're going to delete spam on lojban.org, which I *hugely*
appreciate, the following things need to happen or I'll just have to
go do it myself in the end anyways:

1.  The pages need to be *deleted*, not just emptied of content

2.  The users in question need to have their email address and
passwords changed.

3.  Ideally, you'd be on the wikineurotic mailing list so you can
see *all* the spam.

Anyone who wants admin perms for this stuff, you have only to ask.

-Robin

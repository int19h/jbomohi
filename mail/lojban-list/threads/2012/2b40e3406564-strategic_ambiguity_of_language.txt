I agree that such "ambiguities" are important and I think it's a Lojban
plus to be able to reflect the same ambiguity.

{mi noroi cusku le sedu'u ko'a zerle'a lo jdini pe mi}

WIth the added benefit of being able to convey the disambiguation
grammatically (i.e. without resorting to typographical conventions like
bold) using {ba'e}:

{ba'e mi noroi cusku le sedu'u ko'a zerle'a lo jdini pe mi}
{mi ba'e noroi cusku le sedu'u ko'a zerle'a lo jdini pe mi}

etc.

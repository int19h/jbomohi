Okay, to record a session in G+:

Start a "hangout", then choose option to broadcast/record.  Up to 10 people
can participate, any number of people can watch.

Skype:

Download one of the apps here:
http://shop.skype.com/apps/Search-Results.html?q=&category=Call_recording_audio_video&cost=free&lang=&os=&date=&sort=rating#results
(There are more than just the four listed on that page, but they require
money.)

Follow the instructions for the particular app.


It looks to me like G+ would be much easier for the recording thing. From
what I've read, Skype has the same 10 person video limit, is harder to
record sessions with, and doesn't allow people beyond the 10 to at least
watch.

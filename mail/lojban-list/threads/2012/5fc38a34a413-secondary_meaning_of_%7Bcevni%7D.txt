{cevni} can refer to many different things, but not angels. Gods,
goddesses, demi-god(esse)s, oni, and so on and so forth.

But not angels. Angels are not gods, they aren't even god-like. Except for
maybe the archangels, they're barely above humans, and in many ways are
lower on the totem pole (like the complete lack of free will, emotion, or
creativity, for example).

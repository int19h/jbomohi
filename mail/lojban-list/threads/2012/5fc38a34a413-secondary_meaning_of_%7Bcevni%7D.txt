When I speak of gods, I don't bother about what is actual, specifically
because there's literally no what to know what that is. I have my own
beliefs about the matter, of course, but I don't pretend to think that my
beliefs are the definite truth. So, when I talk about gods, I talk about
beings like Thor, Ra, Gaeia, Minerva, and Pluto; beings which more than
likely do not now and never did exist, but which at some point in time-
whether or not "now" is part of that time frame- were believed to be the
real thing by somebody.

about lo cevni will be about those which are believed in, and not the
ineffable actuality, and have no problem with forcing those who do wish to
talk about the ineffable actuality in Lojban to use lo cevni be zi'o to do
so.

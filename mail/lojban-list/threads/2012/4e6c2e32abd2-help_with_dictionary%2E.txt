Here are some words that I've encountered so far what I could not easily 
translate.
apply (for a job)   -   jibri + cpedu ?
blackboard   -   zasterci'a (I've coined this and been using it as any kind 
of temporary surface, like whiteboard / blackboard / chalk board).
bubble   -   fomsle ?

Do take a little bit of time, but keep in mind that tanru are acceptable 
answers if that's what works best (like "book shelf").

Hi all,

Just to let you know you can donate to the LLG via Bitcoin at:

1LojbanMUEZHS22Teeiiiesw8v19Q25Xhy
or
1LojbANzafAZT2hBNuKtpwotfz3xFKa2qK (firstbits address: 1lojban)

Please note this is experimental so I wouldn't recommend sending more than a Bitcoin or two for now, although if we got many milli-Bitcoin donations that would be great :-D

If you're wondering how to get hold of Bitcoins, if you're in the US you can get them by depositing cash at any major bank and many grocery stores via BitInstant.  Outside the US it's probably easiest to get them from a currency exchange.  If you don't want to pay for them you can also get them just by looking at adverts and filling in CAPTCHAs (e.g., http://skude.se/BTC/) and accumulate them in a free online wallet (e.g., https://instawallet.org/) until there's enough to donate.

Thank you,

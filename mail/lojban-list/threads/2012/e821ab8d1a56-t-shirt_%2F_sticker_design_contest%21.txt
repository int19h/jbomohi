So, we're looking into who can produce our merchandise. The biggest problem 
seems to be how much we have to charge. The *cheapest* we've seen for 
individual (read: non-bulk) orders is 15$ for a plain white shirt with a 
design. So, to make any kind of money, we'd have to mark it up. That brings 
it to... let's just say 20$. Now unless you  surpass some number (around 
50?) of dollars on your order you'll also end up paying shipping, which 
we'll laughably say 5$ even though that could vary a lot in either 
direction.

So,  somebody is paying 25$ for a shirt and we're making 5$ off of it.
That doesn't seem fair for the buyer or us.

Suggestions?

The construct in question was not the "Surely you don't mean" part, but the
"x" part of the sentence.

For example, {ju'o do na skudji ko'a} doesn't do it, because it indicates
that {ko'a} is bound somewhere in context to mean a particular thing, and
instead I'd like to provide a template, sort of like {ce'u} or {ke'a} or
even {ma kau}, but not quite any of them.

{la'e do'i} might solve it, but feels like a hack.
{ko'a bi'u} also might, but seems ambiguous with "a new referent of the
same {ko'a}" rather than "a new {ko'a}".

mean that you're not allowed to call a particular person stupid but that
you're not allowed to call anyone stupid; however, I'm not using {da} to

using that template.

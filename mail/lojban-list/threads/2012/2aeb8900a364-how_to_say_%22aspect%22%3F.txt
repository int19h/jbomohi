Using {sekai}, you can give a property for which the x1 of the parent
bridi is, in this case srana1, presumably the argument to the
function. Chances are you're going to want to use a -kau inside that
property in order to be specific as to what way, exactly, the things
are related.

For example, {lo nu mi se jibri cu srana lo da'i nu mi klama lo
jugygu'e kei sekai lo ka lo se jerna be fi ce'u cu se pleji fo tu'a lo
pikta be fi makau}

Of course, someone who's never seen this type of thing before might
not be certain of what exactly that ce'u is taking as an argument, but
in this case, context is largely sufficient.

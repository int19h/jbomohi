Today I was going over my poetical work from the 1990s, mainly with a view to seeing what can be discarded. Among a section in a longer piece that was about loss and gardens, there was an unfinished bit in Lojban. I will never return to or finish it, so I bid it farewell by posting it here:

Le nunrozrulpezmaxpanci
U’a nai ze’e tolvi’o
I lo xarnu mursyti’o
Ca’o lacpu lo vi vanci
Loi xekmurta be lo si’o
Oi ba ba’o culno ci’o
Gi lei lagzgu ga’orbi’o
Gi [.....] fa
Le kanla e le purdi i’o
Fe le carmi skari a
Le li’i to’ermanku manci
[...]
Le so’i xrula cu se lanci
[...]
La’e do’i ro roi canci
I ra uo nai mo i ni’o


--And.

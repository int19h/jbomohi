coi ro do

I've just finished the initial version of "lojbo jufsisku" (Lojban
sentence search), which is a bilingual (Lojban-English) translation
search environment in order to facilitate dictionary and translation
look-up for anyone who writes in Lojban:

http://lojban.lilyx.net/jufsisku/

I've added sentences from Tatoeba, some part of CLL (Chapter 1 to 4),
lu la teris. po'u le tirxu li'u, and jbovlaste.
I'm planning to add more sentences myself, but it'd be great if
jbopre's in the community help make it a better tool by adding more
(hopefully high-quality) sentences.

Quality and quantity of sentences are critical to its usefulness.

Any feedback is appreciated!

Thanks,
Masato

Hello there,

today someone asked on IRC whether there was a map that showed where 
they could find other speakers and learners of Lojban and whether there 
were such people in their area.
I remembered then, that we have such a thing for Toki Pona and it would 
indeed be very nice to use it for Lojban as well. It would make it much 
easier to find jbopre in your area and meet up with them.
Here's what it looks like:
http://mapservices.org/myguestmap/map/janKuti

It's a free service and it can be hosted on other websites as far as I 
know, so why not put such a thing on lojban.org?
(It might also give us a better esimate of how many Lojban speakers 
there are overall)

Looking forward to opinions.

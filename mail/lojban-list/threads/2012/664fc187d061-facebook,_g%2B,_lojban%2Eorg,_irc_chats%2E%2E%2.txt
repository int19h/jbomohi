I believe there are too many places to talk about lojban now. 
Although it's nice that lojban is spreading I see the same people talking 
in different places.

I can remember

   1. lojban.org wiki pages
   2. different Google docs edited by the community
   3. lojban.org archive, primarily unsorted and chaotic
   4. lojban irc chat. At least four channels #lojban, #ckule, #makfa, 
   #jbopre. Although some extracts from it are selectively being put onto 
   lojban.org wiki
   5. https://groups.google.com/group/lojban
   6. https://groups.google.com/group/lojban-beginners
   7. https://groups.google.com/group/jbofanva
   8. http://lojbantime.blogspot.com
   9. http://lojban.livejournal.com/ 
   10. yahoo group (rather dead)
   11. jbotcan.org
   12. https://www.facebook.com/groups/2213906704/ 
   13. https://plus.google.com/111885648105308968553/posts
   14. twitter channel
   15. Mumble channel
   16. Lojbanic Skype group
   17. vk.com/menli.bangu (Russian forum)
   18. lojban-ru.livejournal.com (Russian forum, rather dead)
   19. google groups Japanese forum

Items #9 through #13 actually duplicate each other. If facebook or G+ allow 
creating topics in a group then they also clash with this google group

I understand that #8 is created by individual lojbanists. I'm grateful that 
they are spreading info on lojban further.
I understand that facebook and G+ are struggling to defeat each other (what 
does lojbanists has to do with it ?)

May be I forgot about some other resourses but isn't this factionalism 
enough ?

Why not select few services and put everything there ?
e.g.

   - lojban wiki
   - lojban discussion forum that allows creating subforums for non-English 
   lojbanists
   - lojban voice chat

P.S. Google Wave was nice for chats but I'm not sure whether current Apache 
Wave is reasonable.

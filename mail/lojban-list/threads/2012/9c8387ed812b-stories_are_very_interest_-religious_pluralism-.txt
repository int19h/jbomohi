Hello !

Since you're engaged in the process of translating religious books
into Lojban, let me add that you'll certainly be lacking some
terminology, for, indeed, such theological notions such as "tawhid" or
"jihad" or "dhikr" or anything you like, are *not* logically
determined, but culturally determined, and, as such, need terms for
their own sake.

Perhaps, you'll have to coin such words as la tau'id., la dji'ad., la dikr. ?

And to exit the theological issue, the same is true for any
"culturally determined notion", not only for islamic ones...

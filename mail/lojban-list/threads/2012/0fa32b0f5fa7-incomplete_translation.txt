  Well, first off, you need a gadri before the "nu", because he is not a
hearing-type of event.  Also, isn't the shouting in the scope of what he
heard?  And I am not sure why the translator is using "lo ko'a" type
formats for gismu that include the possessor in them, but that's a
stylistic point.  I'd proabbly go with something like this:

  .ije ko'a tirna lo nu ge lo voksa be ko'e cu krixa lo cmene be ko'a gi
lausku bu'u lo foldi fa'a lo bi'u foldi tezu'e lo nu simxu jungau tu'a lo
nu le bloti cu klama

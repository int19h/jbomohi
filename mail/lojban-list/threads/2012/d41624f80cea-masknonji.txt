it is, but I'm not sure about xorxes's rules, because the "n" could be 
syllabic.

Although "klonji" is a slinku'i, "klaunji" is not; it means "blackseed".

Or rather, it is the valid sentence "ma sklonji"

>("lonji" has gismu form,
> therefore "paklonji" has lujvo form, therefore "klonji" is a slinku'i,

The conclusion is correct, but I don't follow the reasoning. I don't
think "klonji" being a slinku'i has anything to do with it.
"sklonjiju" is  valid too, and yet "klonjiju" is not a slinku'i.

> Is "masknonji" valid? By my rules and by vlatai
> it is, but I'm not sure about xorxes's rules, because the "n" could be
> syllabic.

If -sk was allowed as a valid coda, then "mask,non,ji" would be valid.

By my rules, the "n" cannot be syllabic here, because that would leave
the following syllable without an onset. By my rules a syllabic
consonant must always be followed (as well as preceded) by another
consonant, or be at the end of a cmevla.

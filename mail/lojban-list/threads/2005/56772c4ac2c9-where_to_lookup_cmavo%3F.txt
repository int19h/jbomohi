ta}).

I think you want either {ta noi cifnu} or {le va cifnu}.

I'd try {le cifnu cu pinxe lo ladru lo tatru be lo mamta be cy.}. Or
maybe with {lo ladru be lo tatru...} instead of {lo ladru lo
tatru...}; I'm not sure which is better. (One puts "its mothers
breast" into the x2 of {ladru} -- the origin of the milk --, the other
into the x3 of {pinxe} -- the source of the drinking.)

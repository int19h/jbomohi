Perhaps we could introduce a special symbol to indicate
that what follows is a conventional *written* abbreviation 
of something that has to be expanded when read aloud.
So something like "lo /ml 750 botpi" where "/ml" would 
always be read as "milylitce be li". Using {ml} by itself
could be confused with the name {ml.}, and some other units 
might cause even worse ambiguities.

> WTF is that, btw?

A unit of inductance.  When the current through a one-henry coil changes by 
one amp per second, the voltage across the coil is one volt.

Personal name units are a pain in the butt.  I've thought about translating 
them as personal names (la henry) with some kind of tag on them.  

Do we have the "sverdrup"?  That's 1e15 cubic meters per second, a 
reasonably scaled unit for ocean currents named after a founder of that 
field.  I would deprecate all the scaled units like this one, and make 
everyone use E format, e.g. "the flux of North Atlantic Deep Water was 8e15 
M^3/s before Bush's environmental policies pushed..."

In some cases, IE (and *ONLY* IE) does this.  I think only IE on
Win2K, as well.  And not all the time.  And only in the Simple CSS
style.

The work-around is to change your Style.  In the left hand bar, look
for something that says "Style: simple.css".  Just below that is a
dropdown box.  Pick something else.  neat, moreneat, and smartiki
are all popular.

Speaking personally, rather than as a board member, it seems to me
that wdiff would solve your problem.  In my automated Palm export
program, I use wdiff to colour the last set of changes:

http://www.teddyb.org/~rlpowell/hobbies/lojban/palm/la%20nicte%20cadzu/nic-2.2-2.html

could have a personall CSS that over-rides the CSS definition in the
text to visually remove the deleted stuff (although I don't know how
one would do this in CSS; if nothing else one could match the
background colour or use a 1 point font) and make the new stuff look
normal.

=== 5 | 2005-02-20T00:12:46Z | xah lee <xah@xahlee.org> | 6efc4677397176bc150e3f5799e54043@xahlee.org | mail/lojban-list/cur/1108858366.bcbc8e1050a59797.jbomohi:2,S

The current license is really juvenile and unworkable.

No publication or scholar is going to have his work in every sentence 
saying what has been modified from original.

the mentality of the current license is great, where one can try to 
preserve a lojban standard yet have a good heart to let others do 
derivative work. The license itself however prevents any derived work 
other than students and personal notes of the blog class.

i and other people would have made derivative works out of it, if not 
for the license. Sure, i can seek the official lojban group members and 
make arrangements or change the license, but that is besides the point 
and another step extra.

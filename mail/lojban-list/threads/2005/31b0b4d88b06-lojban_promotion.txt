I have an idea how to promote Lojban.

I remember that I met Lojban for the first time in Wikipedia article about Genghis Khan:
http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genghis_khan 
I wondered which languages were also interested in Genghis Khan and then I saw a mysterious language called "Lojban".  ........    That is how I am here.  :)

The idea is, what if translate into Lojban or even create Lojban stubs for the most popular/edited English articles in Wikipedia? 
http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special:Mostrevisions 
(most popular pages are somewhat more difficult to find)

Let's start with George W. Bush !   :)

I went there and found a stublet. A bit of a problem is the initial. There are 
two George Bushes, both presidents, distinguished only by an initial. {la 
djordj. vybu. buc.} is ungrammatical, and initials in names are somewhat 
unlojbanic. (I had this problem with the gombessa article. I don't know what 
"JLB" stands for and ended up putting {jylybyb}.) We could put {uokr}, but 
both of them have a W and both W's stand for Walker. Ideas?

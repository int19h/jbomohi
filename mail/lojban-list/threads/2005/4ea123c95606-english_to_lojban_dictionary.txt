I found this [barsoom.hezekiah.glossary] definition of "vrude" (which I found 
in one of Robin's now-online stories):

vrude    virtue    x1is virtuous | saintly [morally good] by standard x2; 
(cf. palci, xamgu, marde, zabna) 

The keyword is "virtue", but that's obviously a bit off.  Is "virtue" then 
translatable as "ka vrude"?  Is there an English-Lojban dictionary that defines 
words that are derivatives of basic Lojban words rather than just the base 
words themselves?  I've seen lujvo lists and non-initial place structure keyword 
lists, but I have in mind now lists with other cmavo, such as "ka".

stevo

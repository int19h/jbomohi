Here's a fun orthography I made up. It's not very useful for
computers, but it would be great for watches or alarm clocks with the
7-LED characters. I also made a script version, which is fun to write
on paper and looks somewhat like Hindi script. It's pretty easy to
learn, because the consonants are grouped into voiced-unvoiced pairs.

http://www.calpoly.edu/~bwirick/orthography.jpg

pulenumi
mórsikeiku
roleilójbo
bateckíre

mi'e .uirik.

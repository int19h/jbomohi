For my computer-parsing experiments, I've been using a minimal Lojban.
BRIVLA, LE/LO/LA, I, DU'U, KA, CU, numbers, and the pro-sumti. (it's 
soooo easy to parse)
 
It can express about 90% of what I want to say.
Something like that would make a good "reader" for kids to learn Lojban. 
(or adults!)

[insert picture of the dog Spot]
ko viska la spat.
See Spot.

[insert picture of Spot running]
ko viska le du'u la spat. bajra
See Spot run.

mi'e la iuban

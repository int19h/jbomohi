Umm, why?

The sole purpose of the WikiDiscuss forum is to be attached to the
"discuss" links at the bottom of wiki pages.

If you go there through those links, you end up at pages that have
not, in general, been spammed.

In fact, most of the usage of wikidiscuss is done through the
mailing list, which never sees that spam.  I have no idea *how* the
spam gets posted, in fact, such that it never gets sent to the list,
but as long as it stays that way, I don't much care.

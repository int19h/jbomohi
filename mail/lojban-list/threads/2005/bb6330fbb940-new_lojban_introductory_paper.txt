Hello Lojban community!

I have more or less recently started to learn Lojban and promptly
decided to pass the knowledge so gained on at the 22nd. Chaos
Communication Congress (see http://www.ccc.de/congress/2005/).

So, I'm going to hold a short talk introducing Lojban. It is titled

hacker/geek/whatever crowd to have a look at Lojban.

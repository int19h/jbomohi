> I was rhyming solely on "ai".  If there's something wrong with
> "REMpai" for that purpose, I don't get it.

For two lines to rhyme, the last stressed vowel and everything 
after it must be identical in both lines, so REMpai will rhyme 
only with things that end in EMpai. The others will all rhyme 
if they are stressed.

It is possible to use less stringent rhymes, for example
allow {EMpai} to rhyme with {EMbai}, and things like that, 
but relaxing the stress rule is too much for me.

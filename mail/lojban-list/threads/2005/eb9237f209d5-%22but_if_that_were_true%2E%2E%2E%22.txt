{lo ja'a se go'i} amounts to {lo nu na broda}, since {pacna} wasn't negated 
the {ja'a} has no effect. I would say {lo nu ja'a go'a}. {go'i} repeats 
{pacna}, and {go'e} whatever top-level bridi preceded that, so {broda}, being 
embedded, is fair game for {go'a}.

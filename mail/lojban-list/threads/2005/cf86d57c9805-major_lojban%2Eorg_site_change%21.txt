I've just done the initial parts of the move of lojban.org from a
set of more-or-less static HTML pages to a Wiki (TikiWiki,
specifically) based system.  The goal here is primarily to make my
life easier (because I can edit things on the fly), but I'm also
hoping it will make the site more functional.

I'm sure there are going to be some growing pains, but one of the
thinsg I've taken pains (ha ha) to ensure is that all old URLs
continue to work, so you don't need to worry about your links going
away.

Please let me know if you run into any problems.

Thanks.

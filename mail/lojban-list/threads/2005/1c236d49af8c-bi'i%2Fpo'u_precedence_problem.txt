> I'm translating some verses of Esther. Here's my attempt at the first:

> ni'oni'o la bi'u .axacuiroc. turni la xingug. bi'i la .itiopias. po'u pareze
> jecpau
>
> I ran it through jbofi'e, which said that Ethiopia is 127 provinces. It isn't;
> the whole empire is 127 provinces. How do I say this right?

{la xingug bi'i la itiopias vu'o no'u lo pareze jecpau}

Without {lo} or {loi} it would say that the whole region is each of
127 provinces,
i.e. that there are exactly 127 provinces each of which is {la xingug
bi'i la itiopias}.

mu'o mi'e xorxes

The issue is that there is no whitespace in speech. So the rule for breaking
up words has work for spoken lojban (hence the remark about how the
stressing changes things). Whitespace in written lojban only serves to infer
stress (at least formally).

No. The tosmabru test applies to an apparent lujvo such that removing CV from 
the beginning results in another apparent lujvo. "respageti" is two words, not 
a fu'ivla, but it is not a tosmabru. "pageti" is not a gismu, a rafsi, or a 
concatenation of rafsi, so "respageti" is not an apparent lujvo.

Here is a draft of another crossword puzzle in lojban. This time is a
"proper" crossword puzzle all hand made by me :)

http://dl.dropbox.com/u/7681579/kuc_01.pdf

I spent the last couple of days trying to figure out how to easily
publish a crossword online and didn't find anything that I liked so,
for now, it's just a PDF that has to be printed to be completed.

I have considered so far the following options:

  create a PDF form
  create an ad hoc javascript version
  buy a crossword editor (that would not insist on uppercase!)
  release as an OpenOffice spreadsheet
  release as a Google Document spreadsheet

each of this will have its pros and cons.

I would be very happy to hear any other suggestion.

Of course the same goes for clues, beside the errors,  I'm sure not
everyone will agree with them :)

remod.

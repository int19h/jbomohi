Yes, a tu'e...tu'u bracket can mark the scope of a prenex. In that
case, the variables da, de, di ect. would remain the same until tu'u
is uttered.
Another way is to string several bridi together with logical
connectives: {da klama ije da cliva} does the same as your sentence.
(note that you can drop the prenex).
If neither of these two methods are used, the prenex scope ends with
the bridi.

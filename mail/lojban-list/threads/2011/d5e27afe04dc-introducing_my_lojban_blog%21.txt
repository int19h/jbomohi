coi Daniel.  If I can just be permitted to nitpick some of your lojban
(since I assume practice is part of the reason you want to start a blog):



On Thu, Jul 7, 2011 at 11:35 PM, Daniel Golani (Negative Clock) <
dgolani@gmail.com> wrote:

> coi rodo .i mi puzi cmibi'o ui ti girzu


  Not grammatical.    You want "lo vi girzu".  "ti" is a prosumti
(essentially, like a pronoun).  It occupies the whole sumti slot - "this
thing right here". "girzu" on the other hand, is a selbri.  It can't go into
a sumti slot, and you've ended up with a sentence with two main bridi, a
no-no.  "vi" is a space tense that  means "very close by (to...)"  Without a
sumti following it it means very close by the speaker. So "vi grizu" means
"nearby list", and the you make the whole thing into a sumti with "lo". (in
point of fact, one can argue the reality of the list being located
physically in space near to you, but that's still the convention. "le girzu"
might be less problematic form a epistemological point of view)


> .i e'o ko tcidu le mi cnino snukarni je cusku ma noi rodo pensi ku'o mi

  Grammatical, but not what you want.  What it says is "I suggest you read
my new blog/sayer from source what?, which you all think about, and
something to do with "me" relating to reading."

1) You want us to read and you want us to tell. "je" is not the right word
here.  That links the two selbri "cnino snukarni" and "cusku" (which then
become a sumti with the "le"). You want "gi'e", the bridi-tail linker,
making us the subject of both parts of the sentence.

2) "ma" asks a direct question.  It expects an answer.  Instead, use "makau"

3) pensi is not right here. You want our opinion, about it, not our musings,
I assume.  Therefore, use jinvi.

  Putting it together, changing it as little as possible: ".i e'o ko tcidu
le mi cnino snukarni gi'e cusku makau noi rodo jinvi ku'o mi"

  (although really, the "rodo" is wrong, too, since it implies that we
should tell you what each of us thinks.  I have no idea what others think,
only myself.  Better to leave off the "ro", since you've already addressed
us all in the beginning)

  You might find tools like http://genrei.lojban.org/ or
http://lojban.org/jboski useful for checking your grammar, and for seeing if
things are really attaching to the places you intended.

While I appreciate the enthusiasm, I have found a rather large flaw.
Wrap your lunch in tinfoil, put a bit of masking tape on it, and write
down what it is.

"vaigus"
"seikaix"
"koimmai"

While it may seem obvious upon further examination, the fact that the
orientation isn't immediately obvious without reading gives us a bit
of trouble. It becomes especially bad for names. I wote down my name
on my sandwich and zaimc's name on his, but realised something:

".gnoidkai."
".ftzi."

It takes a second, but there's still only one way to read it, right?
What if I just initial stuff? Paul might accidentally get my sandwich
and I his.

Ideas are certainly welcome.

Another note for if you decide to write up an official design doc for llm,
lindar:  I think that it would be helpful if you made any examples in the
design doc to be as un-creative as possible.  If someone asks me for an

ku batci lo nanmu}.  The best kind of examples are the kind that describe
the fundamentally important attribute that you are trying to exemplify and
nothing more.

stylistic variations.

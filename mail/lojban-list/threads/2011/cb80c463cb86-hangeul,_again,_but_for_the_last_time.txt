Since each new writing system adds another layer of difficulty to learning an 
already not easy language, it seems unlikely that this system will have any 
pedagogical value.  But it is pretty in its way and so has positive aesthetic 
value, which is not a bad thing.  The use of non-standard scripts for Lojban 
(and every other conlang that gets a following of more than about four) seems 
primarily to be to create interesting looking displays of texts which might not 
be all that interesting in Latin script.  So a hangul (sorry, I learned stuff a 
long time ago) version might gussy up a translation of Korean poetry, for 
example, or even a recipe.  Thank you for your efforts.

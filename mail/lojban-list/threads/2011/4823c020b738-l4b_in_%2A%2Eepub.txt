I've done a test conversion with Calibre starting from the HTML version, but
the result isn't any too good as many of the pre-formatted, fixed font
examples overflow the right margin and cannot be read - at least not with my
Sony PRS-650. Also, on my computer, the conversion choked on the Index, and
I had to leave it out. However, my purpose was to just chart the major
problems and try to find reasonable solutions to them, solutions which take
into account the limitations of epub readers. Anyway, preparing a decent
epub version of the CLL will involve a lot of work and is best left until
CLL v1.1 is ready.

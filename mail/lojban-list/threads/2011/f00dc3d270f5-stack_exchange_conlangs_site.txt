Matthew Martin has proposed (http://www.suburbandestiny.com/?p=786)
the creation of a new Stack Exchange site for conlangs.

For those of you are unfamiliar with Stack Exchange, it's an extension
of Stack Overflow to other topics. Basically it's a very enhanced Q&A
site.

Cf. http://mathoverflow.net/ as an example of a successful Stack
Exchange project; browse around to see how it's used.

If you want this to happen, you should
1. visit http://area51.stackexchange.com/proposals/12409/planned-languages
2. log in / create an account
3. 'follow' the proposal
4. submit more example on and off topic questions
5. rate others' example questions as on/off topic and contribute your
opinion to the site-definition etc discussions

 - Sai

Right now, this proposed site just needs
a) one more person to follow it, and
b) 2 more proposed questions to have >20 votes with 4:1 in favor of it
being "on topic"

… to get to the next stage.

So, if you haven't yet, please join the proposal and vote "on topic"
some questions that have a high level but <20 votes. (If it has >20 or
4:1 majority already, it effectively wastes your vote, and you only
get 5 on and 5 off topic votes.)


At the next stage, 'commitment', it'll need
1. 200 committed people
2. 100 committed people with 200+ reputation on any Stack Exchange site
3. a sufficient "commitment score", based on number & reputation level
of committed people

Also, you may want to consider joining the Linguistics Stack Exchange
proposal, here (which is in the 'commitment' stage):
http://area51.stackexchange.com/proposals/6673/linguistics

- Sai

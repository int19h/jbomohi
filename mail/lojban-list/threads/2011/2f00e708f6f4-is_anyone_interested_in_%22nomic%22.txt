  I haven't played Nomic in years, but have always enjoyed playing (although
I've never played online).  (And every time I've played, they've ended,
Robin ;-) I even have two kids who are interested in plyaing (unfortunately,
they don't speak lojban).  I'll play.  I think this has been proposed
before, but I've never seen it come to fruition.

  P.S.  My favorite session we had a rule (dubbed the Gramm-Rudman Bill (
see http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gramm-Rudman-Hollings_Balanced_Budget_Act
))whereby
if the set of mutable rules ever exceeded the number allowed, then rules
would be repealed randomly (each rule receiving an equal chance of repeal)
until they fell below the limit.  And then some idiot proposed dropping the
limit to 10 mutable rules (we had about 23 at the time), and the other
idiots at the table voted it in, triggering a flurry of automatic repeals.
Things like turn sequence got repealed before the rule-repealing rule got
repealed, but luckily judgment rule stayed, so we were able to judicially
decide how to proceed....

               --gejyspa

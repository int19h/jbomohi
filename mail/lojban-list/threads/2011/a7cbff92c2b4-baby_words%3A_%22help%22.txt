> On Sunday 06 November 2011 01:43:04 Robin Lee Powell wrote:
>> "Sleeping helps you grow strong".  I'm sure there's an obvious gismu
>> here, but I'm missing it.
> 
> lo nu sipna cu sidju do lo nu tsaba'o

I think {sidju} might be intended to be agentive. I think this is clear and precise:

    lo nu tsaba'o cu prali do lo nu sipna

(or)

    lo nu sipna cu te prali do lo nu tsaba'o

Note that {prali} x1 specifically can be an event, and that {prali} implies that growing strong is a benefit.

Also, whether {prali} or {sidju} is used, I do not think we truly need to say {tsaba'o} rather than {tsali}; Lojbanic tense vagueness covers it. Or perhaps

    lo nu sipna cu te prali do lo nu ba tsali

For language acquisition, shouldn't we prefer sentences with "simple words" over ones with lujvo, when the lujvo are there for a "variant" meaning rather than one that's fundamentally not a gismu?

I checked the definition of {sidju}:

x1 helps/assists/aids object/person x2 do/achieve/maintain event/activity x3.

I've been assuming that x1 needs to be a person, but having an event in the x1 seems to be perfectly fine. Another possibility might be {to'e zunti} but maybe that goes beyond kiddie talk:

x1 (evt./state) interferes with/hinders/disrupts x2 (evt./state/process) due to quality x3 (ka).

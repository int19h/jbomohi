I can't find anything in CLL about counting, but I think the way to
count in Lojban is:

pa .i re .i ci .i vo .i mu ...

If you don't insert the ".i" between numbers, you end up saying one
large number instead of counting.

mu'o mi'e xorxes

http://dag.github.com/cll/18/12/

The ability to utter bare numbers as grammatical Lojban sentences is
primarily intended for giving answers to “xo” questions. (Another use,
obviously, is for counting off physical objects one by one.)

This implies {pa .i re .i ci}, not {pa re ci} for counting, right?

Because honestly, I've used both when counting to myself (stitches, during
knitting and crocheting), depending on whether the extra syllable was
convenient.

Similarly, I can imagine counting time in music with {pa re ci} when
counting quarter notes and {pa .i re .i ci .i vo .i} when counting eighths.
I'm not sure what horrible mangling of grammar I would do for 16th notes.

2011/2/13 Jorge Llambías <jjllambias@gmail.com>

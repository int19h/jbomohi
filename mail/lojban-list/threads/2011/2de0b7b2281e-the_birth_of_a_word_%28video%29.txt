Agreed. It might be a bit much to go as far as to pepper one's house with
cameras, but the idea is really intriguing.
I have to say that some of the "discoveries" are pretty obvious, in the
sense that the word "bye" being said more frequently near the door than
elsewhere can already be surmised without such in depth analysis.
Furthermore, the proof that a large portion of US population is watching an
announcement of the President is far from surprising. If there's one thing
that's doubtlessly true about all Americans, whether all such persons like
it or not, is that they have the same President.
All in all though, it's true that it was interesting to see all this
proven, notwithstanding that the sheer amount of data collected is
impressive.

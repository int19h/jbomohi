So, everyone knows that the most important quality of any language is its 
ability to tell jokes. (sarcasm)

Regardless, in trying to translate a classic lightbulb joke, the closest I 
got was:
nitcu xo lo prenu ku lo pu'u zbasu se carna lo cukpultergu'i

Basically, how do you describe "screwing in" a lightbulb, and how do you 
say "lightbulb"? In my example, I went with a sort of 
"putting-together/installing kind-of turning" for "screwing in", and "round 
protrusion light-source" for "lightbulb". (I figured out the latter based 
on suggestions from the plant bulb.)

If this is the actual good way of doing this, then I guess {tolzbasu carna} 
would be for "unscrewing"? Is this something worth making a lujvo for, what 
with using an actual screwdriver? (How would you say "screwdriver," even? 
{carna selpilno}?)

Regarding the dot-side issue: was a consensus ever reached on 
the pro-dot-side whether cmene that contained the sequence 
"la" not preceded by a consonant, but which were not cmevla 
needed dot-side "protection"?; eg, "la labno"; "la xrula"; or 
if "la ka trina" is valid, then a name containing "la'u" should 
also be valid; finally, tanru, lujvo, and fu'ivla can also 
contain the sequence "la" not preceded by a consonant.  
I wouldn't think that these names would need dot-side 
protection; only those which are cmevla.

Pre-dot side, non-{cmevla} containing "la" were treated exactly the  
same as other non-{cmevla}, and the dot side doesn't change that.  The  
only changes the dot side makes are (1) {cmevla} may freely contain  
"la", "lai", "la'i", and "doi" (as long as they still end in a  
consonant and obey all the other rules of Lojban morphology), and (2)  
each {cmevla} must be preceded by a pause (and, as before, followed by  
one), but as periods and spaces are functionally nigh-identical in  
written text, they are often not represented.  As before, you have the  
option of inserting a pause/period between any two words (but never in  
the middle of a word!); pauses are only mandatory before & after  
{cmevla}, before words beginning in a vowel, after words ending in "y"  
under certain circumstances, and in ZOI quotes.

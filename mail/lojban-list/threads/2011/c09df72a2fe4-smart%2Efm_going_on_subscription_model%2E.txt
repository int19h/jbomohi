Anyways, I don't think this is a big loss. I initially used smart.fm
and liked it, until I tried Anki, and learned how pathetic smart.fm
is. You literally spend at least twice as much time to review the same
amount of material, and you don't even remember it as well because the
algorithm is so bad. Also, smart.fm is multiple choice, which is not
nearly as good for memory as complete recall like in Anki.

Right now, I can easily go through ~200 reviews per day with Anki, but
doing that much in smart.fm would be painful...

I personally liked SmartFM more so than anki. With anki, it seemed to be "Do 
you remember this?" and I could say yes/no/maybe. With SmartFM, it said the 
word aloud, had me pick from multiple choice, then showed me the definition 
and had me type the word in. It had a lot of different ways to test me for 
one word, not just "Do you know  this word?". Plus, the audio and sound 
effects provided entertainment and positive reinforcement for remembering 
the words. Really, truly, honestly, the sound was half of what I liked about 
SmartFM over anki.

"na'e zei .abu zei na'e zei by" parses differently in jbofi'e than in camxes. 
In jbofi'e it's a tanru consisting of "na'e zei .a" and "bu zei na'e zei by". 
In camxes, if I'm reading the tree right, "na'e zei .a" is a lujvo, which is 
then converted into a letteral with "bu", then joined by "zei" to "na'e zei 
by".

Robin, the dictionaries (.dsl) are just plain text files with a different
extension and some formatting.

For instance, here's the first few lines of jbo-eng_jbovlaste.dsl:

#NAME "jbovlaste (Jbo-Eng)"
#INDEX_LANGUAGE "Lojban"
#CONTENTS_LANGUAGE "English"

Indias
    [c gray]Type: [/c][c green][i]cmene[/i][/c]
    [m1]India.[/m]
    [m2][c darkslategray]Cf. <<xingu'e>>.[/c][/m]
KEnydys
    [c gray]Type: [/c][c green][i]cmene[/i][/c]
    [m1]Canada[/m]
    [m2][c darkslategray]Cf. <<kadnygu'e>>, <<kenyDAS>>,
<<gugdecu'a>>.[/c][/m]

And the first few lines from eng-jbo_jbovlaste.dsl:

#NAME "jbovlaste (Eng-Jbo)"
#INDEX_LANGUAGE "Lojban"
#CONTENTS_LANGUAGE "English"

India. Cf. <<xingu'e>>
    Indias
Canada. Cf. <<kadnygu'e>>, <<kenyDAS>>, <<gugdecu'a>>
    KEnydys

I'm relatively certain you could make the dictionary you request yourself. I
think I'd be able to as well, assuming I understand your request.

Give me a bit and I'll post my attempt.

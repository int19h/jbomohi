tezu'e (cmavo) and zgana (sumti) is required. It's not necessary two
words to be interchangeable in one sentence, but it's enough when a
thought can be equally expressed using one word or another, even with
different sentense constructions. So I'm looking for a full list of
cmavos that have such pairs with sumtis, or maybe a list of pairs. For
example, all cmavos of selmao BAI and UI1 is right what I need because
I have found relation to sumti for each. I have got some poor drafts,
but hope on your help; probably such list already exists.

1) That distant thing (x1) runs for the purpose of a star.
2) That distant thing (x1) observes a runner (x2) by means of a star (x3).

Not equal senses.


> and the relation like between words tezu'e (cmavo) and zgana (sumti) is required.

"zu'e" comes from "zukte", not "zgana". "zgana" (which isn't a sumti)
corresponds to "ga'a".


> It's not necessary two

CLL 9.17 has a list of BAI with their gismu equivalents:
http://dag.github.com/cll/9/17/

The sumti after "zu'e" means zukte1. If "te zu'e", it means zukte3.

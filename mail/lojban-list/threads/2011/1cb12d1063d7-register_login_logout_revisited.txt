Given that we have our wiki home page 
in the lojban language, it would be good 
to have this issue sorted out once and for all.

I sent this to the jovlaste list, but it doesn't 
seem to get much attention.

I notice that {plivei} has been used for 
'log in' (as an instruction) , but also 
for 'log'.  Also, {pliveigau} for 'Register'.

Some problems there.  All these are 
inconsistent with each other (they need 
to be considered together) And I don't 
understand how {plivei} can mean 'log in'.

Here are some suggestions:

LOG
{plivei]

LOG IN
{ciste nerkla}.  As instruction on web page: {nerkla}
{ciste cmesanji}. As instruction on webpage: {cmesanji}

LOG OUT
{ciste barkla}. As instruction on web page: {barkla}


REGISTER
{cmeveigau}

Comments?  Suggestions?

Lojban doesn't really have any good/obvious ways to talk about sides
of an object, i.e. "you have a sunburn on your left side".

I've thought of using {zunle pagbu} for that, and that's decent
enough.  There's a case this doesn't cover, though, which is "on one
side", i.e. "one side of the mushroom will make you grow taller".
mlana is of no help at all.

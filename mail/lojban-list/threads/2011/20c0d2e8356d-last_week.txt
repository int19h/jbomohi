I think {balvi} has two antonyms, {lidne} and {purci}.


> Second, is li'elamjeftu a good lujvu for "last week"?
>

No. Since the stress is on "jef", there would be no difference between
{li'elamjeftu} and {li'e lamjeftu}. To overcome this, add a hyphen-r after
{li'e}: {li'erlamjeftu}.

But, based on the analogy of {bavlamdei} ('tomorrow') and {prulamdei}
('yesterday'), I suggest {prulamjeftu}.

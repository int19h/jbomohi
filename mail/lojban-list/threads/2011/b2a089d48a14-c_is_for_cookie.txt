Made up while talking to the babies about {lo mamta ladru cu kukte}:

zo ky zo kukte cfari
.i banzu tu'a mi
zo ky zo kukte cfari
.i banzu tu'a mi
zo ky zo kukte cfari
.i banzu tu'a mi
.ua kukte kukte kukte cfari .ui

In retrospect, I obviously want {krasi} rather than {cafri}, but
that doesn't change the meter or rhyme.

Not sure whether {zo ky} does what I want, or if I technically need
{me'o} or something, but I think it's good enough for a song.

A fairly simple question: how do embedded bridi interact with scope? Do they
have their own scope? A motivating example, that if you can answer it should
answer almost all my questions, is the following:

.i mi ba'o terpa lo nu no da jundi
is equivalent to which of the following:
.i no da zo'u mi ba'o terpa lo nu da jundi
.i mi ba'o terpa lo nu no da zo'u da jundi

Intuition and convenience would say it's the latter, but I am really not
100% sure.

mu'o mi'e latros

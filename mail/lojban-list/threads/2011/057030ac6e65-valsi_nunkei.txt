So in Russian, there's a game called "Слова" (Words), in which players take
turns saying singular nouns in the nominative case.  The main restriction is
that each noun must start with the letter the last noun ended with.  Has
anyone done anything similar for lojban?  The concept doesn't translate too
cleanly, as lojban gismu start with consonants and end with vowels.

Would cmavo be valid words for the game?  I'd say no.
Would lujvo?  I'd say no, but less strongly than about cmavo.
Would fu'ivla?  I'm not sure.

This game might be a fun tool for building up a vocabulary of gismu, even
though the definitions of the words aren't needed in the game.

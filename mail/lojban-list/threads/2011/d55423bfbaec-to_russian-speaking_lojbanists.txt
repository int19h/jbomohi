Anyone volunteer to replace me as an international contact for
Russian-speaking people?

Currently my address is listed at
http://www.lojban.org/tiki/Contact+Us page. I believe the fluent
Lojban knowledge is not required for this role. Knowing the current
"state of affairs" in community is much more important: a contact
person is expected to point newbies to the right people and sites
which hold the current Lojban activity. And I definitely do not
qualify the later: I skip most of the messages on this list, I am not
on IRC, and [o'anai] I was not even aware of a new Russian-language
Lojbanic forum emerged during last few months.

If no one volunteer, I suggest to replace my personal address on the
"Contact Us" page with the references to the two existing Russian
Lojbanic forums, one on Livejournal.com and other on vkontakte.ru (the
Russian Facebook clone). I believe that forums' people can be more
helpful than I currently am.

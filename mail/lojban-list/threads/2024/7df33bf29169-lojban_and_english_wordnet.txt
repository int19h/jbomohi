=== 3 | 2024-10-13T16:50:52Z | Robin Powell <robinleepowell@gmail.com> | capr8qalsv5ipkd9=ahmk4b7ofprf9txe0ercxczw2pc-62drrq@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-list/cur/1728838252.39b9ffe7be0d94fb.jbomohi:2,S
jbovlaste has mappings between places of Lojban brivla and word-sense pairs
in English, and jbofihe has internal data files that encode similar info:

$ echo "le crida cu batci le cribe" | jbofihe -x
[([batci1 (bite-r(s)):] le /the/ crida /fairy(ies)/) cu /is/does/ <<batci
/bit-ing/>> ([batci2 (bitten thing(s)):] le /the/ cribe /bear(s)/)]

Back when I was active, I dreamed of making a really excellent machine
translator using the jbovlaste data, because you can do it in the Lojban ->
English direction without needing tons of ambiguity handling like you do in
other languages.

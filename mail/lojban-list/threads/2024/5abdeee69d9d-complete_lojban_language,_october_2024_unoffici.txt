Short list of changes compared to the previous version 'geklojban-1.2.15 
October 2022'
   
   - 
   
   issue #409 <https://github.com/lojban/cll/issues/409> added two tables 
   with phonology 
   <https://la-lojban.github.io/uncll/romoi/xhtml_section_chunks/section-anglophone-phonetics.html> for 
   American English and separately British English speakers.
   - 
   
   issue #518 <https://github.com/lojban/cll/issues/518> such examples as *le 
   nanmu cu ninmu* (*The man is a woman.*) are replaced with *le mlatu cu 
   gerku* (*The cat is a dog.*) because they better reflect the idea of 
   non-veridical reference.
   - 
   
   issue #515 <https://github.com/lojban/cll/issues/515> one text from the 
   Chrestomathy is replaced with a larger text 'Forest Nymph' (a translation 
   of a short story by the Bulgarian writer Elin Pelin)
   - 
   
   issue #499 <https://github.com/lojban/cll/issues/499> definition for 
   tanru grouping fixed. The new text: A second rule relating to bo is that 
   where more than one bo -marked connective is used in succession, the normal 
   Lojban left-grouping rule is replaced by a right-grouping rule.
   - 
   
   issue #513 <https://github.com/lojban/cll/issues/513> French diacritics 
   fixed
   - 
   
   issue #506 <https://github.com/lojban/cll/issues/506>, issue #507 
   <https://github.com/lojban/cll/issues/507>, issue #508 
   <https://github.com/lojban/cll/issues/508>, issue #511 
   <https://github.com/lojban/cll/issues/511> fixed glosses in several 
   examples (thanks to Oheto for noticing)
   - 
   
   issue #510 <https://github.com/lojban/cll/issues/510> incorrect 
   translation of *ti liste mi ce'o do ce'o la .djan.* fixed (thanks to 
   Oheto for noticing)
   - 
   
   issue #509 <https://github.com/lojban/cll/issues/509> in several 
   examples the name of the dog "Alice" was fixed to "Fido" as in other 
   examples (thanks to Oheto for noticing)
   - 
   
   issue #504 <https://github.com/lojban/cll/issues/504> *ke'a* was 
   incorrectly attributed to GOhA - fixed
   - 
   
   fixed other minor mistypes in the text

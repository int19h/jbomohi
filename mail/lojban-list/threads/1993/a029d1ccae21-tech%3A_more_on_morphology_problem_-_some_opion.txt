Iain Alexander quotes me:

> > Names are still just ...C<pause>, with no impermissible medial consonant
> > pairs.

and apparently reads this as meaning that no medial consonant pairs are
impermissible.  What I meant was that medial consonant pairs which are
impermissible (by the usual rules) are not allowed in names either.
Thus "*djeimz" = James and "*bradfrd" = Bradford are forbidden.  This is
a fairly new restriction.

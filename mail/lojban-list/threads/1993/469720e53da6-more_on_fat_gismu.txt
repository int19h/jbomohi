so I'm certainly not going to get any work done) to say that I support this
change. It's consistent with Lojban (in which we encode intransitives as
single gismu more often than transitives), and it makes sense to be able to
talk about uncaused, vehicle-less motion in at least one gismu, when the
caused version, {muvgau}, is so straightforward to derive.

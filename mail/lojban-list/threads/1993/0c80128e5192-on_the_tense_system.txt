Is there any semantic difference (under the present
interpretation) between:


        ___mi morsi ba'o lenu mi jmive___

and:

        ___mi morsi ca lenu mi ba'o jmive___

I think that they mean the same.


But, on the other hand:

        ___mi morsi ba lenu mi jmive___

and:

        ___mi morsi ca lenu mi ba jmive___


are completely different! (As they should be.)

> Hm. Shouldn't that properly be {djica}?

"pacna" and "djica" are pretty similar: one has a probability, the other has
a purpose in x3, but both mean "x1 desires that event x2 come to pass".
Actually, "a'o mi darno ..." would be less heavyweight, and I may adopt it.

> #                .i mu'i tu'e

Yup.  You are explicitly allowed to put either a <tag> or a prenex before
a "tu'e...tu'u" structure.

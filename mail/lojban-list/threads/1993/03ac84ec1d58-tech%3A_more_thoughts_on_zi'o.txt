> No, I'm specifically talking about the distinction between xodit'
> and id ti
> my xodim po parke
> We walk around in the park.

Tsk, tsk!  _My xodim po parku._

> my idyom v park
> We walk to the park.

Rather, `we are going/walking to the park'.

> and though I haven't seen it, I suspect that
> my idyom v magazin po park(a/e?)
> is
> We walk to the store via (route) the park.

No.  It is _My ide"m v magazin cherez park_.  _Po_ is only used for
movement to and fro.

The rest is correct.

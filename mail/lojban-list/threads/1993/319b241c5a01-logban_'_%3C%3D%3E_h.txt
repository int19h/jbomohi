I agree with this also, I think that handwritten Lojban text looks better
(easier to read as well) when "h" is used in place of the apostrophe, but
the apostrophe in typed text looks nice. I would prefer "h" in handwritten
text but the apostrophe in typed text because it breaks up the monotony of
the text and gives the eyes a little bit of a rest.

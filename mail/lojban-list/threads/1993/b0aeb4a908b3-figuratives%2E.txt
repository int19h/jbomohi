> Can somebody bring me up to date on the current status of pe'a and po'a?

In a word, "po'a" is dead, and "pe'a" is a UI meaning "figurative", with
the usual attachment and scope rules.  Like other UI, it can have a scale,
so "pe'anai" means "NOT figurative despite your prejudices".

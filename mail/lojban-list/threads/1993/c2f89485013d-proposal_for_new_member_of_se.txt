David suggests:

+++++>
 I like to ask for opinions on another alternative to the example
which does not require the addition of a new member of SE.

What about:

le marce po le nunklama

The Vehicle associated with the event of going.
>+++++

That will pragmatically work in a lot of cases. The problem is:

1) It is not as specific as 'le sexixa nunklama' - 'po' is a
different relation from 'faxixa'

2) In another sense it is too specific.
na ku ro xe klama cu marce
(not all means of transport are vehicles).

3) There happens to be a convenient selbri - marce - which
more or less does in this case. There will not always be so.


The question was of how to extend an existing, precise
mechanism beyond the arbitrary limits in the cmavo provided.
There is a mechanism provided to do this, but the questioner
thought that this mechanism was too unwieldy, and was asking
for a more compact way. Your suggestion does not answer the
question - it merely provides a work-around.

        Colin Fine

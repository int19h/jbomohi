I've just come to tamsmi in my jvoste, and the place structure *I* get for
it is:

 s1=t2 s2=t2 t1 s3

Namely: x1 resembles in form x2, and has form x3, the resemblance being in
manner x4; this contrasts with your definition:

        x1 has form x2, similar in form to x3 in property/quality x4

Not that I'm asking you to change it, but, well, make of it what you will.

The subscription address is "listserv@cuvmb.cc.columbia.edu"
send the message "unsubscribe lojban"
or "set lojban nomail" if the absence is temporary (followed by
"set lojban mail" when you return)
and "subscribe lojban first name lastname" at your new address.
Each in single line subjectless messages.

These will work IFF the address you are mailing from is seen by the list as
being the same as the one you receive list traffic at.

If not, you'll get refused.  Then contacvt the human backup which is
lojban-list-request@snark.thyrsus.com
and someone will delete or add you as needed, or whatever.

No.  This is a "jutsi"-type predicate, with an arbitrary number of arguments,
and we don't have those in Lojban.  Where we might need them, we use
logical or non-logical connectives instead.  But in order to >explain<
non-logical connectives, we can >postulate< such predicates in "deep
Lojban" even though they don't exist in "surface Lojban".

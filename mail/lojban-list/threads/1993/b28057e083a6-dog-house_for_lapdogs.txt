Not long ago I had dinner with a friend who has two very small dogs.
As far as I am concerned, these are not `real' dogs at all; my cat is bigger.

    .i naku zo'u lomi pendo cu srana lo'e gerku
    .i ji'a lomi mlatu cu zmadu barda ni canlu kei ra

    It is not the case that my friend is associated with truly typical dogs.
    In addition, my cat is bigger in quantity of volume
    compared with them as a standard.

I very nearly laughed out loud when saw

    lo zdani be lo gerku be la cmalus be'o ku be'o
    Truly a nest for what is truly a dog of species Miniature

It was a small basket!

    .i .ua .u'i .i'e ba'e cmalu lanka

    <discover, with amusement, with approval>  <observe> very-small basket

Actually there has already evolved considerably different styles in written
Lojban from the styles used in the rather more limited examples of spoken
Lojban.  The latter is of course more forgiving of error, uses simpler 
constructs, and a whole bunch of other traits that you'd have to know the 
language for me to describe effectively.  But modulo some amount of error
correcting that each person tolerates in the different media, the Lojban
grammar and wordlists describe a superset of both 'languages'.

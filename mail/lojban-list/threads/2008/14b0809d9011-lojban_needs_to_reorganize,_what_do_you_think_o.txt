I agree with the blog article 100%. The given example redesign seems
okay, although maybe not very "web 2.0" (which is not necessarily a bad
thing). I will not comment to using MoinMoin, because I simply have no
experience with wiki software.
Concerning the forum: well, what we seriously don't want is spread of
discussion. But then again, the mailing lists aren't very "accessible"
to those not good with computers. Maybe a bit of help on using mailing
lists is in place?

As a sidenote, I don't think this should be called "reorganizing
Lojban", because that's just a different thing. Rather, this would be
"redesigning the community website and discussion boards".

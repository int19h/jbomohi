end):

             / ZOI-clause free*
             / LOhU-clause free*
             / lerfu-string !MOI BOI? free*
             / LU text LIhU? free*
             / (LAhE free* / NAhE BO free*) relatives? sumti LUhU? free*
             / KOhA free*
             / LA free* relatives? CMENE+ free*
             / (LA / LE) free* sumti-tail KU? free*

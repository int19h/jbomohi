install the 3.5 framework. I think before it was just failing to do

installed at lojban.org aside jboski, or perhaps some other server,
where it's more easily accessible to everyone. But I do want the
windows version to run for people, so please contact me with any
issues you have with this release. (E-mail is probably the best way--I

Robin Lee Powell scripsit:

The termset_85 rule makes it clear that the first (unbracketed) "nu'u"
in 14.15.9 doesn't belong there, and is causing the error.  Removing it
will cause the sentence to parse.  This is of course not the same as
saying it's *correct*.

The correct sentence corresponding to the English is:

      nu'i ge la djan. bau la lojban. [nu'u] 
      gi la frank. bai la djordj. [nu'u] cusku

Or {joi gi} instead of {ge}, given that the text is about
non-logical connectives. It is not clear what distinction is
15.9 supposed to make with respect to 15.8.

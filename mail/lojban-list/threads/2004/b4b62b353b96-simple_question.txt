On Monday 27 September 2004 17:50, MorphemeAddict@wmconnect.com wrote:
> I want to (know how to) say, for example, "I want to read a book".
>
> Does "mi cukta tcidu djica" mean that?
> Is something like "mi djica le nu mi tcidu lo cukta" more usual?

I would say "mi djica lo nu tcidu lo cukta" if I don't care which book I read, 
and "mi djica lo nu tcidu le cukta" if I want to read a particular book. 
Another way to say it is "mi djica co tcidu lo cukta".

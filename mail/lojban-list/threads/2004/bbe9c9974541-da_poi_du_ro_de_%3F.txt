--- Robin Lee Powell <rlpowell@digitalkingdom.org> wrote:
> If I say:
> 
> da poi du ro de zo'u da broda
> 
> Is that strictly equivalent to:
> 
> ro de zo'u de broda
> 
> In either case, why?

No. The first one says: that thing which is every thing, is a broda.
Unless there is only one thing in the universe (and it brodas),
then it can't be true.

The second one says that everything is a broda.

(Now if {da} were a plural variable, that could take more than one
value at a time, they might be equivalent. But in that case we would
need to define {ro} better.)

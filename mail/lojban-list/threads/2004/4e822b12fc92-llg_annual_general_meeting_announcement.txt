The Logical Language Group's annual general meeting will be
held this Wednesday on IRC (server irc.lojban.org, channel #llg).

The meeting will be called to order at 2200 UTC.  That is 3
PM Pacific, 6 PM Eastern, 11 PM British/Irish time, and midnight Western
European time.  (The rest of you are on your own for conversions.) This
meeting will be *strictly* limited to 2 hours, with a closed agenda.

In particular, those wishing to become new members are urged to
participate.  #lojban will be used as the chatter channel, as
parliamentary procedures will be in place on #llg.

Committee reports, such as they are, can be found at
http://www.lojban.org/llg .  Officer reports should be there shortly.

The following straw votes will be taken (we can't make Bylaw
changes this year due to the lack of formal notice):

9a) Direct election of officers by the membership instead of the board
9b) Genericizing the kinds of communications allowed in the by-laws
9c) Abolish proxies, or abolish blind proxies.

In addition, we have the following possible new official projects:
Worldcon, jboske, Lojban Java list.

If anyone has new business to propose, please mention it ASAP.
I apologize for the shortness of this notice.

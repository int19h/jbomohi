Hi all.

I am a member (albeit a recent one) of the online communities of polyglot (http://www.polyglot-learn-language.com/) and unilang (http://www.unilang2.org/), both of which have esperantists among their members and aim to help people two learn new languages.

One of the things which doesn't happen often, which may encourage future lojbanists is to witness a conversation in lojban. What I would like to organise is (slightly unethical I suppose) "random" meetings of lojbanists on the irc channels of these sites. The idea being to give the impression that we met on those channels and taught each other lojban. You will find me under nick Tirno on the following channels during 21h-23h most evenings central european time:

#polyglot on irc.kikoochat.com
#unilang on irc.unilang.org

Btw, it would be a good idea to encourage lojban to be put on the lists of the polyglot website (it does have esperanto, ido and quenya already), and to develop the lojban part of the unilang wiki

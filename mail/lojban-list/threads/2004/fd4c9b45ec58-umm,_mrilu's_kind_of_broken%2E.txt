Robin Lee Powell wrote:
>x1 of mrilu is the sender of the mail, I think.  x2 is the mail
>itself.  I don't know what x3 and x4 are.  I think x3 is the
>recipient, but then I don't know what x4 is.  I also don't know if
>x1 and x3 can be stations in the mail network.

x1 is the agent that causes the mailing.  In RFC822 terminology, that's
the "Sender:".  I don't think it could be an intermediate node in the
network.  x3 is the destination of the mail, which presumably could be
the intended recipient ("To:") or a location.  x4 is the origin of the
mail, which I think could also be either a location or a person ("From:").

-zefram

Actually, I have also a question about {lea}

According to Loglan 1, Sec. 4.20
http://www.loglan.org/Loglan1/chap4.html#sec4.20

[quote]Lea preda designates, for any preda, the non-empty set of all 
the predas. It is universal, not intentional. Like leu, its 
designatum is always a set, not the members of some set. Unlike leu, 
it means 'the set of all (predas)'.[/quote]

Lea ficli ga laldo lea mamla  = Fishes are older than mammals. 

What is the lojbanic equivalent?

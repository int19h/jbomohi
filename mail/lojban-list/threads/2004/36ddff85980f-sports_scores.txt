I recently saw on #lojban that there's been some more work in getting the
speech synthesizer stuff working in Lojban.  Sounds great, and I was
immediately thinking of ways to apply it.  Currently, I have some sports score
alerts sent to me by email, and I'm using procmail and some scripts to rewrite
them a little (because I've got a TTS program that reads me the From and
Subject line of incoming mail, I'm parsing them and making sure the vital
information appears in the subject).  So I started thinking of how I could
translate them into Lojban.

The tricky thing is, I haven't been able to come up with any good ways of
saying what I want in Lojban.  Ideally there'd be a single selbri that meant
something like "In game x1, visitors x2 has score x3 and home team x4 has score
x5 at time (inning, quarter, time remaining, whatever) x6" or something like
that.  Of course, that might be really complicated to work with, but...

I've looked at things involving kelci, jivna, ni jinga, tcini, but I can't
quite figure out a good way to put them together to get what I want.  I realize
that it will probably just come down to creating a lujvo and using it, but I
wanted to get some suggestions.

To score points, off the top of my head:

lo vitke/se vitke be la <game> cu snada fi'o gradu li <points> ti'u
li <time>

Although note that "points" in the sense of points in a game is in
jbovlaste is http://www.lojban.org/jbovlaste/dict/nilji'a.  The
definition needs work, but it seems like it could do most of what
you want.  There's also
http://www.lojban.org/jbovlaste/dict/kelsnada, which again needs
work.

WRT the thing you said, I have no idea what you would put in the x1
place.  Seems that the date and the teams fully encode the game, no?

Oh, unless you mean *type* of game (soccer, hockey, and so on).  In
that case:

<game> se kelci la <team1> poi nilji'a li <points for team1> ku'o .e
la <team2> poi nilji'a li <points for team2> ku'o ti'u li <time>

=== 3 | 2004-10-13T16:15:09Z | Jorge "Llambías" <jjllambias2000@yahoo.com.ar> | 20041013161509.64021.qmail@web41905.mail.yahoo.com | mail/lojban-list/cur/1097684109.6ebcd13802fcad10.jbomohi:2,S

jinga [ jig ji'a ] win ; 'gain' 
x1 (person/team) wins/gains prize x2 from/over x3 
[competitors/losers] in competition x4 

kantu [ ka'u ] quantum
x1 is a quantum/ray/elementary particle/smallest 
measurable increment of property/activity x2 

So I would suggest:

ji'arka'u 
x1 kantu lo nu x2 jinga da x3 x4
"x1 is a point scored by x2 against x3 in competition/match x4"

In a sports context, {lo kantu} would be enough for "a point", 
"a goal", etc.

ka'urji'a
x1 jinga lo kantu x2 x3
"x1 scores/wins a point from/over x2 in competition/match x3"

To report on an ongoing match, I would use {ca'o}. Something
like:

la blanu ca'o jinga fi la Xunre la'u li vo pi'e re
"The Blues are beating the Reds by 4 to 2."

{vitke} and {se vitke} seem right for visitors and home team.

la blanu noi vitke cu jinga la xunre noi se vitke

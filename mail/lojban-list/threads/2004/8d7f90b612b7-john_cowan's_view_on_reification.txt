My view on Lojban's view of reification:

Everything that a sumti refers to, not within the scope of a negation, is
reified.  That includes abstract le nu/lo nu things, le'e things, lo'e things,
and the works.  You may perhaps find an exception to this general principle,
but I can probably explain it away.

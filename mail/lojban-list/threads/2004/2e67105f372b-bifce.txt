{bifce} is glossed as "bee/wasp/hornet". "Bee" means any member of the 
superfamily Apoidea, "ant" means any member of the family Formicidae in the 
superfamily Vespoidea, and "wasp" means any member of Apocrita that isn't a 
bee or an ant (including the hornets, which are in Vespoidea). (I am ignoring 
spelling bees, WASPs, and any other senses that aren't Hymenoptera.) What 
words can we use to distinguish bees and any other bifce that might need 
distinguishing?

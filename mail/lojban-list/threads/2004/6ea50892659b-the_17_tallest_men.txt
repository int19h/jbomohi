MorphemeAddict@wmconnect.com scripsit:

> why is it so hard to say "the 17 tallest men" in lojban?  i would have
> thought that this had a standard form in logic long ago, and it would
> be a simple transfer to lojban.  what's the deal?

The standard first-order logic expression is unreasonably hairy.
It's something like "The set of A and B and ... and P and Q, such that A
is not B and A is not C and ... and B is not C and ... and P is not Q, and
A is not shorter than B and B is not shorter than C and ... and P is not
shorter than Q and A is a man and B is a man and ... and Q is a man and
there does not exist Z such that Z is a man and Z is not shorter than Q."

This can probably be improved on, but it obviously doesn't scale.

> The standard first-order logic expression is unreasonably hairy.
> It's something like "The set of A and B and ... and P and Q, such that A
> is not B and A is not C and ... and B is not C and ... and P is not Q, and
> A is not shorter than B and B is not shorter than C and ... and P is not
> shorter than Q and A is a man and B is a man and ... and Q is a man and
> there does not exist Z such that Z is a man and Z is not shorter than Q."
>
> This can probably be improved on, but it obviously doesn't scale.

But Lojban has grammatical setness and massness, so we can say "The subset 
which, considered as a mass, is tallest among the 17-element subsets". Since 
the place structure of pazemei is "x1 is the mass of set x2 which has 17 
members x3", {le clarai ke nanmu pazemei} refers to the mass and {le clarai 
ke nanmu te pazemei} to the individuals.

=== 4 | 2004-04-14T13:01:23Z | Jorge "Llambías" <jjllambias2000@yahoo.com.ar> | 20040414130123.42426.qmail@web41904.mail.yahoo.com | mail/lojban-list/cur/1081947683.6d8142e90052b1af.jbomohi:2,S

{lo paze clarai nanmu} will also work.

What counts as a member of {lo'i clarai nanmu} will depend
on context. It is a set of nanmu, but the tanru does not say 
exactly how clarai applies. Knowing the meaning of {clarai},
and that the set in question has 17 members, {lo paze clarai
nanmu} will pretty clearly refer to the 17 tallest men.

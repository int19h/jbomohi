I notice you equate nouns with cmene. While that works, a way to think about
it that I find easier is that cmene are the only words which are inherently
nouns. My way of thinking about it is that (most - li and la are obvious
exceptions) gadri serve as particles which convert verbs to nouns and
articles all in one nice little package.

When I started writing the Lojban beginners' lessons, one of the 
comments that prompted me was a request for teaching materials which did 
_not_ have too many English grammatical terms in them (although a number 
crept in as I proceded).  I think on the whole it's a good idea to avoid 
thinking in terms of English grammar as far as possible, as it can cause 
problems later on. Lojban does not have nouns, verbs etc., and I am 
convinced that our endless debates about Lojban articles are partly 
caused by the fact that we still think of them as articles.

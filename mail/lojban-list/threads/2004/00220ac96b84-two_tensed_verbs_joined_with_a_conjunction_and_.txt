Pierre Abbat wrote:
> ca le xlima'i dorduncte co'u crisa je co'a critu fi le berti xabyboi
> It doesn't parse. How to fix it?

ca le xlima'i dorduncte co'u crisa gi'e co'a critu vau fi le berti xabyboi

At Virgo-month(=September) equinox is the end of the summer and the
beginning of the autumn in the Northern Hemisphere.

{je} is tanru-internal connective. Tanru as a whole is tensed, not
individual tanru units. Therefore {co'u crisa} and {co'a critu} should
be bridi-connected. Also to share the third place sumti, you need
{vau} to terminate bridi.

mi'e sanxiyn.

It seems to me that ki'o is basically the special case of pi'e for
base 1000, with the additional convention that a leading ki'o gets
a 1 in front. (Also, that there has to be at least one ki'o after pi 
for the digits after pi to be treated as base 1000, so that 
{reki'ocipimu} is 2,003.5 and {reki'ocipimuki'o} is 2,003.005)

CLL does not quite agree with that, but at least one of the examples
with ki'o after pi has to be wrong.

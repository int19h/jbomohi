I recently found out about an essential oil and would like your opinion about 
what to call it in Lojban. Here are some possibilities:
lelcua, from Masai leleshwa, which is what it's usually called in English.
tarxonanto, from the genus name Tarchonanthus. Someone explained this as 
meaning "funeral flower", but he's not sure why.
klambati, from Swahili mkalambati.
kanferbosu, from Afrikaans kanferbos "camphorbush".

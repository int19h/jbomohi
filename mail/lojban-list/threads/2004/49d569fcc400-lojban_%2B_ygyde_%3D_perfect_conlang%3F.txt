Lojban has very logical grammar but its root words
are not good -- they are too long (5 letters),
difficult to pronounce (consonant clusters), and
difficult to remember (1350 roots + short forms
called rafsi).

Ygyde has only 180 two-letter long root words and
a sophisticated way of combining them into short,
easy to pronounce and understand compound words.
Its grammar, however, is too simplistic.

It seems that a perfect conlang can be made by
combining Lojban's grammar with Ygyde's root words.

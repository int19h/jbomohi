thing to look into is the phonetic inventory - what sounds most 
people in the world can produce/distinguish, including computers' 
ability to recognize/distinguish these sounds.  This would then be 
followed by a study of the most efficient methods of preventing vocal

method of making the most commonly-used/useful words the shortest.

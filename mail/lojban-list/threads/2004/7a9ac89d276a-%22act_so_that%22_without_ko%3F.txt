Make another selbri that *does* have a place for it :)

{ko klagau mi}
[act so that] you cause-to-leave me

Perhaps you could make "you" the point of departure:

   mi klama fi ko

   Act so that I go with point-of-departure you.

or alternatively...

   mi cliva ko

   Act so that I leave you.

If there is no place that could logically be filled by "do" (e.g. you 
want to be made to go somewhere, but not away from the person), you 
could try a causal modal:

   mi klama mu'i le nu ko tarti

   Behave so as to motivate me to go.

Or with sumti-raising:

   mi klama mu'i tu'a ko

I have always thought that the "ko" construct is a bit of a kludge. What
especially bugged me was the example in CLL "ko ko kurji". Why replace
both "do" with "ko"?

To me, it would seem better just to have a cmavo that works like "xu",
meaning "act so that the following bridi will be true".

It could well belong to the same selma'o as "xu".

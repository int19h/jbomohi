Many of you have heard me attribute most of my success with Lojban
to a memory aid (read: flashcard on steroids) program called
SuperMemo, specifically the PalmOS version.

I've updated my help file on this app (see
http://www.teddyb.org/~rlpowell/hobbies/lojban/) to include
downloads for the PalmOS emulator for Windows.  There are also Mac
and Linux versions.  All are free to download.  For the Linux
versions, you don't even need to subscribe to the PalmOS dev group.

If you're serious about learning Lojban, and you're willing to put
in the 15 minutes or so a day it takes to properly use this app, I
can't reccomend it enough.  You'll still need to buy a copy of
MapleTop's SuperMemo, but I'm willing to buy copies for people that
really want to use it and really can't afford it (it's vanishingly
cheap compared to my current budget).

Dear Lojban enthusiasts,

I am a recruiter for Lionbridge Technologies and would like to ask if any of you would be willing to participate in a (well-paid!) project regarding the Lojban language.

We are seeking to improve our understanding of how machines can learn to use and respond to all kinds of languages. This process has already been done for larger language groups, but usually the minority languages are neglected in modern technology.

You would have to:
-          Have very good skills in Lojban
-          Understand English instructions and guidelines
-          Have a computer and internet access
-          Have a PayPal account or be willing to create one

If you feel up to the task and are interested in joining our project, please apply directly via email to me and I will set you up or answer any questions regarding the project!

Digital printing is a process of using digital or electronic files from 
computer or other digital storage devices as a source. Digital printing 
<http://www.dot2dot.com.my/digital-printing/> won’t use press plates or 
setup bedding in the printing process. With the advancement in the 
technology and in printing techniques, digital printing has replaced all 
other printing techniques in markets.

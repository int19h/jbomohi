I would like to issue a survey about how to Lojbanize foreign names 
containing a semivowel directly preceded by a consonant sound. Since the 
end of 2014, after a vote of the BPFK, any sequence of a consonant 
directly followed by a semivowel (for example "ia" in "kenias") is 
banned from Lojban words (cmavo, brivla and cmevla included). However 
the BPFK did not give any recommendation on what alternative 
Lojbanization to favor. I have thus made this little survey to gather 
the opinions of Lojbanists on which Lojbanization they prefer with 
regard to semivowels.

Here is the link to the survey:

• 
https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScW66FzOnPcCDxn-v0gcd7IzXXrS8vPEG1GoubIom1XQyfb8g/viewform

I'm looking forward to reading your opinions.

—Ilmen.

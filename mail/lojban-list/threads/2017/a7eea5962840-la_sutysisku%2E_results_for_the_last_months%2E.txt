This post discusses la sutysisku
<https://la-lojban.github.io/sutysisku/en/#sisku/zandapma> dictionary app.

This is another type of frequency lists. Here core verbs of Lojban (lo
gismu) are sorted according to how often they are searched for in la
sutysisku app.

https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1T1YDh4ug59iUe7WG6Ge3
k-251OfA8rithJmMGdD7S_M/edit#gid=950897042

(some are pseudogismu. Users simply entered something that has the form of
lo gismu).

The column to the right has lo valsi and lo valsi poi nu'o mo'u se ciska
without filtering pseudogismu. This means some people stop typing a word in
la sutysisku forcing the app to output possible results.

Why is this important? You can see what people were trying to find, which
concepts are still absent in the dictionary and thus might need some work
so that new users finally find highly needed words.

If someone wants to dig more into the stats tell me, I'll provide you with
access to the analytics account.

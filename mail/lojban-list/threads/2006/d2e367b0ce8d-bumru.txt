some place, so it is no real difference between fog itself and foggy 
place (again, in colloquial speech only).

Of course, if you need exact translation, you should use more narrow 
definitions. In this case tanru solution is good enough.

{o'a} - pride; {o'a nai} - shame.

Yanis: Several gismu signifying the material composition
of something behave like {bumru}:
{rokci} - x1 is a quantity of/is made of/_contains_ rock/stone...;
{djacu} - x1 is made of/_contains_/is a quantity of water;
{xalka} - x1 is a quantity of/_contains_/is made of alcohol...;
and so on.
Something can be partly foggy ({pagbu bumru}, nebbioso,
brumeux) or "totally foggy", fog itself :) ({mulno bumru}, nebbio,
köd etc.).
I would use {pagbu co'e} "partly" and {mulno co'e} "fully" for
precisely describing what I'm thinking of.

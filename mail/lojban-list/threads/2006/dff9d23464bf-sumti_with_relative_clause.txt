Only to {la djil}.

To get the other meaning, you need to use the LAhE/NAhE-BO
terminator, which is LUhU:

{ro na'e bo la djil lu'u noi zutse}

=== 5 | 2006-03-21T14:25:02Z | John E Clifford <lojban-out@lojban.org> | 20060321142502.97743.qmail@web81302.mail.mud.yahoo.com | mail/lojban-list/cur/1142951102.b18708901586c664.jbomohi:2,S
Insofar as Lojban follows Logic (and I think it
does here), a relative clauses attaches to the
shortest complete sumti preceding, in this case
to {la djil}.  To attach to {ro na'ebo la djil},
that sumti would have to be completed, in this
case by whatever closes off this sort of sumti
{ku}?) before the {noi}.

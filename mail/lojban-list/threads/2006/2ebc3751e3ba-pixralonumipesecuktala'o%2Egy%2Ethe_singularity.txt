> and the complete lojban language?

{mi dasni lo xekri pastu}

The x2 of dasni is for what you are wearing, x3 is for what you are
wearing it _as_. So, if you were wearing the robe as a hat, you would
put "a hat" in x3. Since you are wearing the robe as a robe, you can
just put it in x2. You could also say {mi dasni lo xekri lo pastu}, "I wear
a black thing as a robe", but that would at least suggest that what you
are wearing is not actually a robe.

For "hold" you can use {ralte} or {jgari}.

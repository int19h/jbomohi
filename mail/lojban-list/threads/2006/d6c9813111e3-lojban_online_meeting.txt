The results of the availability questionnaire are now in. Monday,
November 13, from 7 to 8 PST, 8 to 9 MST, 9 to 10 CST, 10 to 11 EST,
Lojbanists will meet on the IRC channel and the Voice Over IP software
Teamspeak.

Download the free Teamspeak software here:
http://www.goteamspeak.com/index.php?page=downloads

Set Teamspeak to access the server "chain.lojban.org", and set the
channel to "lojban". If you don't have a microphone, you can join via
the Lojban IRC channel: http://www.lojban.org/irc/

-Eppcott

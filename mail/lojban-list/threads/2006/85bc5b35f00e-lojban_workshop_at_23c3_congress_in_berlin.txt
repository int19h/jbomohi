Hello,

for those of you who are attending the 23rd chaos communication congress 
(23C3) in Berlin, an every-year hackers' meeting of the chaos computer club, 
I want to point out that there is a lojban workshop for beginners.

It takes place at 2006-12-29, 17:00 -- 18:15h. For more information, see
http://events.ccc.de/congress/2006/Fahrplan/events/1753.en.html. 

As it is a workshop and not a presentation (there will be no video projector), 
every helping lojban speaker is appreciated. So if you are attending the 
congress and are a lojban speaker (simple sentences are enough), please 
contact me. 

It would be nice to meet other lojbanists at the congress.

Best Regards,
Alex.

Binary is base two digital.  Digital is a quantized method for representing
information where intermediate steps between levels do not exist at the
functional level.  What if computers were ternary?  They'd still be digital.
What if tomorrow there were spintronic CPUs in every desktop that were
sensitive enough for 16 spins?  That would be base 16 digital.

On Wednesday 30 August 2006 07:58, MorphemeAddict@wmconnect.com wrote:
> Does jbofi'e recognize the form even if it doesn't know what the form
> means?
>
> Or does it choke because it doesn't know the word?

If it doesn't know the word, but recognizes it as a well-formed word, it puts 
question marks for the meaning. It doesn't recognize these as well-formed 
words. For {srutio} and {largectremia}, if you put an apostrophe between the 
last two letters, you get a lujvo, and jbofi'e considers a word like that 
invalid. As to lujvo made with fu'ivla, when jbofi'e was written, all that 
was worked out was that it is possible to use fu'ivla of the form CCVVCV, and 
you have to specify an option to jbofi'e to use them.

Most definitely.
If you and I together are looking at a stage full of children, including 
Billy, and I want to draw your attention to Mary, I could tell you: "Mary is the 
girl to the right of Billy", which means in your and my frame of reference, 
regardless of the orientation of Billy. 
If I say "Mary is the girl on Billy's right", then the frame of reference 
shifts to Billy's frame of reference.

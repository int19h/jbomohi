I did not appear here for long time (because of lack
of free time). However, I am still inspired by this
language and I slowly learn it.

I have realized that Lojban-part of Internet is
growing quite fast and it is already quit large at the
moment. So I think we could try a banner exchange
program to make our sites closer and easier reachable
for each other.

If somebody is interested in that idea I am happy to
say that I have created a site which organize a banner
exchange. It works quit simple. First you have to
register your site (give URL-address and its short
description as well as specify its subject). Than you
have to put one line into HTML code of your page (this
line will be generated after registration).

The inserted line will display a frame with banners of
other Lojban-sites.  The content of the frame is
dynamical. System will try to show banners in such a
way to send to your page as many visitors as you send
to other pages.

If you want to try it, during subject specification
you will have to select:
1.Science.
2.Linguistic.
3.Constructed Languages.
4.Lojban.

If you think that my idea is bad, pleas tell me why.
If you want to take a part in the banner exchange
program but something is not clear, just ask me.

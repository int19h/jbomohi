Yesterday I started "Ubuntu Lojban Translators" team which attempts to
translate Ubuntu operating system into Lojban. Feel free to join the
team and/or spread the news about the project. Joining requires creating
an account to Launchpad development environment. The team itself is open
for anyone to join and doesn't currently require confirmation from a
team leader or member.

Team page can be found at
  https://launchpad.net/people/ubuntu-l10n-jbo

Launchpad accounts can be requested at
  https://launchpad.net/+login

More information about Ubuntu from
  http://www.ubuntu.com/


    --Toni Ruottu (cyberix)





To unsubscribe from this list, send mail to lojban-list-request@lojban.org
with the subject unsubscribe, or go to http://www.lojban.org/lsg2/, or if
you're really stuck, send mail to secretary@lojban.org for help.

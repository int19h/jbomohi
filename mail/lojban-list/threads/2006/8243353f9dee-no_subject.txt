I of course made research on all the projects proposed at this time, but I

Thank you in advance.

Neutral universal language

In the design of the creation of a world State, one of the fundamental
questions is posed: that of a universal language. Universal in its extent
with all planet. Neutral to avoid any reserve and any sign of a minority
superiority.

In our current world, we will speak rather about international language
before even of being able to allot the universal title to him.

However the project can be posed in five questions:
* Is it useful to found an international language?

If the project would be considered:
* Which organization can have legitimacy to make unspecified decisions on
the introduction of an international language?
* Does it have to be a natural or built language?

In the possibility of a built language:
* Does it have to take for base an already existing language or to be
created by itself?

If an international language is accepted:
* Does it have to be one second language or to supplant the mother tongue?

With these questions, I propose to you to answer to the following address:
www.steph_corvisier@yahoo.fr

Cordially.

The system looks for translations that:
- haven't been reviewed by you earlier;
- haven't been skipped by you earlier;
- are not translations of void posts;
- are not abandoned;
- fit into your difficulty span;
- were not created by a banned user.

If you are sure a particular translation doesn't fall under any of
these restrictions, and still it doesn't appear in "Review
Translations", it's definitely a bug. Unfortunately, the jsk database
is on Robin's server and it's impossible for me to debug. :(

The Logical Language Group decided to get two hotel rooms in the
convention hotel, complimentary to jbonunsla registrants who need it
the most. This helps defray the overall costs to active members of the
community. We hope it will encourage Lojbanists from nearby areas such
as Maryland, New Jersey, or NYC to stay later for our activities and
not just make a day trip. Currently the rooms have not filled up.

Also, one of our members has offered a guest room in a home 15 minutes
from the hotel, to two people who would not mind sharing a bed.
Attendees can email me off-list to inform us of their lodging needs
and means.

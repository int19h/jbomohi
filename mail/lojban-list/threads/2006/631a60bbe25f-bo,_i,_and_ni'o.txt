After running into this problem a few times, I concluded that {bo} is a
connector dependent on the previous statement. Two statements connected by a
{bo} are kind of like a single sentence. E.g.

mi pensi i se ni'i bo mi zasti
I think, therefore I am.

So the dependence requires the {.i} which at least implies a previous
statement, & precludes the {ni'o} because a sentence can't spill over into a
new paragraph.

My explanation probably suffers from quite a bit of imprecision, but it's
something like that.

mu'o mi'e komfo,amonan

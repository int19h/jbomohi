=== 5 | 2006-01-28T17:31:56Z | Evgeny Sklyanin <eks2005@gmail.com> | 4c4e58c10601280931v7863ee0fi68990d4730cae0d@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-list/cur/1138469516.9882528884f1786f.jbomohi:2,S
An "event" abstraction makes sense only if the events combined together
happen at the same time. One therefore can try  the temporal marker {ca}

mi nelci le nu mi pinxe le birje ca le nu le bersa be mi cu kelci = "I like
to drink beer while my son is playing."

In this way it is difficult to combine more than two sentences. Another
suggestion: use topic-comment construction:

le fasnu noi mi nelci zo'u tu'e mi pinxe le birje .ije le bersa be mi cu
kelci tu'i

= The event I like: "I drink beer and my son plays".

mi'e .evgenis.

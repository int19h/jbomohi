lo makfa mlatu

.i lo mi cmalu je mlatu
ti'u ro vanci cu vitke
gi'e cusku di'e mi
.i coi lo mi tixnu

.i my dasni lo cutci
be fi lo xunblabi dilnu ku
.e lo ka'arxa'i je se jadni
.e lo blanu je mafygrana

gi'e damba seva'u mi
bu'u lo mi se senva
je se terpa lo pacycrida
to'o lo nitmu'e

-- 
Seo Sanghyeon

The original is written in Korean, and I translated from Korean to
Lojban. Here's my attempt at translating from Korean to English. As
both are free translations, Lojban and English won't match in some
places.

Lojban translation is only up to the first repeat. English translation
is complete.

Le Chat Magique

My little cat
comes to me every evening
and greets me,
"Bon soir, ma petite fille".

Pink shoes made of cloud
Jewel sword the angel gave
Blue wand of the magician
Dressing herself

>>From the hell to the heaven
my little cat
fought for me
against devils of nightmare.

Pink shoes made of cloud
Jewel sword the angel gave
Blue wand of the magician
Courage born with

Ma petite fille, ma cherie,
avec toi et moi,
big and black devils
I am not afraid of

>>From the hell to the heaven
my little cat
shined for me
with pale gloomy light.

My little cat
comes to me every morning,
and says good-bye to me,
"Bon jour, ma petite fille".

mi'e sanxiyn.

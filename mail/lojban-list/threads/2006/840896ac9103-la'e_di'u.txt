> 2) levi litki cu skari claxu .i lo du'u go'i cu nibli lo du'u ra tolckape

>
> What is the difference between {la'e di'u} and {lo du'u go'i} in these two
> lines?

Not much as far as I can tell. {la'e di'u} is generally more vague than
{lo du'u go'i}, because {di'u} is not limited to the single preceding bridi
like {go'i}, and {la'e di'u} could also be {lo nu go'i}. But in the example
given I would say they are the same.

According to Wikipedia ( http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elephants_dream ):
Elephants Dream is a <A HREF="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-generated_imagery">computer-generated</A> <A HREF="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_film">short film</A> made entirely <A HREF="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#_note-0">[1]</A> using <A HREF="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_source">
open source</A> <A HREF="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Application_software">applications</A> and premiered on <A HREF="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_24">March 24</A>, <A HREF="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006">2006</A> after about 8 months of 
work. Beginning production in September, <A HREF="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005">2005</A>, it was developed under the 
name Orange by a team of seven artists and animators from around the world. Its 
name was later changed from Machina (the pronunciation of which the creators 
couldn't decide on) and renamed to Elephants Dream (named after the way in which 
Dutch childrens' stories abruptly end). <A HREF="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#_note-1">[2]</A>

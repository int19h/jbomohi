> Is that right?  In fact, is it stronger in that before gismu or lujvo there

A stressed cmavo must be separated from any following brivla by a pause.
For cmene, the rule is much stronger:  ANY preceding word, except for
"la", "lai", "la'i", and "doi", must be separated from a following
cmene by a pause.

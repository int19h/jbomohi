John Cowan is correct that there are several people who have either
purchased, or plan to purchase copies to donate to libraries.  I therefore
want to repeat a comment I made about this a month or so ago.

***  Make sure to specify that you want the book copy back if it is not
retained by the library for use***

Libraries without the above specification are free to add the book to their
stock of materials to sell for benefit of the library.  They do _not_ put all
donations on the shelf.

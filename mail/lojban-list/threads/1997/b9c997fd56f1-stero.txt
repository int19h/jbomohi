    What does {stero} mean?  The gi'uste gives the English gloss
    as "steradian".  Unfortunately, neither I nor my dictionaries
    know what "steradian" means.

A steradian is a unit of measurement of solid angle, just as a radian
is a unit of measurement of a two-dimensional angle.

A radian is the angle subtended by a unit length on the circumference
of a circle of unit radius (180/pi degrees or about 57 degrees); a
steradian is the solid angle subtended by a unit square on the surface
of a sphere of unit radius.

`Steradian' is a fairly common term and I am surprised to see that my
Webster's Collegiate Dictionary is missing it, too.  My OED, which is
notorious for lacking scientific terms, has a definition but I find
its definition to be hard to understand.

A steradian is a measure of a 'solid angle'. An area of the surface of a
sphere equal to the square of the radius subtends an angle of one
steradian. Frequently used in talking about electromagnetic radiation, as
in antenna theory, particle physics, and optics. Intensity of a radiation
source is often expressed as Watts/steradian. A candle or light bulb
radiates through an angle of slightly less than 4 pi steradians, because
the wick, base, or support obstructs a small portion. A star radiates
through a full 4 pi steradians.

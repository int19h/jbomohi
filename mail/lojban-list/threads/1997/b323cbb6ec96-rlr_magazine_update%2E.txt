The new magazine format is now designed, and articles are being
added.
So, the new "Rap Lin Rie KOS" will be going out in January. It is
32 pages,
counting the cover. First issue has a picture of Dutton on it.
The first picture
I have ever seen of him. It was first printed in 1916-1919. There
will be articles
on Dutton, obviously, & on his International Symbolic Language,
the precursor
to Speedwords; lessons in RLR, editorials, translations into RLR
of old but
valuable IAL books, ads, articles on health, success, finances,
history, science,
religion, etc. all, or mostly in RLR. Plus, some current world
news events.
Some jokes, poems, and a whole lot more is planned. Obviously,
not all of
the above will be in one issue, but will be covered over the
months and years.
To begin, the magazine will be out quarterly, and maybe
bi-monthly if it grows
to the point the effort to do so is not overwhelming. Size of the
magazine is about
the size of a pocketbook, about 41/2 by 5 inches. After looking
at the dummy copy
I can't wait to get it done and on its way.
First issue may become a collector's item, who knows.
Tentative first run is for 1,000 copies.

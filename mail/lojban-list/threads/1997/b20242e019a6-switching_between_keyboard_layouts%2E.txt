say that it isn't impossible at all to get used to several different
keyboards. The difference is of course much smaller than between
(US) QWERTY and DVORAK, but punctuation is very different, and the
(for the purpose of typing in Swedish) important letters =E5,=E4,=F6
are sadly missing on the US keyboard. (On the other hand, brackets and
such are more comfortable to type, which is nice when you're programmin=

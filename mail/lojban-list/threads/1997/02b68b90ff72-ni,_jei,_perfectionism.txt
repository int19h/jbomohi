truth value of a preoposition is a number, yes.  But it is a number with

which jei was translating were examples of indirect questions.

having an effect on the language ONLY of those who plow through them, and
only then if they happen to agree as to the analysis.

jei in any form since the whole issue of indirect questions was raised.

jei as an indirect question because he did not agree with his critics'

an indirect question.  It is also possible that he changed the usages -

proposition, which is generally expressed as "true" or "false" or "0"/"1".

meaningfully.  Likewise probabilistic functions can use 0/1 scale of
truth value meaningfully.  We identified something meaningful that someone

time we did not have du'u in the language yet - only nu, ka, and ni.

rather less rarely invoked in language than indirect questions.  But then
re'a matrix tramspose/dual is likely to be even more rarely used %^).

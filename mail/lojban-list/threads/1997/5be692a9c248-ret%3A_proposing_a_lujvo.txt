Given the context you presented, perhaps something like

depri'a/depygau fu(?) le pu'u solri xaksu

although your explanation might better be converyed with masno rather than
denpa since you aren't really trying to temporarily stop the process
either, but rather to slow it down.  Just using these two words (masno and
denpa) and their places, you can see that there may not be just one Lojban
word for English "delay/postpone".  Add in the gismu for interfere and
interrupt and the possibilities grow greatly.

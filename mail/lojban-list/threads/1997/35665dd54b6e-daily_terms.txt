Hrm.  Is "xamgu" really the right word?  I think it's more like "fitting"
or "meet".  A fruit can be "good for eating" or something, but somehow that
seems not quite right for mornings.  I suppose a morning can be good for

Yeah.  Who's to say that "good morning" really has anything to do with "may
this morning be good"?  It's more like "Hey there, I'm greeting you in a
polite way, and it happens to be morning around here."

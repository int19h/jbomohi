>I always assumed the word was "adenoids", but I'm not sure
>either.  What's an adenoid anyway?

The adenoids are paired lymphatics in the posterior nasopharynx (where the
back of the nose meets the top of the throat). They are often removed at
the same time as the tonsils during a tonsillectomy.

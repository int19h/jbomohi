There used to be a Lojban room on MediaMOO, ages ago... I remember I

Lojban environment, but a MUSH or MOO would work quite well.  Besides, I
tend to prefer those anyway.  I certainly know what it takes to run a MUSH
(I'm one of the admins of the KLI's MUSH that someone mentioned) and rather
like MOOs, though they take a bit more getting used to.

~mark

The printer has said that this kind of thing (called a hiccup) can happen
and is usually quite rare.  Typical causes include power fluctuations.
It apparently is not a bad sign that 3 bad pages were found in one book,
since they print entire copies of the book at one time, rather than printing
in signatures of 16 or 32 pages in the old-fashioned way.

The bottom line is that we guarantee all copies.  If you send a bad one back
we will re0place it with a hopefully good copy and reimburse you for the
postage (and hopefully collect from the printer).

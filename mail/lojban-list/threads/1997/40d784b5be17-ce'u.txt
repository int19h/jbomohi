> What's the official definition of
> {ce'u}, then, and where can I find a ma'oste that has it?

It means "lambda(x)" and is used in ka-bridi, thus:

	le ka ce'u melbi
	the property of being beautiful

	le ka melbi ce'u
	the property of perceiving an (unspecified) beautiful thing

	etc.

It's like the analogue of ke`a in ka clauses.

  ka ce`u prami ce`u = "the 'love' relationship"
  ka ce`u prami ko`a = "the property of loving ko`a"
  ka ko`a prami ce`u = "the property of being loved by ko`a"
  ka ce`u tavla ce`u la lojban = "the relationship between a
                        speaker and addressee in Lojban discourse"

In Lojban, all tanru are made by putting two (or more; technically one
brivla also
makes for a tanru, but anyway) brivla next to each other.

I'm considering something of a dialect/preference/whatever that would
require a tanru
to be enclosed in {ke} to make it a tanru; otherwise, it would break into
two selbri.
For example, {lo blanu stizu} would parse as {<lo blanu KU> [CU stizu
VAU]}, and to
express the intended 'blue chair', it'd be required to say {lo ke blanu
stizu}.
What do you think about such a convention?

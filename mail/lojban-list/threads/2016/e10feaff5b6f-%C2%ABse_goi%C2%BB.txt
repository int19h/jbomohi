The problem with «goi» is that it is ambiguous—one can not know which way
the assignment goes.
I was thinking about a nice solution, and found one. Basically, «goi»
assigns the left sumti to the right one. If one wants to do it the other
way around, he says «se goi». For example:

   - *ko'a goi ko'e* – it-1, from now on referred to as it-2;
   - *la .alis. se goi ti* – Alice, which from now on refers to this thing
   here.

Actually, this syntax can work with any other GOI! I will throw more
examples at you:

   - *lo vi ninmu se po lo junla* – this woman here owning a watch («po»:
   owned by..., «se po»: owning...);
   - lo vi nanmu se po'e lo panzi – this man here having a child («po'e»:
   intrinsic property of..., «se po'e»: owning intrinsically...).

What do you think?

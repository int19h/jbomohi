Hi there.

I'm not going to elaborate on the idea: I propose a weekly magazine (or
whatever) of Lojbanists' poems (on some topic).
Once a week, I would announce a topic, to which everyone (who can, and I
think everyone can if they want; beginners would also be welcome) would
write a poem. It could be short, long — you'd decide.
I think that writing seventeen syllables (if you'd write haiku all the
time) a week is a thing that takes one minute, so I encourage everyone to
try once I have heard suggestions here.

Please share your thoughts with me,

— mi'e la uakci mu'o

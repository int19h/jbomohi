https://mw.lojban.org/papri/Learning_with_context

This is a picture book not for children.

It's for those who speak English and want to learn Lojban.

The book is not "yet another picture book in Lojban, which nobody will
read".

It contains glossing of Lojban sentences inside.

The idea is that we tune up Lojban and English style so that those two
languages resemble each other more. This helps accumulate Lojban faster.
Other feature not present in English can be learnt later by other means.

You may use this idea if you speak languages other than English:
1. You need basic knowledge of Lojban
2. You need to select styles of your language and of Lojban so that they
match each other as close as possible.
3. If you have problems with making an actual picture book you may send
your adaptation to me, I'll compile everything for you.

The license of the book is rather open so the output is for humanity.

P.S. Also if you have suggestions on how to improve this book or what else
to do for Lojban please report.

Hi hi!

I'm going to start a new pemkarni (poem magazine) I shall call [pemci fi
ko]. Everyone is welcome to take part, beginners too: I'll do my best to
correct every poem I get!
Please write anything that is a poem. It needn't rhyme or have a consistent
rhythm. It needn't have a hundred words - you can even go for a haiku if
you feel like it.
pemci fi ko shall be weekly. I'll introduce a topic and you write something
about it. Next week, I'll release the submissions and introduce another
topic. Everyone, please do your best at doing it weekly!
This week's topic is
    fulta "float"
Please send me your creations *before* November 15 (Tuesday) to my email
address (ciuak.prog@gmail.com). I'm waiting!

~ uakci

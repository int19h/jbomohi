Hi, I want to describe some word patterns which, actually, aren't complete,
using Kennings that, I don't know if you know about that, but are composed
of four words, or phrases, and state that the first one is to the second as
is the third to the fourth, also that I'm going to use them like that, and
the main one of this whole sentence is, "noi : poi : * :: ckaji : klesi :
panra", which basically says that noi and poi parralel ckaji and klesi,
respectively, but also that panra has no such equivalents, and I wrote this
Kenning while I want to invent such cmavo, so that one can use it as "ti
NOI barda", and not "lo panra be ti bei lo ka barda"; and also am asking
you what do you think about it

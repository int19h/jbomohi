Hi.

Up to this point, no submissions for the next pemkarni have been sent in.
Thus, I conclude that pemkarni is no longer a thing, thus

1) I'm no longer doing pemkarni. It was supposed to be a project for
everyone to enjoy and for as many as it's possible to take part in. The
first thing I noticed is that all but one of the submitters are newbies.
Also, most of them come from Reddit. The inevitable conclusion is that I've
failed to interest you even though I've bothered you with 4 emails. I
wanted pemkarni to have the premise of engaging the whole community (the
'big' community, a.k.a. the ones that have anything to do with Lojban) in
making a smal gift to themselves (in form of any poem/prose) every week...
but no. Even though I've thrown a thousand 'please's around and begged you
on my knees, you didn't bother to try. So, I've failed. If anyone wants to
continue pemkarni, go ahead (you can even reuse that name), but I won't do
anything of that type anymore for a longer period of time.

Speaking of the not bothered, I expected that if one gets an email just
before his eyes, he would at least check if it's interesting. That didn't
seem to happen, so

2) I'm leaving the mriste, a.k.a. mroste. I don't want to participate in a
20-year-old mailing liste that has already served its purpose, but now is a
place of hypothetical discussions and total lack of interest. The 'big
fishes' that've been here from the beginning is now among the least
interested. Even some discussions get completely ignored or put down.

I thought that my contribution and engagement in mroste would help, but it
didn't. (Or maybe I had the illusion it helped, while I just spammed your
mailboxes.) Many Lojbanic groups that have been spontaneously created are
more active, even if they have only a few members to begin with.

That all doesn't mean I'll stop being a Lojbanist or something. The
opposite will happen. I'm probably going to collect Lojbanists from more
active places (probably Reddit) and create a literary group of works. Works
and translations by the eager to be Lojbanic for the eager to be Lojbanic.

So, that's it. No need to reply to this, because I won't have been here
anymore.

Average regards,
uakci

What did you expect to happen?


Lojban serves no purpose as a scientific endeavor since the Sapir-Worf Hypothesis has been discredited by other means. It serves no purpose as a machine interlanguage because artificial intelligence projects are focusing on natural language processing. It serves little purpose as spoken logic because it can't handle more complex or "interesting" logics. And it has never, despite some people's desires, been suited as an international auxiliary language.


Lojban is a hobby language with an aging community that has little time for hobbies.


If anything, this is a sign that we need to begin a new logical language that learns from the failures of the Loglan and Lojban projects.

=== 3 | 2016-09-11T19:55:41Z | 'John E Clifford' via lojban <lojban@googlegroups.com> | 1988663459.3238110.1473623741795@mail.yahoo.com | mail/lojban-list/cur/1473623741.a93f39a49f93dec3.jbomohi:2,S
While I am not sure what "more interesting" logics Logjam can't handle, the general point here seems about right.  That machine translation has taken the down and dirty bilingual approach rather the interlingual is unfortunate (in the long run and for broader uses) but has always been the case.  The rest pretty much follows.  Of course, going to a better "logical language" won't help that situation much, nor the international language one.  Still, it remains an ideal that Loglan (and so Lojban) threw away on day one and that could be developed from scratch (well, bits and pieces of it have been done over on engelang over the years).  Starting from a developed logic (say a Montague intensional system) and proceeding by reversible transformations step by step, one could achieve a speakable language fairly quickly.  Certainly more quickly than the Logjam procedure of starting with a speakable language (i.e. basically English with strange flourishes) and trying to build something like a logical system out of it (60 years and still not certainly successful). Of course, just what constitutes a speakable language (and just why an intensional logic lexicalized isn't one) constitutes an initial problem to be dealt with early on, as does the exact formulation of the logic, since there are several competitors, not all obviously equivalent.  A nice task for another couple generations, not, happily, mine any more.  pycyn, from before creation.

​Recently I was wondering about sumtcita of default sumti places.
For example, djedi2 defaults to li pa.
So, if one adds a djedi2 place with fi'o and not fill it in, does it mean,
that the default will be used?
In other words, is fi'o seldei ku the same as fi'o seldei li pa,
and does lo fi'o seldei fe'u ciska mean lo ciska be fi'o seldei li pa?
I haven't found information on that in the CLL.

Technically (in my interpretation - as it is not really addressed anywhere tobmy knowledge), this would happen anywhere in a bridi, including in the implicit terbri of a sumti (the ones which are made explicit and then filled by use of BE), as well as in the case about which you are asking; so, yes. Luckily, there is an experimental cmavo, invented by yours truly, which helps with such problems: "de'au" (or its relatives).

I am not sure why you would want to use a sumtcita without filling it explicitly, unless you wanted the default to be there too, but I might just not be sufficiently imaginative. In any case, you have your answer and the work-around/fix (depending on how you favor it). This should be documented in the CLL2 - thanks!

By the way, I do not think that this happens with BAI, even those which are related to/derived from words which have default values programmed into their terbri, unless the BAI word itself explicitly has a default value assigned to it in its definition.

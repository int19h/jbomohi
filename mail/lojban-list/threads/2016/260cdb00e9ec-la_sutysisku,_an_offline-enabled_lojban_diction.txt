http://lojban.github.io/sutysisku/


Latest changes (since October 2015):

   1. To increase stability it is now located at two new addresses:
   http://lojban.github.io/sutysisku/ and https://lojban.herokuapp.com
   2. Now loads faster
   3. Some dictionaries now support search for word combinations:
   http://lojban.github.io/sutysisku/jb/index.html#sisku/lo_na_se_viska
   (not yet available for La Jbovlaste-based dictionaries)
   4. when entering phrases it will provide translations for each of the
   word
   5. lujvo are decomposed (fuhivla rafsi supported)
   6. compound cmavo are decomposed even if written without spaces
   7. Regex search available: use "^" or "$" normal regex special symbols
   to enable it. Can be useful for searching for rhymes, e.g. via "ami$" (will
   result in {prami}, {slami}, {skami} ...)
   8. Sorting results is now smarter (e.g. for equal relevance results put
   cmavo and gismu higher).
   9. You can use "h" instead of " ' " to search for words.
   10. "Back" button to return to the desktop screen
   11. Better interface on mobile devices
   12. Breadcrumbs: by pressing on the "sutysisku" title you return to the
   initial screen and can see your last searched words or word combinations.
   13. Spanish sutysisku added.


Please note that previous addresses of la sutysisku from
https://mw.lojban.org/extensions are no longer guaranteed to be constantly
working due to periodic downtimes of mw.lojban.org. Please update your
bookmarks to http://lojban.github.io/sutysisku/

In short this is just an interface to jbovlaste.lojban.org + some more
databases like tatoeba.org and the crash course dictionary.

I'm not sure what you mean by "orphan entries". You mean WITHIN the same
dictionary like e.g. word {basfa} is not  referenced by any other word in
the English-Lojban dictionary?

Then yes, there are a lot of such words. Not sure how useful it'd be to
have backlinks for every word.

But it'd be nice to have every word to be linked to at least one word so
that we can construct a semantic network for Lojban.
Again this concept can be extended to "define every word in Lojban and
construct a network of how close are every two words in their definitions
based on
1st network. grammatical structure of their definitions (e.g. lo clani = lo
to'e tordu, lo tordu = lo to'e clani)
2nd network. semantical structure of their definitions (e.g. "cat is an
animal", "lion is an animal" so the two words must be somehow close
semantically).

One can add this to their todo lists.

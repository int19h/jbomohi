So there are bad copies of the new version of the CLL in the wild,
for which I apologize.

The only way to get such a copy was to order one from Amazon or
another major bookseller between about 1 March 2016 and 1 May 2016.

You can tell you have the bad version if you have a glossy red cover
that looks like this one:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n816WyH09L4

but if you look in the Glossary, you'll find errors like "NO
JBOVLASTE DEFINITION FOUND!".

If you have such a book, *please* let us know.  The LLG will pay for
you to get a new copy.

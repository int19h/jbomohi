la deivyd rasditc,r cu preti la'edi'u:
>Does  Lojban clarify thinking and improve communication?

That's the $64,000 question.  My opinion:
learning *any* language clarifies thinking and improves communication.
Learning Lojban using the existing materials requires reading and thinking
about a lot of issues in logic and language, and I think you can't help but
learn something about it all along the way.  Whether the language itself,
as learned in a more "natural" way, would have any particular effect, no one
knows.

    One of my greatest problems in writing lojban is that I can rarely
    find the word I want.  ... What I want is a plain text file with
    lots of words in it, many words per line, but where the first word
    on each line is the lojban word I want.

The gismu list at

    ftp://ftp.access.digex.net/pub/access/lojbab/wordlists/gismu

has a line for each gismu; and starts with that gismu.  The file is
365 kbytes.

Here is a sample:

gubni gub          public                                    x1 is
 public/un-hidden/open/jointly available to/owned by all among community x2
 (mass)          8c   7    (cf. sivni)

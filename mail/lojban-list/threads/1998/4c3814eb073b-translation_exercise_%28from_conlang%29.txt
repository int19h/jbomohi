People on ConLang have been translating the following
conversation into their various conlangs.  I may have missed it,
but I haven't seen a Lojban version yet.  I've had a go myself
but all I have to work from is gerna-28 and the word lists.
I've made a simplified version of the gerna but it's still
really difficult to work one's way through the layers of rules.

-- jP --

           for I wish to purchase a train ticket.

           from around here, are you?
Man 1. Why no, sir.  I am from [insert your country here].
Man 2. Well, sir, you speak top notch [insert your Conlang
           here].
Man 1. Why thank you, sir. ...

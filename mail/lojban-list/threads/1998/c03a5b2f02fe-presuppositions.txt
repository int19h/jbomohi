Yes, you are right!  Except for this discussion we want {lo}.

           .i mi te pixra lo finpe zo'e

    x3 (moved) artist-maker                     mi

    x2 (same place) what-is-illustrated         lo finpe

    x1 (moved) is-a-picture                     zo'e

And the x2 refers to that which is illustrated, so it can be
{lo finpe}, a veridical fish or fishes in the current universe
of discourse.

Then, to respond to Lojbab, we are presupposing the fish that the
artist is making a drawing of does exist, it being veridically a fish
in the universe of discourse.

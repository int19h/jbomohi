I don't know that any G.S. people would deny the mathematical axiom
of identity, only the "is of" identity particle of language. Within the
context of mathematics x=x seems merely tautological. We disagree with
the idea that any word "is (identical)" to some existant event in space-
time. Or that my conception of the event (what's behind my use of a
word), "is (identical)" to das ding an sich.

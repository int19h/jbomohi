I need to represent the German "Dampfeinspruehung" (spraying in of
steam) as a Lojban lujvo.

In the gismu.html list I have,
there are some obstacles to this:

(1) pambe doesn't have a rafsi.  I assume pam
(2) febvi doesn't have a rafsi that can be put before 'pam'.  I
    create 'fev'
(3) I am not sure if x1 of febvi is the steam or the liquid that is
    turned into steam when it boils.

So I get fevpam.

Is the epistemology place of {djuno} to be filled=20
by the reason or method by which the knower
came to have the knowledge, or is it for the
theory by which the x2 statement is considered
to be knowledge? I'm assuming it's the latter.
(The former would be obtained with ki'u, ra'i
for example.)

This becomes clear if we consider this selbri:

toldjuno: x1 ignores that x2 is true of x3=20
              by epistemology x4;

Obviously the x4 is not the reason why x1=20
ignores fact x2. It is the epistemology in which
x2  is true.

"Ignore" is like "know" in that it requires that
the x2 be true, but in this case the x1 doesn't
know it.

co'o mi'e xorxes

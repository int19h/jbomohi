la kir cusku di'e
> e.  doi marca. .i me do cu prami
>         ^^^^^^
>               Can cmeme end with vovel?
 
Yes and no. Any selbri can be used as a name, so those kind of names
do end with a vowel. It is not clear what happens with gismu-form names
like {marca} that are not real gismu. Someone once used {la melbi burno}
and {la pitsi burgo} for "Melbourne" and "Pittsburgh", even though
most of those gismu don't exist.
 
On the other hand, {doi marce i mi do prami} is perfectly fine
and it means: "O Vehicle, I love you".

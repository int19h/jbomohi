Yet Another Beginners Question:

    Is {ro da poi broda} equal to {ro lo broda} ?

I belive I know one difference: we can refer to {da} after first example but
can not after second. But is it only difference?

And if yes, the next step is {ro lo broda goi ko'a} - can we use {ko'a} after
this in the same sence as {da} after first example?

Of course the question is not restricted to {ro} quantifier - is it also true
for {su'o}, {no}, {pa} etc... ?

>     Is {ro da poi broda} equal to {ro lo broda} ?

This is one of the major unsettled questions of Lojban: the relationship
between "ro broda", "ro da poi broda", and "ro lo broda".  It is clear that
they have similar meanings, but there are at least two places where they may
differ:

	1) if there are no brodas;

	2) in sentences like "re [lo/da poi] nanmu cu viska re [lo/da poi] gerku"
	where it is not clear whether there are at most 2 dogs or there
	might be 4 distinct dogs.

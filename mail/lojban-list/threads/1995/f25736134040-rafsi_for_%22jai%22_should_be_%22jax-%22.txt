>Is {jai broda} grammatical? Doesn't it have to be followed by a BAI?

yes.  no.

>And shouldn't the substitute for {broda gasnu} ne {jai zei gau zei broda}?

Yes.  {da jai broda} means {tu'a da broda}.  Assuming {jai broda} will mean
{broda gasnu} or {jai gau broda} is probably malrarna, but maybe true in
practice.

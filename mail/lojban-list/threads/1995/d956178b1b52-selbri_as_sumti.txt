.i na go'i

no'i pe'i lu da zu'o ca ro djedi mi denci jisgau kei lenu mi paroi denci
             jisgau li'u xamgu
.ije lo smuni bezo zu'o cmima lo'i smuni bezo nu

.i .e'u ko krefu co tcidu lo smuni bezo zu'o

I don't agree.

I think:
      Ex: x is an activity of my brushing teeth every day composed of
          events of my brushing my teeth once
and   'activities' is subclass of 'events'
 =>   Ex: x is an event of my brushing teeth every day

I suggest you read again the definition of {zu'o}

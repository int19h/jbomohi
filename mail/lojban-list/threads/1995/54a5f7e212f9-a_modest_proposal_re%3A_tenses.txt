Not at all.  {lei re gutci} is a mass of two feet, and I count on a
cooperative audience to think of them aligned one after the other.  It
is equivalent to {le gutci remei}, which is also a mass of two.

I agree that {le gutci be li re} is more precise, but it is also more
awkward, so in general I think {lei re gutci} is best for the everyday
expression.

An intermediate expression would be {le li re gutci}.

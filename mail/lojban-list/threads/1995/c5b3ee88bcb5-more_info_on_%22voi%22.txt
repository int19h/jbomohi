lo nanmu voi ninmu cu klama

A man who-I-describe-as woman goes.

A man, whom I saw as womanish, went.

Not much confusion, as I saw it. {voi} is to {poi} like {le} to {lo}.
I think I saw it mentioned once that there were three more grammar papers
still waiting to be written. One of them is, if my memory serves me,
the one on relative clauses.

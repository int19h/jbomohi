> Is it really so?  The "r" is used vocalically here, and is unambiguously

Terbasna, please, not terbansa.  Anyway, this is an unclear case, because
normally stress does not fall on "y"s or syllabic consonants.  "trston"
does not have a clear stress, although (as Chris Bogart said) the natural
stress would probably be "TRston".

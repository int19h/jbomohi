There are differences between masses and myopic singulars, as I'm
sure you're aware. {ci da stedu loe prenu} is probably false (or at
least conceptually really weird), and {ci da stedu lee prenu} is probably
false too, if we're referring to a person, while {ci da stedu loi prenu}
can be true, if the person mass contains three people.

There are differences between masses and myopic singulars, as I'm sure
you're aware.  {ci da stedu loe prenu} is probably false (or at least
conceptually really weird), and {ci da stedu lee prenu} is probably
false too, if we're referring to a person, while {ci da stedu loi prenu}
can be true, if the person mass contains three people.

> > Since there is no point in using quantifiers with

> Yes. Can you accept {mi nitcu re lo'e tanxe} on the same grounds?
> i.e. "I need two of Mr Box"?

Well, I accept it as much as I accept {re loi tanxe} or {re la xorxes
jambias}.

> (5a) What do VI, ZI, VEHA & ZEHA used as sumtcita mean?

I think there is agreement that {ve'a <sumti>} means "in the medium
interval given by <sumti>", and similarly for ZEhA.

To me, {va <sumti>} means "at a medium distance given by <sumti>",
and similarly for ZI.

In all cases, the sumti is the interval or distance in question.
But that meaning for VI and ZI is not agreed to by all.

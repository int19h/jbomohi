=== 2 | 1995-11-20T17:01:42Z | John Cowan <cowan@ccil.org> | bmaipuuybcg.a.w2b.q60klb@chain.digitalkingdom.org | mail/lojban-list/cur/816886902.7149f55a3259fa12.jbomohi:2,S
> How can you tell whether the {lio} is quoted or not? (Same question
> for {si, sa, su} - how do you quote these?)

The assumption is that "li'o" isn't quoted even inside a quotation.  You
can emphasize this with "li'osa'a", or force it to be quotational with
"li'osa'anai".  To quote "sa'a", say "sa'asa'a".  To deprive si/sa/su
of force within a quotation, the best bet is to use lo'u/le'u quotation.

> An observative is
> a bridi with absent x1 - full stop. For absent x-other-than-1 we
> haven't bothered with special names.

Part of the trouble is that the term "observative" has two senses, one
semantic, one syntactic.  In the first sense, it means "sentence with x1
not expressed."  In the second sense, it means "sentence with no terms
before the selbri".  These often coincide, but not always.

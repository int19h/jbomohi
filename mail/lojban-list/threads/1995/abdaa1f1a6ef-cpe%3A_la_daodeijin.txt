From the Tao Te Ching by Lau-Tze:

  "Jen fa Ti: Ti fa T'sien: T'sien fa Tao: Tao fa tzu-jan"

    In english:
    "Men are created by the earth, earth is created by the universe, and
     the universe is created by Nature's Process, and Nature's Process just
     happened."
     or
     "Man is under the law of the earth, the earth is under the law of the
      universe, the universe in under the law of the Tao, and the Tao is the
      Law itself."
     or
     "Man takes his law from the earth; the earth takes it's law from the
      Universe; the Universe takes it's law from the Tao.  The law of the
      Tao is it's being what it is."

   lo'e remna ku se jitro lo'e terdi javni ku .i le terdi ku se jitro lo'e
   munje javni ku .i le munje ku se jitro lo'e la daos javni ku .i la daos
   se jitro la daos

      {la daos.} can optionally be replaced by {lo rarna pruce ku}

      "Things-which-are-human are-controlled-by earth-rules;
       The earth is-controlled-by universe-rules;
       The universe is-controlled-by [(that-called)Tao]-rules;
       (that-called)Tao is-controlled-by (that-called)Tao."

Here's another possibility:

        lo'e remna cu seltru le terdi
        i le terdi cu seltru le munje
        i le munje cu seltru la daos
        i la daos. cu seltru ri

        Humans are ruled by the earth.
        The earth is ruled by the universe.
        The universe is ruled by the Tao.
        The Tao is ruled by itself.

I prefer using {turni} rather than {jitro} because it doesn't have that
distracting x3 place. It still doesn't achieve the compactness of the
Chinese phrase, though.

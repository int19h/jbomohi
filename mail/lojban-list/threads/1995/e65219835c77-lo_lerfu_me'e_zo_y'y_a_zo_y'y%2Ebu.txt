I am, but not in the design of grammars. There are bits of baroquery
in Lojban that I like, e.g. delimiters for {zoi} and {lao}, but the
charm of these is that they represent the best solution to the
problem they solve, and are baroque through being uncompromising.
In contrast, Lojban morphology is rather like the Soviet economy:
the outcome of numerous rational but bad decisions.

I said:
>         le ci nanmu cu bevri pa tanxe goi ko'a
>         i ko'a blanu
>
> Expands to:
>
>         i ci da voi nanmu pa de poi tanxe zi'e goi ko'a zo'u: da bevri de
>         i ro da poi du su'oko'a zo'u: da blanu

The first expansion is wrong for two reasons. It should be

     i ro da voi nanmu ku'o pa de poi tanxe zi'e goi ko'a zo'u: da bevri de

The {ku'o} is needed so that {pa de} doesn't fall in the x2 of nanmu,
and it has to be {ro da} and not {ci da}.  The {ci} is implicit in the
{voi}, it can't replace the {ro} because it would change the meaning.

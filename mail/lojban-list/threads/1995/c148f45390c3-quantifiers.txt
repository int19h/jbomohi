They're inside a {ka}, which is where {ke'a} would be used as a
"lambda".

I intended {le ka xokau ke'a cu nenri le kumfa} to mean "the property of
how many of them are in the room".  "Them" plays the role of the
variable in English, the "how many" part is the usual indirect question.

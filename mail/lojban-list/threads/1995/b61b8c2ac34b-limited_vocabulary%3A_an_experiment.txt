Here's the list.  Many of these names wouldn't be suitable for Lojban 
compounds, since they're mostly based on English, and some of them are poorly 
defined in English.  Also, they're influenced by the words available in Basic 
English, and I probably would have made different choices based on Lojban 
vocabulary.  (Umbrellabird is a good example; I only used it because it's an 
English name that translates directly into Basic without any changes.)  But 
you're welcome to use any of these that fit the Lojban guidelines.

Historically JOI entered the language as a tanru connective.  A black-and-red
beach ball is neithr black nor red because it is equally both.  Thus JCB used
 "ze" which we made "joi".

nanmu jo'u ninmu jgina
presumably talks about all the non-Y chromosome genes.

This is fine, but I would explicitly associate the scale with "zmadu" using

    .i le vi rozgu cu zmadu le va tujli le jei melbi kei li pivo
        ci'u le li'i dirba mi

which says that the more-ness of the beauty is on the scale of experience of
emotional value to me.  The restrictive clause is joined to the number, not
the whole relation.

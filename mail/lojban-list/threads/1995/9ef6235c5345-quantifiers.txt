That's how I would interpret it. What is the usual sense of fatci?
Doesn't {le du'u le cukta cu cpana le jubme cu fatci} mean that
that the book is on the table is a matter of fact, not open to
dispute? In what other sense could it be a fact? The sentence
with {le cukta} implies the one with {makau}, which says the
same but without mentioning what's on the table, just as in the
case of {facki}.

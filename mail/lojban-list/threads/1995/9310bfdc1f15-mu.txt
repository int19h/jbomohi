> Except to say that _piro loi pavyseljirna_ probably has no

Aha!  To disprove ".i piro loi pavyseljirna cu blanu", one must needs to
come up with a counterexample - where is this unicorn which is not blue? ;-)

There is an inherent problem with proving assertions (hypotheses?) of this
nature.  The Medieval example was "All swans are white".  The scholar would
'prove' this by reasoning that since no one had ever seen a swan that was not
white, it was true that all swans are white.  Many years pass.  Australia
is discovered, along with the orange beaked, black swans native to that
continent.  The counter-example annihilates the assertion.

The presupposition that I imagine goes with _piro loi pavyseljirna_ is that
there is at one least one unicorn.

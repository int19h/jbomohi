.i pe'i di'e se fanva fuzoi gy. You! Tell John that it is you! whom I

.i glifanva fuzoi gy. I think this translates to: "You! Tell John that
it is you! whom I command to meet Mary at the park", i.e. "Meet Mary at
the park! Tell John that you'll do so!" I thought that only DOI and COI
selma'o can change the value of {do}. I think it's necessary to quote to
avoid the premature evaluation of {ko}. I suggest {ko cusku fi la djan.
fe lu ko penmi la maris. le panka}. Besides, neither English nor my
language use two imperatives together.

{kau} is not a problem. {ko} is.

Huh? What has Russian got to do with this? .gy.

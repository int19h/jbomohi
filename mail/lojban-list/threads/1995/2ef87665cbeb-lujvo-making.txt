I think I understand now. My confusion came from a misunderstanding of
the stress rule from the beginning. It should be emphasized that not
only must the penultimate syllable be stressed, but no other syllable
may be stressed, at least in some cases. This is not a problem for two
or three syllable words, but for longer ones it can be difficult in
practice. Fortunatley, very long lujvo are rare.

different but whose spelling is the same:


   - do vecnu le ciska mi (You sell the scribe to me)

(CLL 4.1: "A stream of Lojban sounds can be uniquely broken up into its 
component words according to specific rules")

Q: Are these correctly formed bridi?

Q: Is the thing I thought I loved about Lojban most -- unambiguous

Finally published! Mozilla Firefox and Thunderbird now have basic Lojban 
spell checking on their *official* website. Hope you'll appreciate!

Feel free to test, criticize, and propose enhancements.
For now the main issue I see is that the spell check engine does not 
recognize ".i" as a whole, and thus always underlines "i" alone (as it is 
noted ".i" in the dictionary).

Firefox addon: https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/lojban-spell-check/
Thunderbird addon: 
https://addons.thunderbird.net/fr/thunderbird/addon/lojban-spell-check
GitHubs, if someone wants to participate: 
https://github.com/Sukender/lojban-spell-check-dist and 
https://github.com/Sukender/lojban-spell-check . Any improvement is welcome!

If you have knowledge about how to open spell checking for other software, 
please share!

Hi everyone,

I've just read about LFK creation. It is said to be "a more open model 
accessible to all interested persons". This makes me think about something 
I discussed long ago, and maybe it is time to re-check if the idea is worth 
it or not.
Nowadays, some "open organizations" solutions do exist. Their goal is to 
provide organizations with tools to avoid the need of trusting people 
BEFORE everything. Roughly speaking, those tools use blockchains to avoid 
fraud, handle reputation, etc.

One of them (*) is https://colony.io . To my very-personal point of view, 
the currently-beta version is a "bounty hunter" board (tasks with rewards). 
Later on, a reputation system will be implemented, according to developers. 
Maybe it would be interesting testing things with a few motivated people? 
We could try with:
1. Basic tasks: translating a thing, writing an article, ...
2. A completely fake reward with no actual value (say a "JBO-Test" token).
3. No "true" administrators (this would only be for testing purposes, so 
why matter?).

Who would like to try? I can create and setup the dummy/test organization.

(*) I'm not advertising, just giving partial info! ;-) Another one is 
https://aragon.org , but I don't know much of it.

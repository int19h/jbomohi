The full sentence is
{se pìnfu ro la màxpre ze'a lo nànca be li so'i fau lo nu la màxpre cu
sèlfu ze'a lo nìcte .e lo dònri}.

Isn't it intended to mean {ze'a lo nicte jo'u lo donri}? From what I
understand, {ze'a} is used to indicate the full duration of the event. In
http://www.lojban.org/tiki/BPFK+Section:+Distance
the proposed tag is {fi'o krafamtei}. This is as non-distributive as it can
get.

Another possibility, with weaker implications is {ca lo nicte .e lo donri}.

What do you think?

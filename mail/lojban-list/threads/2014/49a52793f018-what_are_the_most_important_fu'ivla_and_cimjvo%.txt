We know that gimste represents more or less most basic concepts.
But what about fu'ivla and cimjvo?
Although, we have a frequency dictionary what are in your opinion the most
important of them?

Wu Pan, E.Sklyanin and Jonathan Derque made an excellent work translating
gimste to their native languages (only the people that came to mind just
now). I want to extend the template "vlaste for translation" with fu'ivla
and cimjvo as well.

Here are some of my suggestions. You continue!
stomaxu x1 is a/the stomach/digestive organ of x2
aftobuso $x_1$ is a bus/coach for carrying passengers $x_2$, propelled by
$x_3$
alna $x_1$ is an alder (Alnus) of species $x_2$
asna $x_1$ is a body position/posture/stance/asana of body $x_2$
characterized by property/(set of) properties $x_3$ (ka).
astrato $x_1$ is a grammatical abstraction of type $x_2$
balnema $x_1$ is a baleen whale of species $x_2$
blondino $x_1$ is blond, fair-haired
biorka $x_1$ is a birch of species $x_2$
zigzagi $z_1$ is a line with the shape of a zigzag.
pelmeni $x_1$ is a piece/pieces of dumplings/ravioli/pelmeni/dim sum
(Hongkong/Xianggang cuisine) containing $x_2$
skientia $x_1$ (knowledge) is science about subject matter $x_2$ based on
the Scientific Method $x_3$
pincivi $x_1$ (body) excretes urine/urinates/pees/pisses $x_2$
omle $x_1$ is an omelette containing $x_2$
monsuta $x_1$ is a monster
krokodilo $x_1$ is a crocodile (family Crocodylidae) of breed $x_2$
korpora $x_1$ is a corpus of/a large collection of texts $x_2$.
irci $x_1$ is an IRC user on channel $x_2$ in network $x_3$.
gredile $x_1$ is a grill/grid/graticule consisting of lines $x_2$
enge $x_1$ is an architect/designer/engineer of $x_2$
delfinu $x_1$ is a dolphin of species/breed $x_2$.

*Now you continue!*

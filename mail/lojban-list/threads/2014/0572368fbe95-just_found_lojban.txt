fi'i do/Welcome! Besides the Book, here are some resources on the Internet you 
can use:

*lojban-beginners@googlegroups.com. Subscribe the same way you did to lojban@.

*irc://chat.freenode.net/#lojban and #ckule. ("ckule" means "school"; the 
channel is for learners.)

*http://vlasisku.lojban.org/. Type in any English or Lojban word and get its 
definition, words that have it in their definitions, etc.

*http://jbovlaste.lojban.org/. Once you know Lojban well enough to contribute 
vocabulary, this is where you enter them.

*http://camxes.lojban.org/camxes/. An implementation of the Parsing Expression 
Grammar for Lojban. ("camxes" is a portmanteau of the Lojban names of two 
prominent Lojbanists.)

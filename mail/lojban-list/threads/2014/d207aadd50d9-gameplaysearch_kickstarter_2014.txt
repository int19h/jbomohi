Hello,

We'd like to inform the community about a Kickstarter that may be of 
interest.

GameplaySearch is a new kind of search engine for video games.  It uses a 
query language to help users find gameplay situations.

This query language parses a subset of English, but can easily be extended 
to parse full-blown Lojban, which is why we call your attention.

----

It is our hope that the Lojban community sees that development of 
GameplaySearch will lead to a new and significant way in which the language 
can be used in the context of the broader world community; many more folks 
in the world will have more chance at exposure to this language via 
technology like this.

Please check out the web site and Kickstarter page, learn more, and tell 
your friends.  Consider pledging if you think this is good technology and 
want to show support.

Thank you.


https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/968302478/gameplaysearch-the-search-engine-for-gameplay

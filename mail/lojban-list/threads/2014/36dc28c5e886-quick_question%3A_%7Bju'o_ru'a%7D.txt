My take is that when she says `I must be shutting up like a telescope.' she
is making a hypothesis as opposed to stating it as a fact (hence "ru'a")
and also expressing a high degree of certainty that the hypothesis is
correct ("ju'o"). It may well be that her certainty comes from experience
or direct observation, and we may infer that from the situation, but I
don't think she is actually expressing that. This "ru'a ju'o" would work
just as well for other such uses of "must" that don't involve direct
experience, as in "that must be your brother" or "she must be home by now".

mu'o mi'e xorxes

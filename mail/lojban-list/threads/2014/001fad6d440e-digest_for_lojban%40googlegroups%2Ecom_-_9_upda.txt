Wait a minute.... At the end of the included post Jorge makes a statement I
find very wrong. He says he doesn't see a problem using kavbu for the
phrase "caught a cold." I do not believe this is the concept behind kavbu,
and I find it horribly malglico. You can certainly catch things
unintentionally, like a bug in your mouth when it flys in. The problem I
have is that a cold is not something you catch in concept, but rather
something that invades or attacks you. "Catch a cold" is an idiom.

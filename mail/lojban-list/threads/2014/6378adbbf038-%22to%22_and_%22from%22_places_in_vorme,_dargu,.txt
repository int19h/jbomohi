cripu: x1 is a bridge/span over/across x2 between x3 and x4 [unordered, 
typically destination first].
dargu: x1 is a road/highway to x2 from x3 with route x4 (x2/x3 may be 
unordered).
vorme: x1 is a doorway/gateway/access way between x2 and x3 of structure x4.

I would interpret x2 and x3 same for all of these (as places/points/rooms 
connected by the structure).

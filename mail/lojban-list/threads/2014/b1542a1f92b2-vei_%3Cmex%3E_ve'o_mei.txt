> "lo drata cu pasomei .ijo lo se drata cu vei reno vu'u pa ve'o mei" doesn't
> parse in camxes or jbofi'e. What's wrong with it?

The numeric selbri only take pure numbers and/or letterals directly, but 
no MEX. You can still use MEX with MOI by using {me li MEX me'u MOI}. 
This is due to a parser restriction.

    lo se drata cu me li reno vu'u pa me'u mei
    "The others are 20-1."

Note that in some newer (unofficial) PEG parsers you can just say {reno 
vu'u pa mei} directly.

The researchers from the University of Laguna Verde are reporting that they
have made a new breakthrough in deciphering The Voynich Manuscript.

The study pointed out the fact that the language of the manuscript couldn't
be a normal human language due to the clearly seen low entropy of the text.

The article hasn't been published yet but the authors kindly provided me
with the draft of their paper that very soon will be published in Laguna
Verde University proceedings.

http://mw.lojban.org/files/docs/voynich.pdf

This might sound strange but they came to conclusion that the manuscript
was written in a variation of the logical language Lojban.

Several words from the manuscript have been translated after a laborious
digitalizing of the manuscript and careful analysis of the pictures,
history of Mediaeval Italy and advances in modern logical science.

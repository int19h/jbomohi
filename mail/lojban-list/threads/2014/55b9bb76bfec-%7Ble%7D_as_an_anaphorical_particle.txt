Forgot to add about {ka'e}. Well, yes. Probably {ca'a/pu'i} might be more
relevant here. I meant {ka'e} to refer to semantic inclusion of SELBRI in
{le SELBRI} into another selbri in the context or in the current/previous
text.
Consider

{la djan cu cusku zo coi la meris .i le bacru cu gleki}

Would {bacru} refer to {cusku}?

Clearly, those are edge cases since the most common usage of {le} would be
{le fetsi} for "she" and similar.

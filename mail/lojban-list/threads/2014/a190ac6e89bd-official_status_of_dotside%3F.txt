I think it isn't official, but I would hardly agree to tag such things as
disputed.

Firstly, the extra dot is valid Official Lojban, so there is really no
argument in this particular case. Secondly, marking every deviation from
the official language "disputed" will lead to a proliferation of such tags
in the Lojban Tatoeba corpus, and that is a terrible promotion of the
language. I think it is better to just add a new translation under the
convention you prefer, and possibly comment it.

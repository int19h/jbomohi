I've just noticed that new soi "always takes top scope". Why is that? Not
knowing the discussion that led to that choice, I incline to the view that
in a reformed Lojban there would be a new syntax that syntacticizes the
left-to-right scope rule (so that clause structure is binary branching, as
in Xorban), in which case you'd expect soi to scope over what it precedes.
Most if not all tags (e.g. tense) could be seen as abbreviations of soi
phrases, and hence you'd expect soi phrases to have the scopal properties
of tags.

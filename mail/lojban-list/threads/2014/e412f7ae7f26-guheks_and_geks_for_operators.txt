as was just discovered in the chat:

http://dag.github.io/cll/14/17/
----
The basic components of mekso are operands, like “2”, and operators, like
“+”.
...
Operands are connected in afterthought with eks and in forethought with
geks, just like sumti. Operators, on the other hand, are connected in
afterthought with jeks and in forethought with guheks, just like tanru
components. (However, jeks and joiks with “bo” are not allowed for
operators.) This parallelism is no accident.
...
17.4)  li re ge su'i gi pi'i re du li vo
       The-number two both plus and times two equals the-number four.
       Both 2 + 2 = 4 and 2 x 2 = 4.
----

Example 17.4) contradicts what is stated before. It is not parsed neither
by http://www.lojban.org/jboski
nor by http://camxes.lojban.org

However, if we change it to
17.4)  li re gu'e su'i gi pi'i re du li vo [boi]

then it would parse (if we add {boi} for jboski)

Is it an error in CLL? Has it been already described?

The full passage is:
  {lo nàrge cu tai cmàlu .i ja'e bo ny poi cy ke'a fàlcru cu ja'a ru'e se
zmàmei ny poi cy ke'a pùnji lo lànka}

Here, {ny poi cy ke'a falcru}, clearly means something equivalent to either
{lo narge poi cy ke'a falcru} or to what I would write as {lo me ny poi cy
ke'a falcru}. To me, an anaphoric expression always has exactly the same
referent as the antecedent. What do you think?

doi la selpa'i, what is your idea behind this usage? Do you think the
anaphora just occurs at the superficial textual level, so that {ny} would
be equivalent to {lo narge}? Or do you think that {ko'a poi broda} is
meaningful in general, so that {ko'a} can in effect refer to a strict part
of ko'a, i.e, as in {lo me ko'a}?

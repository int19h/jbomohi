> 1.  What is the largest book size we can get in hardcover? 
>

For "case laminate" binding, up to 8" x 11.5" trim. The cover can be 
printed in 4 colors, with a matte or glossy finish. I found an example here 
<http://paperbackrocker.com/tag/lightning-source>.

If you go 6.14 x 9.21" ("Royal octavo') or smaller -- the current PDF seems 
to be that size -- you also have a choice of cloth binding, blue or grey, 
with or without dust jacket. I found an example here 
<http://www.novelpublicity.com/2012/02/how-to-produce-a-hardcover-version-of-your-novel-via-lightning-source-and-why-youll-want-to-have-one/>
.
 

>   1 (a).  What the hell do all those cover options mean and which 
>   one should we pick for the CLL second edition? 
>

 I found a discussion 
<http://www.spannet.org/group/bookproduction/forum/topics/case-laminate-binding> 
which refers to case laminate as "a trademark of academic bindings," but 
also, refers to such books as a "perfect bound book masquerading as hard 
cover." I assume clothbound is more expensive. Do people have strong 
feelings about this one way or the other? Is a dust jacket something that 
we'd want to consider?
 

> 2.  What do the dimension requirements of that size mean in terms of 
> my PDF? 
>

It looks like the PDF is configured to print at a size which provides the 
most binding options.
 

> 3.  Does my PDF actually match all their requirements? 
>

The margins seem to conform to the 0.5" (13mm) requirement. The font size 
seems adequate -- even generous -- to me, but it would be good to have more 
eyes on it. At 951 pages, it seems like there's room to enlarge the font 
before reaching the maximum page size of 1200. I assume a smaller book 
would be less expensive, if less impressive.

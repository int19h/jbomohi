Evolving a 2D Language
http://dscript.org/note.pdf

So Dscript allows strings of letters various "degrees of freedom" in 2D. 
Now I have finally organized the first run at entering the realm of 2D 
language

There are tons of existing notations to allow 2D information to be 
represented.

To do this I have decided that instead of trying to build a lexicon 
methodology from scratch, I will instead cannibalize these standards. 
Trying to assimilate and unify them into a more all inclusive method based 
on Dscript.

It has a ways to go yet, but this "second foundation" is starting to 
solidify and going better than I even hoped.

Any thoughts? having a ton of fun so far myself

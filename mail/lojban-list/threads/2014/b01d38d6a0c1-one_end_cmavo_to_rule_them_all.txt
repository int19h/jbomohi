"We can't close any open clauses until we've closed that most recent one".
This only sort of true. If there are a stack of open clauses, we can close
several at a time by closing one in the middle of the stack, which forces
all the ones more recent than that to close. If there was only one word for
closing, you would have to repeat it a lot, and people aren't so good at
numerically counting levels.

-- Ross

The formal grammar is not for nintadni, of course. Is for establishing
without doubt what is grammatical and what is not.

PEG is indeed simple to read. It is more difficult to create one as there
might be "side effects" that are not apparent at first sight (i.e. indirect
left recursion or undetected "prefix").

I think that whatever changes is proposed to the language, the first step
is to evaluate the impact on the formal grammar. Especially for any
experimental cmavo.

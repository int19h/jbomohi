Hi, everyone! In short, I'm going to organizing a Lojban language meetup on 
29th August. Looking forward to some logbanists living near Hong Kong to 
join us! Thanks for Open Innovation Lab for hosting the event.

   - Date/Time: Friday, August 29 at 8:00pm to 9:00pm
   - Location: Room 831, Ho Sing-Hang engineering building, The Chinese 
   University of Hong Kong, Hong Kong
   - Host: Open Innovation Lab
   - Facebook Group: https://www.facebook.com/groups/lojbanhk/
   
Yesterday I was invited to join a group chat about open-tech. None of them 
knew about Lojban. However, I wanted to do something surprising there so I 
said "coi rodo" to others. To my surprise, then someone replied "coi" to me 
and introduced themselves by "mi'e <name>". Later I sent them the Lojban 
IRC cheat sheet and we started some basic conversation in Lojban. This was 
mind-blowing and it turned out that adopt someone to learn Lojban is not as 
difficult as I thought. Therefore, I decided to start a Lojban language 
meetup with that group of people.

I know that Lojban community is great, and it would be great if you can 
offer some help to this meetup:

   - If you are near Hong Kong on 29th August, whether you are a beginner 
   or an expert of Lojban, please come and join!
   - Did anyone organized Lojban meetups before? It would be great if you 
   can share some experience!
   - What is the best way to get someone interested in Lojban? Any good 
   examples?
   - How do I say if I want to relate Lojban with open technology? (Since 
   the host Open Innovation Lab is related to the topic about open technology)

Looking forward to your replies!

Brendan

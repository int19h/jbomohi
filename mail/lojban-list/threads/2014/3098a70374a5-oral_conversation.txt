{ba'a nai ju'o ru'e} Robin speaks to his two daughters at least partially
in lojban, and they speak back.

As for clarity of vowels, I try to think of the vowel pronunciations in
*any* language other than English, which I feel has the worst examples for
vowel pronunciation if you're looking to speak lojban. I think Italian,
Spanish, or Japanese vowel pronunciation is the best for me to model after.

One of the other things I know I need to pay attention to (as an English
speaker) is *not* accidentally saying {ou} when I should be saying {o} in
lojban at ends of words.

> Does any one have *oral* conversations in Lojban?  More than a few sentence?

Yes, several of us have had longer conversations on Skype or Mumble. 
It's just not a regular thing (time zones are one big problem, another 
one is laziness, and perhaps insecurity plays a role, too).

As for mishearing words in noisy channels, it doesn't seem to happen any 
more than it does in English. And when you do fail to hear a word, you 
can ask for the other person to repeat.

If you are looking for someone to practice oral communication with, I 
recommend you come on IRC and let the people there know.

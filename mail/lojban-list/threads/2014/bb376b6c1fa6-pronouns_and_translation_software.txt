I'm curious about what translation software is recommended to do with 
pronouns that do not have context:

[She] gave it to [her].

[ri] and [ko'a] have nothing to point to
and
[zo'e] seems too vague

Out of context the sentence means: [a female] gave it to [another female].
Would [lo ninmu] be appropriate? 
How can we indicate [another female] or is it implied in {lo ninmu cu pu 
dunda zo'e lo ninmu}?

I would translate that as {ko'a fi ko'e pu dunda}, "They gave it to them."
in a slangish way. If I wanted to get the full meaning across formally, I
might try {lo ninmu pu dunda fi lo drata ninmu}, "The woman gave (it) to
the other woman.".

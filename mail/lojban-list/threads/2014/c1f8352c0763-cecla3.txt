I think this usage of cecla3 is wrong.

cecla = x1 launches/fires/shoots projectile/missile x2, propelled by x3 
[propellant/propulsion].
Also: x1 is a gun/launcher/cannon; x1 hurls/throws/casts (more general than 
renro in that propulsion need not be internal to x1). See also renro, 
danti, jakne, jbama, spoja.

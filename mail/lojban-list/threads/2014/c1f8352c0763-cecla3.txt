I think this usage of cecla3 is wrong.

cecla = x1 launches/fires/shoots projectile/missile x2, propelled by x3 
[propellant/propulsion].
Also: x1 is a gun/launcher/cannon; x1 hurls/throws/casts (more general than 
renro in that propulsion need not be internal to x1). See also renro, 
danti, jakne, jbama, spoja.

On Sunday, October 19, 2014 15:46:23 Gleki Arxokuna wrote:
> it is used as if it had the place structure or renro

But it doesn't. As far as I can tell, there are three differences between 
"cecla" and "renro":
*"renro" implies that the means of propulsion is internal to x1, "cecla" 
doesn't.
*"cecla" implies that x2 is detached, "renro" doesn't (see "sezre'o").
*x3 of "cecla" is the means of propulsion, x3 of "renro" is the direction.
Would "bikre'o" work?

a bit of a chore trying NOT to make them. E.g. a word for "the holidays"

holidays celebrated, but only moderately: CNICI CESYCITSI DJICA...

render "lifelong acknowledgement"...

I think i'm proudest of a word that is as revolting to say as
to think: KALCYJOLZGU...

contest?

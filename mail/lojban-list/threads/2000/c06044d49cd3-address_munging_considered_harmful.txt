Why does the Lojban list cause everyone to reply direclty to the
author by default, rather than to the list by default?

1) It always has.

2) It's easy to substitute a single address (lojban@onelist.com);
the other way around, one must find the author's address and
paste it into the "To:" field.

3) For a full explanation, see http://www.unicom.com/pw/reply-to-harmful.html

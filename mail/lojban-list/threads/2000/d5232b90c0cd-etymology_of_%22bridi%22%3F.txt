I wrote a shell script (it's not yet ready for public consumption) which, given
a gismu, looks it up in lojban.voc and outputs the corresponding line of the

citr
kartin

o
broda

a20a

1

Russian? What is "broda" doing in here?

process the original file, which is "finprims" in http://www.lojban.org, 
etymology directory.  In that case, bridi and broda->brodu all share the

lines after a bridi entry is the etymology and that was not the case for 
this one word.

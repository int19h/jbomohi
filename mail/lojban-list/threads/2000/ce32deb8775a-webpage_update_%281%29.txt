>FILES WE NEED--
>Book errata (are there 3 now?).

Until and unless Cowan acknowledges them, I recognize no book 
errata.  There remain officially no changes in the book-described language.

(I have no idea what the three you are referring to are, in any event).

>Rafsi conversion table (Middle to Late Lojban). (--i started
>to do this a year ago, but i got bogged down._

I have a program that converts lujvo, so I never use a table.  I can 
consider putting the program on line.

>Loglan-Lojban gismu & cmavo equivalents (& vice versa).

Dependent on TLI, which threatened us with a lawsuit if we published such a 
list.  Nonetheless, the file is available to people who ask me.

>Ziryroi (as published, with notes to be added later).

You type it in.  All I have are the several hard copies you sent.

As it is, other than the list archives (which has nearly all such text), I 
will not have much Lojban text up from after 1994, because that was the 
last date through which I have systematically processed the list 
traffic.  If someone else wishes to comb the archives for the best of 
Lojban texts, that is fine by me.

>Possible list of gismu &/or cmavo debated on Jboste (with or
>without consensus-results).

Not sure what you want to see.  But this and ...

>Contributions toward a Lojbanic Culture (so far, this consists
>mostly of Ckafybarjalia, but my Tcati & Menynundamba essaylets
>also).

and ...

>List of Things-we-Need-A-Word-For (much material already on
>the List), & attempts at coining lujvo for them.

are inherently covered by putting the up to date list archives on the 
site.  Editing items from those archives into something useful and coherent 
will either be left to you guys, or I will tackle it whenever I turn to 
creating a new JL issue.

None of the above is saying that we won't put any of that stuff up, but 
none of these things is available to me now to put up, and I am trying to 
minimize the amount of actual work that I have to do.  (It already is 
taking much longer than I expected, since the last two weeks I've been 
dealing with a couple major snowstorms, and a series of sewer and toilet 
backups, one of which did significant water damage to my household.)

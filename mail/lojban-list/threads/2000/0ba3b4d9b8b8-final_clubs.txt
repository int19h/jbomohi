Not just "some configurations", practically all configurations.

If preclusion is taken as symmetrical, the situation is even
less interesting. In that case, for final clubs to be well
defined, membership in any club must preclude membership in
every other club. And then I must go back to my original
solution:

       Definition: Every club is a final club.

To disprove it all you have to do is find a configuration
where some clubs are not final and yet final clubs are
well defined. I don't think there is one.

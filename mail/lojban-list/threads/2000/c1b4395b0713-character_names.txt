It is important to note that these names are not for the characters per se 
but for them in some particular function.  That is pretty much what the 
description of {bu} suggests, but it can lead to minor confusions when the 
same character is used in different functions: slash for division and 
subaddressing, say,  and when the same function is ma5rked by different 
characters -- in different programming languages, for example.  It might also 
be sueful to have use-independent names for the characters, for discussion of 
their various uses.

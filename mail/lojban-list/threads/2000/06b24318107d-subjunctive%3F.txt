To CDW missing the subjunctive, it is not so much that lojbanists have gone 
off on other tangents (though we have and always do) it is that subjunctives 
(in this case mainly contrary-to-fact) turn out to be, in Lojban, not much to 
do with either "if" or things that look like tenses, and if you have your eye 
on those factors, the discussion of how to deal with English subjunctives may 
well have slid by.

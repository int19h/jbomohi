The x3 of {tolcri} is of course the place where the thing
is found:

    mi tolcri lo sfani le stasu
    "I found a fly in the soup."

{facki} is not strictly for finding things, but finding
out about things:

    mi facki le du'u lo sfani cu nenri le stasu kei le stasu
    "I found out about the soup that there was a fly in it."

The place structure of {facki} is very inconvenient for the
basic meaning of "find".

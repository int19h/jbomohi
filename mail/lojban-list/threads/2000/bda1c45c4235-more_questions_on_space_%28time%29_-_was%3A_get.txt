{pamoi} is a selbri, and so is {pu fanva}. You can't have
two separate selbri for the same bridi. You can use
{pamai} instead of {pamoi} there.


>One more question on {jai}:
>
>1) le mlatu ca pavbu loi ratcu vi le foldi
>2) le foldi cu jai vi ca pavbu loi ratcu fai le mlatu
>3) le jai vi be ca pavbu loi ratcu (fai le mlatu) cu  foldi

That one should be:

  le jai vi ca kavbu be loi ratcu (bei fai le mlatu) cu foldi

>4) le jai vi be *le mlatu* ca pavbu loi ratcu cu  foldi (???)
>
>Is 4) correct?

I don't think so. {jai vi} works more or less like {se},
it can't be followed by {be} or by a sumti.

>I doubt whether it's 2nd place can contain {le mlatu}
>in this order. Why then at all use the place conversion tag
>{jai}?

{jai vi kavbu} is a selbri with the same places of
{kavbu}, except that the first place is replaced by a
location and the usual x1 place is now in the fai-place.

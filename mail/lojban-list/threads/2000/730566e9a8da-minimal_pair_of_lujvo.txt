Here is a pair of lujvo that speakers of many languages will confuse:

cunpoi     random sequence (cunso porsi)
cumpoi     possible sequence (cumki porsi)

Some languages do not have "np" and if a morpheme ending in "n" precedes one
beginning with "p" the result is "mp". Similarly "md" becomes "nd". Should "md"
and "np" be forbidden consonant clusters which require "y" when forming lujvo?

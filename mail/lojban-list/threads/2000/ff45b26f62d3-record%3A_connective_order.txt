>Are there things like

The only thing that occurs to me that could make a difference
is {bo}. Maybe A abo B C e D does expand the other way?
It's still dubious though, because {bo} already has another
function, and it will conflict in more complex cases.

>Does the difference between sumti connective and
>various predicate connective make a difference?)

Obviously selbri connectives should expand before trailing
sumti, but I don't know what happens with the sumti in front
of the selbri. They probably should expand first. This may
cause conflict with negation though, let's see:

    ko'a e ko'e na broda gi'a brode

Is that:

    ko'a na broda gi'a brode
    ija ko'a na broda gi'a brode

where {e} went to {ija} because of the negation, or is it
something else?

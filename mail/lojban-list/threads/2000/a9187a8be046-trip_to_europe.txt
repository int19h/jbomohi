I'm leaving for Europe on Thursday, where I will be speaking on free software.

On Saturday and Sunday, I am speaking at  2. Braunschweiger Linux-Tage

  http://Braunschweiger.LinuxTage.de

The first talk is introductory -- how the GNU General Public License,
a legal tool, shaped the technology of software; the second is
somewhat more complex, about access and empowerment.

Then, Monday - Friday, I am giving the keynote speech at each of that
week's one day Linux@work conferences.  

  http://www.ltt.de/linux.html

This is one of those `one-day per country' jaunts: Amsterdam, Paris,
London, Frankfurt, Zurich.  I'll be giving a more or less similar
talk in each city, about what freedom with regard to software brings
to the economy.

The next Sunday, I am talking about ethics and free software at the
Technical University of Clausthal, 

  http://www.esg.tu-clausthal.de/

and on the following Tuesday, I speak on `The World of Free Software'
in Stockholm.

  http://www.iir.se/

While I am, in one way, looking forward to this trip, in another way,
I am not: there is not enough time for me to enjoy Europe or visit.
But if any of you are interested in any of the events, please come by.

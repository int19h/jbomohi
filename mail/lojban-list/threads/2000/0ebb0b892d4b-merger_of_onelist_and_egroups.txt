As many of you may know, onelist and egroups have merged.  There is both
a lojban@onelist.com and a lojban@egroups.com, but they are carrying the
same traffic.  More people are subscribing to onelist (174)
than egroups (5), so I have requested that the two be merged as
onelist, at least for the present.  Since the merged company is
named egroups,  I suspect we will eventually be lojban@egroups.com.

I have no idea which Web software will survive, though.  I will
keep you posted on whatever I learn.

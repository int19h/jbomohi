Well, for one thing, the 'll' definitely doesn't work in Lojban. You'd need to
separate them with a y.

Putting this into jvocuhadju (jbofihe's lujvo-generating program) gives the
result of 'jilylivla', but that's because ordinary lujvo can't end in a vowel,
so presumably 'jilylil' would be the acceptable form for a name. Of course,
that still has the annoying l's.

How about putting dikca (electric) after jilka? That'd give you 'jildic'.

Two consecutive same consonants are "illegal" in Lojban orthography. You can
fix these problems by adding a "verbal hyphen" -- y.

jillil --> jilylil

But you might want to go for a three-part cmene which avoids the y --

jildiclil

jilka - alkali/base
dikca - electrical
livla - energy source

This would also give a possible lujvo for an alkaline battery --
jildiclivla. (I'd personally prefer "jilka cmadiclivla" -- alkaline
small-electrical-energy source).

Well, it's not clear that {na nei} *is* a sentence, since the {nei} has
no referent to repeat; I don't think it is intended to be self-
referential.  On the other hand, as you would expect in a logical
language, double negations do cancel out rather than reinforce (I
am not clear where the double negation comes in here, but that
goes pack to point one.)

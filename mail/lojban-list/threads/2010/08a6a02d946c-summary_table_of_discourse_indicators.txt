Nice, since these buggers give me more trouble than just about anything else.  
BUT  There is little (no?) indication of the various semantic and pragmatic 
ro9les that these indicators play.  Some of them are just passing comments on 
the attached sentences, some of them change the role of those sentences (move 
them off the purely factual into the directive or performative or subjunctive 
role), some just mark places, some give warnings about reliability, and so on.  
These, perhaps more than  the putative meanings of the expressions, are what we 
need to know about them.  Admittedly, there is still some controversy about the 
exact role of some of these, but most could be pretty neatly put to rest, I 
hope.  Not that I want to be the one to do it.

mi te lisri fi zoi
.puf.

Frank: Hey Bob... where are we?

Bob: You should know that.  We're in "zoi"

Frank: What is "zoi"

Bob: "zoi" is a magical place where you are free to do and say anything you
want.
     You can use magic words and special symboles.  Heck, you could draw a
picture
     in zoi!

Frank: wow, sounds awesome, but how did we get here?

Bob: well, the world of zoi starts and ends with a "magic word".  When that
word
     is spoken, the world of "zoi" begins and when it's spoken for a second
time
     the world of "zoi" ends.

Frank: wait, wait, wait.  That means that if we say whatever _that_ word is,
everything
       will just... stop?

Bob: that's right.  Everything and everyone you've ever known would just
stop

Frank: Jeeze, that's scary.  Well, what's the word, I want to be sure not to
say it
       by mistake

Bob: nobody knows.  Think about it.  Nobody inside "zoi" was there when the
word
     was spoken and the first time that it is spoken, everything will end.
     We have no way of knowing what the word is.

Frank: wait, so at any time, someone in "zoi" could say this "magic word"
and everything
       would be gone, just like that.  And we don't even know what this word
is that
       we can't say?!

Bob: that's what I'm saying

Frank: Holy crap!  It's a precarious life that we live

Bob: It sure is.

Frank: To think that one little bit of unfortunate conversation could lead
       to the destruction of our existence, splat, squish, boom.

Bob: Personally, I think it will sound more like a poof

fa'o

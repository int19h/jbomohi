You can use "zei" to make lujvo out of any Lojban words.

> i.e. if I want to make a lujvo from {ka ci} (i.e. lo ka me li ci) I can't
> really do that because I end up with {kamci} or {kamcib}.  Neither of these
> is grammatical.  Is there a way to make a lujvo from cmavo alone?

For "threeness" I would say "ka ci mei", which can be lujvoed to "kamcibmei".

When squeezing lojban text in a comic baloon, it would be very helpful
to break words between lines.
I've not found anything specific in the CLL in section 3 where "," is
defined as the syllable separator but no explicit example is given
about breaking words across lines.

So I thought I would be allowed to do things like:

nin,
mu

Would it be ok? After all no word is supposed to end with ",".

I would try to avoid it as much as possible. If you must must must do
it, I suggest

nin-
-mu

might be less confusing. The comma could be confused with an end of
word period, and the second part of the word has good chances of being
a word by itself, so marking it too might be a good idea. But try
different forms and see what looks good.

mu'o mi'e xorxes

Breaking within consonant clusters would be best. Second best is to
break between a consonant and a vowel.

cus-
-ku

ti klAm-
azdAni

But even better is to layout your speech bubbles, or rewrite your
text, so that it isn't necessary.

since, it seems.

The following pages (among others) have non-English translations:

 http://www.lojban.org/tiki/Lojban+Introductory+Brochure
When I set the site language to e.g. German on /Lojban, it takes me to the
German page, /Lojban (de), no problem. But when from there I click
"Introduction" in the menu column, it takes me to the English page,
/Lojban+Introductory+Brochure, rather than the expected German page, which
does exist. This prohibits me from staying in a non-English environment
throughout the browsing wherever translated pages exist, forcing me to
manually change the language either with the pull-down box on the bottom
left or on the right above.

I thought I should remind the admin of this issue, because one Japanese
contacted me again about his interest in making contributions to the site,
and he is (as well as I am) distracted with its navigation. If no immediate
improvement is likely, please let me know, and I'll get back to him to
report that.

All of Lojban's approximately 1300 'gismu' (root-words), 
along with their 'rafsi' (short versions of the root-words) 
are now implemented in the Quizlet flashcard 
memorization website. Grouped alphabetically in 28 sets, 
they bring the total of Lojban word sets to 169. 

If you've ever wondered how active is the Lojban 
community compared to others, take a look at this.  
By comparison, Esperanto has 274 sets, Interlingua 22, 
Toki Pona 22, Ido 18, Klingon 10.

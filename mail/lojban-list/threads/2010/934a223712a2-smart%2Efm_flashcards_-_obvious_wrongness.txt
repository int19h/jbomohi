The smart.fm program chooses the various selections itself- there is no way
to alter it. Fortunately, only the first few runs (like, say, the first 4 or
5 sessions of 10) really have this problem. As you progress in the goal, the
items for review become increasingly less ordered- that is, increasingly
unlike the order presented when looking through the content list. For
instance, I've been doing that lesson for about 3, 4 weeks, and of the 10
items I have in a session, absolutely none of them are from the same gismu.

(This, by the way, is part of why I argued against sorting them in usage
order, and leaving them alphabetical- after a (very short) while, the
original order doesn't matter much at all- the program feeds you the items
based on how well you retain them. True, the items are presented to you for
the first time in the order they are in on the content list, but that
doesn't mean you'll be going through them in that order.)

Well, I don't know what smart.fm's model is, but it at least manages  
to present only the appropriate *type* of phrases (e.g. if the  
question is "something x" then the answers are all "lo [SE] BRIVLA"  
and not "BRIVLA" or in English); I assume, however, that it might be  
impractical to extend this to per-gismu associations, or that it would  
eliminate ever seeing a wrong answer with the wrong gismu.

Having to stop and think about my own performance was one of the most  
tiresome aspects of using SuperMemo on the Palm, for me. smart.fm as  
it is seems to be much more conducive to getting into flow. [But is  
that good for learning?] I don't have to remember *not* to click ahead  
(except that I wish the study screens, which are display-this, pause,  
display-that, *looked more different* from the other screens because I  
often click through them without recognizing them since I'm in the  
mode of 'glance at question, click next, pick answer'. On the other  
hand, perhaps it's just that it hasn't caught up with my actual skill  
level yet and once I have more not-immediately-recognized items the  
steps will be more natural.)

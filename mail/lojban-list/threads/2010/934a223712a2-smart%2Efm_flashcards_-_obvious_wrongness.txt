I'm trying using the gismu set on smart.fm <http://smart.fm/goals/ 
266809>.

I find that whenever I'm quizzed about a gismu place, the answer is  
always obvious because the current session is dealing with only a few  
gismu, and out of all the possible answers there is only one item  
which (if Lojban->English) is plausibly for that gismu or (if English- 
 >Lojban) is one of the gismu sumti being used in this test.

As a result, it doesn't really challenge me to learn the *positions*  
of the places of a gismu (e.g. that the route is {te klama} as opposed  
to {ve klama}, because the answer will always be the only one having  
to do with klama/going).

Is there any chance of some sort of solution to this?

The smart.fm program chooses the various selections itself- there is no way
to alter it. Fortunately, only the first few runs (like, say, the first 4 or
5 sessions of 10) really have this problem. As you progress in the goal, the
items for review become increasingly less ordered- that is, increasingly
unlike the order presented when looking through the content list. For
instance, I've been doing that lesson for about 3, 4 weeks, and of the 10
items I have in a session, absolutely none of them are from the same gismu.

(This, by the way, is part of why I argued against sorting them in usage
order, and leaving them alphabetical- after a (very short) while, the
original order doesn't matter much at all- the program feeds you the items
based on how well you retain them. True, the items are presented to you for
the first time in the order they are in on the content list, but that
doesn't mean you'll be going through them in that order.)

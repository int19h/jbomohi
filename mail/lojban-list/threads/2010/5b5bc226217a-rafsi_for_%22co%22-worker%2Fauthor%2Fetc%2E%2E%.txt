The other day I wanted to translate something that had the word "co-worker"
in it.  My first thought was something like {datygu'a} but that just says
that the worker is someone other than me, no necessarily that they work with
me.  Then I thought {djugu'a} but that seems to carry the (incorrect)
connotation that I am the primary worker and this "co-worker" just helps,
that's not what I want.

This same question works for "co-author", "co-conspirator", etc...

Any ideas?

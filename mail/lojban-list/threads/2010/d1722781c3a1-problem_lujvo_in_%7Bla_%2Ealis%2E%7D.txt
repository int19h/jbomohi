> These three lujvo from alis have components I can't find:
> jve : camri'ojvebla = carmi+crino+?+blanu = intense+green+?+blue

"jve" is a rafsi for "je".

> jiknu : xasyjiknu = xamsi+? = sea+?

It should be "jukni".

> zra : za'uzra = zargu+? = buttock+?

"za'uzra" is a fu'ivla, because "zr" is not a valid initial.

"cizra je za'uzra" was the translation of "curiouser and curiouser".
I've changed it to "cizra je ci'izra" in the revision I'm working on.

from "ko'a selsmu no da ko'e"

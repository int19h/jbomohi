coi rodo

vensa on the irc just asked for the best way to ask "among florolf and you, who
was the first lojbanist?" and he came up with

  ma poi me do ce la florolf ku'o cu du pamoi le jbopre

and that's too long for my taste.
After considering a few different words to make it work (ji, zmadu/traji, moi) i
came up with the still rather flawed

  xu la florolf do zmadu lo ni clira lojbo binxo

because it only works with two people, which vensa immediately noticed.

What would be a good way to express that?

mu'o mi'e timos

  Could be all sorts of interesting ways:

la florolf ji do pamoi le'i cilre be fi la lojban [lo detri]   seems
to me to be the most straightforward.

For a real ridiculous one, try this:
ge'i la florolf ce'o do gi ra sece'o ri cu porsi lo prula'i be fi lo
nu cilre fi la lojban

For a less ridiculous one, try:
ma cu porsi lo  prula'i be fi lo nu cilre fi la lojban la folrolf ce do
            --gejyspa

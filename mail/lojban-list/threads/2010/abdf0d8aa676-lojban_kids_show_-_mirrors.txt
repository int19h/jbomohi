I figured out how to solve the problem of moving the mirrors without 
generating huge bending stresses. The three sky windows are surrounded by a 
paraboloidal honeycomb frame which supports elliptical mirrors 10 by 14.14 
meters that turn from 0 to 90 degrees. The frame is tied to the body of the 
space station by metal cables. The end of the frame farther from the sun has 
a radius of 16.49 kilometers and is subject to 4.12 times Earth gravity. A 
spider-like robot is used to fix it.

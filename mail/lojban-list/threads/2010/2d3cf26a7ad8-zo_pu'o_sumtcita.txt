> (lo cibysfecedra is the Triassic.)

The way I interpret it, you are specifying "ba'o jutmro" and "pu'o
jutmro", but you would be saying that "ba'o jutmro" happened before
"pu'o jutmro" (given that lo cibmoicedra follows lo bakrycedra), which
doesn't really make sense.

I think I would say:

 lo dinsauro jenai cipni cu jutmro ca lo tersei be lo bakrycedra bei
lo cibmoicedra

mu'o mi'e xorxes

"lo bakrycedra bi'o cibmoicedra" doesn't sound sensible, as each is a span of 
time, and the first ends when the second starts (many paleontologists believe 
that the boundary was an asteroid impact). How about Jorge's version 
with "jutmrobi'o"?:

lo dinsauro jenai cipni cu jutmrobi'o ca lo tersei be lo bakrycedra bei lo 
cibmoicedra

So OK, if you want jvozba running online but from GoldenDict
do the following:
1. Open GoldenDict > Edit > Dictionaries > Sources > Websites.
2. Hit "Add" button.
3. Enter two positions:
Name: "Create a lujvo" Address: "http://jwodder.freeshell.org/lojban/
jvozba.cgi?tanru=%GDWORD%"
Name: "Split a lujvo" Address: "http://jwodder.freeshell.org/lojban/
jvozba.cgi?lujvo=%GDWORD%"
4. Hit OK. Now two more buttons appear on the toolbar.
5. Enter lujvo or tanru. See the results. They are loaded from the
internet.

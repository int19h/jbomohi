I am starting a new forum called "xedbig".  It's private, invitation
only, and moderated.  Any of you will gladly be invited, but only if
you agree to these rules:

1. You must be nice.
2. You may only speak in Lojban.
3. You may not criticise or correct how other people use Lojban.

That third rule is hard and fast.  You may not correct anyone's use of
Lojban in any way.  If someone uses Lojban in a way that you don't
like, or even a way that is certainly and uncontroversially entirely
wrong, you simply must hold your tongue.  If you think that someone
would enjoy your criticism and find it very helpful, you still must
hold your tongue.  You may not write to people off-list to correct
what they've said on the list, either, and if found doing so you will
be removed.  No criticism, none at all, ever.

If you can agree to those rules and you'd like to join, let me know,
either here or by private mail.

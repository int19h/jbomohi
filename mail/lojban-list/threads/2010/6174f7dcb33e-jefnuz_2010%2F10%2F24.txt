INTRODUCTION

Hello, and welcome to the newest incarnation of Jefnuz, the Weekly
News of Lojbanistan.  Jefnuz is collectively edited by the Lojban
community.  Feel free to edit anything here, except for parts that are
quotes attributed to particular people (don’t put words in their
mouth)!  You can either contribute stories to be collectively edited
and expanded, or add stories as quotes (either attributed to yourself
or anonymously) so that other people will leave them alone.  Please
note that anything included in the Jefnuz will be placed into the
Public Domain.

The focus of Jefnuz is the direct observations and experiences of
Lojbanists.  Please share the events of your life, whether or not
they’re Lojban related, so that we can get to know each other better.
Other short creative works related to Lojban, such as Lojbanic poetry
or opinion pieces on Lojbanic matters, may also be appropriate.

ALAN POST INJURED IN BICYCLE CRASH

Alan Post, inventor of the game “lo do ckiku ma zvati” (a Lojbanic
version of the language learning game “Where Are Your Keys”), has been
injured in a bicycle crash.  He broke two bones and needed stitches,
but he’s recovering.  Best wishes for a speedy recovery, Alan!  More
info (and a picture) can be found here:
http://groups.google.com/group/lodockikumazvati/browse_thread/thread/682ca9038f3dfb74

BPFK report
Work is coming along nicely. With the exception of some of the lerfu
sections which have seen little updating (especially the lerfu shifts
section), we seem to be banging on all four cylinders here. Currently
our two most important tasks are searching the mailing list archives
for any past points of contention starting at 1998 to present, and
identifying what cmavo are rare or incorrect in usage. Any past issues
need to be documented in the relevant sections for the selma’o in
question with a summary provided next to the link. Additionally, any
issues with the language or proposals need to be included in the
appropriate section and summarised in a few short sentences with
contact information should we pursue an interest in said proposal.
-Lindar

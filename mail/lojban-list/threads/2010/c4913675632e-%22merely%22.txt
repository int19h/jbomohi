express "I did not kill john.  I hit him <which is a comparatively
non-lethal thing>".  It seems like "merely" in english has a connotation
that links it to the previous sentence (or to some implied other action as
in the case of "I only want what's best for you <as opposed to the more
lethal 'I enjoy ruining your life'>").

Would it be legitimate to say something like "mi na catra la djan
.ipo'oje'ubo mi darxi .dy"?  Would that have the connotation that I'm
looking for by linking the sentences with {bo}?

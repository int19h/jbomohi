Hello fellow sampla,

I took the liberty to create a free organization on github called
"lojban" and I added the first few people who turned up in a search for
"lojban". I'm hoping to get all lojbanic projects on github in one place
to increase visibility, awareness, collaboration and synergy effects and
all that. If you are not yet on github, consider joining and managing
your lojbanic code there, it's pretty neat. You can mail me your github
username and I will add you to the organization.

I would also be incredibly happy to get either a discussion among coders
started or an entirely new mailing list for lojban programmers, so that
"normal people" don't get bothered by techtalk.

Please tell me what you think.
  - Timo

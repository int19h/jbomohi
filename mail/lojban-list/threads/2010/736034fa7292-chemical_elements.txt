The elements are named in Wikipedia:
http://jbo.wikipedia.org/wiki/dikni_selratni_cartu
radium (Ra/88) = dircyjinme
palladium (Pd/46) = jinmrpaladi
gold (Au/79) = solji
iron (Fe/26) = tirse
helium (He/2) = solnavni
nickel (Ni/28) = nikle

The letter {y'y} can occur only between vowels, so {xukmi'fu'e} is an
invalid word.

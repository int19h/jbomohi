The former is "julra'o be li nopi'emupi'epaxa bo berti ku'a julra'o be li 
pazecipi'ecimupi'evore bo stici cange". I don't know if anyone's made up a 
word for "minute" or "second" in the angle sense; they could be "jgamentu" 
and "jgasnidu".

The latter is "la marbi rirxe ma'arbi'i", though I'm not sure that "ma'arbi'i" 
(which means "between mountains") and "valley" mean the same, although that 
is its gloss.

I prefer the latter, unless I'm trying to find the place with a GPS. It is 
possible in Lojban as well as in English for a name to be ambiguous. "la bil" 
can refer to any number of people named Bill, or to a city in Switzerland 
also known as "la bien" (it has two names because it's bilingual and the 
names in the two languages are different). I've seen two subdivisions in the 
same county with the same name.

I just want to strongly emphasize here the scare quotes around "definition".

> This just give a bit more detail, for learning.  Examples:

I would scarequote "learning" too. It should be strongly emphasized
that memorizing keywords, most especially in the case of cmavo, is in
most cases nowhere near learning those words.

For example:

> se      2nd conversion (2nd conversion)

If you don't already know what "se" does, "2nd conversion" tells you
next to nothing about "se".

Even if you do have a vague idea, it still doesn't tell you whether
"se broda brode" is "(se broda) brode" or "se (broda brode)" for
example, so learning "se" involves something other than flashcards.

> lo      the really is (veridical descriptor)

which is ok, as long as you don't get the impression that "lo" is a
definite article because "the" is a definite article, or that "really"
has anything to do with existence as opposed to veridicality, or that
"is" implies singular, and so on. I'm not proposing any alternative
better keyword, but people should be reminded that learning keywords
is not the same as learning what the word means or how to use it, and
this is especially true for structure words. So flashcards are fine in
helping with memorization of cmavo you have already learned, not so
good for learning cmavo you have never met before.

/end rant

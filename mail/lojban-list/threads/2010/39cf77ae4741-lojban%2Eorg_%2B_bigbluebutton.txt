I wasn't sure if this was exactly the right place for this, but it is
regarding the lojban.org site, so I hope that's ok. I'm a user of the
TikiWiki software myself and have recently become aware of a new
feature, or rather integration, with a program called BigBlueButton.
Looking at it, it seems to me that it would be a great way for
virtually anyone to give lojban lessons, or even broadcast lojban
meetings, or maybe even other uses as well. And it doesn't require the
users to have anything but a decent web browser to use (webcam and
microphone are optional). You can see more here: http://doc.tikiwiki.org/BigBlueButton

Does anyone else think that this might be useful? I know I would like
to see it on the site.

Not such a big problem, no different than IRC anyway. ;)

First of all, you can schedule meetings or seminars in advance on the
website, as events. Second, if one is on IRC they can always say "Hey,
I'd like to do a little session on (topic), who's in? Meet me at this
address." or if someone asks a question where video, desktop sharing,
or a whiteboard would help, they can just move on over. Some people
might even just want to stay on there like they do IRC, and check
occasionally for others. It might even be useful for more official
meetings, like for the BPFK.

Seeing this made me think of some of the videos on youtube I've seen
of people giving little lessons in lojban and wondering if some
interactivity might help in some cases, like hearing something
pronounced in addition to seeing it spelled... that sort of thing.

Anyway, just an idea. :)

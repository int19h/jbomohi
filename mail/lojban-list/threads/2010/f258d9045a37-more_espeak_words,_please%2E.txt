For this reason the definition for bakfu, which in the gismu list is "x1 is
a bundle/package/cluster/clump/pack [shape/form] containing x2, held
together by x3", becomes "x1 is a bundle or pack containing x2 held together
by x3"

The reason the sumti-fied gismu need to edited is because the phrasing
jbofi'e uses is quite often very awkward if not downright confusing. The
phrase jbofi'e returns for {lo te bakfu} is"any/some hold-er(s) of bundle",
which can easily be confused for "a person holding a bundle", which is
wrong, as the x5 of {bakfu} is "what is holding a pack together".

Quite a lot of the entries can be used almost as is, but they still need to
be gone through for that very reason.

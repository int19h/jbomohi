that is an awful lot of vagueness.

So, {binxo lo tai darno} -> {binxo zo'e noi ke'a tai zo'e broda}.

To make it easier for myself, I'm going to use ba which is easier when
trying to demonstrate stuff involving TAGs

cinri lo ba viska -> cinri zo'e noi ke'a ba zo'e viska -> (re-ordering
because it's easier for me) -> cinri zo'e noi ke'a viska ba zo'e ->
(something) <is interesting to> (the one who sees (after something) )

So {binxo lo tai darno} is something like {binxo lo darno be tai zo'e} or
"becomes (the far thing (in form zo'e) )"....

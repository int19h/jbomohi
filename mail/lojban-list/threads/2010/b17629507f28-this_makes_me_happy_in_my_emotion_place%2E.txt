Can we come up with some of these?

The rules:
1. Make it about Lojban.
2. Keep it simple. The previous "game ideas" have kinda flopped. Keep

3. Submit as many ideas as possible. There's no reason we can't McRib
each idea to keep them fresh.


My vote:

A chat bot secretly picks a word from the ma'oste or gi'uste. Each
week, the person that said that word the most gets a mention from the
chat bot or something silly like that, like every three hours (eight
times a day) {ni'o la $name cu banli no'i} or something silly of that
nature. Perhaps there's even a statistic page that shows the two
runners-up. The person that wins the most times in a three month
period gets a prize (honourable mentions on the website? 5$ starbucks
card?). If one week is too long, perhaps every two or three days?

I'm very excited about this because it seems very easy to do.

=== 5 | 2010-11-17T20:58:38Z | Oren <get.oren@gmail.com> | aanlktimp2u80hgkh8uqtqzmgedetgtrm_jpucd=jsyvr@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-list/cur/1290027518.52b3828ceaa7d006.jbomohi:2,S
In a nutshell:
It's a user-generated database of example sentences that automatically
links sentences that have been "indirectly" translated (through
another language). The result is any given sentence will have a dozen
or more translations. Applications for this data abound, but the
website is a great resource for language learning.

For reference:
It's similar to dict.cc, but with just sentences (not a dictionary),
many-to-one translation, more languages, and (much less) moderated
audio.

For more:
The introductory video on their homepage is very good at describing it:
http://tatoeba.org/eng/

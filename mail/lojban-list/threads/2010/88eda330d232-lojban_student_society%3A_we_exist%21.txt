Hello all;

 I have recently started a student society centred around Lojban at the
University of St. Andrews, here in Scotland; I thought someone on this list
might be interested/happy/intrigued to know about us.

 We're meeting once a week at the pub to discuss Lojban. Right now we're just
moving through Lojban for Beginners, but in the future we hope to discuss
tricky grammar points, share original works, and provide support for St.
Andrews students undertaking Lojban-related projects.

 Having sent some blanket emails to the departments, we've received some level
of interest from about 25 people, about 10 of whom have actually shown up for a
meeting. Although we've only had 5 meetings so far, the first 2 of which weren't
publicly advertised, so maybe this isn't too bad.

 I can't imagine anyone (outside St. Andrews) reaping any huge benefit from our
existence; nevertheless, I encourage ya'll to contact us with questions,
comments, ideas, words of encouragement, Lojbanic inspiration, good cocktail
recipes, etc.


Many kind regards,
Geoff Beresford
President,
Lojban Society: St. Andrews

  Before jbovlaste, and still in existence, there were and still are)
multiple word lists.  There is gi'uste (the list of gismu), ma'oste
(the list of cmavo), a rafsi list, and multiple lists of lujvo.  The
most up to date one before jvbovlaste came into existence was called
noralujv, after it's compiler, lojbabs wife, Nora (la noras.).  It can
be found at http://www.lojban.org/publications/draft-dictionary/NORALUJV.txt
 And is reference on this page

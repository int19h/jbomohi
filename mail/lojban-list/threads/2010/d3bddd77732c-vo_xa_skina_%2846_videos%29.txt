I think it's harder to make one than to make a bunch!  When I hadn't
made many videos, I felt like any video that I made was very
important, so I was self-conscious about them.  Now that I have a lot
of videos I don't feel like each one is a big deal.

For instance I made a video a few days ago when I saw two geese in the
park.  I looked up "gunse" in vlasisku to make sure I had it right,
but then I went ahead and said "ganse" over and over in the video
anyway!  Oops.  If that was my only video I'd feel more concerned
about how it would reflect on me!  But the truth is it'll probably
teach people "gunse" even better than if I had gotten it right.
People can watch it and think, "Ha!  He said 'ganse'!  That's so
wrong.  *I* would never make that mistake."  And then they'll remember
"gunse". ;)

It would be nice if there were anyone else making a bunch of videos
with me!  Come on, anyone?  Surely I've broken the ice by now, you
couldn't possibly get more wrong than I have.  We could have
conversations, perhaps. :)

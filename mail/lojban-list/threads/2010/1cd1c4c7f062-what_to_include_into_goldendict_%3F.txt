I believe there is no official dictionary for Lojban.
As jbovlaste is now imported into such quite handy software as
GoldenDict I wonder what else to include into it.

There are several lists mentioned at [http://www.lojban.org/files/
roadmap.html#draft-dictionary]

They are "the English-Lojban version of the gismu" and "Nora
LeChevalier's working lujvo-list with place structures".

But they date back to < year 2000. Are all the words from them in
jbovlaste now ?

Are there any good wordlists ?
I know
* jbovlaste
* complete sets of Lojban words for all countries, languages and
currencies, based on ISO codes by Jorge Llambias and Leo Molas
* gismu synonyms from lojban.org
* gismu etymology from lojban.org

If anyone point to other well-formed wordlists they will certainly be
included into the set of Goldendict.

The lack of the full dictionary is the main reason why I'm not
learning Lojban seriously.

jbovlaste has both of these, even though most definitions from
"noralujv" don't have any votes yet. They have often somewhat
nonsensical sounding definitions (take cintyfaigau for instance: "x1 is
a do with distributed-paint x2 with receive share x3.") and usually
someone else re-words the definition, submits the reworked definition
and votes on it to be included.

jbovlaste itself is, actually, the official dictionary for Lojban. At
least I see no reason not to consider it as such.

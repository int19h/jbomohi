It is pretty standard in color photography, and presumably in other
graphic arts as well, to discuss color balance in terms of six
`standard' colors: magenta, red, yellow, green, cyan, and blue.
Color photography is not such an arcane art that one would really want
to consider its nomenclature to be specialized technical jargon.

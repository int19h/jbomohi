associate the Lojban with to help you remember the definition.  Hence
vofli is 'flight' while 'sfani' is 'fly'.

you wish to do that, I recommend a separate column from the keyword
column.

other than cleanning up for posting things that are already written, we
will not be generating anything special for on line use.

Further to the problems with prenex-connective interaction -

16.10.5:
	roda zo'u mi prami da .ije naku zo'u do prami da

and 16.10.6:
	su'oda zo'u mi prami da .ije naku zo'u do prami da

do not parse. Try them on jbofihe. You can use ge...gi instead, and that
seems fine, but (as I mentioned the other day) it looks like you can't
have individually prenexed sentences connected in afterthought.

Damned annoying, if you ask me.

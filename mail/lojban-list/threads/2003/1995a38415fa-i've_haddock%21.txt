On Thursday 20 November 2003 21:01, Theo Reed wrote:
> > soncrzuaua
>
> Something sounds like a chihuahua?

Nothing to do with Chihuahua. It's a type-3 fu'ivla. The first four letters 
are the beginning of a gismu, which tell what semantic category the word is; 
the last five are the name of a people, who are nowhere near Mexico.

Btw, {tciuaua} is a valid type-4.

I would use lujvo based on atomic number only for the transuranic elements 
which don't have names yet, such as unununium (pavypavypavycmu). The rest 
should be gismu, lujvo, or fu'ivla on a case-by-case basis. If you want to 
specify the atomic weight, you can say {fi'o te ratni li N}, which is wordy, 
or someone could add another word to XI for superscripts since subscripts in 
chemical formulas mean the number of atoms.

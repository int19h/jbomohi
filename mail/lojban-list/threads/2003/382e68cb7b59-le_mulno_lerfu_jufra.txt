"The fire in the beed pot can be a mysterious interpretation
of forty-two" is a pangram(complete-letter-sentence) in Lojban.

But it's too long -- 40 letters long, and Lojban uses only
24 letters. They are 26 alphabets, minus h, q, w, plus apostrophe.

I once proposed:

   .o'i mu xagji sofybakni cu zvati le purdi
   [Caution!] There are five hungry Soviet cows in the garden!

(It will also work with French (fas-) or Palestinian (fis-) cows
instead of Soviet ones.)

It uses 33 letters counting the apostrophe, with no repetition of
consonants (and it includes the dot). The number of vowels could
be reduced a bit, but I don't think it can be done with exactly
24 letters unless you use cmene or fu'ivla.

mu'o mi'e xorxes

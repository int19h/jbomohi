So, here's what's up.

NORALUJV cannot usefully be loaded into jbovlaste, because the
definition format in no way resembles the lojbanic standard.  For
example:

fazyta'a                annoy+talk
fanza+tavla:  chatter:   x1 = tavla1 (talk) = fanza 1 (pest), x2 = tavla2
(talked to), x3 = tavla3 (talk subject), x4 = tavla4 (talk language), x5 =
fanza2 (annoyed)

So, I'm seeking advice on ways to make this easier.  

Possible Options:

1. Load it all up as-is under a special user and announce that user's
password here?  Disad: we don't know who did any given def'n.

2. Put a shared copy of NORALUJV somewhere and have people remove lines
as they get to them.  Disad: where to put the shared copy?  How to
control access?

Comments?  Ideas?

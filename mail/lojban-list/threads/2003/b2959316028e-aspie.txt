A translation of "Asperger's Syndrome" into Sino-Korean(Korean word
composed of Ideographs) is "japyejeung". Unicode is

ja(U+81EA) pye(U+9589) jeung(U+75C7)

and it literally means "self-closedness-disease". So I suggest
"sevzi ganlo", or "sezga'o" for "x1 is self-closed", i.e. "x1 is
autistic".

properly proceed: to look up translations into multiple languages to see 
how they express them.  This also tells us when a borrowing is appropriate: 
if other languages borrow rather than make a tanru or do a 
loan-tanru-translation from the roots in the other language, it probably is 
better as a fu'ivla than as a lujvo.

bi'i BIhI unordered interval
non-logical interval connective: unordered between
... and ...



bi'o BIhI ordered interval
non-logical interval connective: ordered from ... to
...



mi'i BIhI center-range
non-logical interval connective: ordered components:
... center, ... range surrounding center


Note "bi'o" and "mi'i".

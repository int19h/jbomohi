I have noticed that the lojban list at yahoogroups and the list at
lojban.org (Ecartis) has become desynchronized. There are about 25 
messages in the Ecartis archive between 23 and 27 of October which
are not mirrored at yahoogroups archive. I do not know if it happens 
for the first time or it already happened before.

To me, this is a disturbing news. The existence of two mismatching 
lists will lead to chaos. I am still using the list at yahoogroups 
and I am not pleased to find that I can miss some messages.

If the Ecartis list has became de facto the primary one then
it might be better to migrate entirely to Ecartis. 
Of course, it should be done with a timely notification,
and the list at yahoogroups should be then closed for posting.
It seems that the function of yahoogroups list as an archive is
now compomised by missing messages.

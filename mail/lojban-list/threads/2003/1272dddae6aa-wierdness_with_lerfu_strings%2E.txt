Robin Lee Powell scripsit:

> Apparently, lerfu strings cannot be names.  Was that intentional?

Yes.  Following "la" is either a cmevla (ending in a consonant) or else
a predicate.  Lerfu strings are neither.  So either add a consonant,
la byfyf. or else just say byfy. and let the referent of this anaphora
be glorked.

There is an option in the web browser itself to use your own colors instead of
those on the page. For instance, in Internet Explorer, go to Tools/ Interenet
Options, click on the 'Colors' button to choose what colors you want as
default, then click the 'Accesiblity' button, and check the box that says
'Ignore colors specified on Web Pages' click on 'OK' a couple times and you're
set. In Netscape (6) go to Edit/ Preferences, click on 'Colors' under the
'Appearance' tab, and click the 'Use my chosen colors, ignoring the ones
specified' radio button. Click OK and you're done.

I think Pierre made it quite clear what we are trying to say:
{ci da zo'u mi viska lo gerku be da}.

The question is if we can say it in the form {mi viska XXX}, i.e. if
we can avoid having to front the quantifier.

How do we say "I buy the same three newspapers every day"?

I think maybe: {mi te vecnu ci le'e karni ca ro djedi},
{mi pu viska ci le'e gerku}.

The issue is not to do with understanding of stereotypes. It is to do with
whether le'e has anything to do with stereotypes. If le'e is to lo'e as e-gardri 
are to their o-gadri counterparts, then le'e would not mean "the 
stereotypical". So there is a kind of inconsistency in CLL, in that le'e is 
semantically irregular. This is one of the things the BF will be looking at.

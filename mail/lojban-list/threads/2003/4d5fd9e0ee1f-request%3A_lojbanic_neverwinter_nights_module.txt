NeverWinter Night is a computer role-playing game with a heavy
"create your own" element.  See http://nwn.bioware.com/

To give you an idea of how popular this is, and how easy it seems to
be to create modules, there are ~250 *hours* (in terms of game play
time) of modules on the NeverWinter Vault's Hall Of Fame *alone*:
http://nwvault.ign.com/Files/modules/HallOfFame.shtml

I think a Lojbanic NWN module would be *incredibly* cool, and I'm
putting it out to the community to make one (I don't have the time
:-).

The only Lojban needed would be the character dialog, and I'm sure
there are plenty of people who can help.  I think it should be
something completely original, though.  We have enough translations.

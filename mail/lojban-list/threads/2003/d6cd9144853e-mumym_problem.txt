Regarding 2, it does care:

self.gismu_validator    =
re.compile("^[bcdfgjklmnprstvxz]([bcdfgjklmnprstvxz][aeiou][bcdfgjklmnp
rstvxz]|[aeiou][bcdfgjklmnprstvxz][bcdfgjklmnprstvxz])[aeiou]$")

All guesses are checked against that regexp. So you *can* guess fake
words, but they must be valid gismu. However, it will never choose a
gismu that is not in the gi'uste (as distributed in the lojban-common
debian package).

The other two will be fixed tonight. I also plan on adding a scoreboard
feature.

jbovlaste has been updated.  The two major changes are:

1.  rafsi are now per-valsi instead of per-definition, and cannot be
edited in any way save by forking the database by hand.  This was a
decision made by all 3 jbovlaste admins to make the barrier to
changing rafsi as high as possible.  It is not subject to appeal.

2.  When you reply to a comment on jbovlaste, everyone else in that
thread is mailed.  This should make the comments actually *useful*.

And I just realized that, in addition, the creator of the definition
should be mailed in the case of per-definition comments; that change
will be in shortly.

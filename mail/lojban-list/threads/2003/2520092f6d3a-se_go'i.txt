You can use se with go'i but it doesn't mean that. It just changes the
order. In your example, it means "I love you, but I doubt  that it is
you whom I love." go'i pulls along all the sumti with it.
 
> Another question. Can I change a sumti other than x1 in go'i like
> this:
> 
> lo carvi cu pluka mi - go'i mi
> I like rains.- So do I.

Yes. That works.

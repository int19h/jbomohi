The reason why I brought up the no-capitals orthography idea is that
the CLL, and other sources, explicitely state that:

    Lojban uses a variant of the Latin (Roman) alphabet, consisting
    of the following letters and symbols:


    ' , . a b c d e f g i j k l m n o p r s t u v x y z

    omitting the letters ``h'', ``q'', and ``w''. 

when this is, in fact, patently false (I don't see G or A er E
there!).

Not a big deal, but I find it mildly annoying.

fanva selsanga me'e la djingylbelz.

Verse 1:
Dashing through the snow, in a one-horse open sleigh, 
Over the fields we go, laughing all the way. 
Bells on bob-tails ring, making spirits bright, 
What fun it is to ride and sing a sleighing song tonight.

ni'o pamo'o
snimygre sutlitru  fi le xirmycpu salcarce
.i cpana lo naldargu  ja'enai le zu'o cmila
.i xirma janbe sance   seki'u lenu gleki
.i se pluka lenu litru   dursanga ca le nicte

Chorus: 
Jingle bells, jingle bells, jingle all the way! 
O what fun it is to ride in a one-horse open sleigh. 
Jingle bells, jingle bells, jingle all the way! 
O what fun it is to ride in a one-horse open sleigh. 

ni'o to dei rapli toi
zu'o sicni  tonga janbe  vi le mi'a pluta
.i je'a nelci lepu'u litru fi le xirmycpu salcarce
.i zu'o sicni  tonga janbe  vi le mi'a pluta
.i je'a nelci lepu'u litru fi le xirmycpu salcarce

Verse 2:
A day or two ago, I thought I'd take a ride 
And soon Miss Fanny Bright, was seated by my side; 
The horse was lean and lank, misfortune seemed his lot; 
He got into a drifted bank and we got upsot 

ni'o remo'o
puza so'u djedi   mi djica lenu litru
.izibo la selprami   cu zutse lamji mi
.i le xirma pu sutcinla  gi'e raktyfri seldimna
.i ri stapa lo conderxi .ije mi'a janli juxre 

(repeat chorus) 

to tigni le rapli toi

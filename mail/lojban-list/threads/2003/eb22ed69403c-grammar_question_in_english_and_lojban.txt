> {vo'a} correct, or is the sentence comparing baboon etc. arms to human legs? 

I would say it is correct, as VOhA are supposed to be reflexives and
reflexives work like that. To get the other meaning you should repeat
{ma'a} instead of using {vo'a}.

> .i ne'i zoi gy. We are the only catarrhines whose arms are shorter than *** 
> legs .gy ma .ei sumka'i posyvla gi'e basti zoi gy. *** .gy?

i mi smadi tu'a zoi gy their [own] gy

It is, although there is also a lojban-beginners list, which would be a 
good place to post basic questions (some of the discussion on this list 
can get very technical!).

> mi rinsa le cmima  (is that right?)

That says "I greet the member", which is perhaps what one of us might 
say to you.  If you want to greet a group which you are joining, you 
could say "mi rinsa rodo" ("I greet all-of-you") but it would be more 
normal just to say "coi rodo" - "Hello everyone!"

Incidentally, I note that "rinsa" has "the arrival of" before x2, so 
perhaps it would be inappropriate to say when it is you who are arriving.

What letter should be used in an acronym if the word
- begins with a case prefix, e.g. ctile barbe'i gugde terganzu?
- begins with a rafsi whose first letter differs from the gismu's, e.g. mlatu 
labyblukenra vidru?

Another way to shorten such phrases is to form a cmene or fu'ivla from pieces 
of the words: la ctibarg., le mlalavi.

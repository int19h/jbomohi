Must be a bug in cmafihe. jbofihe accepts it. cmafihe accepts {skalduna} but 
thinks it's a color. I put in the nonsense phrase {mraigo skalduna kapsiku 
bauksita} (Welsh Basque chili-pepper bauxite) and got this:
mraigo <BRIVLA> [shelter-?] skalduna <BRIVLA> [color-?] kapsiku <BRIVLA> 
[pelt-?] bauksita <BRIVLA> [language-?]
The same phrase through jbofihe -x results in question marks, which, since 
none of them are type-3's and it hasn't been told what they mean, is correct.

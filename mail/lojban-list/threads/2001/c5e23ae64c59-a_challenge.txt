The challenge:

To come up with solvable, but non-trivial, problems of the sort presented 
on these pages for Lojban.

The benefits:
1) They might be interested in including a good puzzle on Lojban some year, 
giving us publicity in our most fruitful audience (language-interested 
younger scholars)

2) We may get some ideas on how to teach Lojban inductively (darn, I've 
used THAT word again %^).

The List or the Wiki would both be appropriate places to post ideas, it 
seems to me.

Here's an idea:

Write a list of lujvo using a small set of rafsi and their translations. Then 
put a few translations and see if they can make the lujvo. Be sure to include 
a few hyphen letters and variant rafsi.

gerzda		doghouse
lange'u		sheepdog
lamyzda	adjacent-house (next door)
gerfi'e		dogfish (probably not the shark)
mlafi'e		sidefish (flatfish)
?		houseside
?		sheephouse

Throw in some three-rafsi lujvo, as the rules for inserting 'r' start being 
counterintuitive.

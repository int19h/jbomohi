Jorge Llambias scripsit:

> The only time I remember needing 2-ce'u properties is with {simxu},
> does anybody else have an example where one would need them?

You can transform sentences like "mi prami do" into
"lo ka ce'u prami ce'u cu ckaji mi ce'o do", and so on for any
number of places.

The x2 places of bridi, gismu,  ciste, sanji, ciksi, and the x4 of girzu
can accept ka's with any number of ce'u spots.

The x3 of ckini *demands* a 2-ce'u ka.

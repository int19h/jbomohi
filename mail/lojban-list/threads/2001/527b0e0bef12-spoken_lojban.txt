But...there are lots of sound samples on the web! Elrond made a recording
of Fox and Grape Bunch (which I don't remember the address of), and I made
my own recording which I've put at: http://home.nvg.org/~arj/lojban/lorxu.mp3

sleeping.

which was mentioned in message #77 (in the Yahoogroups archive).  They

others found the pronunciation horribly americanized.

Tying in with a preceding not-strictly-relevant thread, I'm letting you
know that I am taking over the task of editing and publishing the Level 0
introductory Lojban booklet, as agreed at the last Logfest. The intent was
to have something readily available for print-on-demand and online.

This means, of course, that a manipulable text format is required. The
tagset I am familiar with is TEI (www.tei-c.org), which is designed for
the marking up of literary and philological texts. Nonetheless, in the
interest of advancing my CV :-) , and given the background of people here,
I will be doing it in DocBook.

I am by avocation a Mac user --- which doesn't help much, as the Mac is
hardly a priority platform for SGML/XML apps. :-( The XML work I do for a
living, I do on NT. Furthermore, I switched from computer science to
linguistics just before I would have learned TeX; I don't know it, and haven't
got a free year to learn it. :-) This means I may need help from people in
customising the TeX/PDF output. The real fun, obviously, is going to be
customising the stylesheets, though other than introducing a Lojban font,
I'll try and keep this to a minimum.

It also seems I have undertaken to finish off Robin Turner's lessons,
which will be included in the package. I will be posting these on my
website for comment as I do them.

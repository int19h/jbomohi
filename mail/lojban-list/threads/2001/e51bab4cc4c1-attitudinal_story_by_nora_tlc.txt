As translated, the two sentences are indeed different.  The first (fi'o 
pleji mi) says that I am paying.  fi'o selpleji mi says that I am the 
payment, and the colloquial translation given is "on my tab".  Nora says 
she thinks she should have said pleji mi for that one as well, but the 
existing text isn't entirely wrong (probably a tu'a mi" works better for 
"my tab") and we probably don't have record of discussing it back when it 
was written (someone could hunt the archives).

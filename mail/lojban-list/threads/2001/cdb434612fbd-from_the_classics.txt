I ran it through jbofi'e and got several parse errors. "la brn. cribe" it took
to mean "la brn. cu cribe" and choked on the following "e"; and the second
paragraph should have "i" or "ni'o" at the beginning, or else it parses as "la
brn. ractuc. cusku lu .ie .i ji'a condi li'u la brn. ractuc.", at which point
"skicu" is the second verb in the sentence, which is therefore a run-on.

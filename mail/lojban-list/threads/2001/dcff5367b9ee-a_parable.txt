all of these. Perhaps none of these. I have not told you, and it is for you
to divine. It matters naught; I am thinking," said Confucius, "that I am
wise."

(2) "I am seeking," said Diogenes, "someone wise."
.i mi sisku sei la diogenes. cusku se'u leka ce'u prije
"It is someone wise about strategy you seek? Is it someone wise about
philosophy? Someone wise in the ways of love? Perchance one wise in the
orations of men?"
Diogenes scowled. "I will be happy if you bring me one wise on strategy. If
you bring me one wise in the ways of love, I will be astounded. If you
bring me one wise in rhetoric, I will think you a liar.  But it matters
naught; whatever he claims wisdom in, just bring me that man who truly is
wise."

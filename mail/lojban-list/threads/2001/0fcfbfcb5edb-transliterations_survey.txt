I'd say take the phonemic system that yields the best correspondence with
Lojban. Inter alia, this applies to English:

RICK     rik  (not ryk)
RECK     rek  (not rik)
RACK     rak  (not rek)
ROCK     rok  (not rak)
ROOK     ruk  (not ryk)
RUCK     ryk  (not rak)

The forms in brackets are phonetically appropriate for some accents of
English. So King John, for example, should be /djon/. But John Cowan
should be /djan/, because we know that's what he favours for himself.

Not really. The difference between lujvo and tanru is not frequency but
that the lujvo meaning is fixed and more specific. Hence it is right and
proper that the greater specificity be marked by the presence of
glue. Also, allowing multiple glue markers allows for multiple lujvo
from the same tanru.

As for general strategies of shortening forms whose length is excessive
relative to their frequency, I've been discussing these in other messages.

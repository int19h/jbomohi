>Why does:
>
>le ciftoldi ce la alis.
>
>not parse in jbofi'e?

After {ciftoldi ce} another brivla is expected. When
instead it finds {la}, it cannot backtrack and change
to {le ciftolddi ku ce la alis}. The terminator {ku}
can't be elided in such cases.

{la alis ce le ciftoldi} should parse ok.

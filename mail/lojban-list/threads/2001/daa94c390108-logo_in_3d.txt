But I dislike the way the rings and rods interpenetrate, perhaps

physical objects, and I myself don't have the ability to

How about:

  * The vertical and horizontal bars be left as is, with the
    connection at the center.

  * The top and left parts of the right hand blue ring go behind the
    top vertical and left horizontal bar

  * The bottom and right parts of the right hand blue ring go in front
    of the bottom vertical and right horizontal bar

  * The top and right parts of the left hand blue ring go in front of
    the top vertical and right horizontal bar

  * The bottom and left parts of the left hand blue ring go behind the
    bottom vertical and left horizontal bar

  * The bars and rings be separated from one another by a small space,
    perhaps .37 of their diameter (that is 1/e) or perhaps .6 (i.e.,
    1/phi), or maybe some amount considerably less; but the bars and
    rings should not touch.

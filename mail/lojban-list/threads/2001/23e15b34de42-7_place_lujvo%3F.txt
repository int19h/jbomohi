>Are brivla supposed to be able to have that many places? If so, what is
>the upper limit? 8? 10? 100?

Innocent looking {du} has an infinite number of places. So if you're
bored by the same old {li pasu'ipa du li re} for "1+1=2" you can
creatively say {sexi892 du faxi735 li pasu'ipa faxi38 li re}, thereby
taking full advantage of the seldom used 38th and 735th places of
du, and bringing in to the foreground (as an observative some would
say) the interesting 892nd place.

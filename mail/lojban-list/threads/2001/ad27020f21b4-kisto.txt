My memory -- not, need I remind you, a reliable source of information -- is 
that "Pakistan"  means "land of the pure" in Urdu (or so) and is also an 
acronym of the provinces of the Raj that were intended to be included in it 
(Kashmir not yet making it, northern Bengal coming in as a surprise and then 
leaving -- I'm not sure about the identity and fate of the others). "Pushtu" 
is a Persian (of one sort or another) word that almost certainly doesn't mean 
"pure," and probably just means the people of that name (if not worse).

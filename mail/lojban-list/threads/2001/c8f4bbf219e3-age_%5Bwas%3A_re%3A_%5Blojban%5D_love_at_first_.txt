>do renvi fi xo se nanca .i

{xo se nanca} means "how many numbers of years?".

If it's three numbers of years, they might be 7, 24 and 56.

You want {do renvi fi ma} or {do renvi fi lo nanca be ma}
or {do renvi fi lo nanca be li xo}.

>renvi fi xo .i
>
>renvi nanca xo

These are incomplete. {xo} is a number, it can't stand
alone as a sumti. Either {ma} or {li xo}. But I don't
think the x3 of renvi can be a number, it has to be
something with duration.

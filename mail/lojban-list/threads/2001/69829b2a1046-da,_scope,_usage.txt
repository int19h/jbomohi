>Which brings me back to the original thing I asked: why use {da} when
>you don't plan to use it again, and only want to claim existence? Why
>not {zo'e} or some other KOhA?

You use {da} when you want to use a quantifier. You can't
distiguish between {su'o da}, {so'i da}, {ro da}, etc with
{zo'e}. In fact, the main function of {da} is to give
substance to quantifiers.

Esther in the Douay has 16 chapters. The Hebrew ends with 10:3, and the Greek
column in the NIVTOT has 10:4-11:1 in a footnote. That is all of Esther I have
in Hebrew or Greek.

The Torah has passages in which the Greek is in a different order than the
Hebrew. I checked Exodus 38 and it follows the Hebrew. There are other places
where the division of chapters differs.

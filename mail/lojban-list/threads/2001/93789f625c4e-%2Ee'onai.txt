>I think this is "e'unai", not "e'onai".

First of all, recall that an attitudinal does not negate a sentence 
logically, so a negative request will likely refer to something that is 
going on that you want to stop, but it remains a request.  The difference 
is really the subtle one in tone between "Will you and "Wouldn't you":

e'onai ko na krixa
Wouldn't you please not yell!?

You could say the same thing with e'o, but the nai reinforces the negative 
aspect of the request.  It becomes more important when you don't explicitly 
use a "ko".

do krixa e'onai
You are yelling (Please don't!)

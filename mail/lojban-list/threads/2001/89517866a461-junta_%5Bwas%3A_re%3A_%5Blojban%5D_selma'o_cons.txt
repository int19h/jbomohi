I believe it was indeed included because of the weight/mass distinction 
(with mass being ni marji, IIRC).  Most of the other things that can be 
measured are not subject to this potential confusion, and were presumed to 
be expressible as ni abstractions, or klani/tergradu.  I specifically aimed 
to cover all the major SI units with words for both the unit and the thing 
measured, but the latter method usually worked.  junta could have been ni 
tilju, but I remembered how easily lay people confuse mass and weight and 
left junta in for Zipfean reasons.

BTW, noting that Lojban defaults to using SI units in measurement, I 
intended that you can express such messes as g m/sec**2 (newton?) as lujvo 
based on such tanru as ni bapli [kei] gradu and acceleration as ni sutra 
cenba [kei] gradu.

This seemed better than JCB's approach, which in 1986-7 included adding 
gismu for "volt" and other commonly used units, though those additions seem 
not to have made it into his revised Loglan 1 gismu list.

	They may not have made it into the latest edition of Loglan 1, but 
all these units in a Loglanized form remained
in the dictionary.  It is much more convenient to say that such and such 
newtons the number 2 than to explain it in
terms of fundamental SI units.
	On the other hand, I believe JCB would have considered revri for 
all forms of E dreaming, but would have strongly
encouraged specification by the speaker/writer with lujvo to indicate 
the type of mental activity involved.  Indeed, the current Loglan 
dictionary has the lujvo bad-dream (nightmare) awake-dream (daydream, 
reverie) dream-look (wonder) and awake-dream-desire (one sense of 
fancy). JCB was very fond of metaphor in luvo, such as time-stretching 
for boring.

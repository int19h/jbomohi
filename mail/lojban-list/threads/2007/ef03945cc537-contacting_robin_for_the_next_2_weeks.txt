cell phone in Japan.  I will have (very sporadic) access to e-mail,
making e-mail the best way to get ahold of me.

I get on the order of 100 e-mails every day, most of them automated.
This makes it tricky to see real mail.

If you wish me to actually read something you send to me in the next
two weeks, please have:

    READ NOW

in the subject line.

    EMERGENCY

in the subject line.  Please save that for things that would make me
seriously consider hopping on the next plane home.

If you have one of these things in the subject line, there's a
chance I'll see it within 24 hours.  If you don't there's basically
no chance at all that I'll see it in the next, umm, month or so.

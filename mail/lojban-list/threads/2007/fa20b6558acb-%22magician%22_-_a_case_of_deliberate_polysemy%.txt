The English word "magician" can both mean the stage magician, and the
sorcerer. Now *termakfa* is definitely correct for the second sense.
However, because of the tendency to believe that the stage performer
actually performs magic, I suspect it would end up applying to the stage
magician as well. This would be a case of human desires deliberately
creating ambiguity. *C'est la vie*. (*ti selkai loi selfri*?)

Perhaps that is what the x2 of *makfa* is for: something may be "magic" to
me, but not to the performer. *zo'o*

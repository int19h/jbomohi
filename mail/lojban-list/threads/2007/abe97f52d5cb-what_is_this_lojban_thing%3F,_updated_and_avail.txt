Greetings, fellow Lojbanists...

Those of you who attended jbonunsla 2006 may remember the fun we had
trying to set up for my presentation, "What is This Lojban Thing?".

The slides for this presentation are now available online, at:

  http://peapod.podzone.net:1234/hive/lojban-thing

The presentation has been updated with (most of) the suggestions from
people who attended this auspicious event, and it's been split into
modules for easier swallowing.  As always, your continued feedback on
the talk is welcome.

If anyone would like to mirror the presentation, your help would be
most welcome.  The host above isn't guaranteed to be up 100% of the
time, and I'd like to make the presentation as reliably available as
possible.  The whole thing occupies about 60MB of storage.  Just let
me know, and we'll coordinate an off-peak rsync, or something.

Enjoy!
Dave Montenegro

P.S. Does anyone in the audience know where I can find information on
the Loglan-related conlang known as "Plan A"?

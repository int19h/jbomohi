Yesterday, 28 November 2007, the online version of "What is This
Lojban Thing?"  (http://peapod.podzone.net:1234/hive/lojban-thing/)
saw its ten-thousandth hit.

The hit, from a DSL user in Germany, was the result of a Google image
search.  Overall, 95% of the hits to "What is This Lojban Thing?" have
come from Google.  About 94% of the hits have been from queries on
http://images.google.com.  The presentation, first presented to the
Seacoast Linux Users' Group (http://slug.gnhlug.org) in October of
2006, was first put online about a year ago, in November of 2006.

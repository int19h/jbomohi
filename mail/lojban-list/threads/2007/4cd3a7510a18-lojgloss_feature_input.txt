I'm thinking about changing the parse output so that it doesn't show
the entire structure at once, but rather just a bridi or sumti at a
time. So initially it would just show you a bridi, with the selbri and
sumti and miscellaneous cmavo marked. Then if you clicked on the
selbri or sumti, it would hide the structure of the other parts
(perhaps optionally) and just show you the internal structure of that
part. Once I put in glossing, it would do the same things with the
gloss words.

Would people like this feature? (I think I would.)

And further, would anyone want to see the entire parse tree at once
for any reason? I'd just as soon not support both modes.

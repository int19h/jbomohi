OK, I have a plan to do some curriculum. It is very similar to the
same curriculum I was planning to do X years ago, but I think this
time I might actually get somewhere on it. Here's the idea: have a web
application that lets you browse through some lessons, which consist
of two types of items: 1) Explanations/lectures/lessons and 2) Flash
cards. The lessons can just be Lojban for Beginners, and I'm in the
processing of writing flash cards to go along with the lessons. You'll
be able to read the lessons in any order, and add the flash cards to
the review process with a click of a button. They'll be scheduled for
review in a Supermemo-type way. (In fact, I expect I'll use the
Supermemo dll to schedule repetitions.) The user comes back and logs
in again every day (ideally) to review the stuff that's scheduled.

Some flash cards will be simple memory items, and others will be
exercises with answers. Between the lessons and flash cards, I think a
person should be able to learn Lojban enough to converse without ever
leaving the application. And all in a single web site.

Has anyone done anything like this, or have any other input, lest I
waste much effort? (Also, suggestions?)

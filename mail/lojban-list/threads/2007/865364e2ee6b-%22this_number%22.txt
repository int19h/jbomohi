What's the typical method to express "this number of" in Lojban? Example:

"I need one more pencil": *mi nitcu vei [?] su'i pa ve'o pinsi* ?
"I want-- I mean, Aslan wants-- some more nuts." (Quote from *The Last
Battle*): *mi djica sisi la .aslan. djica tu'a za'u[?] narge* ?

Or is there another method?

"One more pencil" and "some more nuts" here really mean "one additional
pencil" and "some additional nuts", it's not so much about how many pencils
or nuts are needed or wanted, it's about what things are needed or wanted
in addition to the ones already had. In that case, what is required is a brivla
meaning something like "x1 is additional/extra/supplementary to x2".
Perhaps {bacydrata}:

  mi nitcu lo bacydrata pinsi
  la .aslan. djica lo bacydrata narge

mu'o mi'e xorxes

=== 4 | 2007-12-14T23:59:54Z | Penguino <lojban-out@lojban.org> | 97f5058c0712141559s7e303235s9f29320bd1922172@mail.gmail.com | mail/lojban-list/cur/1197676794.83fb65b2a51c46ce.jbomohi:2,S
Thus the first example goes to "*mi nitcu paza'u pinsi*", and the second "*la
.aslan. djica su'oza'u narge*". The interaction between multiple inexact
numbers might have to be worked out, but in this case it is unambiguous. "At
least two more" would be *su'oreza'u*, which would be the same whether
interpreted as (*su'ore*)*za'u* or *su'o*(*reza'u*)

Hi all,

I have a suggestion.  I think adoption of Lojban could
be greatly accelerated with specifically audio
oriented lessons.  Lessons where the learner does not
have to be tied to a computer, or deal with flash
cards, or what have you.  Something that could be
listened to in a car, or while walking to class, for
instance.

Lately I've been using Pimsleur to learn German (and
for some reason Cantonese) and I absolutely love it. 
I think an approach similar to something like Pimsleur
or ChinesePod would be great.  Something with high
quality audio files and perfect pronunciation/grammar.
 You could even release it as a torrent (among other
methods).

It wouldn't seem like this would take too terribly
much effort.  Scripts could be worked out via
newsgroup.  I think the toughest part would be
assembling two or three high quality volunteer
speakers in proximity to appropriate audio equipment.

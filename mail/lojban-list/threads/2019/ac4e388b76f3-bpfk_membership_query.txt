At present, the LLG membership is debating whether to continue the 
existence of BPFK, because participation has been too low in the opinion 
of the BPFK chairman (jatna), who is known by the handle selpa'i. 
Normally you would contact him at his email to join.

To become a voting member, the only real requirement is to sign up and 
eventually show up at the annual meeting.  You can do this *now* (and 
participate in the meeting discussion, even if your vote cannot count 
until you are a member), because the online meeting that started last 
year is still continuing (we're trying to fix that, but health problems 
have interfered).

The website is:

https://framateam.org/signup_user_complete/?id=17b4ir6u6by6px693dm8kmy7yw

The site may come up in French, unless someone figured how to change the 
default.

You change the language (if I remember correctly) by clicking on the 3 
bar "Main menu" icon at the up left, then clicking on the first option 
which is "Account settings" and then "Display" which  has an eye icon 
next to it, and the last item is the language, which you can edit to 
make it "English".  It may still say Francais, but everything should now 
be English.

Several public channels can then be joined.  The Members Meeting is 
called "LLG Members Meeting".  It is open to all interested members of 
the Lojban community.

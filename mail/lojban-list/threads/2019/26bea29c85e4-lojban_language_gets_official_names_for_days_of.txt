Apparently, Lojban got official names for days of week:

sandei - a shortcut for {spano djedi}, Spanish day, a reference to full 
cultural neutrality of this system of weekdays.
mandei - dark day (aka "Broken-light day 
<https://www.reddit.com/r/Jokes/comments/8bo92h/how_many_lojbanspeakers_does_it_take_to_change_a/>" 
aka "Broken light-day"")
tuzdei - location day (a day symbolizing a firm place taken by Lojban, 
everyone knows how useful Lojban is in our world and where to apply it)
vendei - seller's day (optimal day for selling copies of CLL, the reference 
grammar book of Lojban)
sozdei - 9th day (probably, traces of an ancient calendar where week had 
days numbered from 0 to 9)
fradei - reaction day (a day devoted to various reactions of Lojbanists to 
weekday and other proposals, see also {besto 
<https://la-lojban.github.io/sutysisku/en/#sisku/besto>} )
satydei - sugar day (also known as cake day)

So yeah, the new system is a piece of cake. And here is the link to the 
official documentation on the new weekday 
system: http://lojban.github.io/sinxa-lo-judri/rkr.html

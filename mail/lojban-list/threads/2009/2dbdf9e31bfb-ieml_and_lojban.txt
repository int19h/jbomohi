IEML and lojban

Author: candide

I'm (loosely) involved in the development of meta language called IEML: http://www.ieml.org/. I wonder if people from the lojban have ever heard of it, since the two projects are loosely related. IEML's main feature is the notion of categories and algeabrically computable relationships between concepts. OTOH, it is utterly unpronouncable, and wasn't meant to be.
More specifically, I'm thinking that lojban could be a very nice user interface to the language, which is primarily meant to enable semantic navigation and search.

Anyway, I'd be very interested to hear your take about it.

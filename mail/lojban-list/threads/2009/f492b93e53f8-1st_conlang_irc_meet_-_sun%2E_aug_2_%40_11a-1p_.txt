The first conlang* IRC meet will be Sunday, August 2nd at 11am-1pm PST.

Initial topic (from Jim Henry): What are your favorite natlangs? What
features/words have you "borrowed" from them?

Please think of something to contribute on this topic - but don't
bring it up on list; save it for chat.

Location: irc.freenode.net #lcs

Accessible from http://conlang.org/chat.php or by using your own IRC
app (e.g.: Windows mIRC, Linux xchat, OSX colloquy) to join the above
server and channel.

You can of course go there and hang out at other times if you like (or
on Freenode #conlang, Sorcery #isharia, etc) - this is simply an
excuse for everyone who's not into "just hanging out" to all come
together and have an enjoyable discussion.

- Sai

* "Conlang" here means the superset of art-, aux-, enge-, and other
constructed languages. I.e. everyone in the community is welcome.

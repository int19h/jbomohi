It has the same place structure as "cidja". Based on the gloss, I think it 
would refer to the parts of food that supply energy: protein, carbs, and fat. 
It doesn't apply to the vitamins and minerals, which are necessary to use the 
energy. And it wouldn't do for "nutrition", which is in some senses abstract.

I disagree that lujvo "can be recreated from the basic wordlists". While you
can certainly determine the underlying tanru (or just gismu and cmavo), you
cannot determine which meaning of the underlying phrase is selected as the
meaning of the lujvo, nor can you with any certainly determine the argument
structure of the lujvo. I entreat you to include at least some lujvo if the
book is to be made available to other people.

stevo

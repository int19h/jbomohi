On Wed, Nov 18, 2009 at 12:10 PM, Luke Bergen <lukeabergen@gmail.com> wrote:
> Looking at "the two person car".  How would one differentiate "the (two
> person) car" from "the two (person car)(s)"?  And what would be the default
> behavior (e.g. {lo re prenu karce} )?

"lo prenu re mei karce" vs "lo re prenu karce".

The "inner quantifier" is not a part of the tanru. To use a number as
part of a tanru you need to convert it with "mei".

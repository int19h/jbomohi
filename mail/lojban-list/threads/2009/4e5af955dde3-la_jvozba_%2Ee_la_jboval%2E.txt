I have just finished & made available a CGI form for combining {tanru} into
{lujvo} and splitting {lujvo} back up into {tanru}.  It is available at
<http://jwodder.freeshell.org/lojban/jvozba.cgi> and allows the user to choose
whether to create {brivla}, {cmene}, or both, and it also displays a complete
list of all possible {lujvo} for each {tanru}, sorted by score.

Related to this, I have also released a new version of the set of Lojbanic Perl
modules & scripts formerly known as Vlasisku and now renamed jboval, which is
located at <http://jwodder.freeshell.org/downloads/jboval-src.tgz>.  The most
noteworthy change is that users can now access the morphology regexes present
in the Lojban::Vlatai module.  Also, the jvozba program now splits compound
{cmavo} and converts H's into apostrophes before processing, and the {lujvo}
formation routines now catch impermissible consonant triples.

"lo broda be fa co'e" is equivalent to "co'e poi broda"; you're 
stuffing "co'e" in x1, then taking x1 as a sumti. So what you said means "mi 
nelci nei poi te cizra", that is, the bridi is a strange property. Is that 
what you meant?

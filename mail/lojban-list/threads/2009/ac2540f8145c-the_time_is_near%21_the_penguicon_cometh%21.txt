pe'i di'u ca'o se smuni na'ebo da poi do jinvi lodu'u ke'a smuni di'u

As part of their ability to appear virtually anywhere, attitudinals don't have
any syntactic effect on surrounding text (unless followed by "{nai}", I
suppose), and so the "{.ai}" in that sentence does not terminate "{lo drata}."
Hence, you still need a "{cu}" before "{sidju}."

mu'omi'e .kamymecraijun.

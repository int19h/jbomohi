distinguishing feature". I could find none. So I noncely made
"pretcila". But, come to think of it, "prenu" is already defined such
that x1 displays personality/persona. This "personality/persona" can
well be a matter of sumti. Why don't we have "prenu" x2, then? We
could make good use of it, e.g. "ro verba cu prenu lo frica", "lo
steci selpre cu sarcu lo nu ti jibri da".

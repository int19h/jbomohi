> I dunno.  Is there time implied in {gi'e}?  If not, do we want one there in
> this case?

"gi'e" is a logical connective and as such is time-free.  To get time in,
use "gi'enaibabo" = "and >then< didn't" or "gi'enapubo" = "and >before that<
didn't".

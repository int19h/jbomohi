Given the current renaiscance in Lojban text processing (Washington Tuesday
nights), I propose the following standard procedure for text published on
the list:

1. Publish your text on the list
2. Endure the comments of your fellow list-ites for the next week or two.
3. Revise your text to the extent that you accept these comments.
4. Dump your texts into some corpus. I don't know if the PLS is suitable.
5. Have the Washingtonians review only text that has been through this
procedure and ended up in the corpus.

If Mark doesn't object, I'll start doing this with my Wallop texts.

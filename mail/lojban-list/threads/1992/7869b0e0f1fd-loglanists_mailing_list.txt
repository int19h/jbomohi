Welcome to the LOGLANISTS mailing list.

You can send mail to everyone on the list by mailing to
	loglanists@ucsd.edu
(use INTERNET:loglanists@ucsd.edu from Compuserve).

If you don't wish to be on this list, please send mail to
	postmaster@ucsd.edu
and ask to be removed.

If you know folks that would like to be on the list, they may add
themselves
by mailing to
	listserv@ucsd.edu
with the single line
	sub loglanists emailaddress
(where emailaddress is their Internet mailing address)

Seriously, I'm a bit worried about this. The syntax of {pa'i}, a VUhU
rather than a GOhA, imply that a ratio is a number. It's just that I think
of a ratio as a predicate, a relation. In either case, the meaning of a
ratio as a quantifier is problematic. I look up my World Book Dictionary:
"1. the relation between two numbers of quantities expresses as a quotient
[...] 2 a quotient expressing this relation". The former is a predicate;
the latter, the number 0.9 . The former looks wrong, but is defaulted out
of by syntax. The second still makes me uncomfortable.

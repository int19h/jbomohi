Well, it seems that JCB and I agree almost perfectly on lo (our loi)
and disagree on "me".  This may even be reasonable since "me" was Scott
Layson's invention rather than JCB's and is not really part of the original
language.  It's essential nature is to convert an argument into a predicate,
and the semantics of such a conversion are something that has to be defined.
We have defined and successfully used "me" a lot based on our definition,
which is not compatible with using it as an absolute solution to the relative
clause problem.

It will be interesting to get pc's opinion on this issue when he resurfaces
from his summer vacation.  I don't know much about Holmes knowledge of 
logic or of Loglan, but I do know of pc's knowledge.  If he should weigh in
with an opinion supporting Holmes and TLI's solution, we might rethink "me",
though we will still feel our solution to the relative clause problem is
superior on the basis of intuitive learnability of the language.

Lojbab's comment that UI+pei asks for the intensity to which the UI applies
to the listener needs to be supplemented by an explanation of pei+UI.
This construct asks whether or not the UI applies at all.  For example:

	oipei
	How much pain are you in?

	pei.oi
	Are you in pain at all?

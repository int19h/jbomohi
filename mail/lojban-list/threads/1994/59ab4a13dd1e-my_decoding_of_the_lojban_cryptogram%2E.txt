Night circles night. Names name voices. Clouds cover clouds of voices of the
 names
of the night.
Silence creates out of silence. Life dreams that magic is alive. Memory anchors
 the
memory that silence is magically alive.
What silence orbits, creates out of night.  Magic's voices name it 'life that

in aliveness'. Cloud establishes the memory that it covers.

 make

secrets, and leaps the stars of greenness.

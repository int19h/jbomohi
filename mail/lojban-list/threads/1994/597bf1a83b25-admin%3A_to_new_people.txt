WE have had a LOT of new people join the list in the last couple of weeks.

1) Feel free to join in (English allowed, in spite of the current dominance
by Lojban posters %^).

There is no such thing as a stupid question about Lojban.  If you ask one
that has been asked a lot of times before, you will help us determine some
new questions to add to an FAQ.

2) I am assuming that most people have learned about Lojban from the WWW
site, which has had a steady stream of around 10 new people a day exploring
it.  I am thus assuming that most subscribers have about as much intro
information about Lojban as they want.  If you have NOT gotten any files
from the Web or the ftp site, I have a few files that I used to send to
every new subscriber as being the logical starting point in learning about
Lojban.  Just ask (all are files available on the Web site, or are closely
similar to them).

3) There is now a very drafty English-Lojban dictionary file on the ftp site.
It is a BIG file.

4) I f you want to be added to our snail mail list, or just to let us know
who you are, name and snail mail should be sent to me at this address.  (I
assume you want the hard copy intro material unless you specify otherwise.)

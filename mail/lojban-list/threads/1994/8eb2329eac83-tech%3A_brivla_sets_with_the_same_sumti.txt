la .and. cusku di'e

> What is the simplest way to render into Lojban "Jack loves and admires
> Jill", i.e. "X Y broda .i X Y brode"? (Not "Jack loves-cum-admires Jill"
> - this is not what I mean, though if you wish to remind me of how that
> is done, please do.)

Well, if for ".i" we read ".ije", logical conjunction, the correct form is:

	la djek. broda gi'e brode vau la djil.

where the "vau" forces the parallel VPs to a close, leaving "la djil." outside
the scope of the conjunction.

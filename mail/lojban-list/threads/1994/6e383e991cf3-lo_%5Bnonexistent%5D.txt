> Is this grammatical?

No.  "coi ro do" or "coi doi ro do" probably expresses the intent;
colloquially, "Hello, everybody!"

(Literally, that would be more like "coi ro da" or "coi ro prenu", but
we do not usually wish to address literally every person, still less
inanimate things.)

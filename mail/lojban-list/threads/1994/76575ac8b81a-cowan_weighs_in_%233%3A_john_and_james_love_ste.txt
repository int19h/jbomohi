Jorge is correct that the meaning of:

	la djan. .e la djeimyz. prami la meiris. .a la salis.
	John and James love Mary or Sally

is not defined in the paper on logical connectives.  It should have been.
The definition, per Randall Holmes, is:

	John loves Mary or Sally, and James loves Mary or Sally.

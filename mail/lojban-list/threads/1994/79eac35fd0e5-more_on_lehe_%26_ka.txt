*lo* ka does not pick out any particular kind of property in my estimation

lo'e refers to a hypothetical object that has all of the defining properties
and all of the non-defining properties in medial values.  

le'e refers to a hypothetical object that has whatever properties that the
speaker wants to ascribe to the referent, including possibly some properties
that are neither defining nor non-defining (i.e. imaginary or prejudicial).

As I think I said in an earlier message to you Randall, Lojban #+mei has
a place structure such that the mass, the set and the individuals are
all related:

 x1 is the mass formed from set x2 with member(s) x3

Thus if I did the third conversion (Lojban te, your fu), then I think
we are correct:

le nanmu cu te cimei
The men are individuals who comprise a set with 3 members.

GK>And now I am beginning to be haunted by a Beatles lyric:
GK>"Something in the way she walks.."
GK>How do you say that "something" in lojban when you don't really want it

That is either "loka ko'a cadzu" or "losu'u ko'a cadzu", where I prefer
the latter, since it isn;t clear that you are referring to a property of her
walking.  This is similar to the original example that prompted the
introduction of "su'u":  "Three blind mice, see *how* they run".

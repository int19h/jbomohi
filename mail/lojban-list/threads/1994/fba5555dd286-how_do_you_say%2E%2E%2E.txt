I would think you want "serai" which is the place that takes the property.
and would explicitly say

     ko klama le zarci serai leka do ka'e sutra

to get the "As you can" part in.

A more realistic request would use troci

     ko klama le zarci sekai leka do sutrarai troci

(Philip's original "sekai" was missing the "leka")

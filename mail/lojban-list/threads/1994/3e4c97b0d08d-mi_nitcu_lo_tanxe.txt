> >What's the difference between frica and drata?

la kris bogart. cusku di'e

> .uinai zo frica zo drata cu drata frica lo na se djuno be mi

"frica" is the polar opposite of "simsa", whereas "drata" is the polar
opposite of "mintu".  The first pair have to do with likeness (similarity),
the second pair with sameness (identity, in the looser sense, not the
"du" sense).

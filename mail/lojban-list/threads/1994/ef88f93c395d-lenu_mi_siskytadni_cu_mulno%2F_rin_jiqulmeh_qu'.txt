Lojbanists and Klingonists, I am happy to announce that my Master's thesis,
_Problems in the Application of Rhetorical Structure Theory to Text
Generation_, has finally been completed. My schedule is still rather hectic
at the moment, because of my attending the Australian Linguistic Institute,
but I hope to be able to get back into Lojban and Klingon work in a week or
so. I look forward to speaking to you over the phone during Logfest and/or
the Philly Klingonists' gettogether (you really should give yourselves a
title, Lawrence.)

Translations in Lojban and Klingon follow.

doi lojbo joi bangrtlingana. bo tadni mi gleki mu'i lenu mi ka'e xusra di'e
.i le la masters. tadnyprosa pe mi zi'eneme'e _lu loi nabmi befi lenu pilno
le stidymu'i selskugri bo stura sidbyci'e lenu loi skami cu cupra loi prosa
li'u_ cu .u'o mulno .i leimi zukte se platu pujeba'eca jairi'a tatpi ki'u
lenu mi zvati le sralo bauske cilrynunpenmi .i ku'i mi ka'e.a'o di'a gunka
tu'a la lojban .e la tlingan. ba lo jetfu be li pabi'ore .i mi gleki pacna
lenu mi tavla do sepi'o le fonxa ca'o le jbonunsalci .a le la filadelfias.
bangrtlingana. nunpenmi (to doi LOrens ko .ei te cmene le nunpenmi toi)

.i le se fanva befo le glico cu lidne le dei prosa .i lo te fanva befu la
tlingan. cu se lidne dei

lojban HaDwI'pu' tlhIngan Hol HaDwI'pu' je, rIn *maSterIS* pob vISuqmeH Qu'
DaH 'e' vImaqlaHmo' jIQuch. _ghItlh lIngmeH De'wI', _chay' SoQvaD chen
mu'tlheghghom 'e' qelbogh nger_ lo'mey Sengmey_ pong ghaj 'oH. DaH wej Huv
Qu'meywIj mem, *'ostlhelya* HolQeD ghojmeH qepDaq jISaHmo'. 'a qaSpu'DI'
wa' cha' ghap Hogh, lojbanvaD tlhIngan HolvaD je jIvumqa'laH 'e' vItul.
qaStaHvIS *loghveSte*, *vIlaDelvIya* tlhIngan Hol HaDwI' qep joq (*lo'renSe*,
tlhIHvaD pong vay' Da'oghnISlaw'), tlhIHvaD jIjatlh, ghogh HablI' vIlo'taHvIS,
'e' vItul.

ghItlhvam nung mughmeH DIvI' Hol, lojban je lo'bogh ghItlhmey'e'.

Are you sure you mean zanru? It doesn't have an x3:

zanru zar     zau  approve
x1 approves of/gives favor to plan/action x2 (object/event)

I would still like to use cnemu, because a congratulation is more than
a simple approval, it implies some merit on the part of the person being
congratulated. How about zarne'u?

zarne'u  c1=z1 c2 c3=z2 (c4=nu zanru)
         x1 congratulates (rewards with approval) x2 for action x3

Jerry Altzman just provided stats on the first 1113 days of Lojban List
at its current Columbia listserv address (thru 26 August 1994).

A total of 4541 postings by 170 posting addresses, giving an average of
4 postings per day.

Those addresses include several identifiable multi-addresses for single
users (e.g.  Cowan has posted from 8 different addresses of his own,
plus 2 shared with me), so I spent a few minutes combining data for
those I recognized.

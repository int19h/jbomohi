Jorge admits that "re loi tanxe" is meaningless ("two masses consisting of
all boxes") but argues for "le re loi tanxe", as in "piro loi blanu tanxe"
plus "piro loi xunre tanxe".  This won't work, because "loi blanu tanxe"
isn't "loi tanxe", but "lei tanxe"; there is an in-mind restriction to blue
boxes only.  This gives "le re lei tanxe" which is fine.

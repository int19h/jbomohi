> "I have something" is "mi zohe ponse".
> But how does one say "I have it"? - i.e. something as uninformative
> as "zohe", but +specific.

Probably "mi ponse le co'e", as "le co'e" means "the specific thing(s)
alleged to be of the obvious type".  To get the singular number
of "it", you need "le pa co'e".

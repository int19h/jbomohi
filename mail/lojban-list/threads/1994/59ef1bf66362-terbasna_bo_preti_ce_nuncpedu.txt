Unvoiced = {p,t,k,f,x,c,s}
Sibilants(?) = {c,j,s,z}
Liquids(?)   = {l,m,n,r}

Impermissible: Double consonants, voiced+unvoiced, unvoiced+voiced,
sibilant+sibilant, xk, kx, mz, and one other exception that I can't
remember now.

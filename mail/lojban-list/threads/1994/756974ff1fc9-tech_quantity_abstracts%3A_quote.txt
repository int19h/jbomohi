> The following out of context excerpt from soc.culture.scientists struck

This sounds more "jei" than "ni" to me.  "jei" is "the truth-value of..."
where it is agreed that truth value may be fuzzy: some propositions may be
only 86% true, in which case

        le jei broda kei du li pibixa
        the truth-value-of whatever is the-number .86

Becuase of its growth, the lojban ftp archive has moved to the site
ftp.cs.yale.edu. The lojban materials are, as before, in the directory
pub/lojban.

We now have online the draft textbook, all the word lists, a draft
reference grammar, all back issues of the Lojban List, some software, and
an assortment of text.

The old site has a note directing ftp'ers to the new site.

Submitters: Incoming submissions still have to go to casper.cs.yale.edu
in directory pub/lojban/incoming, as ftp.cs.yale.edu doesn't allow writes
from anonymous logins. Any uploaded stuff will be moved to an appropriate
directory within a couple of days.
Please upload text! I hope to have a good-sized corpus online. We will
have separate directories for checked and unchecked text.

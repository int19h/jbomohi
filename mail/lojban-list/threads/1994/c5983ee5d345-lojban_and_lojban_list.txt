lojbab is Bob Le Chevalier, president of The Logical Language Group.
His personal address is lojbab@ACCESS.DIGEX.NET 

John Cowan, author of the reference grammar papers (if you haven't read them
yet, I strongly recommend them, you can find them at ftp.cs.yale.edu )
also posts from that same address at the moment, which sometimes is confusing.
(I start reading, and think "lojbab can't be saying this... Ah! It's from 
John... :)

To send to the whole list, send to lojban@cuvmb.cc.columbia.edu

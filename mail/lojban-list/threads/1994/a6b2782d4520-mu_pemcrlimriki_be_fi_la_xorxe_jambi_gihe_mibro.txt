>What *isn't* a culturally dependent reference?    

Well, any use of a brivla where you cannot  fill in the places 
strictly according to the place struture is neither veridical nor culturally  
independent.  If the information needed to fill in the places relies on outside
context, and that context is not universally shared by all cultures, it is
culturally dependent unless clarified.

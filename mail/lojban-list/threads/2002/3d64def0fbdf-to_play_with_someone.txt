What's "to play with someone"?

I suspect {ko kelci mi} means "play with me!" but in the wrong sense -- "use
me as a toy" rather than "join me in playing (this/a) game". How to express
the latter sense?

Ah... [searches jvoste] {ko kankei mi}, perhaps? It has both x1 and x2 as
the players. (It's unclear what the difference is between {kankei} and
{kelkansa}, however.)

What would the expansion be? {ko kansi mi lenu (mi'o) kelci}, perhaps?

I agree wholeheartedly with what Robin says below.

Except that if the vote fails I will not be flaming anyone.  I will hovever be abandoning Lojban as unworthy of any more of my time.  

I have been slogging my guts out for six months trying to learn Lojban and I see no point in continuing if there is no interest in creating a stable environment for learning the language.  This means not only a stable language design but also a community of users adhering to the same basic design.  If the vote fails I don't see how we are ever going to achieve this.  The analogy of Esperanto is an apt one.  Look at how well the Fundamento has served Esperanto.  Then look at Ido.

Paul Dennett

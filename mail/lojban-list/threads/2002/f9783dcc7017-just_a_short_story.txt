>And {lei vanju} instead of {le vanju}

I would have used {le vanju} instead of {lei vanju}
throughout, but in this case they are equivalent.

{le vanju} is "each of the quantities of wine that
I have in mind", while {lei vanju} is "all the
quantities that I have in mind taken together". Since
in this case we are talking of one quantity, {le} and
{lei} both give the same result, so I use {le} which
I feel is less marked.

Oh, that makes sense.  It's assigning sumti attached with "be" to the
places of "klama" before the untagged, unattached sumti, shifting sumti
down the order if there's a collision.

Not that that's the official interpretation, of course.  I wouldn't be
surprised if you're supposed to assign sumti left-to-right regardless of
"be" and not inside-out.  Looks like jbofihe is first assigning sumti
attached with "be" and then starting over with the unattached sumti.
However, I don't think trying to merge two different sumti chains is
something people could do very well in real-time.

Well, bjoern and I could hear each other excellently.  Candide
eventually managed to get things working, xod did not.  Very little
conversation actually occured; we were mostly working on technical
problems.

An important point arose, however:

SpeakFreely uses udp, and the udp packets are sent to your machine from
the outside world without using a two-way connection.

This means if you have a firewall set up, it is very unlikely that
SpeakFreely will work without modification of the firewall.  Please turn
your firewalls off before you attempt to join the session.

Thanks.

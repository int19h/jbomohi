#> Firstly, the fact that quantifier ro is importing
#> does not not entail that cardinality ro entails su'o. Secondly,
#> it is not necessarily ro that it importing: I hold to the view that
#> it is da that is importing, so that just as {ro da poi broda} entails
#> {da broda}, so does {no da poi broda}.

#But {da broda} = {su'oda broda}.

I'm saying that any quantifier + da poi broda entails da broda. 
The range of values that da ranges across is guaranteed to be
nonempty. So {quantifier + da broda} entails {su'o da zasti}; it 
guarantees that the universe is not empty. And {quantifier + da poi 
broda} guarantees that the universe-restricted-to-broda is not empty.

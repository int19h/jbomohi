folgende Frage von Interesse sein (auch von
der Buchbeschreibung):

"... a priori languages are of considerable
theoretical interest, in particular from the point
of view of language universals: if a universal
property holds even of languages created
'from scratch', then it can indeed be seen
as a property of any (usable) human language."

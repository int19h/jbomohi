I wish to suggest relying on the goodwill of all involved instead of only 
automatic locking.  If, as a custom, one who wishes to log out a piece of 
work would put a (dated/timed?) comment to that effect at the top, to be 
removed when the final posting is done, others who are willing to abide by 
the convention will not make changes despite the page not being 
locked.  The duration of the lock may then become a non-issue.

My only suggestion is that rather than working at "paragraph" or "chapter" 
level that you chose an intermediate:  blocks of text of "page" size, where 
a page is no more than a chapter, no less than X lines, and starts and 
stops on a paragraph boundary.  X can be determined by looking at what is 
typically a maximum workable wiki page size (no idea what that is), and 
dividing by 4 (allowing for 1 line of English to 1 line of Lojban to 1-2 
lines of comment).

It has come to my attention that some people are replying to lojban list
mails in such a way as to have it go to both lojban-list@lojban.org and
the @yahoogroups.com address.

Please don't do that; it causes people on lojban-list@lojban.org to get
double mails.

If people keep doing this, I will have to make lojban-list@lojban.org
a closed list, meaning that only people already subscribed can post.
I'd prefer not to do that, to make things easier for confused newbies.

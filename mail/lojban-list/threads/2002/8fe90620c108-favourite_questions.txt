questions "Who? What? When? Where? Why? How? Favorite Question? What would
CowboyNeal[2] do?". Someone decided to translate those into Chinese, and
various translations into languages such as Danish, Finnish, and Old English
followed[3]. I thought I'd try to translate them into Lojban in order to
post them to the board after having them corrected.

lot of major bugs have been fixed in jbovlaste. (Though there are still

http://nehwon.dyn.wiw.org/jbovlaste/


Please make heavy use of the new improved bug tracking system to
report any and all problems you may have. You can also use it to
request new features, or provide translations for language names. Do

and lets me know what I need to write documentation for. "I don't
understand anything" isn't helpful, though.

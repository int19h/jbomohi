described as ..." What it doesn't specify is who describes it as that. Take 
this example:

ko'a's cousin (goi ko'e) met a girl (goi ko'i) that reminds ko'e of ko'a.

information imparted to ko'a by ko'e is that ko'i speaks lojban, so when ko'a 
meets ko'i, ko'a wants to be cute and say in lojban: "So you're the one my 
cousin described as the hot redhead."

So is there any way of specifying the source of a description already, or 
should we look into making an x2 to 'le'?

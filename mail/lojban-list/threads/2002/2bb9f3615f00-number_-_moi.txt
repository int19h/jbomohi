They are tanru, so they don't have a fixed meaning.
{pamoi broda} has the place structure of {broda},
{broda pamoi} that of {pamoi}.

I tend to use {broda pamoi} in the sense of {pamoi be lei broda}.
There is no equally simple expansion of {pamoi broda}, as far
as I can see.

Usually they mean the same (the broda which is nth in some sequence), but in 
{da'acimoi rasyslami} what is da'acimoi is not the acid but one of its double 
bonds. In spelled-out dates, {PAmoi} comes after {djedi}: {le zemoi djedi be 
le masti} means "the seventh interval that is a month days long", which is 
nonsense, whereas {le djedi zemoi be le masti} means "the seventh day of the 
month".

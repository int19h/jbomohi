la xod cusku di'e

>What are the actual differences between ji'a, si'a, and mi'u?

ji'a: also, too, as well, additionally. Adds weight to previous case(s).
si'a: similarly. Establishes parallelism with previous case(s).

I don't think I've used mi'u very much. Supposedly it
translates "ditto" when used alone, but I'm not sure how it
would work as a discourse tag. It's probably closer to ke'u
than to ji'a or si'a. Then there's also va'i to contrast
with ke'u...

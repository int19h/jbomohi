In the paper:

mo'i crane zunle xance
        Put your left hand forward.
mo'i trixe zunle xance
        Put your left hand back.

Those should be {mo'i ca'u} and {mo'i ti'a}, not {mo'i crane} and {mo'i 
trixe}, which are ungrammatical.

ko carna ko carna ko carna

Either put {.i} between them or maybe {ko mu'o mu'o carna} or {ko mu'o cargau 
ko}.

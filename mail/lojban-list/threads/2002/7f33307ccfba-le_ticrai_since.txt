la pier cusku di'e

>3:3. .iku'i le grute be le tricu poi midju le purdi zo'i le cevni cu cusku
>lu ko na citka gi'e na pencu tezu'e do na mrobi'o li'u li'u

({zo'i} should be {zo'u}.)

The structure of {ko na citka gi'e na pencu tezu'e do na mrobi'o}
is something like: {ko na (citka) gi'e na (pencu tezu'e do
na mrobi'o)}, but what is wanted is closer to:
{ko (na citka gi'e na pencu) tezu'e do na mrobi'o}.

Even that wouldn't quite work though. I don't think God was
commanding them to have the intention to not die. Rather this
was given as a good reason to obey the command. I can't think
now of a good way to achieve this mix.

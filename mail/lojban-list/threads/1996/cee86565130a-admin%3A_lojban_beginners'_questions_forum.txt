  I'm back after a prolonged period of studying English, of all
  things (I had to get a certificate to assure the bureaucrats
  that I'm reasonably capable of using English :-).

  I've set up a conversation forum for beginners' questions concerning
  Lojban. I'll set up a number of other forums in due course. The forum
  is accessible from the Lojban Home Page or directly at URL

       http://xiron.pc.helsinki.fi/HyperNews/get/lbq.html

  You must use a forms capable Web browser to enter questions and/or
  responses, but it's possible to get copies of postings by e-mail,
  just subscribe either to the whole forum or to individual threads
  by asking for notification at an appropriate point in the thread
  tree (I'm prepared to enter the subscriptions and responses from
  people with no Web access, with some delay, of course). Any
  responses to postings (and responses to responses) will be
  automatically e-mailed to the person who originally made a posting.

  Referring back to the conversation concerning discussion forums,
  I think this is perhaps the most satisfactory solution.

  Now, I'd like to have some volunteers step forward and subscribe to
  the forum so that we'll have a pool of people ready to respond to the
  questions posted.

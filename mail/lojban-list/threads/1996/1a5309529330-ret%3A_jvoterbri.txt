circtuca: ct1 ct2 ct3 ci2 ct5
          x1  x2  x3  x4  x5

means

x1 of circtuca is le sexipa ctuca (x1 of ctuca)
x2 of circtuca is le sexire ctuca (x2 of ctuca)
x3 of circtuca is le sexici ctuca (x3 of ctuca)
x4 of circtuca is le sexire citri (x2 of citri)
x5 of circtuca is le seximu ctuca (x5 of ctuca)


bijykumfa: k1=b1 k2=b3 b2

means

x1 is le kumfa, which is the same as le briju
x2 is le se kumfa, which is the same as le te briju
x3 is le se briju

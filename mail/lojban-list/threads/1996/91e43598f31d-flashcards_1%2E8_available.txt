A couple of points to clarify:

FlashCards 1.8 replaces the earlier programs MacLogFlash and MacLojFlash.

Also, it expects (as did the earlier programs) the flashcard file to be
a Macintosh text file.  This means that the lines need to be separated
by carriage returns rather than linefeeds.  To ensure this, when you
ftp gismu.lis from the Lojban site to a Mac, make the transfer in ASCII
mode, not binary mode.

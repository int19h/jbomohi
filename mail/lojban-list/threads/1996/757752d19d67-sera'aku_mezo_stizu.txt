cu'u la stiv. zoigy.
*chair, x1 is a |/stool/seat/bench, a piece or portion of a piece of
furniture intended for sitting of material X2 for sitter x3
(person/object/event) /:/ /=/ stizu (tiz)
gy.

Material can be expressed when required using the modal "ma'e" and a sitter
using selbri-to-modal conversion of "fi'o zutse".  .i mu'a zoigy. This is a
wooden chair I sit on gy. selfanva fulu ti stizu ma'e loi mudri fi'o zutse mi
li'u

cu'u la stiv. zoigy.
<jubme> X3 place should be for holding/supporting X3 (person/object/event)
instead of referring to legs
gy.

Again, one can use "fi'o lafti". .i mu'a zoigy. That tables holds the cups gy.
selfanva fulu ta jubme fi'o lafti loi kabri li'u

The objective here is keep gismu as simple as possible without cluttering them
with unnecessary places, only the places essential to the bridi have been
included and a mechanism exists for extension when needed.

co'omi'e dn.

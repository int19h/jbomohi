.i ju'ocu'i zoi gy. I do not think they would have discovered our
ambuscade li'onai .gy. naldrani glibau bo jufra vau xunai

(I can't even think about it without that something. I don't know, I
don't feel "..." to be the correct sentence in English. Is it?)

> .i ki'u ma do pilno lo bridi .i pe'i lenu zo pe'i selpli cu zmadu leka drani

> [I suggest "I think that if something did not happen then they would not have
> discovered our ambuscade."]

.iku'i di'uxire selsmu ji'a la'elu zo'e fasnu .ije ko'a kakne co facki
lomi'a pajygunta li'u .i lu naja li'u cu logji terjo'e gi'e smuni
tcefrica lo glico valsi noi zoi gy. if .gy.

(But that also means that if something did happen, they could still
discover our ambuscade. This is logical connective, and therefore
very different from the english word "if".)

co'o mi'e. goran.

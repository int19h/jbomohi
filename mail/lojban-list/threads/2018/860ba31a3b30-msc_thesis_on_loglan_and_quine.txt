Dear Loglanists,

I am mailing you to inform you of my MSc thesis about Loglan and the
influence of the American philosopher W.V.O. Quine on the creation of
Loglan. This thesis was part of my MSc in Logic at the University of
Amsterdam, The Netherlands. Here is a WeTransfer-link to my thesis, for
those who are interested:
https://we.tl/vzLZc5IJiK (link is valid for 7 days, if there are any issues
with the accessibility, please e-mail me)
Especially of interest to you may be the first two chapters and the
appendices, which contain the correspondence between Quine and Brown.

Here <https://msclogic.illc.uva.nl/> is more information about my Master
programme. I will defend my thesis in June. If you have any questions about
my thesis or the relationship between Quine and Brown, feel free to ask!
Sharing my thesis is permitted, but please mention me as the author when
you do :)

Kind regards,
Laura Molenaar

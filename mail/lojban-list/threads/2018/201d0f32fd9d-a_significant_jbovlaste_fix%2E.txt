As many people have noticed, sometimes jbovlaste's PDF output just
... stops.  This is because people inject invalid LaTeX into the
definitions.  It has, historically, required an admin to actually
*find the problem*, which is terrible.

I've finally fixed that; jbovlaste will now show you enough that
only a modicum of LaTeX knowledge is required to find the problem
(and maybe not even that; the thing I just fixed had a line eding
with "$x_1=y_1", no trailing $, which is pretty obviously wrong).

The change is at
https://github.com/lojban/jbovlaste/commit/294e05574c5701909e89d00d0e14be72b6565094
, if anyone's interested.

On a related note, English now PDFs properly again.

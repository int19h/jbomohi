The time of the annual meeting of the Logical Language Group (LLG) is 
approaching. A date has not yet been fixed, but the meeting, which is 
conducted by email, could begin as early as mid-October: Expect an 
announcement soon.


In the mean time, if you would like to suggest agenda items, or to propose 
changes to LLG's bylaws, please feel free to post them to the lojban 
mailing list/group, or to send them to me by email. A few initial agenda 
items have been suggested by LLG Board members:


   - Clarifying LLG's policy regarding hosting, embedding or linking to 
   copyrighted materials on the web site
   - Doing more to promote and highlight creative works in lojban
   - Maintaining an up-to-date list of lojban projects and updating LLG's 
   official project policy
   - Revising the bylaws to enable the option of using a registered agent 
   service to maintain compliance with Virginia law

At the end of the last annual meeting, there was discussion of instituting 
an some kind of annual recognition for lojban-related achievements. This 
should be raised again at this year's meeting.

An unapproved draft of the minutes of the last meeting is available here:

https://mw.lojban.org/papri/LLG_2014_Annual_Meeting_Minutes


If you're not a member of LLG, and would like to consider joining, or if 
you would like to observe the upcoming meeting, please contact me. 
Prospective members should familiarize themselves with the organization's 
bylaws, in particular articles 2 (purpose) and 4 (membership):


http://tiki.lojban.org/tiki/Bylaws+of+The+Logical+Language+Group,+Inc.


Please feel free to contact me with any questions or concerns, or to 
continue this discussion on the lojban mailing list / group:

https://groups.google.com/forum/#!forum/lojban


Thank you,

Riley Martinez-Lynch
Secretary/Treasurer
Logical Language Group
<secretary at lojban dot org>

Title: different tails

I wouldn't say that "a sumti tail is almost the same as a bridi tail, except that there are many additional possible phrases that can only occur inside a description". There are many additional things that can occur inside a bridi tail and not in a sumti tail as well, namely connectives and trailing terms. That's too much for "almost the same". Things like {le broda gi'e brode} or even {le ge broda gi brode} don't work. In fact, the only thing bridi tails and sumti tails have in common is that they both have a selbri at their core, everything else is different. And there is also a special kind of sumti tail with another sumti at its core, so nothing like a bridi-tail.

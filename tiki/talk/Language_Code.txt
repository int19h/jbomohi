Title: Browser language prefs
Message-Id: mdaniel-0-41843bbc97@www.lojban.org

It occurred to me that the browser has a method for advertising the languages it prefers to receive from the server. Combine this with Apache's mod_mime content negotiation feature and you could see Lojban content where available. I have updated Mozilla's Accept-Language preference to include "jbo" and can't wait for the first server to (pleasantly) suprise me.

Granted, no one but geeks like me actually check for this kind of stuff, but I thought I would mention it.

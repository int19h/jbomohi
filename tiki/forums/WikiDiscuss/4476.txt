> Examples of se mau Usage
> mi'a ca nelci le cmalu trene semau le sorprekarce
> We like the small train, more so than the bus.

This (very frequent) type of usage has been traditionally
objected. (Do we like the small train more than we like
the bus, or more than the bus likes the small train? 
Context clearly disambiguates in this case, but not in
other similar cases.) The usual correction was to 
say {semau tu'a le sorprekarce} or  {le cmalu trene 
ne semau le sorprekarce}, but if we don't want any rules
for BAIs I suppose this one will now become acceptable 
too.

It seems to me, based on the majority of usage, that abu bi'i by is the 
interval from A to B, not a point on that interval. A point on the interval 
could be denoted by {lu'a .abu bi'i by}, but {bi'i} can connect brivla and 
even whole bridi, so I don't see how to denote a point on an interval between 
them. Any suggestions?

What does {mi klama le zarci .ibi'ibo do cliva le tcadu} mean, anyway?

My interpretation:
It asserts to two events that occurred or will potentially occur, not 
necessarily in any particular order, and draws focus to the time-space 
interval between those two events, for purposes that must be inferred 
from context since nothing is asserted about said interval.

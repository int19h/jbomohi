On 8/10/06, MorphemeAddict@wmconnect.com <MorphemeAddict@wmconnect.com> wrote:
> What's a "li-abstraction"?

Mainly numbers, but there are other things such as dates and clock-times
that are li-abstractions in Lojban too.

> Isn't "li" a gadri, like "le" and "lo",

Yes. Not exactly alike because they are in different selma'o, but they are
all gadri. All gadri can be used to refer to abstractions.

>and not
> an abstraction like "nu" and "za'i"?

{nu} and {za'i} are usually classed as abstractors in Lojban, i.e. members
of selma'o NU. (They may be abstractions too in the sense that any word
could be considered an abstraction. Perhaps we should also include
text-abstractions as another sumti class to consider.)

Gee, I hate to go all ruley, but this case seems
different from the cases over in Epistmology. 
This is not about a transformation but about what
a BAI phrase modifies (which I suppose does come
out to be acertain kind of transformation): "We
more than the bus like the small train (trolley?)
vs. we like more that the bus the small train
(damn, this needs diagrams but you get the idea).
 Since these are sumtcita of quantity, this must
add a quantity place to the predication.  But
that doesn't help, since "How  much?" can be
answered by either "More than the bus does" or
"More than we like the bus."  {le ni mi'o nelci
le cmalu trene cu zmadu le ni mi'o nelci le
sorprekarce} v
{le ni mi'o nelci le cmalu trene cu zmadu le ni
le sorprekarce cu nelci le cmaulu trene}.  
One way to deal with it I suppose is to put the
added place next to what it revolves on {mio se
mau le sorprekarce cu ...} v what is given here. 
  With a fronted or reared version that is
ambiguous in the way this seems to be (and is in
English).  The stock response, that the
subject-bus case is has an abstraction at its
base while the object-bus case does not doesn't
really seem to have a lot of basis in underlying
structures.  But then we just got rid of
underlying structures as significant here.  Which
brings me back to thinking this is in principle a
different kind of case, but leaves me no clearer
on what the difference is or why it is
significant.

The notion of a significant number is a brilliant addition.  But "most" ought to be "more than half" (and perhaps in a different section -- with "all of" and "some of," say), not just "all but several."
These numbers are significantly more subjective than the "subjective" ones.
Note that most of these are class-relative: there aren't many tigers but many of the ones there are are in zoos.  A (weak) argument for restricted quantification.

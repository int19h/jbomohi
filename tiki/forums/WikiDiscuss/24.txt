"(quantifiers typically go with the axis = present tense marker)."

I'm not sure what that entails, but in Lojban we have:

ki: axis marker
nau: speaker's (spatiotemporal) present marker 
ca: simultaneity marker

{ca} often indicates simultaneity with both axis and speaker's 
present, but not always, and those two don't always coincide.

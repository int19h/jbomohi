00:02:00 <zipcpi> Lojban words often have very specific and literal meanings
00:03:00 <zipcpi> Take for example, the English word "see"
00:03:00 <zipcpi> In the dictionary it is split into three main definitions
00:03:00 <zipcpi> "perceive with the eyes; discern visually." -> {viska}
00:04:00 <zipcpi> "discern or deduce mentally after reflection or from information; understand." -> {jimpe}
00:04:00 <zipcpi> "meet (someone one knows) socially or by chance." -> {penmi}
00:04:00 <gleki> mi parolas esperanton
00:05:00 <zipcpi> The second and third senses grew out of metaphoric senses of the English word "see", but in Lojban it is encouraged to use the right word for the right job
00:06:00 <hndcchdd> what about poetry?

00:06:00 <zipcpi> There is a word in Lojban explicitly used to mark a word as having a metaphoric sense, {pe'a}. One can assume that a poem is wrapped in it

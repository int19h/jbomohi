05:39:00 <gleki> The most important differences that immediately come to mind are:
05:39:00 <gleki> 1. In Lojban people start to understand more where metaphors start in their speech. Metaphors are important in our speech but sometimes they can lead us to incorrect reasoning. It's nice that in Lojban you can both use and avoid metaphors when you like.
05:39:00 <gleki> 2. In Lojban punctuation like e.g. quote marks is pronounceable. Thus, it's easy to know when a quote starts and ends even in colloquial speech.

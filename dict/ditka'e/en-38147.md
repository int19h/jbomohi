$x_1=k_1$ is able/can do $x_2=k_2=d_2$ (state/event) under conditions $x_3=k_3$  (state/event) in time $x_4=d_1$

## Notes

x4 defaults to the natural duration of the event x2 ({ca'o}) so that it also means "x1 has enough time to do x2". See {ditcu}, {kakne}.

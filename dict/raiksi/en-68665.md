$x_1$ is superlative in $x_2$ (property of $x_1$) among $x_3$ (full set)

## Notes

Applies the property in $x_2$ to every element of $x_3$, compares values produced by $x_2$ and determines the greatest value, which is $x_1$. Use {to'e} to produce the inverse meaning of 'least' or invert the scale in $x_2$.

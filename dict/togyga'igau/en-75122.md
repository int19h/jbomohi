$x_{1}$ tunes $x_{2}$ (instrument) to A4 = $x_{3}$ Hz in temperament $x_{4}$.

## Notes

Pitch A4 refers to middle A in Scientific pitch notation (SPN), which is equivalent to a' in Helmholtz pitch notation (HPN). Default $x_{4}$ is twelve-tone equal temperament. See also {tonga}, {zgike}, {zgica'a}.

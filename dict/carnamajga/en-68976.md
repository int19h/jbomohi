$x_1$ (li; tensor with units of $kg*m^2$) is the moment of inertia/angular inertial-mass/rotational inertia of object $x_2$ around axis/point of reference $x_3$ in basis/coordinate system $x_4$.

## Notes

Physics is all about expressing the same thing in different ways in order to gain new insight about it. There are other ways to express a mathematical quantity which is equivalent to this one (much as gravitational charge and inertial mass are mathematically mutually equal but not conceptually identical).

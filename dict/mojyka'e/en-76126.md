$x_1$ can remember, recall facts $x_2$ about $x_3$; $x_1$ has facts $x_2$ stored in their head.

## Notes

This is different than just {morji} — assuming that *morji* isn’t an ability, but a point event of recalling or retrieving something that you have stored in your brain. (If *morji* is an ability instead — and the English definition is quite ambiguous on this — then use *morji* for the ability, and {mojga'e} for the point event of recalling something).

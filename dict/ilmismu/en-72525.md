$x_1$ is an ismu of $x_2$, having the same meaning, but with all places except the $x_3$-th (li/set) zi'o-deleted.

## Notes

For example, "zo .{inku} .ilmismu zo {jinku} li pa": {inku} is {jinku} with only the x1. Named after la .ilmen, who pioneered defining such words. See {ismu}.

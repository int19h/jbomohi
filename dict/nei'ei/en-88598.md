repeats the current bridi.

## Notes

For those who choose to use a Lojban standard that includes the {jboponei} dialect, {po'au} and {nei'ei} can be used to recover the original {po} and {nei} words.  To avoid confusion, after using {jboponei} in your Lojban, you can say «goi'ai jboponei».  See also: {lo'u'a} (pre-xorlo {lo}).

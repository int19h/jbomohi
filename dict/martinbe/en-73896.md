$x_1=t_1=m_2$ conforms to / is compliant with standard $x_3=t_3=m_1$ with rules $x_2=t_2=m_3$; $x_1$ is standard.

## Notes

Synonym: {marmansa}.  See also: {marbartu} (experimental), {ca'irselzau} (official, e.g. by an owner of a standard through the owner's authority).

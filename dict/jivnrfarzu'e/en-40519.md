$x_1$ (mass/set) do orienteering in competition of type $x_2$ [property of the event] with (other) rule(s) $x_3$ [property of each participant].

## Notes

The x2 is the major type of the orienteering event, be it Score-O, point-to-point, or other types such as MountainBike-O, etc. The x3 is a property of the individual participants, including things like "not allowed to go beyond point X" or "beating up other participants is allowed" etc. The tanru "jivnrfarzu'e se gunma" is sufficient to say "I do orienteering".

$c_1$ requests/asks $c_2$ to go back/return to $x_2$ from $x_3$.

## Notes

Cf. {xruti}, {cpedu}.  Based on proposed redefinition of {xruti} by xorxes: x1 returns to x2 from x3.

## Examples

### Example 157

.i .abu ji'i re roi trixe catlu fau lo nu xadba pacna lo du'u lo re mei cu xrucpe (la .alis. bu'u lo selmacygu'e, Chapter 7).

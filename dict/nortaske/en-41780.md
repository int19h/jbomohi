$t_1$ is neither quenched from nor thirsty for drink/fluid/lubricant $t_2$.

## Notes

On a scale between “quenched” and “thirsty”, $t_1$ is roughly at the midpoint. See also: {taske}, {toltaske}, {no'e}.

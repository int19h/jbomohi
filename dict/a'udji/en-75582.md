$x_1$ is interested in doing $x_2$.

## Notes

People often say {a'u} as an interjection meaning this, but a corresponding verb is missing (se {cinri} is ‘to be interested’ in a thing or a topic, ‘to be fascinated’, not to be interested in doing something).

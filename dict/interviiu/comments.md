i'enai

1. A conversation in person (or, by extension, over the telephone, Internet
etc.) between a journalist and someone whose opinion or statements he or 
she wishes to record for publication, broadcast etc.
2. A formal meeting, in person, for the assessment of a candidate or 
applicant.
3. A police interrogation of a suspect or party in an investigation.

i do jinvi ma

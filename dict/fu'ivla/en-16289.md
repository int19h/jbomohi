$x_1=v_1=f_1$ is a loanword meaning $x_2=v_2$ in language $x_3=v_3$, based on word $x_4=f_2$ in language $x_5$.

## Notes

Also officialy used to describe words in the Lojban morphological class allocated for use by {vonfu'ivla}. Due to not being {jbosarxe} {zi'evla} is prefered by some. See also {pavyfu'ivla}, {relfu'ivla}, {cibyfu'ivla}, {vonfu'ivla}, {fanvyfu'ivla}, {zi'evla}, {le'avla}.

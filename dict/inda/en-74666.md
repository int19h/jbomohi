$x_1$ deserves / merits / is worthy of $x_2$ (property of $x_1$) according to / by standard $x_3$.

## Notes

See also: {pairjerna}, {jerna}.  Based on Ilmen's earlier definition, with an extra {ma'i} place to allow for subjective judgements (objective claims can still be made by representing ‘the objective’ (e.g. «lo fatci») in $x_3$) (e.g. private economic actors may consider goods' worthiness differently by their freedom of choice, and cultures can vary in values).

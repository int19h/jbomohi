$x_1$ is a former/previously was but no longer is a $x_2$ as of time $x_3$ according to standard $x_4$; $x_1$ is an ex-$x_2$

## Notes

$x_2$ should be a role or possession of a property. $x_4$ is the standard by which $x_2$ both was claimed (to have been) and is now no longer claimed (to be); cessation of the state of being an example of an $x_2$ must have occurred in the past and must still (in the present) not apply. It may be regained in the future, but this case is not considered, under normal circumstances, to be resumptive: the state was done/terminated (but not necessarily finished) and then may be started anew. See also: {jgu'ai}

sumti place tag: apply arbitrary sumti application or termset

## Notes

This is {pi'a'e} (see {joi'u'i} and {pi'a'e}) but as FA rather than like LAhE, a subtle distinction.

ni'o One use is to apply a termset bundled as a single sumti (e.g. with {joi'u'i}, {sumpoi}, {lo}{du'u}, or another mechanism).  Additionally, the sumti following {fa'u'i} can also include in its bundle an alternative method of how the (rest of the) sumti is meant to be applied (optionally with brevity with {ju'e}, {joi}, {ju'e}{ma'o'ei}, or a bridi).  How the application is done can be specified metalinguistically (see also {baunpli}), separately, or as metadata inside the sumti.

ni'o See also: {lo'oi}, for one possible way to analogize {se} for {fa'u'i}; {pli'aje}.

ni'o Etymology: {fa} and {nu'i}.  Note that {joi'u'i} builds a composite sumti like a termset, and {fa'u'i} applies it.  {fa'u'i} is a little less regular in its word formation since whereas {joi'u'i} might be seen as signalling the start of a termset, {fa'u'i} doesn't, but instead applies a termset.

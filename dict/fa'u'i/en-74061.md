sumti place tag: apply arbitrary sumti application or termset

## Notes

One use is to apply a termset bundled as a single sumti (e.g. with {sumpoi} or {lo}{du'u}).  Additionally, the sumti following {fa'u'i} can also include in its bundle an alternative method of how the (rest of the) sumti is meant to be applied (optionally with brevity with {ju'e}, {joi}, {ju'e}{ma'o'ei}, or a bridi).  How the application is done can be specified metalinguistically (see also {baunpli}), separately, or as metadata inside the sumti.

ni'o See also: {lo'oi}, for one possible way to analogize {se} for {fa'u'i}; {pli'aje}.

ni'o Etymology: {fa} and {nu'i}.

UI discursive: litotes - - anti-litotes

## Notes

Litotes is the usage of a negative or understatement in order to imply a strong confirmation. Anti-litotes might be taken to be similar to sarcasm of the form of strongly asserting something in order to imply the fact that it is negated or notably mild.

$x_1=m_1=s_1$ is less exact/precise than $x_2=m_2$ is in property / quantity $x_3=s_3$ by amount $x_4=m_4$.

## Notes

{satcyme'a} uses «-me'a» to insert $mleca_2$ at $x_2$ (and append $mleca_4$; see also {fi'o}) (this lujvo construction also adapts $zmadu_3$ to $satci_2$ rather than a relationship that includes it, making it slightly less regular, since it's much more useful and the alternative doesn't seem very useful; see also the difference between {zmanei} and {nelmau}).  See also: {satcymau}.

bu'onai; the idea of this is to create a convenient impersonal pronoun one 
can use for "generic-one" or "generic-you" (many languages have simple 
pronouns for this purpose, even if they avoid using the second-person). For
situations with more generalized/hypothetical actors, there's always {goi}.

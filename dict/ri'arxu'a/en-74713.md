$x_1=xu_1$ declares/specifies $x_2=xu_2=r_1$ causing $x_3=r_2$.

## Notes

The gismu {xusra} is broader than just declarations.  See also: {xu'ai} (I declare), {xu'asmu} (define), {ki'irxu'a} (assign / designate), {te'iski} (synonym), {tinbe} (obey / conform), {javni}, {catni}, {ponse}.

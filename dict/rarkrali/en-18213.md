$k_1=r_1$ (NU) is a natural right of $k_2$ (individual/mass) by standard $k_3$.

## Notes

Rights which are not contingent upon the laws, customs, or beliefs of a particular society or polity, thus necessarily universal; usually contrasted to legal rights ({flakrali}), which are culturally and politically relative. Cf. {krali}.

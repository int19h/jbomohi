the Solar System (notice: proper noun); Sol system.

## Notes

As opposed to a generic solar/stellar system. This is the one which is approximately centered on and dominated by the Sun/Sol and which contains Jupiter and Earth, among many other objects. The native stellar system of humanity; the human solar system. For the generic term, use "{tarci'e}".

$v_{1}$=$x_{1}$ is a rotavirus of species/breed/defining property $v_{2}$ capable of infecting [at] $v_{3}$.

## Notes

x2 may be abu (A), by (B), cy (C),dy (D) or ebu (E). Rotavirus A, the most common, causes more than 90% of infections in humans. See also {vidru}, {norvokovidru} and {remjinkytoldu'evidru}.

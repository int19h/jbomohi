$x_1$ relates to/is an instance of ce-ki-tau dialect $x_3$ in aspect $x_2$

## Notes

ce-ki-tau dialects are a class of Lojban word-swapping dialects in which infrequently-used short cmavo (typically CV or CVV) are swapped with commonly used long cmavo (CV'V). There are several ce-ki-tau dialects, such as {tcekitaujau}. See {bankle} and {banckle}.

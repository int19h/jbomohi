binary operator: complex number from argument and phase, $(r, \phi) \mapsto r e^{i \phi}$

## Notes

For example, {re} {ca'oi} {pai} is −2, and {pa} {ca'oi} {vei} {pai}{fe'i}{vo} is about 0.7071 + 0.7071i.

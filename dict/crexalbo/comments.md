crexalbo does not mean "nerd"

crexalbo: expert/pro - levity
The definition of crexalbo as "geek/nerd" doesn't make sense in several 
aspects
a) parsed from right to left (xalbo as head modified by certu) as with 
tanru this word would result in "someone who uses levity whilst somehow 
being expert of this using of levity" or an "expert of levity"
b) generally (for example at dictionary.com) a geek/nerd is an expert in a 
narrow field, also the lack of social integration is included in this 
definition. Someone who is an expert (probably executing the expert 
activity with levity) would rather apply to some kind of natural talent or 
so (I'd guess)

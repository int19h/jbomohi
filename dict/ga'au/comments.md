Importance

Little gamma is among the seven most important numbers in mathematics, and
the list narrows to five if we exclude integers. The most important
numbers in mathematics are (generally in order of not-necessarily-strictly
monotonically decreasing importance): 0, 1, e, circle constants, i, little
gamma, and the golden ratio. Of these, only little gamma did not earn a
basic cmavo in Lojban.	This wrong has now been righted.

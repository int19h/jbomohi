$x_1$ (text) is the name of family $x_2$ (partial set/list of individuals or, better, a graph structure) by name-user $x_3$. 

## Notes

$x_2$ is not an individual; it is a structure which includes, explicitly or implicitly, every individual who belongs to the family (at least, enough to bear the name implicitly according to $x_3$). $x_1$ need not be part of the official name of any individual in $x_2$; it is just a way by which to refer to $x_2$ as a whole.

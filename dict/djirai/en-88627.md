$x_1$ is/does $x_2$ as much as $x_3$ wants them to.

## Notes

In general usage, the $x_3$ is expected to be left to context: «.e’a do djirai lo ni citka» ‘you can eat as much as wanted’ (i.e. as much as *you* want), but it can be stated explicitly for completeness. See also {cumrai} ‘as … as possible’ , and also {tankomo} ‘as … as …’, the general case.

$x_1$ orbits something which orbits ... which orbits something which orbits $x_2$ via intermediate steps $x_3$ (ce'o).

## Notes

Could also apply to gravitationally bound bodies, regardless of the paths which they take.

## Examples

### Example 1017

lo jakne cu cuklitry'utka lo galki midju lo mluni ce'o lo plini ce'o lo tarci

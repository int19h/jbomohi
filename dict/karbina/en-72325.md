$x_1$ and $x_2$ are elements of the same partially-ordered set $x_3$ (see notes) such that $x_1$ and $x_2$ cannot be meaningfully compared via said relation/in said property.

## Notes

Neither is more nor less than the other because those relations/concepts simply do not make sense for the given pair. $x_3$ accepts sumti of either of two types/structures: (1) a partially-ordered set in which both the underlying set and the order relation on it are specified explicitly (see: "{cmacistura}"); (2) a simple "ka" construct indicating a property or characteristic which roughly functions as the basis of an assumed partial-order relation on an unspecified or vague partially-ordered set, in a fashion which is grammatically approximately analogous to "{zmadu}"'s structure.

$x_1$ is the "ce ki tau jau" dialect of Lojban, used by $x_2$ to communicate $x_3$ (si'o/du'u)

## Notes

Place structure based on {bangu}. Refers to a dialect of Lojban that swaps certain longer, common, cmavo with shorter, rarer, cmavo: see http://mw.lojban.org/papri/ce_ki_tau_jau. Use of this dialect can be marked with "{jo'au} tcekitaujau". Also see {tcekitaujei}.

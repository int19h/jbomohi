$x_1$ is a polygon with $x_2$ sides.

## Notes

sor-jga-tai < {so'i} + {jganu} + {tarmi}. A calque of "polygon" (poly- many, -gon angle). [Even though "many" here actually means "3 or more", {so'i} was chosen for a shorter resulting compound.] Arbitrary polygon names are formed with rafsi numbers plus -jgatai: {cibjgatai}, {vonjgatai}, {mumjgatai}, etc.

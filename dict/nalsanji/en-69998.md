$x_1$ is unconscious/not aware of $x_2$ (object/abstract).

## Notes

This is unconsciousness wrt a specific thing ($x_2$); widen for generalized unconsciousness. This may more or less include (some aspects of) sleep, but sleep is a very specific and complex process, and most forms of unconsciousness - let alone a simple and narrowly-specified lack of awareness - do not function in a manner which is even remotely similar to sleep. For generalized unconsciousness, use "{sojnalsanji}". This is NOT "subconscious" in many meanings or contexts. See also: {sipna}.

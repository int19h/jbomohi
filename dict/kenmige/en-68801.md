$x_1$ is to $x_2$ as $x_3$ is to $x_4$ as $x_5$ is to $x_6$ ...

## Notes

Takes an even number of arguments (is 2n-ary). Also means: x1 is an x2 type of x3. More widely known as a kenning; x1 : x2 :: x3 : x4 :: x5 : x6... One can use sequences ({ce'o}) as x2, x4, x6 if more than 2 things form a row.

$x_1$ is (a file) linked from intermediate files/data/libraries $x_2$ by linker $x_3$; $x_3$ links $x_2$ into $x_1$.

## Notes

Refers specifically to the linking stage of compilation. {samrkompli} can refer to the overarching build process as a whole, or simply as the stage where source code becomes intermediate files, depending on the type of its x1.

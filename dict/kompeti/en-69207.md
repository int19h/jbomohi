$x_1$ (group of entity) compete with each other in competition $x_2$ (property of each member of $x_1$) where each member of $x_1$ has goal $x_3$ (property of member of $x_1$) that is the same for all members of $x_1$

## Notes

$x_2$ is usually a name, e.g. 'mi jo'u la alis cu kompeti la tenis lo ka fo ri ce'u jinga fi ce'u', 'mi jo'u la alis cu kompeti lo ka bajra vau lo ka raiksi lo ka sutra vau lo me vo'a'. When some members don't share $x_2$ or $x_3$ with all other members use {jivna}.

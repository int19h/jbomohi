$t_1$ is the sibling-in-law of $t_2$

## Notes

$t_3$: $t_1$ is, or is a sibling of, $s_1$, $s_1$ is married to $s_2$, $t_2$ is, or is a sibling of, $s_2$, $t_1=s_1$ and $t_2=s_2$ is not true. Cf. {tunba} {speni}

Delete subsequent sumti slots.

## Notes

Behaves exactly like "{zi'oi}" except that the sumti slots which are filled and deleted in the manner of "{zi'o}" are exactly those ones which (otherwise) belong to the tagged word and which strictly follow the natural-last sumti slot (after SE conversion and FA evaluation) which has been explicitly filled (including by "{zo'e}"); all prior sumti slots in the structure of the tagged word (after SE conversion) remain and, definitionally, either are explicitly filled or are implicitly "zo'e"-filled. Use FA + "zo'e" if you wish to access a much later sumti slot without filling all intermediate/intervening sumti slots explicitly. "by klama zi'oi'oi fi cy" and "klama zi'oi'oi zo'e cy fa by" are semantically equivalent, both meaning "B comes/goes (klama) from C (to somewhere unspecified), with the klama$_4$ route and the klama$_5$ means both being deleted"; notice how bothbthe semantic equivalence between the two example phrases and their individual meanings are lost/altered if "zi'oi'oi" were replaced by "zi'oi".

$x_1$ is the PA-th date/time as $x_2$ (interval unit) by standard point $x_3$ (,which is 0th) by calender/by standard of the interval unit $x_4$

## Notes

How to splice is dependent on x_4. 0th's x_2 is associated with / is including x_3. For example, at 14:03 11/14/2014, 0th's day (x_2 = lo djedi) is 11/14; 0th's century is 21 century; 0th's month (x_2 = lo masti) is November; 0th's night (x_2 = lo nicte) is 11/14's night; "lo no moi'o be lo djedi / today", "lo pa moi'o be lo nanca / next year", "lo ni'u moi'o be lo masti / last month"; ".e'o ko klama lo zdani be mi ca lo no moi'o be lo nicte / Could you come to my house this night?"

$x_1$ is up relative to $x_2$ from point of view (frame of reference) $x_3$

## Notes

Opposite of {cnita}. For "up" in the direction of gravity specifically, see {galtu}. On many maps, by convention, "north" ({berti}) is "up" on the page.

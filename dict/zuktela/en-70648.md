$x_1$ (entity) takes action $x_2$ (property of $x_1$) or $x_2$ (entity) takes action $x_1$ (property of $x_2$) in order to $x_3$ (clause)

## Notes

Showcase of semantic autocorrection of places. If $x_2$ can be meaningfully filled with a property and $x_1$ can't then those places are filled accordingly otherwise the slots are to be reversed. See also {zukte}.

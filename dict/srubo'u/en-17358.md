$s_1=b_1$ is a rib of $s_2=b_2$.

## Notes

During the development of mammalian embryos, fused-on remnants of ribs can be traced in neck vertebrae (cervical ribs) and sacral vertebrae. In reptiles, ribs sometimes occur in all vertebrae from the neck to the sacrum. Cf. {bongu}, {cutne}.

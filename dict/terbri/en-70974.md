$x_1$ (sequence of sumti) is the sequence of arguments that, joined by relation $x_2$ (ka), form predicate $x_3$ (du'u)

## Notes

In Lojban, for example, "mi ce'o do cu terbri lo ka ce'u prami ce'u ku lo du'u mi prami do". In the mathematical notation P(x, y, z) for a predicate, the sequence/tuple (x, y, z) is the terbri. Note that $x_1$ is not textual, even though Lojbanists informally tend to call a word or phrase "(one of) the terbri" of an utterance. For this usage see {terbrisni}.

Concerns of similarity with {xutla}

{xulta} is quite close to {xutla}, which could cause problems in learning
and with typos. But there are some other word pairs that are at least
somewhat similar; many lujvo are; among gismu, {xrula} and {xlura} readily
come to mind; as more of gismu space is used up, the problem will become
more common (unless some additional rule prevents such occupations, in
which case we lose much of gismu space). I ultimately do not think that
this issue is that big of a deal.

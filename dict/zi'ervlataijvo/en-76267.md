$x_1$ is a zi'evla-shaped compound meaning $x_2$ with arguments $x_3$ built from phrase/tanru $x_4$.

## Notes

Refers to words are formed the same way as a lujvo by combining words or their affixes but have forms classed as fu'ivla/zi'evla due to different ways of combining. These words often have a fu'ivla/zi'evla at the end (eg. CCV-CVV-CVCCVCV, CV'V-r-CCVCVCV, CVC-CV'VCCV), but may also consist of gismu forms in full combined (e.g. CCVCV-CVCCV) or contain epenthetic consonants besides -r- or -n-, but never the epenthetic vowel -y-. Existing examples include {jugmantodi}, {cmaraimutsteila}, {frikoxazdo}, {ko'elmoi}. See also {lujvo}, {fu'ivla}, {zi'evla}.

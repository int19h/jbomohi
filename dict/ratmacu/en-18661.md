$x_1$ is a mouse/rat (family Muridae) of species/breed $x_2$

## Notes

Cf. {ratcu}, {smacu}, {mabru}. This term is a combination of ratcu + smacu for those speakers (Chinese, Japanese, for instance) who usually don't distinguish between mice and rats.

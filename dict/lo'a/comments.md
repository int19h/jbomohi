Restriction of definition

This word (or another, to-be-invented word) should mean strictly a shift to
the Latin alphabet. Lojban assumes no natural means of transcription, thus 
there should be no favoring of the Latin script by making it the default or
assumed equivalent of a "Lojbanic script". I have proposed {jo'au'o} for 
the natural state ("Lojban shift") since I analyzed this word to be derived
from {latmo}. If this option is dispreferred, and this word is analyzed to 
derive from "Lojban", then I propose {ro'au'o} take on the purpose of an 
explicitly and strictly Latin shift.

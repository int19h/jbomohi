mnemonic for memorizing original FAhA3 in sequence.

## Notes

In English, perhaps ‘near it, by penetration’ may aid in the memorization of «joi'u'i ce'o zo ne'a zo re'o zo te'e zo bu'u zo pa'o zo ne'i [nu'u]».  In Lojban, perhaps «nenri ta'i bi pagre nu nerkla lo kubli», which also happens to describe the original FAhA2 cmavo seen as 6 sides of a cube plus {ru'u} and experimental {ma'a'u} (to the side of).

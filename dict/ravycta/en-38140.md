$c_1=r_1$ looks at/examines/views/inspects/regards/watches/gazes at $c_2=r_3$ through gap/see-through boundary $r_2$

## Notes

$x_3$ is see-through to $x_1$, but not necessarily see-through to all. Cf. {pagre}.

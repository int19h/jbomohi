$m_1$ is the sister-in-law of $m_2$

## Notes

$m_3$: $m_1$ is a woman who is, or is a sister of, $s_1$, $s_1$ is married to $s_2$, $m_2$ is, or is a sibling of, $s_2$, $m_1=s_1$ and $m_2=s_2$ is not true. Cf. {mensi} {speni}

$x_1$ makes $x_2$ angry / mad at $x_3$ for $x_4$ (action / state / property); $x_1$ angers / provokes / stirs up $x_2$.

## Notes

Unlike the usual sense of "to anger", the person causing x2 to be angry (i.e., x1) isn't necessarily the target of the resulting anger (x3).

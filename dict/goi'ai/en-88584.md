vocative: indicate which (experimental) grammar / standard / dialect / language specification is used.

## Notes

Can be a single feature, or a complete specification or standard of many choices.  Can be used after «ko'a broda tau ko'e» to indicate «goi'ai la tcekitau».  See also: {gi'ai} (experimental grammar UI rather than COI), {jo'au} (change language COI).  {jo'au} specifies a language switch, while {goi'ai} indicates or makes a claim of what is being used.

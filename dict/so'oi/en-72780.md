Initiator of subordinating incidental adverbial relative clause with leftwards logical scope. The adverbial clause binds the outer clause to the resumptive pronoun {ke'a}; the outer clause is also incidentally claimed to be true). Terminator: {se'u}.

## Notes

This cmavo is synonymous to the so-called "new-soi", described at https://mw.lojban.org/papri/new_soi

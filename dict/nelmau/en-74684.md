$x_1=z_1=n_1$ is a greater liker than $x_2=z_2$ is of $x_3=n_2$ by amount/excess $x_4=z_4$.

## Notes

This follows the pattern of «-mau» inserting what $x_1$ is compared to into $x_2$ (and appending $z_4$).  While it is still possible to choose an alternative place structure, for different place structures consider inserting «mau» elsewhere.  See also: {zmanei} (prefer).

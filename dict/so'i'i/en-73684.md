digit/number: complement of "{so'e'i}"; a qualifying, controlling, or decision-making minority; a subminority.

## Notes

If a vote needs 60% approval in order to pass, then one more voter than the floor of the 40% requirement would be the minimal value which could be referenced by this word; this word does not necessarily refer to exactly the minimal (or maximal) possible such value. It could also refer instead to, simply, a substantial or sizeable minority, if the context does not well-define a 'qualifying minority'. See also: "{so'i}", "{so'e'i}".

$x_1$ philosophizes/cogitates/ruminates/thinks profoundly about topic $x_2$, with specifics of thought $x_3$ and methodology $x_4$, belonging to school/branch/super- philosophy $x_5$; $x_1$ is a philosopher/philosophe (not necessarily professional or trained).

## Notes

{flese} is an experimental gismu. This word does not imply a professional, trained, expert, credible, or even habitual/common attendance to philosophical (mental) faculty, but does imply a bit more profundity then mere thought or passing notion. See also: {filsofo}, {flese}.

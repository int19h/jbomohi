non-logical connective: stable/static compose - neutral compose - dynamic/changing compose.

## Notes

Using the jacus grammar extension, {jo'a'i} (or {jo'a'inai}) can be used between consecutive NAhE to indicate the scale basis is unchanged (or uses specifically different scales).  See also: {a'a'e}, {pi'u'i}, {joi'u'i}.

$x_1$ loses prize $x_2$ to winner $x_3$ in competition $x_4$.

## Notes

Synonyms: te {jinga}, {tolji'a}. If the frame of {jinga} is ever revised to '$x_1$ wins competition, game $x_2$', and 'win a prize' is moved over to something like {ji'acpa}, {losxa} would follow suit ('$x_1$ loses game $x_2$'), possibly with {lo'acri} for 'to lose a prize'.

discursive: experimental grammar - conformant grammar.  Indicates experimental grammar relative to some standard or dialect.

## Notes

{bi'a} indicates an emphasized word, {zi'a} indicates a nonce word, {ci'ai} indicates an experimental utterance, and {gi'ai} indicates an experimental grammar.  See also: {marbartu} (experimental), {martinbe} (standard).

$x_1=c_1=k_1$ is separate from / not with $x_2=c_2=k_2$ in state/condition/enterprise $x_3=k_3$; $x_1$ is individual / alone / separate.

## Notes

Synonym: {sepli}.

ni'o See also: modal {koi'au}, for specifying individuality in a sumti e.g. with «{lo} {koi'au} {broda}» rather than leaving individuality-vs-togetherness vague and general, or alternatively use {terle'o} to more expressly indicate ‘as individuals’ (see {le'oi} for more information on this pattern of usage).  See also {karncau}, a fu'ivla synonym variant of {kancau} specialized for this purpose of specifying (non)-individuality.

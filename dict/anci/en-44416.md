$x_1$ hints, alludes, provides a clue at $x_2$ (object, event, numeral); $x_2$ is hinted

## Notes

$x_1$ is the object providing a clue to $x_2$. To specify agent perceiving the hint use {ga'a}. {lo} {nu} {anci} - 'a hint / an act of hinting'

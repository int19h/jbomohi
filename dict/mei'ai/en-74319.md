$x1$ is (n) less than $x2$ in metric $x3$ [ni, default cardinality]

## Notes

I.e. $|x2| - n = |x1|$ where $x3$ determines how to measure $|x|$.  Practically, this can be used, e.g. «djica tu'a le pa zau'ei be lei titla» for "I want one less candy". See also: {zau'ei}, {ze'au}, {ja'au} Etymology: {me'a}+{mei} (note that me'a ny. mei means something different).

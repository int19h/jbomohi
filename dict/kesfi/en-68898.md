$x_1$ flies through medium $x_2$ (not necessarily a fluid/atmosphere) using means of propulsion $x_3$.

## Notes

Generalization of {volmna} or {vofli} such that $x_2$ is not necessarily a fluid; in outerspace, spacecraft cannot volmna (thus, they cannot vofli) because there is not fluid there - they do, however, kesfi be lo kensa. This is different from {kru'uze} in that internally-supplied forces (such as rocket propulsion) are possible.

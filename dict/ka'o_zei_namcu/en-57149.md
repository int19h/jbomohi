$x_1$ is a complex number (understood as such).

## Notes

Not necessarily strictly imaginary (or real, for that matter). This word relies on {xo'ei} to implicitly fill both sides of {ka'o}. See also: {ka'o'ai zei namcu}, {noka'ono} (with PA neither immediately before nor immediately after it), {nozeika'o zei namcu}, and {ka'ozeino zei namcu}.

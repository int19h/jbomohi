$x_1$ (text) is a gloss word/keyword (or phrase) for the meaning of $x_2$ (du'u/text)  in language $x_3$

## Notes

If $x_2$ is filled with an abstraction (specifically, a {du'u} construct), the meaning is the content expressed therewith; if $x_2$ is a quoted text/word, the meaning is the semantic referent set or relationship content expressed thereby.

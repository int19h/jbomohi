$f_1$ is the legal system concerning topic $f_2$ in society $f_3$

## Notes

cf. {flalu}, {ciste}. Refers to "the law" as an entity, i.e. the legal system. Also used in compounds describing specific areas of law, such as lanzu flaci'e (family law) or zekri flaci'e (criminal law).

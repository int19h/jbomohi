$x_1$ translates $x_2$ (text) to $x_3$ (text)

## Notes

Names of languages or cultures like {glico} can be used to denote $x_2$ and $x_3$. Example: {ra} {traduce} lo {glico} lo {lojbo} - 'He translates from English to Lojban'.

$x_1$ translates $x_2$ (text) to $x_3$ (text); $x_1$ translates $x_2$ (text) producing $x_3$ (text)

## Notes

Example: {ra} {traduce} lo {glico} lo {lojbo} - 'He translates from English to Lojban'. See {fanva}

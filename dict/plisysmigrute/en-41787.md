$g_1=s_1=p_1$ is a pome [fruit] of species/strain $g_2$.

## Notes

In botany, a pome (after the Latin word for fruit: pōmum) is a type of fruit (={grute}) produced by flowering plants in the subtribe Malinae of the family Rosaceae. The best-known example of a pome is the apple (={plise}). See also: plants in the subtribe Malinae of the family Rosaceae (={plisysmispa}).

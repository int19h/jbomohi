$x_1$ makes $x_2$ see (perceive visually) $x_3$ under conditions $x_4$

## Notes

One sense of "show", but more mechanism-specific than the English word; one can't "visygau fi ko'a" where ko'a is not something that can be literally seen. In these cases {arco} or {sajgau} may be preferred. Also see {jarco}.

## Notes

A {jvocmevo} meaning "{vindu} {plini}", or "toxic planet".  Named so because, in the words of Carl Sagan, it is the planet "most like Hell".  See also {relplin} {remsol} {stoplin} {remted} {remlur} {xunplin} {balplin} {melplin}.

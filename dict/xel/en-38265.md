Hel

## Notes

In Norse mythology, Hel is a being who presides over a realm of the same name. For a more cultural-neutral usage, use {daptutra} or {pacruxtutra}. See also "hell" as in the original text of the New Testament (={ce'ol}), the concept of "hell" in the Catholic Church (={infernus}), the concept of "hell" in Greek mythology (={tartarus}).

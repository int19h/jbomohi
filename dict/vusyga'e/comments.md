Sample or experience

Often when we taste something, it is incidental.  We usually eat because
we are hungry, and only sometimes in order to identify a taste. 
'vusyga'e' says nothing about the purpose of the tasting and is
appropriate when no purpose is stated.	Si I have changed 'sample' to
'experience'.  'vu'izga' by xorxes is more appropriate for tasting as
sampling.

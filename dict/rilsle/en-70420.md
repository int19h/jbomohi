$x_1$ is the unit of a form of rhythm of $x_2$

## Notes

For example, "lo fi'u vo si'e cu rilsle la romans.anonimos i je ri rilti lo ci rilsle (a quarter is the unit of a form of rhythm of Romance Anónimo, and Romance Anónimo is in triple time.)" See also {rilti}, {selci}, {rilsletei}.

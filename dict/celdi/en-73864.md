$x_1$ is a group of people in committed relationships, including individuals $x_2$ connected by ties $x_3$ (ka)

## Notes

Encompasses social unions of all types and sizes; sexual, romantic and platonic. Each member is committed to at least one other member. See also {prami}, {speni}, {pendo}, {kurji}, {gletu}, {pampe'o}, {glepe'o}

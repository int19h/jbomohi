attitudinal-indicator-to-selbri conversion: $x_1$ feels the indicated emotion toward/about $x_2$.

## Notes

The inverse of {ki'ai}. The $x_2$ place is defined as the thing to which x1 would apply the attitudinal indicator to when using the attitudinal to express the feeling: {mi} ci'au {iu} {do} communicates much the same thing as using {do} {iu} in a bridi. See also {inmo}, {cinmo} and the {uinmo} series of zi'evla: {uinmo}, {u'inmo}, {uanmo}, etc. Terminated by {ci'au'u}

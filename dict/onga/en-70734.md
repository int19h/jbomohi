$x_1$ (text) is written in a dialect where selbri in every bridi-tail is assumed to have {so'o'o'oi} applied to it

## Notes

Implicit {so'o'o'oi} is not applied to other selbri like direct children of sumti and selbri rules, mex-operator, operand-3, free, tense-modal. '{sei} {onga}'/'{sei} {na} {onga}' is a method of switching to/from VSO-like style in Lojban. See also {colzu'e}.

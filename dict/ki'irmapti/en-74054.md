$x_1=m_1=k_1$ corresponds to / is as $x_2=m_2=k_2$ in property / aspect $x_3=m_3=k_3$ by standard $x_4=m_4$.

## Notes

Derived from the more general and vague {mapti} with a more specific meaning, in this case as a lujvo (which supports -(')y(')- hyphens with fu'ivla components in the lujvo) rather than a less general fu'ivla.

ni'o See also: {semai'ai}, {le'oi}, {sedu'i}, {sela'u}.

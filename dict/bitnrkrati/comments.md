Reason for the zi'evla nature of this word

It does not inherit the terbri of either {bitni} or {krati} in a 
predictable manner; also, {bitni} does not quite make sense for this word 
({bitni} treats a bit as a unit whereas this word essentially treats it as 
a mathematical object).

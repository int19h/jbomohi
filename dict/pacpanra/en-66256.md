$x_1$ is an evil counterpart to $x_2$ by moral standard $x_3$, in contrast to $x_2$ having property $x_4$.

## Notes

$x_4$ is implied to be (lo ka {vrude}) "being good/virtuous", or at the very least (lo ka na'e {palci}). "being not-evil". See {panra}, {bu'a'a}

$x_1=p_1$ is a little-endian / least-significant-first sequence with ordering $x_2=p_2$ on set $x_3=p_3=j_2$, with least-significant item $x_4=j_1=v_1$ first, least important to $x_5=v_2$ in property / aspect $x_6=v_3$.

## Notes

Synonym: {vajymecyboryraipavmompoi}, {endi}.  See also: {vajli'epoi} (big-endian), {teryraipoi} (endianness).

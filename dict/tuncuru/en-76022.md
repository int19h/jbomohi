$x_1$ is a worm (tube-shaped legless invertebrate) of species/breed $x_2$.

## Notes

More specific than {curnu} (if {curnu} is taken to mean all non-arthropod invertebrates). This is mainly a form classification, as what are referred to as "worms" are from many different taxa. Does not include larval forms of insects that resemble worms. See also {danlu}, {narbogda'u}, {jincurnu}.

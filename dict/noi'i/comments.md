Parallelness

Normally, I would want to keep up the parallelness that exists between
{poi} and {noi}, {po'e} and {no'e}, {pe} and {ne}, {po'oi} and {no'oi},
etc.  But {poi'i} already exists and is apparently without the option for
such a duality, so this word was basically safe.  On the other hand,
treating this construct as a relative phrase is not a necessary
interpretation, and the "restrictive"-"nonrestrictive" duality is not
really extendable to it.

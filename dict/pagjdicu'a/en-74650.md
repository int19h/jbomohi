$x_1=c_1=j_1=p_1$ votes for [ranked choice / top] $x_2=c_2=j_2$ in poll/decision/election $x_3=j_3$ among options/alternatives $x_4=c_3$ among voters $x_5=p_2$.

## Notes

For non-instant-runoff, single-choice-only votes, a ‘vote in strategy’ («pagjdicu'a le'oi loka vampla») can be expressed to express a vote for something in strategy rather than the direct ({setsta}), actual ({fatci}) vote, e.g. in the case of a spoiler vote.

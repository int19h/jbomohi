! specific emotional states

The regular place structure is "x1 feels foo emotion about/towards x2".  Some words have extra places, noted below.

* anger: {fengu} (x2 = target of anger, x3 = reason for anger)
* annoyance: {selfanza}
* anxiety: {xanka} (x2 = object of anxiety, x3 = conditions)
* awe: {manci}
* comfort: {kufra}
* disgust: {selrigni} (x2 = disgusting matter, x3 = conditions)
* envy: {jilra} (x2 = target of envy, x3 = object/cause of envy)
* fear: {terpa}
* frustration: {steba}
* guilt: {zungi}
* happiness: {gleki}
* hate: {xebni}
* interest: {selci'i}
* love: {prami}
* panic: {xalni}
* pity: {kecti} (x2 = target of pity, x3 = reason for pity)
* pride: {jgira}
* regret: {xenru}
* relaxation: {surla}
* sadness: {badri}
* shame: {ckeji} (x2 = conditions, x3 = community)
* shock: {seljenca}
* stress: {dunku}
* surprise: {selspaji}
* tiredness: {tatpi}

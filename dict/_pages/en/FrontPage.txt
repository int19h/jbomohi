!!!Helpful tools
* Please [http://www.lojban.org/tiki/tiki-index.php?page=Corpora!search] for occurences of words before you define them.
* As natlang keywords, we want any and all words for which the Lojban word in question is a reasonable translation. Use services such as [http://www.dict.org/!dict] or [http://wordnet.princeton.edu/cgi-bin/webwn!WordNet] (English only) to find synonyms.
* For finding good keywords for single-word-untranslatable terms, try OneLook's [http://onelook.com/reverse-dictionary.shtml!reverse dictionary].
* For some old and obscure lujvo, it might be helpful to look up the corresponding Loglan complex in John W. Kennedy's [http://john.w.kennedy.googlepages.com/javaloglansoftware!Loglan Online Dictionary].
* For help on wiki formatting, see [http://www.lojban.org/jbovlaste/help/wikitext.html!the wiki text help file].

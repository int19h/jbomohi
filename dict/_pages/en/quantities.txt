! units of measurement

The regular place structure for standard units of measurement is "x1 is x2 (default 1) foo units in the relevant quality, by standard x3".  {mitre} is irregular: it has an extra place x3 to indicate the direction of measurement, with the standard in x4.

SI units:
* ampere (electric current): {xampo}
* candela (luminous intensity): {delno}
* gram (mass): {grake}
* kelvin (thermodynamic temperature): {kelvo}
* litre (volume): {litce}
* metre (length): {mitre} (irregular x3 and x4)
* mole (amount of substance): {molro}
* radian (planar angle): {radno}
* second (time duration): {snidu}
* steradian (solid angle): {stero}

Traditional time units:
* second: {snidu}
* minute: {mentu}
* hour: {cacra}
* day: {djedi}
* week: {jeftu}
* month: {masti}
* year: {nanca}

There are also words for non-standard units, the words being inherently ambiguous as to what unit is meant.  To allow for the use of subunits instead of fractional parts of the main unit, in addition to the places that standard-unit words have, these have additional places x4, x5, etc. (as many as necessary) to indicate subunit quantities.  {minli} and {rupnu} are irregular.

Local units:
* local area units: {kramu}
* local long distance units: {minli}  (x3 and x4 are swapped relative to the regular place structure)
* local major money units: {rupnu} (no subunit places)
* local mass/weight units: {bunda}
* local minor money units: {fepni}
* local short length units: {gutci}
* local volume units: {dekpu}

! numerical multipliers

The regular place structure is "x1 is foo multiple of x2 in dimension/aspect x3 (default units)".  There are no exceptions.

Decimal-based small multiples:

* 10^-1 (deci): {decti}
* 10^-2 (centi): {centi}
* 10^-3 (micro): {mikri}
* 10^-6 (micro): {mikri}
* 10^-9 (nano): {nanvi}
* 10^-12 (pico): {picti}
* 10^-15 (femto): {femti}
* 10^-18 (atto): {xatsi}
* 10^-21 (zepto): {zepti}
* 10^-24 (yocto): {gocti}

Decimal-based large multiples:

* 10^1 (deka): {dekto}
* 10^2 (hecto): {xecto}
* 10^3 (kilo): {kilto}
* 10^6 (mega): {megdo}
* 10^9 (giga): {gigdo}
* 10^12 (tera): {terto}
* 10^15 (peta): {petso}
* 10^18 (exa): {xexso}
* 10^21 (zetta): {zetro}
* 10^24 (yotta): {gotro}

Binary-based large multiples:

* 2^10 (kibi): {samki'o}
* 2^20 (mebi): {samymegdo}
* 2^30 (gibi): {samgi'o}
* 2^40 (tebi): {samterto}
* 2^50 (pebi): {sampetso}
* 2^60 (exbi): {samxexso}
* 2^70 (zebi): {samzetro}
* 2^80 (yobi): {samgotro}

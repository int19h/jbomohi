The standard lujvo scoring formula made by Lojbab and Nora ([http://www.lojban.org/tiki/tiki-download_wiki_attachment.php?attId=194#s12!]) favours apostrophes at the cost of consonant clusters. I don't think that this is necessarily the best scoring method for canonical lujvo forms, and think that the old one should be adjusted, or a new one formulated. -[tsali]

I prefer a more radical change in lujvo scoring. The canonical forms should never include CV'V rafsi. Those rafsi look like cmavo, and have as many syllables as the easily-recognized long rafsi. CVV rafsi should be restricted to final position, where they never require an r-hyphen. Thus canonical lujvo would never contain apostrophes or r-hyphens. -[brtais]

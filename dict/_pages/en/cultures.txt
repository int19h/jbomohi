! words identifying specific cultures

The regular place structure is "x1 reflects foo culture/language/nationality in aspect x2".  There are no exceptions.

* African: {friko}
* Algerian: {jerxo}
* American (United States): {merko}
* Antarctican: {dzipo}
* Arabic: {xrabo}
* Argentinian: {gento}
* Asiatic: {xazdo}
* Australian: {sralo}
* Bengali/Bangladeshi: {bengo}
* Brazilian: {brazo}
* British (United Kingdom): {brito}
* Buddhist: {budjo}
* Canadian: {kadno}
* Chinese: {jungo}
* Christian: {xriso}
* Egyptian: {misro}
* English: {glico}
* European: {ropno} (see also Indo-European)
* French/Gallic: {fraso}
* German/Germanic: {dotco}
* Greek/Hellenic: {xelso}
* Hebrew/Jewish/Israeli: {xebro}
* Hindi: {xindo}
* Hispano-American: {xispo}
* Indo-European: {xinjoiropno} (see note below)
* Indonesian: {bindo}
* Iraqi: {rakso}
* Islamic: {muslo}
* Japanese: {ponjo}
* Jehovist (Judeo-Christian-Moslem): {jegvo}
* Jordanian: {jordo}
* Latin/Romance: {latmo}
* Lebanese: {lubno}
* Libyan: {libjo}
* Lojbanic: {lojbo}
* Malay-Indonesian: {baxso}
* Malaysian/Malay: {meljo}
* Mexican: {mexno}
* Moroccan: {morko}
* North American: {bemro}
* Pakistani/Pashto: {kisto}
* Palestinian: {filso}
* Polynesian/Oceanian: {polno}
* Portuguese: {porto}
* Russian empire/Soviet/ex-Soviet: {softo}
* Russian: {rusko}
* Sanskrit/Vedic: {srito}
* Saudi: {sadjo}
* Scottish/Gaelic: {skoto}
* Semitic: {semto}
* Slavic: {slovo}
* South American: {ketco}
* Spanish: {spano}
* Syrian: {sirxo}
* Taoist: {dadjo}
* Ukrainian: {vukro}
* Urdu: {xurdo}
* Welsh: {kamro} (experimental gismu)

Note on "Indo-European": the Lojbanic Wikipedia uses "xinjoiropno" in the context of the Indo-European language group.  However, the gismu list entry for "ropno" claims to cover Indo-European languages.

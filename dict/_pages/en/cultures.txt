! words identifying specific cultures

The regular place structure is "x1 reflects foo culture/language/nationality in aspect x2".  There are no exceptions.

* African: {friko}
* Albanian: {ckiptare}
* Algerian: {jerxo}
* American (United States): {merko}
* Antarctican: {dzipo}
* Arabic: {xrabo}
* Argentinian: {gento}
* Asiatic: {xazdo}
* Australian: {sralo}
* Basque: {skalduna}
* Belarusan: {labru'o}
* Bengali/Bangladeshi: {bengo}
* Brazilian: {brazo}
* Breton: {fasko'o}, {bre'one}
* British (United Kingdom): {brito}
* Buddhist: {budjo}
* Canadian: {kadno}
* Catalan: {katlana}
* Celtic: {dzeko'o}
* Chilean: {tci'ile}
* Chinese: {jungo}
* Christian: {xriso}
* Cornish: {kernauke}
* Czech: {tce'exo}
* Egyptian: {misro}
* English: {glico}
* European: {ropno} (see also Indo-European)
* French/Gallic: {fraso}
* Gaelic: {skoto}, {gailge}
* German/Germanic: {dotco}
* Greek/Hellenic: {xelso}
* Hebrew/Jewish/Israeli: {xebro}
* Hindi: {xindo}

Note on "Indo-European": the Lojbanic Wikipedia uses "xinjoiropno" in the context of the Indo-European language group.  However, the gismu list entry for "ropno" claims to cover Indo-European languages.

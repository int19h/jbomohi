In addition to the main body of the dictionary, it would be helpful if some of the following things were also in the book:

* Some general info on Lojban, and how to use the book. A starting point might be http://www.lojban.org/files/draft-dictionary/Working/dictbook.ZIP .
* A pronunciation chart. IPA is must.
* Rafsi list, sorted on rafsi. That means that in order to be helpful in decomposing unknown lujvo, it would have to have all of the following entries:
** blo - blo lot lo'i {bloti}
** lo'i - blo lot lo'i {bloti}
** lot - blo lot lo'i {bloti}

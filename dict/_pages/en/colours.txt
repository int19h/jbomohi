! colour terminology

For specific colour terms, the place structure is "x1 is foo coloured".  There are no exceptions.

Basic colours:
* black (or very dark): {xekri}
* grey: {grusi}
* white (or very light): {blabi}
* red: {xunre}
* orange: {narju}
* brown: {bunre}
* yellow: {pelxu}
* green: {crino}
* cyan: {cicna}
* blue: {blanu}
* purple: {zirpu}
* magenta: {nukni}

The six basic colours {xunre}, {pelxu}, {crino}, {cicna}, {blanu}, and {nukni} could be understood to divide up the (circular) hue range into six segments.  {narju} and {zirpu} are perhaps additional segments overlapping these?  Or are they non-overlapping?  {bunre} seems to depend on saturation and brightness for its definition.  Can someone comment on this?  Is there an official mapping between these words and the colour cylinder?  Someone has produced a [http://members.lycos.co.uk/lojbanlinks/skari.html!list of representative colours].

Hesitant

I am extremely hesitant about introducing this word and I am not 
particularly attached to it. I am not sure that it truly warrants a gismu. 
It is not particularly culturally neutral. I am concerned with the 
precedent that it might set. But, on the other hand, the word was screaming
to be made a gismu and I am not confident that this sound sequence can be 
associated with anything else any time soon- even if it is not officially 
adopted, I would not be too surprised to see this word being used in this 
way. Also, it is hard to deny that the concept is an unimportant one in the
modern world (for historic purposes as well as contemporaneous ones). Feel 
as you will about it.

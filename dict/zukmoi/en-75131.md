It is $x_1$'s turn to do (be) $x_2$ (ka).

## Notes

A more generic version of {jidmoi}. See also {bigmoi}. Alternatively in the inverse order as {momyzu'e} ~ {moirzu'e} ({momzu'e} in modern dialects that permit -mz-).

## 2014-05-31T20:06:28Z — krtisfranks (comment 1155, on definition 16717)

Why "fossil"?

By "fossil" I mean the remains of a once-biological entity/individual
preserved in rock. The {veljvo} of this word seems to mean something more
like "solidified rock", which would more readily conjure thoughts of
igneous or perhaps sedimentary rock (which do not necessarily contain
traces of once-biological entities/individuals), personally. Should we not
choose a definition more in line with such a concept?

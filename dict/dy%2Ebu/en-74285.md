letteral for ð (eth)

## Notes

See CLL 17.5 (https://lojban.github.io/cll/17/5/). Thorn (þ) is {ty.bu}. The glottal stop inside this word is mandatory (as in {y'y.bu} 'aitch', {ky.bu} 'cue' and {vy.bu} 'double-u').

$x_1=p_1$ is a big-endian / most-significant-first sequence with ordering $x_2=p_2$ on set $x_3=p_3=l_2$, with most-significant item $x_4=l_1=v_1$ first, most important to $x_5=v_2$ in property / aspect $x_6=v_3$.

## Notes

Synonym: {vajraipavmompoi}, {endi}.  See also: {teryraipoi} (endianness), {vajyje'ipoi} (little-endian).

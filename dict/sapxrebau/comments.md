## 2014-07-02T20:22:54Z — krtisfranks (comment 1248, on definition 17827)

Oxymoronic at best

{sampu} and {mixre} should probably not be joined in this manner. "Simple"
(in the English definition of the former) is being used in the sense of
being "pure"/"unmixed"/"not complicated in composition", not in the sense
of "lacking sophisticated mechanics", in my opinion. As such, something
would have a hard time being sampu and mixre simultaneously.

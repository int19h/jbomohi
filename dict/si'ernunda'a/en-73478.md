## Notes

Includes the use of snow-based attacks other than traditional "snowballs" (e.g. pushing snow off a railing, scooping up snow in a bucket, etc.). See {snime}, {nu}, {damba}, {si'erda'a}, {nunda'a}, {si'erboi}.

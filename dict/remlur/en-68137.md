Name:  The Moon / Luna. Earth's primary natural satellite.

## Notes

A {jvocmevo} meaning "{remna} {lunra}", or "human moon".  Named so as to be consistent with "{remsol}" and "{remted}".  See also {lur} {remsol} {stoplin} {vidyplin} {remted} {xunplin} {balplin} {melplin}.

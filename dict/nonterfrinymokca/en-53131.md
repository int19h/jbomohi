$x_1$ is a singularity of function/object $x_2$ of type/with properties $x_3$;  $x_2$ has a singularity of type/severity $x_3$ at (locus) $x_1$

## Notes

x3 can be used in order to specify cosmetic/removable singularity, pole (and the degree/severity thereof), and essential singularity; none of these aforementioned options are a default. x1 may be at infinity or in various other spaces.

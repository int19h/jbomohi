quote the next Lojban word (not cmavo compound or tanru) and return its grammatical class.

## Notes

If a cmavo is quoted, it is the selma'o of the quoted cmavo: "le selma'o be zo WORD". If a brivla or cmevla is quoted, it becomes "le si'o TYPE". Possible types are: {gismu}, {lujvo}, {fu'ivla}/{zi'evla}, {cmevla}.

Examples: zoi'e pu - "the selma'o PU" / "le selma'o be zo pu". zoi'e broda - "the concept of gismu" / "le si'o gismu". zoi'e comco'e - "the concept of lujvo" / "le si'o lujvo". zoi'e morkova - "the concept of fu'ivla/zi'evla" / "le si'o fu'ivla/zi'evla". zoi'e frankfurt. - "the concept of cmevla" / "le si'o cmevla".

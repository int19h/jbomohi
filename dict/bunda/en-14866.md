$x_1$ weighs $x_2$ units of local weight standard $x_3$.

## Notes

Place structure changed from the baseline. UNOFFICIAL! See also {junta}, {grake}, {merli}, {linto}, {tilju}, {dekpu}, {fepni}, {gutci}, {kramu}, {minli}. $x_1$ is not an agent. $x_2$ is typically one; the number format for fractions need not be decimal. $x_3$ is typically the Avoirdupois pound, but many other possibilities exist. For example: 500g, 300g, grain, dram, ounce, stone, hundredweight, ton.

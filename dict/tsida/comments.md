Etymology

Many of the words in the etymology of {tsida} appear to be the same as 
those (and from the same languages as those) which appear in the etymology 
of {sigda}. However, the intention here was to allow for duplication of 
elements and then reduce them, in a way which is analogous to "iff" for "if
and only if" in English, "ssi" for "si' y solo si'" in Spanish, and 
"ifaf(a)" in Hindi (which seems to be imported from English).

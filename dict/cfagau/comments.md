The noralujv definition votes have been deleted. If the only occurrence of
a gloss word is in noralujv, it won't appear in the dictionary.

> Since one can only vote for one definition for a given natlang word, how
> should "initiate" as a noun and "initiate" as a verb be distinguished? I
> haven't seen a general convention for this, and I can't add just
> "initiate" unqualified as a gloss word for this, since the noun and verb

Put ";verb" if the meanings are the same except for the part of speech, or
a few words that mean the same as the verb if they are different. See
{miptera} for an example (no one has defined a word as "bug;annoy" yet).
Also {pinpedi}, {sperlanu}, {muvdu}.

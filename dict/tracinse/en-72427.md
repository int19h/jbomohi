## Notes

Gender here denotes a social role loosely defined as a combination of how one relates to others socially/societally/culturally and how one presents oneself. Gender here is not determined by chromosomal makeup, genital configuration, or sexual/romantic orientation of $x_1$. No implication of sexual orientation. Cf. binary gender ({reltracinse}), non-binary gender ({nalreltracinse}), cisgender ({duntracinse}), transgender ({datytracinse}), gender identity ({sezytracinse}), gender/sexuality/sexual orientation ({cinse}).

## 2015-05-23T04:49:30Z — spheniscine (comment 1888, on definition 66073)

Possible use independent of toi'e?

I first proposed this as a scope-limiter to {toi'e} but I thought of a 
possible use independent to it; namely in situations where it might be 
useful to separate attitudinals/UI cmavo into discrete chunks despite them 
all applying to the main text.

For example, to say "I'm joking, but it's true", you might use (zo'o je'u 
ku'i), but then the whole mess clumps together and becomes confusing; is it
true that you're joking, to the contrary of some predetermined 
expectation... or what?

On the other hand, (zo'o koi'e je'u ku'i) is much clearer. Technically it 
should be (zo'o koi'e je'u toi'e ku'i), but it should be sufficient to get 
the point across in this case.

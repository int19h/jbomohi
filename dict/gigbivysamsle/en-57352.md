$se_1$ is (close to) a billion ($10^9$) of eight indivisible, most basic computer subunits of computer $se_2$; $se_1$ is a gigabyte

## Notes

In binary a gigabyte is actually 1 073 741 824 bytes, but we can round off for brevity, and to parallel the metric units.

$x_1$ (metric space/set/class/structure) is a metric subspace/subset(/subclass) of $x_2$ (metric space/set/class) which is closed therein. $x_1$ is an closed subset of $x_2$, where closedness is taken to be understood as being considered within $x_2$ and under the metric shared by $x_1$ and $x_2$

## Notes

In $x_2$, $x_1$ is composed exactly of members which are limit points of $x_1$. Confer: {sezrurkle}.

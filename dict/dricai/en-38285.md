$x_1=d_1=c_1$ is extremely/intensely sad/depressed about $x_2=d_2$ as measured/seen/observed by $x_2=c_3$.

## Notes

Composed of {badri}, {carmi}. The advantage of using "carmi" instead of {mutce} here is that {carmi} is subjectively determined by the carmi3, whereas {mutce} implies some kind of real, objective standards for measurements. {dritce} (based on {mutce}) is more appropriate for objectively determined states of depression.

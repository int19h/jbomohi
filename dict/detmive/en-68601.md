$x_1$ has experienced $x_2$ (timestamp-style expression of dimensionful measure of time) units of time of existence/life according to standard $x_3$

## Notes

This word is broader/more general and more versatile/flexible than {nancaji}. $x_2$ may be any expression that is acceptable as the sumti submitted to the first terbri of {detri}; however, unlike {detri}$_1$, which can be interpreted as representing elapsed time from some culturally accepted reference start-time, this word measures running time from the start of the existence of $x_1$, according to standard $x_3$.

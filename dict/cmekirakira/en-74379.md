$x_1$ is the kirakira name (= an absurd, unconventional, ridiculous name) of $x_2$.

## Notes

From Japanese キラキラネーム kirakira nēmu, lit. 'sparkling/twinkling name'. The cme- is from {cmene} 'name'.

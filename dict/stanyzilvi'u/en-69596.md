$x_1$ is a log (fallen/severed trunk) or clipping or cut-flower of/from a plant of species $x_2$, belonging to individual tree (precursor) $x_3$ and leavinf behind remainder/stump/bulb $x_4$.

## Notes

Typically, this will refer to the main stem of the plant; what is left behind will, therefore, not have notable branches. Thus, a flower which is cut from the branch of rose would not qualify, but a flower cut from a dandelion would. Grass clippings (such as those eaten by a rabbit or those cut and collected while lawnmowing) qualify. May not apply to a plucked or uprooted plant left intact. For "log" specifically, use "{ricystanyzilvi'u}".

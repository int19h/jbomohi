letteral for logical AND-OR; common notations: ∨, +, /,  |, OR, A

## Notes

Only to be accepted under the proposal for reallocating letteral space so that the letterals for vowel V becomes .V'y (confer: .{a'y} in this case) and when sumti connectives take the form .V (which does not occur in the MAD proposal, the proposal of la selpa'i, etc.).

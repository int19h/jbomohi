## Notes

A ka's parent context causes the ce'u-places to effectively become filled. A ka abstraction must contain at least one ce'u place, although this place might appear within a linkarg or even a child abstraction. A ka abstraction is what should rightfully go in {bridi}2, and {du'u} is what should go in bridi1. A sumti-list, delimited by {ce'oi}, is what should go in bridi3. Strictly speaking, a ka-abstraction has its ce'u-places filled, and effectively becomes a du'u-abstraction thereafter. bridi's meaning evidences this transformation called "application".

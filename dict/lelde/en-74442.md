$x_1$ is the particular/specific instance(s) of $x_2$ (ka) relevant to the discourse; $x_1$ is "the" $x_2$

## Notes

predicate version of {le}. intended as a possibly less malrarbau alternative to "cu du le" constructions. {poi'i} or its common tcekitau {voi} are also an option for this, but lack the "relevant instance" semantic space.

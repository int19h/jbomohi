$x_{1}$ (ind./mass) has the ethics/morals/moral $x_{2}$  about situation $x_{3}$.

## Notes

Notes: $x_2$ will (typically) be an abstract, a rule or rules of behavior; also principles; also conscience (= {sezmarde}). See also {marde}, {palci}, {vrude}, {lijda}, {manri}, {javni}, {tarti}, {zekri}.

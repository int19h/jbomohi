descriptor: each of the ones named; «lu'a'au la»

## Notes

Alternative interpretation of individuality, with a stronger focus on the individuals and/or being separate.  See also: {lu'a'au} (LAhE grammar), {lo'a'au} ({la'a'au} but for {lo}), {le'a'au} ({la'a'au} but for {le}).

$x_1$ (text) is written in a dialect where {su'o}, {ro} quantifiers are treated as {su'oi}, {ro'oi} respectively

## Notes

'{sei} {esme}'/'{sei} {na} {esme}' is a method of switching to/from this particular plural logic quantifiers' style in Lojban. Expected usage as a 'free' is in the following rules: {jarnezi}, {mei} {zei} {ni'orsu}, {xa zei sumtika}. {lo} {broda} is treated as {su'oi} {broda}.

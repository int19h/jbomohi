$x_1$ (quote) is a lujvo/compound word(s) form which conflicts with other lujvo $x_2$ (quote) according to rule $x_3$ in language $x_4$.

## Notes

In Lojban, this is the case when two lujvo have different meanings/definitions but are identical in veljvo except possibly for the presence of "{zei}" (or similar) and the presence of grouping markers (which may be rendered unnecessary in some veljvo constructs but not in others, such as in "LUJVO1 {zei} LUJVO2" versus "LUJVO1 {bo} LUJVO2"). See also: "{gimkamsmikezypro}".

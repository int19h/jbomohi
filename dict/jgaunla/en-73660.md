$x_1$ is at position or in direction $x_2$ (referenced by clock positions) relative to oriented viewer $x_3$ along circumference $x_4$ (default when applicable: the natural horizontal of $x_3$ or, if none exists, the horizon) and using angle measurement system $x_5$ (default: twelve-hour clock which clicks clockwise as viewed from above).

## Notes

$x_3$ specifies the central point or vertex of the angle as well as the forward direction from which the angle is measured (which, for default $x_5$, is the 12 o'clock or 0 o'clock position).

$x_1$ (ka) is the property/state of being $x_2$

## Notes

Brivla version of "{ka} {ce'u} {du} $x_2$". See {mintu}, {lizdu'o}, {dumdu'o}, {kamdu'o}

## Examples

### Example 798

no datpre cu djuno lo dumdu'o be lo li'imdu be lo kamcedu be mi = "No one else knows what it's like to be me."

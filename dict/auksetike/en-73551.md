$x_1$ is an auxetic structure/material with Poisson ratio $x_2$ (li) composed of physical material/substance $x_3$, having geometry or other properties $x_4$.

## Notes

$x_2$ should be negative for a truly auxetic material, but one may suppose that this word could be used for non-auxetic structures with nonnegative $x_2$.

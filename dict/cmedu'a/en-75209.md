$x_1$ gives the name $x_2$ to $x_3$; $x_1$ uses the name $x_2$ to name, denominate, designate, christen $x_3$.

## Notes

Has implications of giving a new name to someone or something, differently from "te {cmene}", which is moreso just 'call [someone by a name]'.

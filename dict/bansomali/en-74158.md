## Notes

Deprecated. Preferred synonyms: {bangrsomali}, {bansu'omu}. Related: {kulnrsomali} for Somali culture, {gugdesu'o} or {soMAliias} for the Federal Republic of Somalia; {mugdico} or {mugdicos} for its capital city. Linguists place Somali within the Cushitic branch ({bangrkuciti}) of the Afroasiatic language family ({bangrfrixazo}) (see also {frikoxazdo} 'Afro-Asiatic'). See also {frikojirna} 'Horn of Africa'.

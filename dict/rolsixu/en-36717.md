For every x in $x_1$, there exists a y in $x_2$ such that x me'au $x_3$ y; For every y in $x_2$ there exists an x in $x_1$ such that x me'au $x_3$ y.

## Notes

Cf. {me'au}. The lojban definition is highly preferred. For example, "lo so'imei poi loi so'i kulnu cu rolsixu ke'a lo ka kulnu" translates to "many people of many nations" in a precise sense.

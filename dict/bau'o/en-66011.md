attitudinal scope modifier: marks following attitudinal/UI-cluster as applying to an arbitrarily defined text

## Notes

Used to mark an attitudinal/UI-clause as belonging to an arbitrary piece of text. Usage: {bau'o} UI-clause text {bau'onai}

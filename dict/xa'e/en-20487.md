Issues a third-person command. Elevates a sumti to ko status.

## Notes

In Lojban, there're no convenient ways to issue third-person commands, including cohortatives like "Let's go!", which could be rendered with zo xa'e as lu xa'e mi'o klama li'u. cf. {la'e}, {doi}, {ko}, and {la'ei}.

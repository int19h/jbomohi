$x_1$ is superlative in property $x_2$ (ni) among set range $x_3$.

## Notes

In applying the x2 property to every element in set x3 and comparing the ni-values, the x1's ni-value would be greatest. Use to'e to produce the inverse meaning of "least" or invert the selbri in the x2 property.

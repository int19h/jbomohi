non-logical connective: set difference of $x_1$ and $x_2$: ${x_1}dei'i{x_2}={x_1}\setminus{x_2}=\{{x}\in{x_1}:{x}\notin{x_2}\}$

## Notes

JOI and mandatorily binary version of "{kei'i}".

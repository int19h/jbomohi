$x_1$ is arthropod meat from species $x_2$.

## Notes

More general than {cinkyre'u} (if cinki is taken to refer just to insects). Examples of {cubre'u} that are culturally important to humans include lobster-meat, chapulines, witchetty grubs, sago grubs and prawns. Arthropod-eating is {cubzumcti}. An arthropod-eater is {lo} {cubycti}.

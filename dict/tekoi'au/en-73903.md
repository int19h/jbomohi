karncau modal, 3nd place (individually; separately) separately in context / enterprise …

## Notes

Example: «le prenu be {sekoi'au} cu bevri le pipno», or more succinctly «le {koi'au} prenu cu bevri le pipno», declares each person individually carried the piano, rather than leaving individuality or togetherness vague.  However, this approach uses BAI; {lu'a'au} explicitly declares individuality with the grammar of {la'e}.  See also: {lu'a'au}, {koi'au}, {karncau}, {lu'a'ei}.

$x_1$ (agent) consigns (by thought, word, or deed) $x_2$ (patient) to fate $x_3$ (abstraction)

## Notes

So-called "neutral-{dapma}"; {zandapma} would be the connotationally positive form, while {dapma} would be the connotationally negative form. See {pacna}, {dimna}, {a'o}, {di'ai}

$x_1$ is the duodenum (part of the small intestine) of $x_2$.

## Notes

Calque of Latin duodenum (digitorum) 'of twelve (fingers)': -gam- (< {gai}, twelve) + {degji} 'finger', removing the -j- to make it a freeword instead of a true compound. Compare Dutch twaalfvingerige darm, and its calque, Japanese 十二指腸 jūnishichō.

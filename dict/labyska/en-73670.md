$x_1$ is lightly colored with color $x_2$ [ka]

## Notes

In general, lighter colors, i.e. colors with a high brightness in an HSB color model.  In general, use the rafsi "lab" (from {blabi}) or {blabi} as a seltau + a color to form a light version of that color.  E.g. {labybla} is light blue.

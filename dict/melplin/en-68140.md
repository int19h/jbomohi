Name:  Saturn

## Notes

A {jvocmevo} meaning "{melbi} {plini}", or "beautiful planet".  Named so because, in the words of astronomer Phil Plait, "when you ask astronomers or astronauts what got them started, what was the very first thing that happened to them to inspire them to do what they do, overwhelmingly, they will tell you: 'I saw Saturn through a telescope'".  See also {xavyplin} {remsol} {stoplin} {vidyplin} {remted} {remlur} {xunplin} {balplin}.

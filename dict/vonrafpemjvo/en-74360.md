$x_1$ is a Lojban compound-word idiom made of four combining forms (rafsi); $x_1$ is a "Lojbanic chengyu/yojijukugo".

## Notes

{vonrafpemjvo} are Lojban words that imitate the structure of Chinese/Japanese/Korean four-character proverbs. The "ideal" short form is four rafsi, with no epenthetic r's, n's, or y's. Examples of these include {dekpredekpu'a}, {zditeitortei}, {gekfegdripu'a}, and except for the fact that it is literal and not poetic ({pemci} → -pem-), the word {vonrafpemjvo} itself.

$x_1$ is a rational number obtained by dividing $x_2$ (integer) by $x_3$ (non-zero integer)

## Notes

$x_1$ = $a$/$b$ where $a$ is an integer ({dzalene}) and $b$ is a strictly positive natural/whole number ({maulne}).

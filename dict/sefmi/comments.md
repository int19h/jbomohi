## 2026-08-06T17:06:28Z — mati! (comment 5091, on definition 75572, in reply to 5089)



also *-sem-* has been used in the past for a similar function, although it was reciprocal instead of reflexive. see {semnei} in this dictionary, and also *semzvala'i* in solpa'i's «mi za'o citno» ([https://solpahi.wordpress.com/2016/09/26/mi-zaho-citno/)](https://solpahi.wordpress.com/2016/09/26/mi-zaho-citno/). *semzvala'i* here is intended to mean *su'ei* + *zvala'i*, i.e. 'near *each other*', but here I propose it for 'itself', because *-sim-* can already be used to make prefix reflexives.

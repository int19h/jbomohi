$x_1$ does $x_2$ [ka with two blanks] to itself; $x_1$ is in relation $x_2$ with itself.

## Notes

An alternative form of {sefsi} so it can take up the combining form *-sem-*, alongside the *-se'i-* this word already has unofficially (compare {simxu}, with both *-sim-* and *-si'u-*). *semlu'i* or *lumse'i* 'to wash oneself, take a bath'; *spose'i* 'self-destruct'...

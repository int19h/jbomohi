$x_1$ is the subjective conscious experience of being $x_2$; $x_1$ is what it's like to be $x_2$

## Notes

See {li'i}, {li'imdu}, {du}, {kamcedu}, {dumdu'o}

## Examples

### Example 799

no datpre cu djuno lo du'u makau lizdu'o mi = "No one else knows what it's like to be me."

### Example 800

li'a lo zabna cu lizdu'o do = "Clearly, it's great to be you"

### Example 801

le nanmu cu tolve'i le lizdu'o be lo verba = "He forgot what it's like to be a child"

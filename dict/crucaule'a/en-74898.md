$x_1=l_1$ steals $x_2=l_2$ from $x_3=l_3$ without the consent of $x_4=cu_1$ (e.g. $x_3$) under law / custom $x_5$.

## Notes

See also: {po'ecru} (consent), {ponse} (with notes on $x_3$).  A more explicit word with essentially the same meaning is {po'ercrucauzva}.

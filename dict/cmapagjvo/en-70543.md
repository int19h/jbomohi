$x_1$ (text) is a lujvo with meaning $x_2$ and arguments $x_3$, and which is constructed from tanru/veljvo $x_4$ such that each element of the veljvo is ultimately only a cmavo (including "{zei}", "{ke}"/"{ke'e}", "{bo}", etc.), gismu, or rafsi of such cmavo or gismu.

## Notes

This word is itself a cmapagjvo. In intermediate stages of reducing/analyzing the veljvo $x_4$, some constituents themselves may be cmapagjvo; however, after a total reduction, all parts must be or represent cmavo and gismu, only. Contrast with "{brapagjvo}".

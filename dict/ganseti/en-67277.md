$x_1$ is the sensation/qualia (abstract) associated with objects with property $x_2$ (ka), via sense $x_3$ (ka), as sensed by $x_4$

## Notes

{lo} {ganseti} {be} {lo} {ka} ({ce'u}) {xunre} = 'the color red', {lo} {ganseti} {be} {lo} {ka} ({ce'u}) {sudga} = 'the sensation/feeling of dryness'. $x_3$ is the involved sense, e.g. {lo} {ka} {viska} for sight; this can often be left unspecified. See also {ga'ei}, {ganse}, {li'i}, {li'imdu}, {gansetifri}

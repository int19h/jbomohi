A hobby is something you do. The action is a property of whoever performs
it. (compare kakne2, troci2, snada2, zukte2, etc, which are nowadays more
commonly {ka}-places).

   lo ka [ce'u] limna kansa lo delfinu cu zukte'obi mi
   "Swimming with dolphins is a hobby of mine."

(An applied {ka}-abstraction often translates quite nicely to an
infinitive.)

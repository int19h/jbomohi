$x_1=f_1$ favors / supports / agrees with / is for $x_2=f_2$ about $x_3=f_3$.

## Notes

Non-neutral.  For consent, see {po'ecru}, which deals more closely with ownership and property rights.  It is possible to permit something you have ownership over while opposing it.  See also: «tugni», «zanru», «nalpro».

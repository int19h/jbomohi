$x_1$ is the excess money/change (that should be) returned for/after/with the purchase of $x_2$ by purchaser $x_3$ from vendor $x_4$.

## Notes

Beware sumti-raising. If the ownership of an item costs 9.50 dollars and $x_3$ buys it with an acceptable bill of value 10.00 dollars, then the change ($x_1$) is the monetary value of 0.50 dollars (regardless of which bills, coins, etc. carry that value), where 'dollar' can represent any monetary unit.

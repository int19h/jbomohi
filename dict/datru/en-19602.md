$x_1$ (event) is dated/pertaining to day/occurring on day $x_2$ of month $x_3$ of year $x_4$ in calendar $x_5$

## Notes

Moved to {datro} due to conflict with {tatru}.  We felt that {detri} just didn't work as a culturally-independent date system. The use of {pi'e} or {joi} as date mechanisms was insufficient and having the date components built into the place structure seems far more elegant. (Cf. {masti}, {djedi}, {nanca}, {nu}, {fasnu}, {purci}, {balvi}, {jeftu})

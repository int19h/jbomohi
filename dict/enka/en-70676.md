$x_1$ (terbricmi) is bound to $x_2$ (sumti) by relationship $x_3$ (relationship with two {ce'u} for $x_1$ and $x_2$)

## Notes

Used to more precisely express relationship (whether fuzzy or not) between terbricmi (predicate slots) and arguments (sumti) filling those slots.  See also {ckini}, {va'e}, {jei}, {sela'u}.

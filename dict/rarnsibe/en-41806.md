$x_1$ is/are runcible in aspect/dimension/property $x_2$.

## Notes

1871, coined by Edward Lear in runcible spoon, runcible cat etc. Lear does not appear to have had any firm idea of what the word "runcible" means. However, many different definitions exist. See also: smurf (={ctrumfi}).

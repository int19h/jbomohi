Indeed, that's pretty odd since the word literally means
'milk-and-vegetable-eat".
> 2. x2 mixes the ladru2 place with the stagi2 place. This means this
> definition considers “the source of milk” and “the edible part of
> some plant” to be equivalent o_O. Why? I have no idea how x2 could

I'd say that's just a definition quirk. The lacto-vegetarian eats both,
but it is not the case that what the lacto-vegetarian eats is both milk
and a vegetable.
The becomes clearer if we define the word in Lojban, and whether we
include an x2 (which in my opinion we shouldn't.)
ladjvestagycti = x1 citka lo stagi .e po'o lo ladru (no other places; uses
the E connective)
ladjvestagycti = x1 citka x2 noi stagi gi'a ladru (contains an x2,
requires an A connective.)

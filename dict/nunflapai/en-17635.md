$n_1$ is a trial of dispute $j_2$ involving people $f_3$.

## Notes

Cf. {nu}, {pajni}, {tavla}, {fapro}, {nunpai}, {flapaizda}. x3 includes not only the disputing parties but also the judge and juries, which may be connected with "jo'u".

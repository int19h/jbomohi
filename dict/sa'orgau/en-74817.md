$x_1=g_1$ tilts / slants / leans $x_2=s_1$ at angle $x_3=s_2$ to horizon / frame $x_4=s_3$.

## Notes

Agentive {salpo}.  Example: «mi morji lodu'u mi sa'orgau le loldi li 30 jy vo'e'e.a'a'e» (I remember that I tilt the floor by 30 degrees).

$x_1$ is unnecessary Lojban jargon for natural-language equivalent term $x_2$

## Notes

Ex.: zo'oi «modal» valcrimtu zo'oi «preposition». "Modal" or {sumtcita} instead of "preposition", {selbri} instead of "main verb" and so on. Refers to using Lojban jargon for discussing grammar when there's perfectly good English words that mean the same and aren't as opaque.

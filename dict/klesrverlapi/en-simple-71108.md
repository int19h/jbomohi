$x_1$ and $x_2$ are both sets such that: there is at least one thing which is a member of both of them, at least one (other) thing which is a member of $x_1$ but not of $x_2$, and at least one (yet another) thing which is a member of $x_2$ but not $x_1$ (note the reversed order).

## Notes

The sets overlap.

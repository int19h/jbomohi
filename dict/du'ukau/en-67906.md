bridi abstractor: $x_1$ is the answer to the question $x_2$

## Notes

Experimental cmavo compound. Equivalent to {du'u} with {kau} attached to all question words (e.g. {ma}, {mo}, {xo}) contained. See {preti}, {danfu}, {du'umrei}

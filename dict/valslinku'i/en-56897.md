$x_1$ is a slinku'i word

## Notes

Note: for obvious reasons, {zo} will probably not work here. You probably want {zoi} to quote $x_1$. A slinku'i word form looks like a stage-4 {fu'ivla}, but would be ambiguous when following certain cmavo, so it is disallowed. For more see http://mw.lojban.org/papri/lo_valslinku%27i_cu_mo. See also {valrtosmabru}.

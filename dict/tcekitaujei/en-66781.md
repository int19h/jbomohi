## Notes

Place structure based on {bangu}. Refers to a variant of the 'ce ki tau jau' ({tcekitaujau}) dialect that differs on what {jo'u} is swapped with: see http://mw.lojban.org/papri/ce_ki_tau_jau. Use of this dialect can be marked with '{jo'au} tcekitaujei' document.title = 'ro lo do zbepi cu me mi moi';

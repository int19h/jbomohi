$x_1$ is a grandparent of $x_2$ which/who has another grandparent $x_3$ such that $x_1$ is related to $x_2$ by relationship $x_4$, and $x_1$ is related to $x_3$ by relationship $x_5$ which is NOT their mutual mating/intimate partnering, under cultural/personal paradigm $x_6$; $x_1$ and $x_3$ are mutual co-grandparents.

## Notes

This would, for example, apply (for the $x_1$ and $x_3$ slots) to a maternal grandparent and a paternal grandparent of shared grandchild $x_2$ in a simple family tree, but not to/as compared to a pair formed by two maternal grandparents, xor two paternal grandparents, of the same descendant. $x_1$ and $x_3$ are mutually symmetric if $x_4$, $x_5$, and $x_6$ are appropriately reversed (if those are directed/not symmetric). See also: ".{abvroi}", ".{abvele}", "{rorci}".

## Notes

Reflecting greater Persia during the period from A.D. 224 to A.D. 651. This word is derived from Ērānshahr, Middle Persian name for the Sasanian Empire. See also {kulnrfarsi}, {bangrfarsi}, {bangrxironi}, {kulnrxironi}, {gugde'ironi}, {gugde'iru}, {iRON}, {te'yRON}, {faursi}.

$x_1$ is an cone of frozen $x_2$ (quantity) with vertex $x_3$ ; $x_1$ is an $x_2$-cicle

## Notes

Possibly includes stalactites/stalagmites (possibly include {kunra} and/or {pulce} in the description). Examples: icicle (x2 = {loi} {djacu}), snot-cicle (x2 = {loi} {zbiselcigla}), sap-cicle (x2 = {loi} {te} {rickemsatrunta}), rusticle  (x2 = {loi} {furtirse}).

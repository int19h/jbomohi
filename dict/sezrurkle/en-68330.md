$x_1$ is an open set in the topological space $x_2$.

## Notes

$x_2$ is conceptually composed of an underlying set, which is a superset of $x_1$, and topological information/defining information; however, it can also be expressed as a set of some of the subsets of the said underlying (implicitly define) set. Confer: "{sezbarkle}".

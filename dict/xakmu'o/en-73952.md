$x_1=m_1=xa_1$ finishes consuming $x_2=xa_2$ by standard $x_3=m_3$; $x_1$ has had their/its fill.

## Notes

{xakmu'o} focuses more on the consumer.  {selxakmu'o} means exhausted.  Note on lujvo construction: as a lujvo, {xakmu'o} does not elide {se} from the seltau (see CLL 12.9 guidelines) and, rather than applying the general bridi relationship to $m_1$ as a general principle, links $xa_1$ to $m_1$; if this particular nuance is desired, then besides {mulxaksu}, constructions like {nunxakmu'o} and {selselxakmu'o} for the former and latter (or simply using an explicit bridi) may perhaps be used.

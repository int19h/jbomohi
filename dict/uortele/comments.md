la snura

it's a 'me la snura' policy word for "carrot". It's from in Indonesian, 
Dutch and Afrikaans.

An alternative word can be taken from Russian "morkov'" (=> {morkova}) with
"morkva" / "morkov" in Ukrainian, Belarusian / Bulgarian, Romanian. Other 
Slavic languages + German Lojbanized "mure" might be taken into 
consideration but except for Russian/Bulgarian/Romanian this leads to some 
deviation from clean Lojbanization.

More precise numbers of how many people around the world are familiar with 
these uortele/morkova forms are needed.

descriptor: each; «lu'a'au lo»

## Notes

Alternative interpretation of individuality, with a stronger focus on the individuals and/or being separate.  See also: {lu'a'au} (LAhE grammar), {lo'u'a} (old-style {lo}), {le'a'au} ({lo'a'au} but for {le}), {la'a'au} ({lo'a'au} but for {la}).

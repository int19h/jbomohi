descriptor: each; «lu'a'au lo»

## Notes

Alternative interpretation of individuality, with a stronger focus on the individuals and/or being separate.  See also: {lu'a'au} (same meaning as {lo'a'au} but with LAhE grammar), {lo'u'a} (old-style {lo}).

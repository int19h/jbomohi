$ku_1$ is a die/are dice for game $x_2$ with surfaces/sides $ku_3$ (default 6).

## Notes

Shortened from ke nu kelci ke'e kubli. Omitted $ku_2$ (dice have always three dimensions). Cf. {cu'arkubli}.

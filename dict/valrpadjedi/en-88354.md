$x_1$ is a padjedi word

## Notes

A "padjedi" is a type 4 fu'ivla of the form CVCCVCV where the CC is a permissible initial cluster in Lojban, which causes it to split into a CV cmavo and a CCVCV gismu. For example, «padjedi» splits into {pa} {djedi}.

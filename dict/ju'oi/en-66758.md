evidential: I know that...

## Notes

May also be used to say you know the answer to a question asked to you, or an indirect question ({kau}) you had tagged. See {ju'oinai}, {djuno}, {ju'o}

## Examples

### Example 659

ju'oi makau catra la .tom. == mi djuno lo du'u makau catra la .tom. == "I know who killed Tom."

### Example 660

ju'oinai makau catra la .tom. == mi na djuno lo du'u makau catra la .tom. == "I don't know who killed Tom."

$x_1$ is a sibling-in-law (sibling of a spouse) of $x_2$ according to custom/law $x_3$.

## Notes

For "spouse of sibling" use "{tubyspe}", "{bunspe}" (brother's spouse), "{me'ispe}" (sister's spouse); for other "sibling of spouse" use "{spebu'a}" (brother of spouse) or "{speme'i}" (sister of spouse); for "sibling of one's sibling's spouse" use {tubyspetunba} vel sim. See also: {spelanzu}.

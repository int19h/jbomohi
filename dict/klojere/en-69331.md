$x_1$ (set/space) is closed under operator/relation $x_2$; $x_2$ has closure in $x_1$.

## Notes

If $x_2$ is an operator, then for any n-tuple of elements in $x_1$, the application of $x_2$ to that tuple produces an output which is also in $x_1$. The gismu form of this word is "{kloje}".

$d_1=b_2$ is $d_2$ (default 1) bucketfuls in volume, measuring by bucketful standard $d_3=b_1$.

## Notes

$x_3$ can be a volume or, more typically, a bucket. Cf. {litce}.

## Examples

### Example 251

.o'unai ko bevri lo djacu poi baktydekpu li ci

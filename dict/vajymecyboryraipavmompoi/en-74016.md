$x_1=p_1$ is a little-endian / least-significant-first sequence with ordering $x_2=p_2$ on set $x_3=p_3$, with least-significant item $x_4=t_1=m_1=v_1$ first, least important to $x_5=t_2=m_3=v_2$ in property / aspect $x_6=v_3$, towards extreme / as xth-least $x_7=t_3$ among $x_8=t_4=m_2$ (least significant by amount $x_9=m_4$).

## Notes

Synonym: {vajyje'ipoi}.  See also: {pornvaije'i} (little-endian) for a shorter fu'ivlaization, {vairteryraipavmompoi} for endianness, {vajraipavmompoi} for big-endian.

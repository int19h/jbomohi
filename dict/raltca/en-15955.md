$x_1$ is a/the capital of $x_2$.

## Notes

Cf. {ralju}, {tcadu}. More general than {trutca}.  May be the financial (e.g. New York in USA), industrial (e.g. Sao Paulo in Brazil) or religious (e.g. Mecca in Saudi Arabia) capital of its polity but not necessarily the seat of government.

$x_1$ is $x_2$ hours in angular measure of right ascension RA (default is 1).

## Notes

Cf. {cacra}, {radno}, {julra'o}, {sostartai}. {pi'e} used in x2 indicates a base of 60. 

## Examples

### Example 90

'la .proksimas.centauris. zvati lo cacryra'o be li pavo pi'e reso pi'e voci be'o .e lo julra'o be li ni'u xare pi'e vono pi'e voxa ma'i jy. renonono pi'e no' means 'Proxima Centauri was located at RA 14h 29m 43s DEC -62° 40' 46" during epoch J2000.0'.

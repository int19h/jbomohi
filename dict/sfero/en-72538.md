$x_1$ (point) is away from coordinate center $x_2$ by $x_3$ (distance) with n-sphere coordinates $x_4$ ({zi'o} by default), $x_5$  ({zi'o} by default), ...

## Notes

$x_2$ can be {le} {mitre}. For 0-sphere $x_4$ is {zi'o}, for circle places up to $x_4$ are used, for 2-dimensional sphere $x_4$ is the inclination (with zenith specified in its place structure), $x_5$ is the azimuth (with the azimuth reference and positive sign specified in its place structure). See also {sepli}, {galtu}, {berti}, {nacmei}, {clenu}.

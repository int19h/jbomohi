$x_1=t_1=p_2=p_3=j_2=v_2$ is a little-endian / least-significant-first tanru formed with $x_2=t_2$ modifying $x_3=t_3=j_1=v_1$, giving meaning $x_4=t_4$ in usage/instance $x_5=t_5=v_3$.

## Notes

When used as a language feature (with {baunpli}, e.g. «.i mi baunpli la vajyje'ipoitau le vi selsku»), according to this definition, keep tanru order unreversed, so that without {co} the first (left) side remains the {seltau} and the second (right) side remains the {tertau}, and with {co} the second (right) side remains the {tertau} and the first (left) side remains the {tertau}; only tanru ordering would be reversed, and not grammar (in particular tanru grouping and associativity, e.g. with {bo} and {co}, which would retain their right-associativity) or lujvo.

ni'o See also: {vajli'epoitau} (big-endian tanru), {co'e'i'au'i} («sei baunpli be la vajli'epoitau»), {vajyje'ipoi} (little-endian).

$x_1$ feels hope/is hopeful (emotion) about $x_2$ (abstraction)

## Notes

There may be a sense of possibility in actuality, and perhaps some expectation or waiting, but the primary characteristic is really just the complex emotions associated with the possibility of a possibility itself.  The actual probability of $x_2$ happening/being true is of no importance to this word; in fact, even a subjective judgment of what the probability is does not matter, so long as it is nonzero; only the feelings matter.  See also: .{a'o}, .{uenai}, {sorpa'a}, {kanpe}.

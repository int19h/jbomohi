$x_1=b_5$ is the means/medium by which $x_2=b_1$ transfers/sends/transmits $x_3=b_2$ to receiver $x_4=b_3$ from transmitter/origin $x_5=b_4$.

## Notes

{benji} with $x_5$ promoted by means of {xo'ai}, resulting in a different place structure from the otherwise identical {xelbe'i} ({xe} {benji}).

on (n)-th day from a given point (by default from today)

## Notes

"{ni'u} de'ei / yesterday", "{no} de'ei / today", "{pa} de'ei / tomorrow". When tagging a sumti, the sumti specifies the given point. See also {zu'oi}, {djedi}

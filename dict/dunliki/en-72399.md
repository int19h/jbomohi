Something is such that both $x_1$ and $x_2$ are related to it by predicate $x_3$. $x_4$ [quantifier] thing to which $x_1$ is related by predicate $x_3$ is such that $x_2$ is related to it by predicate $x_3$.

## Notes

The first definition is equivalent to the second definition with {lo} {ka} {ce'u} {ce'u} {kancusu'oi} in $x_4$. See {dunli}, {ckini}, {me'au}, {dunlixu}, {dunlija}, {dunlini}, .{utka}

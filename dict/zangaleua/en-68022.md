$x_1$ expresses zangalewa feeling towards $x_2$; $x_1$ tells, informs $x_2$ about $x_2$'s own responsibility for their actions

## Notes

'Zangalewa' is a name of a Cameroon song, the term is a distorted rhetorical question in Ewondo language 'Who called you?' meaning in reality that no one forced the listener to do certain actions.

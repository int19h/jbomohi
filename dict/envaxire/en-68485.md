$x_1$, in aspect $x_2$, reflects the culture/lifestyle/people of a non-native (immigrant/invading/alien) population/culture $x_3$ which at least partially but significantly and somewhat permanently (having established itself) locally supplanted, displaced, or dominated the indigenous/native culture $x_4$ in region $x_5$ (location)

## Notes

Native American cultures  ({rindo}) would qualify as $x_3$, with  $x_5$ being a superset of the Americas ({bemjoitco}), whereas the Euramerican cultures that replaced them would be $x_3$. Foreignness/indigenosity are not sufficient; the foreign population (and, even more importantly, culture) must come to dominate the indigenous one, at least locally.

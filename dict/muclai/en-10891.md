$k_1$ is $k_2$ (quantifier, default: one) spoonfuls/scoopfuls in quantity.

## Notes

Cf. {smuci}, {klani}.

## Examples

### Example 146

'I usually put two spoonfuls of sugar in my tea' is 'mi su'oroi punji loi sakta poi muclai li re ku lo kabri be loi tcati' or 'mi su'oroi punji loi muclai be li re sakta lo kabri be loi tcati'.

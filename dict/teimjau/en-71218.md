$x_1$ (nu, or time-/date-stamp) is the event/moment in time when the elapsed time/duration from the death or end of $x_2$ (entity/object, or process) is equal to the temporal measure of its lifetime (elapsed time from its birth/beginning until its death/end), in frame of reference $x_3$.

## Notes

This is essentially a particular type of {te} {teirmijyfau}.

## Examples

### Example 959

"lo cabjbi be la 2019 nanca cu teimjau la bitmu po la .berlin. lo terdi be lo se remna" = "A particular moment circa A.D. 2019 will be when as much time has elapsed since the fall of the Berlin Wall as there had elapsed between its construction and its fall, as measured on Earth."

$x_1$ is a sonorant (phonetic) sound

## Notes

Essentially, any sound you can hold a note/syllable with. Includes *all* vowels ({karsna}), and *some* consonants ({zunsna}), specifically, semivowels/approximants ({jbikarsna}), nasals ({zbisna}), and liquids ({cimsna});  The opposite of sonorant is obstruent ({fantysna})

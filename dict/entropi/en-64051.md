$x_1$ (amount) is the entropy of system $x_2$ under conditions/in macrostate $x_3$, where the entropy is of type/calculated via formula $x_4$.

## Notes

Any type of entropy (specified by $x_4$, tanru, "{pe}", etc.) is allowed, not just Boltzmann entropy (= ".{entropi} {foi'e} {pe} {la} .boltsman."); Boltzmann entropy should be named as such; the entropy need not be logarithmic. $x_2$ must be a system in the physical sense. See also: {boltsemaku}, {ciste}, {kalte}, {cunso}, {zildatni}.

$t_1=s_1$ is a scarf worn on body part $t_2=s_2$.

## Notes

Includes "neckscarf" ({nebysruta'u}), "headscarf" ({sedysruta'u}), and "waistscarf" ({befsruta'u}). Worn for warmth, cleanliness, fashion, or religious reasons. Cf. {dasri}.

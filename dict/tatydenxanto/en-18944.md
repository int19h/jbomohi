$x_1=d_2$ is a mastodon of species $x_2$

## Notes

The mastodon's English name (and former genus name) comes from "breast-tooth" due to the unusual shape of its molars.  They are not in the same family as elephants or mammoths. This is also {xantrmamutu}.

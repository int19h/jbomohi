$x_1=k_1$ (event) is the instance/recurrence/repetition of $x_2=t_1$ [agent] reading $x_3=t_2$ [text] from surface/document/reading material $x_4=t_3$ for the $x_5=k_3$th time; $x_2$ re-reads $x_3$ from $x_4$.

## Notes

This definition follows the principle of using «-ke'u» to prepend a repetition/instance place and changing the definition such that it's as though the ‘re-’ prefix in English were added (and appending $k_3$; the $k_2$ can apply to the reading as a whole).  lujvo construction can have more flexibility than always following a similar constraint, but this definition consistently follows this principle.  See also: {tcidu}, {krefu}.

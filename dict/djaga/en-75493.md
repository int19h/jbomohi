## Notes

Also ‘amphora’. The second slot is a bottlecap (see the note under {bolva}). A {djaga} is a vessel for pouring, usually with a handle or a spout; some of them have lids or caps, but some don’t. [This word, just like {bolva}, is part of a rework of the container words.]

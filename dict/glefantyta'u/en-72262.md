$x_1$ is a chastity device, for preventing $x_2$ from having sex

## Notes

$x_2$ is an intended wearer, not necessarily an actual wearer, e.g. "lo glefantyta'u be lo {nakni}" is a male chastity device. See {gletu}, {fanta}, and {taxfu}.

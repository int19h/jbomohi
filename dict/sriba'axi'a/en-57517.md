$x_1=xi_1=b_2$ is a horse (genus “Equus”) with natural stripes $x_2=d_1=b_1$; $x_1=xi_1$ is a zebra (common English usage).

## Notes

This term is used to distinguish horses which do have stripes from horses which don’t. The term does not refer to a species. See also: stripe ({sriba'a}), horse ({xirma}), mountain zebra ({xirnzebra}), plains zebra ({xirnku'aga}) and Grévy’s zebra ({xirngrevi}).

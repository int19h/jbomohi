indicate short scope; alias for «sei diklo be lo tordu».

## Notes

This can be used with {baunpli}-based contractions like {co'i'e} to explicitly specify scope.  See also: {ve'a'ai}, {ve'u'ai}, {ve'o'ai}, {xo'e'o}, {vo'a'e} (＝＝ «{vo'a}ve'i'ai»).  Etymology: {ve'i} and UI (to ‘'ai’).

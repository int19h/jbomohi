$x_1$ is a logical statement which says that $x_2$ (du'u) being true logically implies (material implication) $x_3$ being necessarily true, but in which the converse does not hold (it is certain that there are cases in which 'IFF' fails: $x_2$ is not necessary for $x_3$).

## Notes

Strict-'IF'; the truth of $x_2$ guarantees the truth of $x_3$ but the truth of $x_3$ gives no indication about the truth of $x_2$ (in general). This word is like {kanxe}. See also: {sigda}.

$x_1$ (sequence) is the concatenation of sequences $x_2$ and $x_3$ (and $x_4$, $x_5$, ...)

## Notes

$x_1$ is a string composed of exactly substrings which are the various $x_i$'s ($i>1$) in the order of increasing $i$ value, starting with $i=2$, without skipping. See also: {porsi}. Operator/connective: {joi'i}.

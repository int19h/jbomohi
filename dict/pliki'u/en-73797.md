$x_1=p_1$ uses/employs $x_2=p_2=k_1$ (reason/justification/explanation/pretext/excuse) for/causing/permitting $x_3=p_3=k_2$ (event/state); $p_1=x_1$ justifies $x_3=p_3=k_2$ (event/state) via justification $x_2=p_2=k_1$; $x_2=p_2=k_1$ is the reason/justification/explanation/pretext/excuse for $x_3=p_3=k_2$ (event/state) used by $x_1=p_1$ (justifier).

## Notes

$p_3=k_2$ may not necessarily be caused by $p_1=x_1$. $p_1=x_1$ is simply employing a justification for $p_3=k_2$. This word refers to the employment of a justification, rather than necessarily proving or refuting causality. The justification $p_2=k_1$ used by $p_1=x_1$ may not necessarily reflect $p_1$'s true belief; $p_1$ employs it regardless of sincerity.

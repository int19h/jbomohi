$x_1=s_1$ is evidence for $x_2=k_2$ with strength of evidence $x_3=s_3=p_1$ (numerator $x_4=p_2$, denominator $x_5=p_3$, believer $x_6=k_1$, new odds $x_7=k_3$, old odds $x_8=s_2$).

## Notes

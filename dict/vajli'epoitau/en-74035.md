$x_1=t_1=p_2=p_3=l_2=v_2$ is a big-endian / most-significant-first tanru formed with $x_3=t_2$ modifying $x_2=t_3=l_1=v_1$, giving meaning $x_4=t_4$ in usage/instance $x_5=t_5=v_3$.

## Notes

When used as a language feature (with {baunpli}, e.g. «.i mi baunpli la vajli'epoitau le vi selsku»), according to this definition, reverse tanru order, so that without {co} the first (left) side becomes the {tertau} and the second (right) side becomes the {seltau}, and with {co} the second (right) side becomes the {tertau} and the first (left) side becomes the {tertau}; only tanru ordering is reversed, and not grammar (in particular tanru grouping and associativity, e.g. with {bo} and {co}, which retain their right-associativity) or lujvo.  Note that since tanru order is reversed relative to CLL Lojban, meaning might be more easily confused unless this feature's usage is explicit.

ni'o See also: {vajyje'ipoitau} (little-endian tanru), {co'i'e'au'i} («sei baunpli be la vajli'epoitau»), {vajli'epoi} (big-endian).

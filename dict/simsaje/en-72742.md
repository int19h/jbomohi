$x_1$ is similar to/appears like/seems like $x_2$ in quality/property/aspect $x_3$ (ka), and the argument(s) in the $x_4$th (li; 1 or 2) sumti slot(s) of this word actually attains/has the said quality (vel sim.) $x_3$ according to standard $x_5$.

## Notes

This word is similar to but more relaxed than "{dunlije}"; it is also similar to "{jibnije}". $x_4$ must be at least one of 1 or 2, but it can be both; use "{po'o}" or 'XOR' in order to preclude the possibility of the other option. See also: "{trajije}", "{zmaduje}", etc.

$x_1$ (text) is a clause with adverbial $x_2$ (text) and connecting members of $x_3$ (ordered group of text)

## Notes

The first member of $x_3$ is usually called 'complement' or 'argument'; it is what comes after the adposition $x_2$. The second member of $x_3$ can sometimes be called 'noun phrase of adposition'; it can e.g. be the current clause ({lo} {brisni}), $x_1$ of it, $x_2$ of it etc. See also {brisni}, {sumti}, {sumtcita}.

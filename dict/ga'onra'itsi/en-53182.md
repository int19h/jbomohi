$t_1=g_1=r_1$ is an indehiscent fruit/seed-envelope (which remains closed/sealed and whole) of organism $t_2$ with potential to produce/containing/enclosing offspring $t_3=g_3$ preventing passage to $g_2$  (default: external environment)

## Notes

r2 is assumed to be indefinite (indehiscent fruit must be processed/stimulated by active external means in order to release their seeds). There is no reason for the 'fruit' to necessarily be of a plant, although it probably will be.

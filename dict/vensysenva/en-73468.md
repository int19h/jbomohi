$x_1=s_1$ ultradreams about $x_2=s_2$

## Notes

x1 engages in extended intense imagination/daydreaming, similar to a waking dream. Generally connected with a trance-like state of consciousness, and typically combined with roleplay if x1 is multiple people. This word is not a jvajvo; its etymology is based on the symbolism of spring, with events in this state tending to 'spring' out of nowhere, and a certain magical spirit sprouting to life, combined with the fact that {vensa} and {senva} are anagrams.

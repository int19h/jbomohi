the referent of itself; guards the scope of the sumti

## Notes

Used for sumti that are hard to limit the scope of outer quantifiers for and where {lo'e}/{le'e} is inappriproate. E.g. '{cange} {fo} {zi'u} {lo} {vanjba}' where the scope of '{lo} {vanjba}' needs to be self-contained. Alternative to the {xorlo} reform. Equivalent to '{la'e} {lu'e}' and '{do'e} {ku} SUMTI' with the semantics of {do'e} removed.

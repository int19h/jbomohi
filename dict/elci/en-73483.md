$x_1$ is a unit of $x_2$

## Notes

ismu of {selci}. differs from zo selci in that it doesn't necessarily imply an indivisible, most basic subunit, but just a unit being treated as basic and indivisible. It also doesn't imply or have connotations of units building something larger.

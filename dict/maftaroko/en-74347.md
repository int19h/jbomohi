$x_1$ is tarot (the method of divination).

## Notes

Pseudo-stage-3 loanword from -maf- (combining form of {makfa}) and -taroko from Italian tarocco (or DE Tarock, EO taroko). Refers to tarot as a method of divination; the cards themselves are {kartaroko}.

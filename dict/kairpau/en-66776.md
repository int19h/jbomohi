being $x_1$ (ka) is part of being $x_2$ (ka)

## Notes

Or "$x_2$ implies $x_1$", but stronger than the logical sense; this word is definitional or essentialistic, and implies that if something stopped being $x_1$, it'd stop being $x_2$. See {kai'a}, {bi'ai}, {nibli}, {jinzi}

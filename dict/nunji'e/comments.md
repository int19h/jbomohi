Double-meaning of the gloss "life"

This valsi is glossed as "life", which can mean either "the event of being 
alive" or "the property of being alive". We should probably specify that 
"nunji'e" means the event and enter a new lujvo, "kamji'e", for the 
property. Does anyone have any objections?

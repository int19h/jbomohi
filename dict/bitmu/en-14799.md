$x_1$ is a wall/fence separating $x_2$ from $x_3$, and subdividing $x_4$.

## Notes

The order of $x_2$ and $x_3$ is unimportant. See also {jbini}, {sepli}, {fendi}, {canko}, {drudi}, {kumfa}, {loldi}, {senta}, {snuji}, {pagre}, {gacri}, {kuspe}, {marbi}, {vorme}.

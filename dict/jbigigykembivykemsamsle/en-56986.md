$se1$ is appoximately a billion ($10^{9}$) of eight indivisible, most basic computer subunits of computer $se2$; se1 is what we know as a gigabyte.

## Notes

A gigabyte is actually $1024^{3}$ bits. But this takes a long time to say. (In the interest of brevity, this word can be shortened to something such as: bivygigysle (eight billion ($10^{9}$) indivisible units) This is more ambiguous, but if the right context is established it will work just as well.)

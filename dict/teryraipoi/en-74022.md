$x_1=r_3$ [ka zmadu or ka mleca] is the endianness / orientation of most-vs-least-significant-first of sequence $x_2=p_1$ with ordering $x_3=p_2$ on set $x_4=p_3=t_4$, with most/least-significant item $x_5=t_1$ first in aspect $x_6=t_2$.

## Notes

Synonym: {vajraipavmompoi}, {endi}.  See also: {vajli'epoi} (big-endian), {vajyje'ipoi} (little-endian).

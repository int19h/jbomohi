$x_1$ is a Sinitic language used by $x_2$ to communicate $x_3$.

## Notes

Refers to all languages in the Sino-Bai branch of Sino-Tibetan, including Old and Middle Chinese and possibly the Bai langauges. Not synonymous with "Mandarin" (use {ca'irjugbau} instead). See also {jungo}, {bangu}, {jugygu'e}, {djunguos}, {xa'anzu}.

digit/number: a strong/substantial/decisive majority.

## Notes

More than barely a majority (even in any practical sense of "{so'e'uxo'ei}"), but less than "{so'e'a}" or even perhaps "{so'e'i}". Strongly context-dependent. Refers to an integer when possible unless preceded by "{pi}". See: "{so'e}", "{so'e'u}".

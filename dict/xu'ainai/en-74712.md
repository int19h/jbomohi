evidential: I declare - I submit; «ju'a'ei rikyxu'a»

## Notes

As in a causal declaration.  Also ‘let’ as in ‘let there be’.  A shorter version of {ju'a} {ma'o'ei} {rikyxu'a} (or {ju'a'ei} {rikyxu'a}).

ni'o See also {ca'e} (I define - I conform (proposed «nai» interpretation)), {rikyxu'a} (declare), {si'au} (it seems; as in {ju'a} {ma'o'ei} {simlu}).

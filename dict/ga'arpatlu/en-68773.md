$x_1$ is a potato [an edible tuber] stick (not necessarily fried) of variety/cultivar x2

## Notes

This can be used to refer to potato sticks/french fries that weren't yet cooked. Combine with {se} {jukpa} and/or {spagrasu} for referring unambigously to a french fry. See also {patlu} and {grana}, {pa'orpatlu}

$x_1$ is a game concerning or involving $x_2$.

## Notes

The second slot is relatively vague and may be either an action or a thing. Combining forms *-gem-* and *-ge'o-*, the last one being a proposed steal from the cultural word {gento}. This word was mostly made for easier compounding in the names of games (-selkei and -kelci'e are often quite long when attached to a base that is already two morphemes), but it also serves to distinguish playing a game, {gemzu'e}, from just generally "playing" ({kelci}), which can be done with for example a toy ({keitci}). {samgemjo} 'computer game' [shorter *samge'o* with proposed affix], {cungemjo} ~ {cunge'o} 'game of chance', {gemkubli} 'dice'...

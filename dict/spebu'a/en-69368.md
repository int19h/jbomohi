$x_1$ is a brother-in-law (brother of a spouse) of $x_2$ according to custom/law $x_3$.

## Notes

$x_1$ is a brother of a spouse of $x_2$. For "spouse of sibling" use "{tubyspe}", "{bunspe}" (brother's spouse), "{me'ispe}" (sister's spouse)' for other "sibling of spouse" use "{speme'i}" (sister of spouse) or "{spetunba}" (sibling of spouse); for "sibling of one's sibling's spouse" use {tubyspetunba} vel sim. See also: {spelanzu}.

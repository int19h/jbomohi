$x_1$ wants $x_2$ to not happen, for purpose $x_3$.

## Notes

This is usually what one ought say when they say "I don't want you to do that!" Really, one means to say that they want the listener to *not* do something. The desire is affirmative, but the object of desire is negative. See {djica}.

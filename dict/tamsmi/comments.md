There is already x3 for a property.

This lujvo's only reason of existence is to give
a source brivla for {tai}. {simsa} would have been
enough, but unforunately it lacks a "t" and the vowels
are in the wrong order. {tai} was based on {tarmi} 
before there was a strict semantic relationship 
between each BAI and its corresponding gismu. The idea 
was that it mean "like" and such. When it was realized 
that {tarmi} had the wrong place structure for this,
{tamsmi} was concocted. {tamsmi} will probably never
be used other than in a dictionary for the definition
of {tai}. (Notice: this is not an official or approved
story of how {tamsmi} came about.)

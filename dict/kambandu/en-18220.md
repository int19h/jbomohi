$k_1$ is a security condition of $b_1$ protecting $b_2$ from threat $b_3$.

## Notes

When used as "security", x3 is a planned threat. "security" is comparable to "safety" ({kamnu'a}), "reliability" ({kamylacri}), and "continuity" ({kamra'i}).

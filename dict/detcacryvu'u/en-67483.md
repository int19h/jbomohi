$x_1$ (number) is the number of hours (pi'e for minutes) that the timezone of location $x_2$ is ahead (before, if $x_1$ is negative) of location/standard $x_3$

## Notes

See {detri}, {tcika}, {cacra}, {vujnu}. $x_3$ may be {la} .{utc}. to indicate difference from UTC time.

## Examples

### Example 785

li so detcacryvu'u la .tokiios. la .utc.

### Example 786

li ni'umu detcacryvu'u la .panamas. la .utc.

### Example 787

li mu pi'e vomu detcacryvu'u la .nepal. la .utc.

### Example 788

li pavo detcacryvu'u la .tokiios. la .panamas.

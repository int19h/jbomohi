$f_{1}$ is a brush/shrubland [field of plants] with predominant plant species/cultivar $s_{2}$.

## Notes

Not neccessarily limited to bushes, this can refer to any expanses of land with plants, wild or not. Cf. {cicyspafoi} (for wild plants), {foldi}.

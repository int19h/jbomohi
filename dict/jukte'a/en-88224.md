$x_1$ is afraid of arachnids (usually spiders) of species/breed $x_2$, $x_1$ has arachnophobia.

## Notes

The {jukni} element here refers just to arachnids (for other non-insect arthropods use {lokra} or {cumba}). See also {terpa}.

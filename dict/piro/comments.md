My interpretation

This word is distinct from "{pisoi'ai}". It means "literally and exactly 
all of". The referant subset contains all of the elements of the relevant 
superset without exception. In probability theory, this relevant event 
would not only occur with probability 1 ( = 100%), but would actually be 
theoretically guaranteed; any alternative event is actually and literally 
impossible.

$x_1=s_2=m_1$ is less esteemed/respected/venerated/highly regarded than $x_2=m_2$ by $x_3=s_1$ [object of respect] by margin $x_4=m_4$.

## Notes

This follows the pattern of «-me'a» inserting what $x_1$ is compared to into $x_2$ (and appending $m_4$).  While it is still possible to choose an alternative place structure, for different place structures consider inserting «me'a» elsewhere.

tcadu4 is maybe a subset of the union of .urbe4, .urbe5, and the nearby 
rural region. In any case, "tcadu" does not have the the same 
structure/heirarchical ordering/divisions as ".urbe" (modulo any ordering 
of terbri).

to characterize them as having a ton of overlap (klesrverlapi) in terbri 
reference sets.

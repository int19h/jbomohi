$x_1$ (ka) is the relation of predicate $x_2$ (du'u), which has arguments $x_3$ (sequence of sumti).

## Notes

In Lojban, for example, "lo ka ce'u prami ce'u cu selbri lo du'u mi prami do ku mi ce'o do". In the mathematical notation P(x, y, z) for a predicate, the relation P is the selbri. Note that $x_1$ is not textual, even though Lojbanists informally tend to call a word or phrase "the selbri" of an utterance. For this usage see {selbrisni}.

$x_1$ is the captain/commander/leader/boss/superior of/to the captain/commander/leader/boss/superior of/to the captain/commander/leader/boss/superior of/to the ... captain/commander/leader/boss/superior of/to $x_2$ (subject, entity, vehicle, domain), connected by intermediate steps $x_3$.

## Notes

If $x_2$ is an entity, then $x_1$ is $x_2$'s boss' boss' boss' … boss. In the U.S. military, the President of the United States (in the capacity of Commander-in-Chief) is the jatny'utka of every member of the military along some graph-path/hierarchy of command. This word is a {brapagjvo} due to the presence of the {zevla} {veljvo} ".{utka}".

$f_{1}$ is a brush/shrubland [field of wild plants] with predominant plant species/cultivar $s_{2}$.

## Notes

Not neccessarily limited to bushes, this can refer to any expanses of land with wild plants. Cf {spafoi} (without the restriction to wild plants), {foldi}.

$x_1$ is a croissant.

## Notes

{tairmlunra} 'crescent' felt too long for the compound; I went for regular {lunra} instead, even though croissant means "crescent" and not just "moon" in French. Alternatively this could be a loanword ({krasone}?), and maybe this should just mean 'mooncake' instead. See also {jirnytamnanba}, {lurcuknanba}.

## 2015-07-16T02:08:40Z — spheniscine (comment 2304)

Example use-case

I found a example case where {cau'i} has a use besides being a mere 
shortening of {ju'ocu'i}: the second "{va'i}" definition of {mi'oi}: ({mi 
jo'u do jo'u cau'i lo drata be mi e do}). What I needed was something that 
was elliptical between {ja'ai} and {nai}, and could mean either or both. 
{ju'ocu'i} won't quite work here, cause I am very certain about desiring 
the ambiguity!

It was previously rendered as {mi jo'u do jo'u ka'ebo lo drata be mi e do},
but I'm not sure {ka'e} means what we want it to mean here.

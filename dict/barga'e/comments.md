Comparison to "barterzga"

I prefer this option to "barterzga" because:
(1) Its veljvo structure is semantically more sound in that "ganse" is
more general than "zgana" (which implies visual perception only).
(2) It is shorter by phoneme or rafsi count (not by syllable count).
(3) It does not have an internal SE conversion, and thus is easier/more
intuitive to remember and to use, and also has a generally more useful
terbri structure.

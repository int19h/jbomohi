Evidential builder; convert arbitrary bridi to UI2 with the grammar of {sei}.

## Notes

This general mechanism facilitates more general bridging between simple bridi and a specific feature of Lojban grammar, aiding in providing for a more minimal grammar foundation. ju'a'ei can convert to UI2 arbitrary bridi, allowing the creation of new evidentials.  {ju'a'ei} is a shorter version of {ju'a} {ma'o'ei}.  Example: «.i ju'a'ei simlu mi snada .iji'a .i si'au».

ni'o See also ma'o'ei.

ni'o Synonym: {sei'e}.

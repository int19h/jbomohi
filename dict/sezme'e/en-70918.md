$c_1$ is an autonym (self-given name) of $c_2=c_3$.

## Notes

May be a collective name, such as Diné, the endonym of the Navajo/Naabeehó people; or may be an individual's self-given name. Not synonymous with "local name" as in English. Potentially synonymous with {nerme'e}, (which more closely translates "endonym", morpheme-for-morpheme, rather than "autonym"). An exonym or external name might be called {barme'e}.

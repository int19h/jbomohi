$x_1$ is the rhinarium [body-part] of $x_2$.

## Notes

This is for the anatomical structure found on vertebrates and analogous to that of the wet nose of a dog; the sense of the English word referring to some external chemoceptors of some invertibrates is not covered. Basically equivalent to "{rilnari}".

$x_1=s_1$ opposes / is contrary to / against / hinders / [resists] $x_2=s_2$ towards force / opposition / harm / resistance / goal (possibly indirect) $x_3=s_3$ with [direct] force / by means / with $x_4=s_4$.

## Notes

Opposite of supporting ({sarji}).  A stronger claim than merely not supporting something ({narsra}).  Can be active or passive.  See also: {gaurtolcando}, {gaurcando}, {fapro}, {xarnu}, {randa}, {darlu}, {tugni}, {zunti}, {daspo}, {lanxe}, {zanru}.

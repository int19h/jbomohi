attitudinal scope modifier: marks following attitudinal/UI-cluster as applying to the entire sentence or statement

## Notes

Context-free way of attaching attitudinals or other UI-cmavo to entire sentences or statements as an afterthought. E.g. do sidju mi lo nu mi zenba lo ni ricfu i'au ui . See {ji'au}, {fu'e}, {fu'o}. Closable with {toi'o}.

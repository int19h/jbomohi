$x_1$ is a crab (type of aquatic crustacean) of type $x_2$

## Notes

Less taxonomically specific than {braxiiura}; generally refers to any aquatic decapod with a broad shell and pincers, including those aren't classed as infraorder Brachyura, e.g. hermit crabs, king crabs, and porcelain crabs. See {cakyjukni}, {karki}, {lokra}.

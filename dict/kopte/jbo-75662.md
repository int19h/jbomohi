$x_1$ is a helicopter for transporting passengers or cargo $x_2$.

## Notes

Split from {vinji} 'plane' as a form of "dejuknification"; they are both {volma'e} 'aircraft' but generally one wants to describe planes and helicopters with different words. {dartrokopte} 'drone'. Synonyms: {kopteri}, {carna'ivinji}, {carna'ima'e}.

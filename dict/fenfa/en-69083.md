$x_1$ (li) is an $x_3$rd root of $x_2$, with other (identifying) characteristics $x_4$.

## Notes

This word is related to {seltenfa} in basically the same way that {dugri} is related to {se'ai'e} {zei} {tenfa}. Notice that each of {fenfa}, {tenfa}, and {dugri} each can essentially be expressed by permuting the terbri of any one of them. Thus, this word fixes an asymmetry in the definitions. Since there can be many $n$th roots of a radicand, $x_4$ allows for a means of uniquely describing/identifying which one of these options $x_1$ is. See also: {fe'a}.

I got sick of saying "parent('s) parent".

So I made this word.

Besides, this word is slightly more general due to its not being 
necessarily specifically a grandparent. It could be a 
great-uncle/great-aunt, or even a family friend of that generation, or all 
sorts of other things.

Great-grandparents

Use "rirni zei .abvele" or ".abvele be lo rirni" for the generalized notion
of a great-grandparent (which can include 
great-great-uncles/great-great-aunts or any associated member in their 
generation).

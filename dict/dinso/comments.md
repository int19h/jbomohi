Dimetrodons are synapsids

Under the notes, it is stated, "its referent might also/alternatively 
include pterosaurs, icthyosaurs, plesiosaurs, mosasaurs, dimetrodons, 
avemetatarsaliads, and perhaps even synapsids." Pay attention to the last 
bit "dimetrodons (...) and perhaps even synapsids." Since dimetrodons are 
synapsids, I believe the description should be changed to something along 
the lines of "its referent might also/alternatively include pterosaurs, 
icthyosaurs, plesiosaurs, mosasaurs, avemetatarsaliads, and synapsids." The
difference is that "dimetrodon" is removed and "perhaps even synapsids" is 
shortened to "synapsids" since dimetrodons are synapsids.

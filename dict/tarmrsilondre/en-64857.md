$x_1$ is a generalized cylinder (see notes for details) of form/shape $x_2$.

## Notes

$x_1$ is a set of points/shape (of any number of dimensions) such that $x_1$ is connected and there exists a line L such that for any line L' that is parallel to L, if L' has nonempty intersection with $x_1$, then there exists exactly one subinterval (line segment, ray, or line) J of L' such that J has nonzero (Lebesgue) measure and any subset of L' which nonemptily intersects $x_1$ is contained in J. An axially-perpendicular cross-section of $x_1$ probably is necessary to specify $x_2$. See also: {slanu}, {fru'austumu}.

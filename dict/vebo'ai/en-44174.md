## Notes

Wavelength is by default spatial distance, although could conceivably depend on the wave/frequency space ({sebo'au}).  Measured spatially across minimal whole periods/cycles (spatial sense) of the wave, from a given feature to the next spatial recurrence of the same feature (such as from peak to peak, trough to trough).  Typically denoted by lambda.

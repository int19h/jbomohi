$x_1$ considers, deems, finds $x_2$ to be $x_3$; $x_1$ thinks that $x_2$ is/does $x_3$.

## Notes

Similar to {jinvi}, but with the $x_2$ split from $x_3$. («do cidla le mi cnino taxfu ma?» what do you think of my new clothes?)

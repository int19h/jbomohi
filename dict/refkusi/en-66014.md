$x_1$ is recursively related to $x_2$ by applying $x_3$ (number) levels-of-recursion of the predicate-relation $x_4$ (ka with two ce'u)

## Notes

Short form: {efku}. tanru example for common usage: the day three days from now (tomorrow's tomorrow's tomorrow) -> lo bavlamdei refkusi be fi li ci . Cf. {krefu}, {rapli}, {refkusi}

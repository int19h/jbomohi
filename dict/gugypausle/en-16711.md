$s_1$ is a county/district/parish/province (second-level administrative division) of country $s_2=g_1$.

## Notes

Cf. {gugde}, {pagbu}, {selci}. A province can also be a first-level administrative division; for this use {gugypau}, or optionally {vipygu'e} if it is in a country with a federal government.

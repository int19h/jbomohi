$x_1$ (aspect/state/object) is easy for $x_2$ to gain/acquire but hard for $x_2$ to lose/fail to maintain, according to judgment/standard $x_3$.

## Notes

Certain diseases, wealth, and other things fall into this category. "Hard come, easy go" (example: respect, trust): {to'e} {venrelu}; "easy come, easy go": {arkadi'u}.

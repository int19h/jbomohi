$x_1$ is a salamander of species/breed $x_2$

## Notes

Short form of {kaurdata}; there are several other terms for "salamander" but this is a short one that can be useful in compounds. See also {banfi}, {rebybanfi}, {latcybanfi}, {xulylatci}, {aclotlu}, {curbanfi}, {bratcekaurta}, {amfiiuma}, {ple'urdeli}, {rufykaurta}.

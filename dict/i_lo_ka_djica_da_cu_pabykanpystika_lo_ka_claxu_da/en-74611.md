Wanting something suggests lacking it.

## Notes

A revision of an older entry that used «nibli» instead of «pabykanpystika».  It is explicitly about evidence and suggesting a lack, although «nibli» doesn't necessarily mean «mulno nibli», e.g. ‘entails’, but can also mean ‘implies’, so the original version works too.  «nibli» : «pabykanpystika» :: «ni'i» : «ni'i'e».

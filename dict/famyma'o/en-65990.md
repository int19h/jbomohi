$x_1=c_1$ is a terminator word (for use with/that terminates) class/part-of-speech $x_2=f_2$, in language $x_3=c_3$

## Notes

Nonstandard lujvo place-structure but I believe it is the most natural, to allow: zo ku cu famyma'o ma'oi le : "ku" is the terminator for selma'o LE.

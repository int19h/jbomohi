Generalizing

This construct does not perfectly generalize to, say, excitons, which are
hydrogenic but not truly ions (since they are not chemical atoms but are,
rather, strange quantum bound states of a quanton with its lack-of-self).
But, I suppose that {.ionti} could actually be used in Lojban with a
slightly wider meaning than "chemical ion"; it does not quite satisfy the
case for excitons or positronium, but it does work for some other weird
cases.

Ultimately, though, we are going to want a construct that means
"isoelectronic hydrogen-like bound-state".

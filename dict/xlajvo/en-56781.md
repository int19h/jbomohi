$x_1$ is a bad/poorly-made lujvo with issue $x_2$, bad according to $x_3$ and by standard $x_4$

## Notes

The badness need not be an error per se; it may merely be some feature (x2) that is not desirable or relatively preferable.  For example, perhaps the terbri structure is not predictable/regular/algorithmic, or the lujvo is very long, or the lujvo is not the highest-scoring lujvo (for some fixed tanru construct) according to an algorithm/standard. See also: {srejvo}.

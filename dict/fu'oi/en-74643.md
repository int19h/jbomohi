indicates attachment of the following to the next instead of, as normal, previous construct.

## Notes

Place before UI to indicate it applies to the next construct rather than the previous.  See also {fu'e}…{fu'o}, {to}…{toi}, {tu'e'ei}…{tu'u'o}.

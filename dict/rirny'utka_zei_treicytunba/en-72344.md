$x_1$ is analogous to a sibling in a rank/seniority for/of a rirny'utka of $x_2$ by standard/in hierarchy/via partial ordering or relation(ship) $x_3$ (this is the connection between the rirny'utka at hand and $x_1$ which justifies the treicytunba label), where $x_2$ and the said rirny'utka are connected via intermediate steps $x_4$ (ce'o).

## Notes

An indirect ancestor (that is: the near-relation, sibling, or perhaps equal/contemporary of a direct ancestor) is included. The standard by which the direct ancestor (the rirny'utka) and $x_1$ are equals/near-relations is $x_3$; the meaning of/standard by which "ancestor" is meant is specified via relation(ship)s steps $x_4$. See also: "{dzena}" (which is more general).

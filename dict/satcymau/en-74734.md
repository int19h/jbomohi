$x_1=z_1=s_1$ is more exact/precise than $x_2=z_2$ is in property / quantity $x_3=s_3$ by amount / excess $x_4=z_4$.

## Notes

{satcymau} uses «-mau» to insert $zmadu_2$ at $x_2$ (and append $zmadu_4$; see also {fi'o}) (this lujvo construction also adapts $zmadu_3$ to $satci_2$ rather than a relationship that includes it, making it slightly less regular, since it's much more useful and the alternative doesn't seem very useful; see also the difference between {zmanei} and {nelmau}).  See also: {satcyme'a}.

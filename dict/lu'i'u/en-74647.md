discursive: quoted expression / [scare quotes]

## Notes

{lu'i'u} is like adding quotes to an expression in English, while preserving the grammar, as in scare quotes.  {fu'e}{lu'i'u}…{fu'o} can be used to manually indicate the scope.  {fu'oi}{lu'i'u} can be used to attach to the following rather than previous construct.  See also {cu'u}.

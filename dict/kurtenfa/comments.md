I would not recommend borrowing so directly from English. There is a
relationship between square shapes and square numbers, of course, but it
is not very important or even natural. Moreover, {kurfa} applies to any
orthotope or any shape containing a right angle (thus implying also cubes)
and possibly anything orthogonal (which is a universe of possibilities far
greater than may be imagined).

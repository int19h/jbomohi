$x_1$ believes that $x_2$ exists, is real; $x_1$ believes in $x_2$.

## Notes

su'o verba cu zatkri la .santaklaus. 'some children believe in Santa Claus'. Somewhat more specific than {zatyji'i}, which is just 'think ... is real'; {krici} has somewhat of an implication of believing something without evidence.

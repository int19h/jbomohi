$x_1$ is white (or very-light) colored.

## Notes

Compounds with white, such as {labyxu'e} meaning pink, are pale forms of other colors. See also {skari}, {xekri}, {grusi}, {kandi}, {manku}, {carmi}, {blanu}, {bunre}, {cicna}, {crino}, {narju}, {nukni}, {pelxu}, {xunre}, {zirpu}.

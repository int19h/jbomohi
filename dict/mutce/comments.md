"Erg mooi" vs. "very, pretty"

The Dutch definition seems to be based on the English definition, and is 
correct except where it says, "Ook : erg mooi." The mistake seems to be 
seeing "very, pretty" as "very pretty" (i.e. dropping the comma).

I propose removing "mooi" from the Dutch definition, since it doesn't 
encompass this particular meaning of "pretty," and rearranging the English 
words to avoid such confusion in the future. If no one objects in the next 
few days, I'll make the changes myself.

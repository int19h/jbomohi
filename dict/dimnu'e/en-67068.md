$x_1$ swears / takes an oath, promising/asserting $x_2$ (abstraction), invoking fate $x_3$ (abstraction)

## Notes

$x_2$ could be a nu-event or ka-action that $x_1$ intends to happen or perform, or a du'u-proposition that $x_1$ swears the truth of. $x_3$ is a fate (typically bad) that $x_1$ invokes on themself should they break the oath. {tu'a} can be used to raise $x_3$, to swear by a treasured possession (implied fate being loss of that possession), or a deity (implied fate being the wrath of said deity). See {dimna}, {maldimna}, {nupre}, {cevni}, {dapma}

## Examples

### Example 726

mi dimnu'e lo du'u la'e di'u jetnu kei lo ka lindi ce'u

### Example 725

mi dimnu'e lo nu aicai do mrobi'o kei tu'a le xejykre be la .zeuus.

### Example 727

aicai do mrobi'o xoi mi dimnu'e ke'a tu'a le xejykre be la .zeuus.

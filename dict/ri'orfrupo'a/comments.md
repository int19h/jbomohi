Place keywords

Just a little jbotip: the place keywords are intended to be the gloss word
for each of the places, not the function of the x1, x2 etc. So for
instance, for {gerku}, "x1 is a dog of species/breed x2," the place
keyword for x1 would be "dog" and the place keyword for x2" would be "dog
species."

Anyway, good luck and happy lojbanning!

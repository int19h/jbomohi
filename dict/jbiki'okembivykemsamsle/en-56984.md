$se1$ is appoximately a thousand of eight indivisible, most basic computer subunits of computer $se2$; se1 is what we know as a kilobyte

## Notes

A kilobyte is actually 1024 bits. But this takes a long time to say, especially when moving up to higher units. (In the interest of brevity, this word can be shortened to something such as: bivki'osle (eight thousand indivisible units) This is more ambiguous, but if the right context is established it will work just as well.)

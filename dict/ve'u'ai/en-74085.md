## Notes

This can be used with {baunpli}-based contractions like {co'i'e} to explicitly specify scope.  See also: {ve'a'ai}, {ve'u'ai}, {ve'o'ai}, {vo'a'ei} (＝＝ «{vo'a}ve'u'ai»).  Etymology: {ve'u} and UI (to ‘'ai’).

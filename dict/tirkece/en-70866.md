## Notes

Synonymous exactly with the proposed cultural gismu {turko}, but more closely reflects native Turkish phonetics. (The front rounded vowel [y], found in the source word, Türkçe, is an allophone of Lojban's /i/) Alternate form {tirkice}. See also {andolu} 'Anatolian', {gugdrtirki} 'Turkey', {kulnrtirki} 'Turkish', {bangrtirki}, {turkiko}, among others.

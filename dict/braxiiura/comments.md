Brachyura

Is it wise to define "crab" with a fu'ivla for infraorder Brachyura? Quoth 
Wikipedia:

"Many other animals with similar names ? such as hermit crabs, king crabs, 
porcelain crabs, horseshoe crabs and crab lice ? are not true crabs." (i.e.
not in Brachyura)

Except for crab lice and possibly horseshoe crab, we might have a case of 
scientific and laic taxonomy clashing here, just like using "bug" to refer 
to Hemiptera. I doubt the average Joe would be able to look at hermit 
crabs, king crabs, and porcelain crabs, and be able to tell right away that
they are not "true crabs".

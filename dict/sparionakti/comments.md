wrong type fu'ivla

From the meaning, it looks like you meant to make a type-3 fu'ivla, but
this is a type-4. The type-3 is "spatrxionakti" or "spatrjonakti", with a
rafsi ending in a consonant (CVC is permitted, but "spati" doesn't have
one), and the foreign part beginning with a consonant.

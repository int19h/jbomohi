## Notes

like {celxa'i} but not a jvajvo or a lujvo at all. doesn't include everything that "{cecla} {gi'e} {xarci}", but only guns. somewhat similar to {cladaxi} in relation with {cmacelxa'i}. x4 is not necessarily a bullet: it could be a laser, or fire, or anything that could be shot from a gun.

I added it like this because at least at the time, nobody was using
"dontu'u" itself as a standalone word, while the phrase {la dontu'u} was
common. The part of speech does look weird, but of the two that take
entries with more than one word (the other being cmevla) it's the less
inappropriate one.

Adding "dontu'u" itself would be fine of course, but this doesn't go for
all {la} phrases. Consider {noltrube'a} (monarch's son) vs. "la
noltrube'a" (the software used to typeset CLL).

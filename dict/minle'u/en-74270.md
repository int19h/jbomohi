## Notes

Calque of Esperanto kromsigno 'besides-sign'. "Diacritic" here includes not only what you would typically think of as accent marks, but also Arabic harakat, Hebrew niqqud, Japanese dakuten, Devanagari nukta, etc. See also {lerfu}.

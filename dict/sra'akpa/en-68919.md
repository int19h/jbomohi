$x_1$ is an indentation/dent/impression/well/hole/bowl/pocket/invagination/sheath/divot in body $x_2$.

## Notes

This is in contrast with {kevna}, which may be a hollow interior space without connection to the unbounded exterior, and to {torxesrtubnu}, which requires full passage of the hole through the object. With the technology currently available to humans, all holes dug straight down into the earth/Earth are this sort of hole. For example: the well, which will hold liquid, in a coffee cup, rather than the region bounded by its handle and lateral surface, is an $x_1$.

$x_1$ (ka) is obtained from $x_2$ (ka) by uncurrying the first N places

## Notes

"lo ka ko'a ce'o ko'e ce'ai broda" (see wiki 'Abstraction Pattern Syntax') is equivalent to "lo reka'oi co ka ce'u ce'u broda"

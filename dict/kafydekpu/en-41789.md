$d_1$ is $d_2$ (default 1) coffee measuring cup(s), standard $d_3$ (default: 1 volume unit=1 cup of drinkable coffee), $d_4$ subunits.

## Notes

Coffee measure is a unit of volume that is not, strictly speaking, is a unit of measurement , but rather a name for a measuring cup with slightly different size, but usually 15 to 20 ml. The measure is designed to measure the adequacy 1-1.25 cups drinkable coffee .

$x_1$ thinks that $x_2$ exists or is real; $x_1$ believes in $x_2$.

## Notes

mi na zatyji'i lo'e pavjima 'I don't believe in unicorns', xu do zatyji'i lo cevni 'do you believe in a god?'; for "believe something exists without evidence", use {zatkri} ({krici} is more specific than {jinvi}).

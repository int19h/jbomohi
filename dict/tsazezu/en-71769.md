$x_1$ performs workout/exercise/training $x_2$ (ka) using equipment/accessories $x_3$.

## Notes

{jbofuvi} formed from the lujvo {tsazenzu'e} ({tsali} {zenba} {zukte}) but with a bespoke place structure to make it useful for discussing working out. Bodyweight exercise can be expressed by using "lo {xadni}" in the x3. The x2 is a property of the x1 that is the exercise, e.g. "lo ka {limna}" for working out by swimming. The x3 can be used to refer to equipment on its own via "lo te tsazezu" and also for variants of exercises, e.g. "mi limna tsazezu fi lo {tilju}" to refer to swimming using weights.

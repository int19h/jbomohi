$r_1=b_1$ is a herp/herptile [amphibian and/or reptile] of species/breed $r_2=b_2$.

## Notes

"Herp" is a vernacular term for reptiles and amphibians. It is derived from the old term "herpetile", with roots back to Linnaeus' classification of animals, in which he grouped reptiles and amphibians together in the same class.

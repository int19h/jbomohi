$x_1$ is a genetic-familial/'blood' descendant of $x_2$ by bond/tie/relation/of degree $x_3$.

## Notes

$x_1$ must be the genetic offspring of $x_2$ or the genetic offspring of someone who is also a ginlazyseldze of $x_2$; trivially, one may consider $x_2$ to be a ginlazydze of themself ($x_2$), and possibly/probably others. See also: {kraune}, {kloje}, {tseingu}, {dzena}.  This is more specific than {seldze} ($x_3$ is much narrower). This word is equivalent to "{se} {ginlazdze}".

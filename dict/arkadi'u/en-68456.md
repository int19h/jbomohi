$x_1$ (aspect/state/object) is easy for $x_2$ to gain/acquire but also easy for $x_2$ to lose/fail to maintain, according to judgment/standard $x_3$.

## Notes

In some situations, power, fame, favor, etc. qualify for $x_1$. "Hard come, hard go": {to'e} .{arkadi'u}; "easy come, hard go": {venrelu}.

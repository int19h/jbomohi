$x_1$ is native/indigenous/aboriginal/autochthonous/endemic to $x_2$ in property $x_3$ (ka).

## Notes

Can refer to indigenous people/cultures which were the first to inhabit an area, or to native/endemic species. Close opposite of {fange} which the argument structure was based on. See also {fange}, {rarna}, {krasi}.

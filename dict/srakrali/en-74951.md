$x_1=k_1$ is the positive right of / owned by $x_2=k_2$ to obligate $x_3=s_1$ to obligation / action $x_4=s_2$ by standard / under law / custom $x_5=k_3$; $x_2$ has the right to (tu'a) $x_4$ in the sense of active obligation.

## Notes

Part of the negative rights vs. positive rights distinction.  E.g. negative rights: I have the right to not be prohibited from health care.  Positive rights: I have the right to obligate others to provide health care.  See also: {ri'urkrali} (positive right), {krali} (right), {ponse} (own), {bilga} (obliged), {marde} (morality), {flalu} (law).

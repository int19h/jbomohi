## 2015-06-12T07:31:41Z — spheniscine (comment 2004, on definition 66420)

Dialectal/proposal-related variation notes:

Under the "gadganzu" proposal: ( 
http://mw.lojban.org/papri/zipcpi:_Yet_another_gadri_article#Non-gadri:_voi
), this is just {voi}, as the original veridicality distinction has lost 
popular favor.

However, this clashes with the "cekitaujau" ( 
http://mw.lojban.org/papri/ce_ki_tau_jau ) dialectal reassignment of 
{poi'i} to {voi}. Thus, {voi'i} is defined here as an unambiguous variant.

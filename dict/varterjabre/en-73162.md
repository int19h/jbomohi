$x_1$ is an air brake (control surface) of vehicle $x_2$ in atmosphere $x_3$; $x_1$ slows the motion of vehicle $x_2$ by increasing drag in atmosphere $x_3$.

## Notes

Most common on aircraft, but may also be present on other types of vehicles. Not to be confused with compressed air brakes. See {vacri}, {jabre}.

$x_1$ (number) is the date/day notation of the week of event/state $x_2$, in system $x_3$.

## Notes

{jeftu} zei {djedi} zei {detri}. As default, the number $x_1$ is counted from the first day of the week. See {de'i'u} for BAI. See also {detke'u}.

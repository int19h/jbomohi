$x_1$ is a regular polyhedra/polygon/polytope with the Schläfli symbol $x_2$; $x_1$ is a regular polygon with $x_2$ number of sides.

## Notes

also "x1 is a regular polytope". Polytope is a collective term for polygons, polyhedra and other dimentional shapes. Use a vector to express the Schläfli symbol. For Coxeter's extention, use a matrix. Cf. {kubli}

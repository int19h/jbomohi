$x_1$ is huckleberry (some berries in Vaccinium and Gaylussacia) of variety $x_2$.

## Notes

Blend of the two genuses that include plants called "huckleberries", Vaccinium (vak-) and Gaylussacia (-saki, from the end). Coined by Mati and janbe.

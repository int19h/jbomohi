$x_1$ is the ol' Reddit switcheroo.

## Notes

An elaborate running gag on the content-sharing website Reddit. One has to "be there" to get it, but the r/switcharoo (sic) subreddit explains the basic joke as follows: "A switcharoo occurs when a witty redditor feigns ignorance about which of two subjects in a post (typically an image) is more comment-worthy." See also {redit}, {basyzumfau}.

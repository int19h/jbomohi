$x_1$ (proposition) is a clause expressing adverbial relation $x_2$ (property of nonce place, has places for ce'u referring to members of $x_3$) and connecting members of $x_3$ (ordered group)

## Notes

The first member of $x_3$ is what follows after the adposition expressing adverbial relation $x_2$. The second member of $x_3$ can sometimes be called 'noun phrase of adposition'; it can e.g. be the current clause ({lo} {bridi}), $x_1$ of it, $x_2$ of it etc. See also {bridi}, {sumti}, {sumtcita}.

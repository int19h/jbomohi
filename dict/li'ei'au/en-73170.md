ternary mekso operator: retrieves/gets/outputs the $X_2$th entry/term from ordered list $X_1$ under indexing rules $X_3$.

## Notes

If $X_2$ is an integer such that, according to $X_3$,  $X_2$ is inclusively between the least index and the greatest index of $X_1$, then this word outputs the $X_2$th entry. If $X_2$ is a set, then the outputs is the ordered list such that each entry thereof is some entry of $X_1$ such that its index in $X_1$ according to $X_3$ belongs to $X_2$; the output list is reindexed (so that the $j$th entry thereof has index $j$, where $j$ is a positive integer).

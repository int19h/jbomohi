It is cool that the referents of the feizrCe series are themselves usually
an example of a {feizrce}. This means that they can be compounded: for
example, one can construct an event which is composed of a regular
(non-random) sequence of extended events, each of which is itself composed
of its own (independent) sequence of random events, intermixed with either
simple or complex events; a bit of (jazz) music in which improvised and
(internally) wild solos occur at regular intervals, spaced apart by
scripted and repetitive music, could be an example of such.

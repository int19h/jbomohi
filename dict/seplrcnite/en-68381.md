$x_1$  is a Dedekind cut associated with number/point  $x_2$ of totally ordered set $x_3$

## Notes

Let $x_3 = (X, <) $ be an ordered set formed by (underlying) set X endowed with order relation '<'. $x_2$ is a member of X; lethe the singleton of $x_2$ (the set containing only and exactly $x_2$) be denoted by $Y $. Then $x_1$ constitutes an ordered pair of sets $(A, B)$ such that A and B are mutually disjoint subsets of X, A is closed downwardly/lesserwardly under '<', B is closed upwardly/greaterwardly under '<', $x_2$ is not an element of A, and the union of A with B and Y equals exactly X.

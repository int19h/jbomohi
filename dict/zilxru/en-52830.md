$x_1$ returns to $x_2$ from $x_3$; $x_1$ moves/gives back to $x_2$ from $x_3$.

## Notes

x1 goes back/reverts/retreats; (x2 may be a location or a person or an event/situation; the latter may also be expressed with {krefu} or {rapli} and a causative like {rinka}/{rikygau}). See also {benji}, {krefu}, {rapli}, {rinka}. For 'return something to something use {zilxrugau} or {xruti}.

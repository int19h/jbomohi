Why I think this definition is better.

This definition is a variant of sarefo's definition. It removes the last
two places because they are redundant here. “insurance against peril”
and “insurance benefits” are clearly dependent on the insurance
itself, because if you know the insurance, you also know the perils it
protects one from and its benefits. Dependent places should always be
dropped. This reduces the place number from 6 to 4.

Also, I replaced “insurance salesman” with “insurance
salesperson”.

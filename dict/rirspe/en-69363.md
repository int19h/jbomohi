$x_1$ is the step-parent/step-mother/step-father of $x_2$ due to marriage with their parent according to law/custom $x_3$.

## Notes

$x_1$ does not necessarily raise/rear/parent $x_2$ (for that, rear-concatenate "{rirni}" as a tertau). The gender of $x_1$ is not specified (nor is the gender of the parent of $x_2$). See also: "{pa'uspe}", "{mamspe}", "{sperirni}", "{lacpa}".

$x_1$ applies for job $x_2$ to $x_3$ in form $x_4$.

## Notes

The $x_3$ is likely the future employer. The $x_4$ can be used to say "apply online" or "apply by mail". See also {jibri}, {gasnu}, {cpedu}.

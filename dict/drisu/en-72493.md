$x_1$ is medicine used to treat illness/disease $x_2$ ({bilma}/ka/state) via mechanism of action $x_3$ (event) at dosage $x_4$ (ka))

## Notes

$x_1$ is medicinal. $x_4$ is properly a period and an amount, e.g. "loka {milgra} li panono {reroi} ro le {djedi}", but may be abbreviated to either. See also {mikce}, {micyxu'i}, {farmako}, {bilma}.

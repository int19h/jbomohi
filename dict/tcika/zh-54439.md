## Notes

$x_1$位的时间单位可由由pi'e连接的数字构成，也可由由joi连接的各单位值连接而成；参见 {ti'u}, {cacra}, {cerni}, {detri}, {donri}, {djedi}, {junla}, {nicte}, {mentu}, {snidu}, {temci}, {vanci}, {dirba}, {mokca}.

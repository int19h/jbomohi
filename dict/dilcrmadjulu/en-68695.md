$x_1$ (li) is congruent to $x_2$ (li) modulo $x_3$ (li)

## Notes

$x_1$ and $x_2$ are - strictly/technically speaking - symmetric, but typically and canonically, $x_2$ will be a number between $0$ and $x_3 - 1$ (inclusive on both ends) whereas $x_1$ can be any number in the algebraic object being considered.

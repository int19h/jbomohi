$j_1$ is the longitude/right ascension of $j_2$ in system $j_3$

## Notes

From {stuna}, {stici}, {judri},  Cf. {bernanjudri}, {plinyxabykoi}, {jedjipli'i}, {julra'o}, {cacryra'o}.    

## Examples

### Example 88

"la.tyrontos. se sunsicyjudri lo julra'o be li zeso pi'e revo bei vu'a" means "Toronto has a longitude 79° 24' W".

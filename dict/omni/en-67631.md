## Notes

Usable for "omni-" prefix in tanru or lujvo. e.g. omniscient (= {omnyju'o}), omnipotent (= {omnyka'e}), omnipresent (= {omnyzva}), omniglot (= {omnyselbau}), omnivore (= {omnycti}). See {ro}, {roda}, {roldza}

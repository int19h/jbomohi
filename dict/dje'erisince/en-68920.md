$x_1$ ((super)territory) is gerrymandered into pieces $x_2$ (set/list) with properties/with goal/following process $x_3$.

## Notes

In order to say "to gerrymander (agentively)", use {dje'erisince'ygau}. $x_2$ is a collection of gerrymandered territories, not those territories themselves; all elements/entries of $x_2$ are subregions of $x_1$.

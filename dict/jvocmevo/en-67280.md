$x_1$ is a lujvo-like cmevla meaning $x_2$

## Notes

Refers to words like {lojban}, which are built from {rafsi} like {lujvo}, but ends in a consonant and is therefore a {cmevla} rather than a {lujvo}. See {cmevo}. Syn. {jvocmevla}.

$x_1$ is the momentum [vector] of $x_2$ in frame of reference $x_3$

## Notes

$x_1$ can be a vector, e.g. four-vector. Might be pretty general in its semantic scope ("quantity of movement"); at the very least includes both Relativistic (four-vector) and Newtonian (three-vector) momentum (resp.: {nejnimpetu}, {sirmpetu}); might also encompass angular momentum ({cnampetu}, typically three-vector). See also {nejni}, {nejnimpetu}, {sirmpetu}, {ocnerta}, {tcelerita}, {cnampetu}

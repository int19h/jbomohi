$b_{1}$ is a musical band / orchestra of performers $b_{2}$ , conductor(s) $b_{3}$ , playing music $z_{1}$.

## Notes

No implication of the type of orchestra (e.g., string orchestra, wind orchestra, symphony orchestra) or band (e.g. rock band, non-standard band); an orchestra can have multiple conductors, and a band can have no conductors. See also {balzgibe'e}.

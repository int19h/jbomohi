$x_1=v_1=c_1$ is preserved food/feed/nutriment pickled/immersed in $x_2=j_2$.

## Notes

$x_2=j_2$ is salt (= {silna}), vinegar (= {slami}), alcohol (= {xalka}), sugar (= {sakta}), honey (= {bicysakta}), soy sauce (= {sobysanso}), bean paste (= {furso'epesxu}), mold paste (= {mledi} {pesxu}) and so on. {jinru} {vitno} {cidja}. See also {slamystogau}.

$p_1$ is an electronic piano/harpsichord/organ/keyboard instrument.

## Notes

Refers to a keyboard instrument that produces sound electronically (usually digitally), as opposed to mechanically by means of strings, pipes, reeds, etc. See also: {mrupi'o}, {balpi'o}, {sa'irpi'o}, {tu'urpi'o}, {cpupi'o}.

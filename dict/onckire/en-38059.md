$x_1$ wears 'on' (Japanese term) on $x_2$ due to $x_3$; $x_1$ is thankful/feels obligation/owes to $x_2$ due to $x_3$; $x_2$ is kind/grants favor/offers 'on' to $x_1$ in $x_3$; $x_1$ has unpayable debt to $x_2$ for $x_3$; $x_2$ is an 'onjin' (benefactor, Japanese term)

## Notes

In future lojban {ckire} might be equated with {onckire} and {nu ckire} with Japanese concept of 'on'.

$x_1$ (set, group, structure, category, class, etc.) has cardinality strictly more than aleph-null; $x_1$ is mathematically uncountable.

## Notes

There exists no bijection between (the set underlying) $x_1$ and any (possibly non-proper) subset of the set of all natural numbers. See also: {cimni}, {kancuka'e}.

$x_1$ is a binary relation which is left-Euclidean on space/set/under conditions $x_2$.

## Notes

Definitionally, $x_1$ is a possibly-ordered binary relation given by set $R$ such that for all $a, b, c$ in $x_2$ (if such makes sense; else: the universe of discourse restricted by $x_2$), if $(b, a)$ in $R$ and $(c, a,)$ in $R$, then $(b, c)$ in $R$ and $(c, b)$ in $R$. See also: "{takni}", ".{efklipi}".

$x_1$ is classical logic, for deducing/concluding/inferring $x_2$ (du'u)

## Notes

Any logic that has the laws of: excluded middle, double negative elimination, noncontradiction, principle of explosion (false implies anything), monotonicity and idempotency of entailment, commutativity of conjunction and DeMorgan's laws.

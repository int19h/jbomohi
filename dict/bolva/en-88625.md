$x_{1}$ is a bowl containing $x_{2}$, made of $x_{3}$.

## Notes

As a part of a proposal to split {kabri} into 2 words — {kabri} is a container for liquids and {bolva} is a container for solids. Consistent with the place structure of {kabri}, even though it is redundant ({velbotsu'i}). See also {kabri}, {baktu}, {palta}, {tansi}.

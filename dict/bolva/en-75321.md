$x_1$ is a bowl containing $x_2$, made of $x_3$.

## Notes

The second slot is a bottlecap ({velbotsu'i}), as with {kabri}, and should really say 'a bowl ›for‹ containing $x_2$'. This word is part of a proposal to split {kabri} into two words, {kabri} being a container for liquids and {bolva} being one for food. A cup, a glass, and a mug are all {kabri}, but a ramen bowl or a pet bowl are {bolva}.

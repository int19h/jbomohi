## Notes

也指“拯救”；$x_1$抵抗$x_3$；护盾={badgai}；参见 {ckape}, {fanta}, {fapro}, {marbi}, {rivbi}, {zunti}, {snura}, {binra}, {lunbe}, {pulji}

$x_1$ (event) defends/protects $x_2$ (object/state) from threat/peril/potential $x_3$ (event).

## Notes

Also $x_1$ secures $x_2$; $x_1$ wards/resists $x_3$. Protective cover/shield = {badgai}. See also {ckape}, {fanta}, {fapro}, {marbi}, {rivbi}, {zunti}, {snura}, {binra}, {pulji}.

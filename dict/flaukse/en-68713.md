$x_1$ is the vector flux (flow) of quantity/substance (count) $x_2$ through (geometric/imaginary) hypersurface/embedded manifold $x_3$ per unit hyperarea per unit time.

## Notes

This is the transport phenomena sense of "flux": a density of flow, the maximal rate of flow of a subsramce or quantity ($x_2$) through a differential hypersurface. If the hypersurface $x_3$ is $n$-dimensional spatially and the dimensionality (units) of $x_2$ is $U$, then the dimensionality (units) of $x_1$ is $U$/(1 m$^{n}$ * s). Fairly similar to {nildenmi}. For the hypersurface integral of this quantity (with respect to the normal), use: {flaumji}. See also: {flecu}.

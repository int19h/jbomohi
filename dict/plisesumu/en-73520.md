$x_1$ is a secondary meaning of $x_2$; $x_1$ is an indelible connotation/subtext of word/phrase $x_2$

## Notes

Coined by mungojelly (la lunbe): "A plisesumu is an implicit meaning that piggybacks along with the main meaning of something, asserting unrelated things in a way that's difficult to extract & deny." Refers to a specific kind of subtle, unintentional connotation, often visible in the shape or etymology of a word itself.

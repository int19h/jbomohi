Standard

Should there be a terbri for a standard which judges poverty? It is 
somewhat subjective (or at least fuzzy). A person with few coins of 
relatively little value is poor (in money). But what constitutes fewness or
littleness of value? (Note: Absolute lack is too much. A person with only a
single penny is hardly less poor than a person with exactly nothing; in any
case, both are certainly poor, by most reasonable modern standards.)

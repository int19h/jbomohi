on the Nth day of the week.

## Notes

{jefydeidetri} modal. ex.) lo karni cu co'a gubni de'i'u li ci (The journal is published on Wednesday.) See also {de'i}; {de'i'a}, {de'i'e}, {de'i'i}, {de'i'o}.

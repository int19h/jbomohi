$x_{1}$ is a norovirus of species/breed/defining property $x_{2}$ capable of infecting [at] $x_{3}$.

## Notes

From the city of Norwalk, Ohio, and {vidru}. The scientific name of the virus was first "Norwalk agent" and then shortened to "norovirus". See also {xilvidru}, {remjinkytoldu'evidru} and {vidru}.

## Notes

Also known as the Islamic Republic of Iran. Nearly synonymous with {gugde'iru}. Derived from Persian ایران‎ [ʔiˈɾɒn] 'Iran', یرانی‎ (Irâni) [ʔiɾɒˈni] 'Iranian', and more closely reflects native phonetics than the earlier {gugdrxirana}. See also {iRON} 'Iran' ({cmevla} form), {te'yRON} 'Tehran', {rupniruru} 'Iranian Rial', {kulnrfarsi}/{faursi} 'culturally Persian', {kulnrxirani}/{kulnrxironi} 'culturally Iranian', {bangrfarsi} 'Persian language', {islam} 'Islam', {muslo} 'Muslim'. See lujvo {faursygu'e}.

$g_1$ (person/agent) adjusts/regulates/changes $s_2$ (ka/ni) in amount/degree $s_3$

## Notes

Non-resultative (what changes is not changed into something else), causal (the change is caused by something) change. See also {cnegau}, {gafygau} which is resultative, {bixygau} which is resultative, {zasni}, {stodi}. Cf. {stika}, {gasnu}.

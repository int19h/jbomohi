$se_2=sk_1$ is a client [computer] served by server [computer] $se_1$ with service $se_3$ (activity).

## Notes

Networking is implied. Note that the term “client” is used quite broadly in English, even if narrowed down to the networking sense. For “samselse'u”, the $x_1$ and $x_2$ places are only used to refer to the involved computers. See also: {samse'u}

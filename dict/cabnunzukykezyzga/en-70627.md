$x_1$ watches the event $x_2$ unfold before their very eyes/live/red-handed/in flagrante (delicto), using senses/means $x_3$.

## Notes

No implication of the action being considered improper or discouraged. The event must be an action (lo nu {zukte}), not just an occurrence. "Before one's very eyes" is an idiomatic expression and does not require sight to have been involved, nor $x_1$ to even have eyes.

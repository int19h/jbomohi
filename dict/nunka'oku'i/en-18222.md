$n_1$ is a condition of $ku_1$ managing the health of $ka_1$.

## Notes

Usually the prevention, treatment, and management of illness and the preservation of mental and physical well-being through the services offered by the medical, nursing, and allied health professions. x2 and x3 may the same or different. Cf. {kamnu'a}.

{fanduse} claims that the amount/intensity of its x1 is too high for its
x2 to happen, so it's a causative predicate, it claims a causal relation
between two events.
If one wants to add a nuance of expectation, they should add "(mi) kanpe
lo nu..."; similarly, if one wants to express that they opine this causal
relation is true, they should add {pe'i} or "mi jinvi lo du'u...".

By the way, I added an example of use.

mu'o mi'e la .ilmen.

$pr_1$=$pe_1=s_1$ is a sort-of/semi- lover/boyfriend/girlfriend/partner/'special other' of $pr_2$=$pe_2$ under conditions $s_3$.

## Notes

For certain occasions when "it's complicated", a romance is not "official yet", one is "on a break" with another, or any other time when the romantic status betwixt at least two things is not clear. Note that {sorta} is a joke word, but is being used seriously in this context. The relationship status is uncertain, fluid, indefinite, ill-defined, unequal or one-sided, vel sim. s2 is implicit in the definition. Cf.: {pampe'o}, {prami}, {pendo}, {sorta}, {kinda}, {glepe'o}, {cinse}

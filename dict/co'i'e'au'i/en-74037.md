Shorthand for «sei baunpli be la vajli'epoitau»; with big-endian tanru (reversed from CLL Lojban).

## Notes

See {vajli'epoitau} and {baunpli} for more information.  See also: {co'e'i'au'i}.  Etymology: from {co}, {lidne}, and {baunpli}.  Example: «co'i'e'au'i mi ca tcidu fi la bangu lojbo mulno» (＝＝ «sei baunpli be la vajli'epoitau mi ca tcidu fi la bangu lojbo mulno»).  Contraction: {co'i'e}.

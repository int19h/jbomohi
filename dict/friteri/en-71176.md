$x_1$ is a friterie / baraque à frites / friture / frituur / frietkot / frietkraam serving fast food $x_2$ to $x_3$.

## Notes

A particular type of fast food restaurant, selling fries and usually a variety of other deep-fried snacks and sauces, as found in Belgium, the Netherlands and Northern France. The place structure models {gusta}.

Non-animals

"Body" connotes animaless to me, but I think that this word should apply to
plants, fungi, bacteria, and any other 'living' entity (and possibly 
others). We should make a concerted effort to say explicitly in this 
definition that it is the "whole integrated physical/corporeal form of some
entity or object".

$x_1$ is a leap day of year $x_2$ at location $x_3$ in calendary system $x_3$.

## Notes

This is a day which is included or removed from a 'normal' (yearly) progression of the calendar in order to correct the aligment between the reported date and the passage of physical time (such as that which is evinced by the seasons). See also: {tcikydragausnidu}.

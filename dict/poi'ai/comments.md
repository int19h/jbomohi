## 2026-03-22T06:42:10Z — bairyn (comment 4010, on definition 74923, in reply to 4009)



It restricts the bridi, whereas with just «ije» the meaning in one bridi might be more general than intended. Perhaps the speaker wishes to bind them closer together. There are probably other mechanisms to similarly bind them.

​

That said, you could probably restructure a bridi to put the would-be outer bridi inside somewhere or use «ije», and I think I tend to favor basic bridi structures, or at least a path to them, but I think it's still a nice option. And I'm aware of 2 separate propositions for the general idea, {xoi}, and {poi'a}. Some people might like the style.

​

Perhaps ‘there is an integer x such that is\_even(x)’ might sound like a better phrasing than ‘there is an integer x. It is even.’, even if this isn't the only way to say it (e.g. {mansa}).

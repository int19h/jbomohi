restrictive relative clause on bridi; «fi'o du be me'ei nei zo'e poi».

## Notes

{poi} but for bridi.  {poi'ai}, {noi'ai}, and {voi'ai} are bridi variants of {poi}, {noi}, and {voi}.  {poi'ai} etc. is terminated by {ku'o}{ku} and has a grammar like FA with respect to where it is placed.

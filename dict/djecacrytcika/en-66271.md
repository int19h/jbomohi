$x_1$ (number) is the hour/hour notation of the day, of event/state $x_2$, in system $x_3$.

## Notes

{djedi} zei {cacra} zei {tcika}. As default, the number $x_1$ is counted from the first hour of the day. See {ti'u'a} for BAI. ex.) li so cu djecacrytcika lo nu lo trene cu cliva kei u'y ty cy (The train departs at nine o'clock UTC.)

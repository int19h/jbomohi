$s_1$ dives [manner of controlled falling] to $s_2$ from $s_3$ into liquid $j_2$.

## Notes

Different styles of diving: head first (=nitfarstedu jirsu'u), "cannonball" (=boitse jirsu'u), etc. See also {zajyjirsu'u}.

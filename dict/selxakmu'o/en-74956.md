$x_1=m_1=xa_2$ is exhausted / depleted / completely consumed by $x_2=xa_1$ by standard $x_3=m_3$; $x_1$ is all used up / has run out.

## Notes

Examples: «le cidja selva'i zi co'a selxakmu'o» - ‘The food resources will soon be exhausted’.  «mi tatpi jai selxakmu'o lonu bajra» - ‘I'm exhausted from running’.  See also: {mulxaksu}, {xakmu'o}.

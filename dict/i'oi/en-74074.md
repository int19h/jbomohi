modify preceding cmavo to not cross bridi; require its terminator by the next «i».

## Notes

Convenience utility to limit the cmavo before {i'oi} to automatically terminate on the next .{i}.  Useful for {fu'e} and {lu} limited to the current bridi.  Note: if already terminated explicitly, {i'oi} adds no effect.  Example: «.i mi cusku lu .i'oisa'a mi nelci lonu na pilno lo valsi poi marbartu ku'o pu lonu bazycuxlanli ri».  See also: {i'oi'a} (effectively merge to outer block), {i'oi'a'o} (generalization of {i'oi}).

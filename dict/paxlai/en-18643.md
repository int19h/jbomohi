$k_1$ is $k_2$ (quantifier, default: one) potfuls in quantity.

## Notes

Cf. {patxu}, {klani}.

## Examples

### Example 144

'The guests will eat five potfuls of soup' is 'lo vitke ba citka loi stasu poi paxlai li mu' or 'lo vitke ba citka loi paxlai be li mu stasu'.

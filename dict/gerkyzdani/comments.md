seb, don't enter redundant lujvo, please

Please don't repeatedly post the same lujvo in different forms. You have 
done so multiple times for the lujvo made out of ?gerku zdani?.

Note that ?gerzda? and ?gerkyzdani? and all the other possible lujvo based 
on the tanru ?gerku zdani? are *automatically* equivalent in meaning by the
rules of Lojban.
There is no need to explicitly make a new entry for each lujvo form.

Otherwise, you just add redundancy to the database, which is never a good 
idea.

By convention, on Jbovlaste we prefer to enter only the lujvo form with its
lowest lujvo score and don't enter all the other forms. This is to keep the
database clean of redundancy, not to discourage the usage of any of the 
other possible lujvo form.

$x_1$ feels the whole range of human emotions about $x_2$ (conflicted, mixed emotions, maybe an emotional rollercoaster)

## Notes

This is a "poetic" word (it is a {vonrafpemjvo}, specifically), so take it to mean what you feel it means! Structural calque of Japanese 喜怒哀楽 kidoairaku 'emotions', literally 'happy-angry-sad-joyful'. (Meaning slightly changed as I misinterpreted a gloss of this word as 'the range of human emotions', somewhat on purpose.)

$x_1$ (event/date) is before $x_2$ (event/date) by $x_3$ (li; integer) days; $x_1$ is $x_3$ days before $x_2$.

## Notes

Typically will assume linear time. However, recurring events/holidays/named dates will behave cyclically unless the cycle label (such as Gregorian year label for annual holidays) is used; in these contexts, $x_3$ will be multivalued (in terms of acceptable submissions) and could even be negative; for example, a generic Christmas is always $x_3 = 7$ days ahead of the nearest New Year's Day in the Gregorian calendar. See also: "{prulamdei}", ".{utka'au}".

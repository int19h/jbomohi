$x_1$ reflects/pertains to the Pacific Ocean region/culture/history/geography/identity/[nationality] in aspect $x_2$.

## Notes

Pertains to peoples or things bordering, embedded in (or on an island in), submerged in, located under or on the bottom of, or going through/over the Pacific Ocean as well. "Trans-Pacific" is closely related to this word. See also: ".{atlanto}", ".{indi'o}", ".{antarktiko}".

$p_1$ is bikeshedding about lujvo $l_1$ expressed by $p_3$ to audience $p_4$ on grounds (du'u) $x_5$

## Notes

Bikeshedding is the phenomenon of delaying decisions about an important topic in favor of decisions about ancillary topics, e.g. procrastinating decisions about building a house in order to decide the color of the attached bike shed. From {na'e} {vajni} {lujvo} {bo} {pinka}.

detytcika modal, 1st place: with date and time ... ; attaches date and time starting with years and ending in seconds.

## Notes

It is designed to allow users to express date and time in a standard similar to ISO 8601. {pi'e} can be used separate units, or use {ny}, {ly}, {dy}, {cy}, {my}, {sy} to jump to a specific date/time unit. Milliseconds can be added after {pi}. See also {detytcika}.

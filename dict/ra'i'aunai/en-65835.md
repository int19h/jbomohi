attitudinal modifier: want this to last - (cu'i) accept this - (nai) want this to end soon

## Notes

{ra'i'au} means that the speaker wants this situation or emotion to end soon.; From {ranji} + {au}; See also: {ra'i'au}, {ra'i'aucu'i}

$x_1$ (set, group, structure, category, class, etc.) has cardinality less than or equal to aleph-null; $x_1$ is mathematically countable (including the option of being finite).

## Notes

Purposefully designed as a zi'evla. $x_1$ is finite or countably infinite; there exists a bijection between (the set underlying) $x_1$ and a (not necessarily proper) subset of the set of all of the natural numbers. The empty set is finite. For "uncountable"/"uncountably infinite", use "{to'e} ({zei}) kancuka'e". See also: {cimni} / {tolcimni}.

$x_1$ is in the future of/later than/after $x_2$ in time sequence; $x_1$ is latter; $x_2$ is former.

## Notes

Also sequel, succeed, successor, follow, come after; time ordering only (use lidne otherwise); aorist in that $x_1$ may overlap in time with $x_2$ as long as it extends afterwards; non-aorist future = {cfabalvi}. See also {lidne}, {cabna}, {purci}, {farna}, {lerci}, {ba}.

.eu galfi zoi gy inert gy zoi gy noble gy

Inert gases are different from noble gases both in the intension and the
extension. the term for "noble gas" is more useful as gismu, while "inert
gas" is easily expressed with lujvo {tolfragapci}. The extension of noble
gas is composed of helium, neon, argon, krypton, xenon and radon. The
ununoctium, a member of Group 18, may not be a member of noble gas because
of its chemical properties.

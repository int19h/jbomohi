$x_1=t_3$ [ka zmadu or ka mleca] is the endianness / orientation of most-vs-least-significant-first of sequence $x_2=p_1$ with ordering $x_3=p_2$ on set $x_4=p_3=t_4$, with most/least-significant item $x_5=t_1=v_1$ first, most important to $x_6=t_2=v_2$ in property / aspect $x_7=v_3$.

## Notes

Synonym: {teryraipoi}.  See also: {pornternrai} (endianness) for a shorter fu'ivlaization, {vajraipavmompoi} (big-endian), {vajymecyboryraipavmompoi} (little-endian).

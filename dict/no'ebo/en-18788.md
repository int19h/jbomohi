## Notes

Cf. {no'e}, {bo}, {na'ebo}, {je'abo}, {to'ebo}. Midpoint between {je'abo} and {to'ebo}.

## Examples

### Example 153

mi nelci loi glare cidja .ije do nelci to'ebo ri .ije la djein. nelci no'ebo ra: "I like hot food, and you like cold food, and Jane likes lukewarm food."

Article for abstract predicate sumti. Turns a selbri into an abstraction with all open places filled by {ce'u}.

## Notes

Has an inverse: {me'au}

## Examples

### Example 182

.i me'ei lazni me'ei xagji cu se xu zmadu lo ni mi me'au ce'u 
Literal translation: "Does the predicate "hungry" exceed the predicate "lazy" in the quantity of me being an x1 of it?" 
Idiomatic translation: "Am I hungrier than I am lazy?"
Translation to first order Lojban:
.i lo ka lazni cu se xu zmadu lo ka xagji kei lo ni mi ce'u ckaji

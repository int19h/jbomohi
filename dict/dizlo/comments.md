All directions are relative to a point of comparison. The order was rearranged to have the same place structure as cnita, stuna, crane, etc., where the point of comparison is x2 and the orientation of view is x3.



By "gravity", I mean "rotating" or "planetary". In other words, galtu and dizlo are the third axis of the compass directions. Meanwhile, gapru and cnita are not restricted in this way. In my opinion, this is the proper distinction between dizlo and cnita, not "directness".

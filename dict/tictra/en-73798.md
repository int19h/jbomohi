$x_1$ asserts falsehood $x_2$ towards $x_3$ in order to hide $x_1$'s knowledge from $x_4$.

## Notes

$x_1$ asserts falsehood $x_2$ towards $x_3$ as a means to mislead $x_4$ into believing that $x_1$ does not know $x_2$.

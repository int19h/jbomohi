$p_1=c_1$ is a demon (malevolent mythological humanoid) of mythos/religion $c_2$.

## Notes

Also fairy/elf/troll etc. as depicted in folklores where such creatures are seen as innately hostile. Cf. {pacru'i}, {dermoni}, {di'albo}, {orxo}.

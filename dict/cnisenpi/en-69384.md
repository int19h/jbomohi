$x_1$ emotionally disbelieves $x_2$ (du'u) about subject $x_3$

## Notes

May or may not contrast with one's more-conscious or rational beliefs; related to the concept of "alief" ({kricili}). See {cinmo}, {senpi}, {cnikri}

### Example 887

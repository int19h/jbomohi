$x_1=p_1=s_2$ owns private property $x_2=p_2=s_1$ under law/custom $x_3=p_3$; $x_2$ is proprietary.

## Notes

Technically, a private owner can freely give all usage rights as in the case of open source software, e.g. «po'ecru fi la'oi MIT», where an ownership translates into effective non-ownership.  If not specified, whether this is consented to or not might be implied by context, as in the notion of implied consent in some cultures.  See also: {tcinyra'igau} (e.g. «lo se po'ecru cu te tcinyra'igau»/«ro krali cu te tcinyra'igau»), {fingubni} (open source), {po'ecru} (consent), {fukpo'e} (copyright), {krasi} (right), {praselva'i} (capital), {ja'erkai} (effectively).

$x_1$ is a mustelid of species/breed $x_2$.

## Notes

Includes all mammals in family Mustelidae, including weasels, martens, badgers, otters, etc. Intended as shorter alternative to {mabmustelidai}. See also {mutsteila}, {mustlei}, {fulreto}, {jaurmutsteila}, {takside}, {lurtina}, {utra}, {martesi}, {dzibili}, {mabmustela}, {mabmustelidai}, {smidauzlexo}, {mabru}.

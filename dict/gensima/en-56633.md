$x_1$ (text) is a temporary name for $x_2$ (text, object, event) chosen in such a way that it is not used for naming anything else within the current scope

## Notes

Also called 'gensym'. To express gensymmed {da} usually {da}{xi}{rau} is used. See {cmene}, {zasni}

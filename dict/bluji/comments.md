Etymology

Obviously, a valsi for "jeans" should be taken from American English.
It's impossible to add words similar to [ʤiːnz] ([djinz])
as {jinsa}, {djine}, {jinzi} already exist and possible "djinizi" looks
wierd.

So the only possible way is to take it's synonym.
American "blue jeans" means exactly the same as "jeans".
"Blue jeans" -> [bludjinz] -> {bluji}

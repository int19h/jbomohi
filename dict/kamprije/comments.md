Incorrect use of ka

using -kam- (the rafsi of ka) in a lujvo implies that the x1 of the tagged 
tertau is removed (since it's how properties work; you can imagine that 
that x1 is "automatically" filled with ce'u). That means that it's not k1 
p1 p2, but rather k1 p2. If you *really* want to stress on the proprietor, 
use the atrocious "selkaikamprije": k1c2 p2 c1.

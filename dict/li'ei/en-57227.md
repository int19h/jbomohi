Signals a list of words without connectives.

## Notes

Each succeeding word is a succeeding place on the list (not necessarily ordinal). So "li'ei pa re ci" is equivalent to "1 and 2 and 3" or "1, 2, 3". "zei" or "jav, jev/jve, jov, juv" can be used to connect words that need to be on the same place within the list by combining them into a one-word lujvo.

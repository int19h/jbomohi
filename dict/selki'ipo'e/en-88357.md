$x_1=p_1=c_2$ has / is with / loosely owns $x_2=p_2=c_1$ under law / custom / in matter / relationship / association $x_3=p_3=c_3$.

## Notes

In contrast with {selsivypo'e} (privately own).  If you have a chair, but do not own it, you can say «selki'ipo'e».  Compare {pe} to {po} and {selki'ipo'e} to {selsivypo'e}.  See also: {ralte}, {tolcau}, {avju}, {tcinyra'igau}, {ki'irxu'a}, {ponse}, {krali}, {kansa}, {dunda}, {plicru}, {sabji}, {ckini}, {srana}, {ckaji}.

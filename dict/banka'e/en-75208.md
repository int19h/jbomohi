$x_1$ can speak language $x_2$; $x_1$ is a speaker of $x_2$.

## Notes

An alternative to "se {bangu}", given that the second slot of {bangu} is often considered a bottlecap ({velbotsu'i}) — not every language has speakers. Does not imply expertise or fluency in the way that {baucre} might.

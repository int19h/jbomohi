No, because I don't think it really *means* "drop". "Drop" implies that
the one who drops it, has previously held it. "falgau" could be "shove

You know, that really is a problem. Often, the most semantically relevant
word is not the first in the translation. There should be a way of marking
a word in that way, so that the dictionary could say, for instance:

fall: make something fall: /falgau/

$cr_1=cl_1=j_2$ is a tube top of material $cr_2$.

## Notes

A shoulderless, sleeveless "tube" that wraps the torso, generally very tight over the breasts in order to prevent the garment from falling. "halterneck" ({nebdadycreka}) is its subtype. Suggested shortest fu'ivla form: ancrka. Cf. {creka}.

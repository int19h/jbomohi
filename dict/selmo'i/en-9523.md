$x_{1}$ is a memory recalled by $x_{2}$ about subject $x_{3}$.

## Notes

From {se} {morji}. {mojysi'o}, a near-synonym, is perhaps the more accurate translation for the English word 'memory'; pedantically, selmo'i refers only to memories consciously recalled at a given moment, not those that are merely retrievable.

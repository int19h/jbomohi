$c_1=p_1$ is a vocal synthesis software, performing function $p_2$.

## Notes

Vocal synthesis software in a broad sense, which is not limited to "singing voice synthesis". $c_1=p_1$ refers to the software itself, not the virtual singer. See also {voksaroido}(can refer to either software or a character), {voksa}, {zmiboto}.

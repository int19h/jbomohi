$x_1$ is a proposition/predication (statement with a truth value), stating that $x_2$ is true

## Notes

Brivla-form of {du'u}. $x_1$ and $x_2$ are identical (filling them would equate the two sumti). See {fatci}, {jetnu}, {jitfa}, {jufra}, {bridi}, {nundumu}

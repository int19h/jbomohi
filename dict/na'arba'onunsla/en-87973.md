$x_1$ is the birthday of $x_2$ with activities $x_3$; $x_1$ is the annual celebration of $x_2$ growing in age by 1 full year.

## Notes

Unlike all the other terms for "birthday" which all focus on the date of birth, this focuses on the fact that $x_2$ gets older by a year. See also {jbedetri}, {terjbe}, {jbedetnunsla}, {jbenunsla}, {nanca}, {banro}, {nunsla}.

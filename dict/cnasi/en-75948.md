$x_1$ rises to $x_2$ from $x_3$ in frame of reference $x_4$.

## Notes

Antonym of {farlu}. Having a to'e-negated term {tolfa'u} for "rise" was unsatisfactory, and there were no other lujvo that had the same noun places. See also {lafti}, {lafmuvgau}, {galmu'u}, {muvdu}, {grava}, {gapru}, {galtu}.

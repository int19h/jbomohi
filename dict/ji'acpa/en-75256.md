$x_1$ wins, gains prize $x_2$.

## Notes

An attempt at separating the meanings of {jinga}, which officially tries to do double duty for both 'win a prize' and 'win a game'. See also {jinga} (the unofficial definition with only two slots), {losxa}, {lo'acpa}.

$x_1=k_1$ (event) is the instance/recurrence/repetition of $x_2=c_1$ producing $x_3=c_2$ [product] by process $x_4=c_3$ for the $x_5=k_3$rd time.

## Notes

This definition first follows the principle of using «-ke'u» to prepend a repetition/instance ($k_1$) place and changing the definition such that it's as though the ‘re-’ prefix in English were added (and appending $k_3$; the $k_2$ can apply to the producing as a whole).  lujvo construction can have more flexibility than always following a similar constraint, but this definition consistently follows this principle.  See also: «fukpra» (reproduce).

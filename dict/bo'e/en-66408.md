dispreferred: elliptical/unspecified/vague letteral/symbol

## Notes

Note: {bu'o'e} is preferred. Has use in mekso at the least; in this context, it might subsume {xo'e} (but see: {xo'e'o'e}). It also may be useful in spelling, initialisms, etc. If used as a pro-sumti (as other letters are), it means {zo'e} but only for reasonable or previously specified letterals assignments.

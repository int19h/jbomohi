number/quantity: nif, thirty-six, six squared.

## Notes

«nif» is a term used by jan Misali, see seximal.net/words for reference. See also {xa}, {te'a}, {re}. Can also be expressed as «xapi'ixa» (six times six) or «sopi'ivo» (nine times four) or «gaipi'ici» (twelve times three).

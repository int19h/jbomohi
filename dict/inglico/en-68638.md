$x_1$ is English/pertains to English-speaking culture in aspect $x_2$.

## Notes

Generally assume broadest sense of 'English'; may refer to 'English', pertaining to the country/people/culture of England (normally requiring constraint = {inglicygu'e}); British English, the norm language of the UK (= {inglicybau}). This word is part of a movement to supplant cultural gismu.

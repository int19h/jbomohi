quotes a nonce Lojban word (an onomatopoeia), turns it into selbri unit meaning "$x_1$ makes a sound like (quoted word)".

## Notes

This is Loglan's "sue". For example, "sau'e uaf" has place structure "$x_1$ barks (utters 'waf!')"

$x_1$ is a champagne made from grapes $x_2$ by standard $x_3$.

## Notes

Based on lakanro's earlier definition.  Cf. {vanju}, {fomvanju}, {risyvanju}, {xalka}, {koinka}.  To explicitly indicate (or deny) true AOC conformance rather than AOC champagne-likeness, prefix with ‘AOC’, e.g.  «lo me .abu.obucy. [co'i'o] campana» ({co'i'o} is optional).  Alternatively, use a ‘by standard $x_3$’ place or use {ma'i} or {fi'o} for a specific sub-type of {manri}.

## 2015-08-27T05:11:18Z — spheniscine (comment 2500, on definition 67724, in reply to 2499)

Re: New meanings?

I added that new meaning to e'e myself (though I don't think I did it to 
e'i), unfortunately I can't beat the 100000 votes automatically given to 
officialdata. These changes were done by the BPFK long ago, following a 
desire to make all e'V cmavo to be a subset of the imperative mood 
(represented by {ko} / {ko'oi} / {koinde})

Another way to think of it is that each e'V cmavo has a magic -nde brivla 
(from {minde}, and inheriting its place structure) associated with it:

{e'ande}, {e'ende}, {e'inde}, {e'onde}, {e'unde}

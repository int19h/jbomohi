$x_1$ is liminally/neutrally $x_2$ (ka); $x_1$ is between the state of $x_2$ and the state of the inverse of $x_2$

## Notes

(ko'a norkai lo ka broda) = (ko'a {no'e} broda). Also see {nutli}, {tolkai}

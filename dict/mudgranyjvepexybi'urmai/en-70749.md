$x_1$ is a quantity of/contains/is made of wattle and daub (woven sticks covered with a plaster-like substance).

## Notes

From {mudri} {grana} {je} {pesxu} {bitmu} {marji}. Wattle and daub was commonly used for the walls of buildings in many pre-industrial cultures in Europe, Asia, Africa and the Americas, and is still used in some regions. The ingredients of daub vary, but may include straw, clay, mud and/or dung. See also {mudga'a}.

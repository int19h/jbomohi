unnestable zoi; foreign quote that cannot be nested.

## Notes

Currently this is a duplicate of {zoi}.  However, by allocating a word that specifically precludes nestability, if the grammar is ever updated, or if a dialect is chosen that modifies {zoi} to be {zoi'oi}, then {zoi'oi'au} allows access to old-style {zoi}.  See also: {zoi'oi} (nestable {zoi}).

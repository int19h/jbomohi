Georgia/Sakartvelo

## Notes

The nation of Georgia (natively, Sakartvelo (IPA [sɑkʰɑrtʰvɛlɔ]) 'land of the Kartvelians'); population ~3,718,200 in 2017, capital Tbilisi ({tyBIlisis}). The modern state gained its independence from the Soviet Union ({sofygu'e}) in 1991. Alternate forms include {sakartulos} and {gugdegu'e}; see also {kartuli} 'Georgian'. May also refer to historical Kartvelian geopolitical entities.

$x_1$ speaks $x_2$ (language) in an environment where $x_3$ (language) is expected; $x_1$ crocodiles $x_2$ for $x_3$

## Notes

Esperanto cultural term. Originally referred to the practice of speaking one's own native language at an Esperanto gathering, but the concept has since spread to other constructed language communities.

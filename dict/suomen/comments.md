## 2009-06-27T05:29:58Z — phma (comment 394, in reply to 392)

Re: What's the difference between suomen and suomis?

Totemz wrote:
> I don't know why suomen get an e but not i. 

"suomen" is the genitive of "suomi". That and "suomeen" (the illative) are
the only forms that end in consonants. All case forms, except "suomi"
itself, have "e".

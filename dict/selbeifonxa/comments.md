Smart 'phones'

This word is good for the cell phones of old, but does not really, in my 
opinion, capture the totality of modern (especially smart) 'phones'. These 
new devices really are small computers that emphasize (but are very much so
not restricted) to communication; the phone program is just one function 
that they have.

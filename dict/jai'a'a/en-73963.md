{jai} variant but as {zo'ei} rather than {tu'a}.

## Notes

{jai'a'a} parallels {jai} but as {zo'ei} rather than {tu'a}.  When used without a modal, {jai}, rather than swapping x1 with one of the sumti in {fai}'s abstraction, swaps x1 with {fai}'s old {zo'ei} sumti.  The new x1 is {zo'ei} the the new {fai} sumti.  For more complicated uses, consider using {zo'e} {poi}. ni'o Etymology: from {jai} and {ra'a}.

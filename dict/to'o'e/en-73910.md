Apply Lojban text to an unspecified point in text.

## Notes

Can be used to apply e.g. {be} applications or {noi} relative clauses in alternative locations without specifying what they modify.  Same grammar as {to}…{toi}.  See also: {pe'oi} (same concept, but as UI3c (technically for UI3c the grammatical structure must otherwise still be valid, even if it's indicated to be more flexibly interpreted)).

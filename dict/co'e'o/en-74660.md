## Notes

This allows tanru to produce a merged place structure more flexibly and freely.  See also: {co'au} (flip tanru place structure basis between tertau and seltau; SE grammar), {co'ai} (new grammar to apply to the remaining attached sumti), {do'e'e} (‘sticky’ {co'e'o} with a more complicated grammar).

$x_1$ is relevant logic / relevance logic, used to deduce/infer $x_2$ (du'u)

## Notes

A substructural logic where the antecedent and consequent of implications are constrained to stay "relevant" ({srana}) to each other. ("Weakening", adding irrelevant truths to the set of antecedents, is not allowed.) See also {xaklogji}, {xakyziflogji}, {fadylogji}, {zbalogji}.

$x_1$ is a microscopic lifeform or life-like object or structure, of form/type/with characteristics $x_2$.

## Notes

Superset of "{jurme}" and "{vidru}"; also includes things like fungal spores, prions, organelles, genetic code in and of itself, etc. It itself may not be fully alive. See also: "{jmive}", "{prejume}". In some ways this approximates the vague descriptor of "bug" (as in a disease) in English, although it need not have noticeable or adverse effects.

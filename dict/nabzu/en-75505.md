$x_1$ happens now (absolute time reference).

## Notes

Synonym: {ca'abna}. Differs from {cabna} in that {cabna} may be relative — “at the same time *as* $x_2$”. Also see {tizva}, the spatial equivalent. Very tentative combining form assignment: -naz-, stealing {nazbi}.

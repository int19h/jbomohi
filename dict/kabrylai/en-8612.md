$k_1$ is $k_2$ (quantifier, default: one) cupfuls in quantity.

## Notes

Cf. {kabri}, {klani}.

## Examples

### Example 142

'She added two cupfuls of sugar' is 'ko'a jmina loi sakta poi kabrylai li re' or 'ko'a jmina lo kabrylai be li re sakta'.

## 2015-06-15T14:48:30Z — spheniscine (comment 2015, in reply to 2012)

Re: Invalid word?

It is valid under the rules for zi'evla-lujvo formation, and is accepted by
both versions of camxes parser. It's similar to how {lacri} + {inda} = 
{lacy'inda}; except that since {uidje} starts with a semivowel, 
{canky'uidje} is not allowed.

$x_1=tcit_1=s_3$ is a modal (BAI, «fi'o broda», PU, etc.) of/based on/derived from $x_2=tcit_3=tcin_1$ (e.g. «bai», «se cabna» ({se} used for tense)) for sumti $x_3=tcit_2=s_1$ of selbri $x_4=s_2$.

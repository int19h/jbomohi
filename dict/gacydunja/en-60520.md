$x_1=d_1=g_2$ desublimates from the gaseous state at temperature $x_2=d_2=g_3$ with pressure $x_3=d_3$.

## Notes

The process of this is called “deposition” or “desublimation” in English. From “{gapci} {dunja}”; refers to a direct change of the state of matter from gaseous to solid. Cf. {ligyfebvi}.

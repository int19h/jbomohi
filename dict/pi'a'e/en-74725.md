fill multiple places from a single sumti representation; splat sumti.

## Notes

Similar grammar to {la'e}, but can fill zero or more places.  To specify a custom applier or application function fo'a to ko'a, you can use «pi'a'e be fo'a [be'o] ko'a», «pi'a'e bei'i fo'a [be'o] ko'a», and «pi'a'e me fo'a [me'u] ko'a».  See also: {joi'u'i} (merge sumti), e.g. «broda pi'a'e joi'u'i ce'o ko'a ko'e ko'i» (as in «broda ko'a ko'e ko'i»), however alternative representations, of different types, of possibly tagged terms are possible.  {fa'u'i} is {pi'a'e} but as FA rather than like LAhE, a subtle distinction.

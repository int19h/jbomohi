$x_1$ is a Rubik's toy, being of shape/solid $x_2$ with sides $x_3$ and degrees of freedom $x_4$, made from/composed of $x_5$.

## Notes

This word may be mildly dispreferred to {carkemkasygaukepyblikemkeitci}. $x_2$ is not restricted to three-dimensional objects (despite the definition of {bliku}). Degrees of freedom should be descriptive (not just a number which counts how many there are); so, $x_4$ would be a list of possible moves available.

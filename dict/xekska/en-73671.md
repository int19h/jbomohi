$x_1$ is darkly colored with color $x_2$ [ka]

## Notes

In general, darker colors, i.e. colors with a low brightness in an HSB color model.  In general, use the rafsi "xek" (from {xekri}) or {xekri} as a seltau + a color to form a light version of that color.  E.g. {xekri'o} is dark green.

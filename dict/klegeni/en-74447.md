$x_1$ has $x_2$ (ka) as their gender; $x_1$ identifies as gender $x_2$.

## Notes

From -kle- (< {klesi}) and the ending of {trangeni}, {singeni}. The -geni ending is ultimately from Latin genus and its descendants (cf. English gender).

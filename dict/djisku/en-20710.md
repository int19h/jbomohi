$x_1$ expresses/says that they desire $x_2$ (nu/ka) to audience $x_3$ in medium $x_4$, desired for purpose $x_5$

## Notes

Note that in the past djisku was used for what {skudji} is more commonly used to mean nowadays. The latter is a more regular lujvo and has mostly supplanted the old djisku in usage.

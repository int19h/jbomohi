## Notes

Frequency is by default temporal, although it could conceivably be different (based on referent of {sebo'au}).  Measures duration of minimal whole waves/periodic features, from one given feature to the next occurrence (in time) of the same feature (for example the elapsed time between the peak or minimum of a signal).  Commonly denoted by "f" or nu. See also: {boxna}, {bo'ai}, {bo'au}

$x_1$ (text) is a zi'evla made of UI cmavos, optional glue, and a rafsi or piece of a gismu; meaning $x_2$ (ka) in language $x_3$.

## Notes

Examples include {iicmo}, {le'onmo}, {aidji}, and even {uirnainmo} ({ui} zei {nai} zei {cinmo}). But not {iedra}, which means "ivy" and not something like {ie} zei {drani}: to qualify as a uivla, the meaning needs to be shaped by the parts. Not to be confused with {u'ivla}, a clever/funny word.

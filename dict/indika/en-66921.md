$x_1$ (event/state) implies / suggests / is circumstantial evidence for $x_2$ (du'u); the probability that $x_2$ is true, given $x_1$, is greater than the probability without $x_1$

## Notes

Similar to {nibli}, but merely means circumstantial/forensic, not logical, evidence, that may be contradicted by other circumstantial evidences or interpretations. See also {krinu}, {lakne}, {didni}, {nusna}, {tolna'e}

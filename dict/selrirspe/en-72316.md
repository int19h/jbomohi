$x_1$ is the spouse of the child/offspring/protegé of $x_2$ by standard/law/custom $x_3$.

## Notes

Son–/daughter-in-law. This does not mean "child bride" in the sense of a juvenile who is wed; use "verba speni" for such purpose. Compare to: "{rirspe}", "{sperirni}", and "{speselrirni}".

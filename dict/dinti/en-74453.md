$x_1$ is a hermaphrodite (not intersex), evidencing hermaphroditic traits $x_2$.

## Notes

'Hermaphroditic' in the way that some worms and flowers are hermaphrodites. Not to be used for intersex characteristics; i.e., humans are not {dinti}, as they cannot be hermaphrodites. See https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermaphrodite#Use_regarding_humans for the difference.

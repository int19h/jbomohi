$x_1$ is an event/state described by bridi $x_2$

## Notes

Predicate version of {nu}, which is a kind of relation between a bridi and an event/state. However with a regular predicate version it is possible to access the $x_2$ just like any regular sumti place. See also {nu}, {du'u}.

$x_1=xu_1=c_2=s_3$ defines / assigns meaning / attributes definition $x_3=c_1=s_1$ to $x_2=c_2=s_2$; $x_1$ defines $x_2$ to be $x_3$.

## Notes

Synonym: te {xu'asmu}.  See also: {ki'irxu'a} (to assign / attribute).

omittable text; one sense of ‘[]’ square brackets (bracketed text).

## Notes

Can be used with quotations.  See also {sa'a} for editor-inserted material, and {na'i'a} of UI3b, which can be used like {po'o} except to express optionality, rather than uniqueness.  See also {li'oi}, which is very similar to {la'i'a} but also claims that the result would still be grammatical if the construct {li'oi} applies to is removed, whereas {la'i'a} does not necessarily make this claim.

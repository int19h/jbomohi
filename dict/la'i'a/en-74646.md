omittable text; one sense of ‘[]’ square brackets (bracketed text).

## Notes

Can be used with quotations.  See also {sa'a} for editor-inserted material, and {na'i'a} of UI3b, which can be used like {po'o} except to express optionality, rather than uniqueness.

## Notes

Similar to pharanx (gr. cleft, chasm). Nasopharynx (={zbigalxyfe'a}), oropharynx (={molgalxyfe'a}), laryngopharynx/hypopharynx (={galxyferni'a}). See also {moldruji'o}, {galxe} and {fenra}.

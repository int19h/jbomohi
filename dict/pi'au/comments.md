## 2023-11-22T07:04:23Z — krtisfranks (comment 3792, on definition 72224)

Why not "{pai'i}"?

"{pai'i}" is much more clearly connected to both the English and Greek
pronunciations of "pi", is more similar to "{pai}" (which facilitates
relearning), and follows the common pattern of repeating the off-glide for
cmavo which are generated in such manner (see "{tau'u}"). This word is not
very obvious to me even in retrospect.

$x_1$ is the sum (combined addition result) of $x_2$ (plurality of the same type as $x_1$)

## Notes

$x_2$ may be {jo'u}/{joi}/{ce'o}/{ce}. See {si'i}, {sumji}, {gripi'i}

## Examples

### Example 758

li xa grisumji li pa jo'u li re jo'u li ci

I suppose it depends on how one looks at definition of te-{fuzme}; I tend 
to interpret "authority" here somewhat loosely, in that if I consider you 
responsible for something goi ko'a, then {do fuzme ko'a mi}, regardless of 
whether I have any real authority over you.

{to'ai} does have a proposed rafsi, -toz-, but I'm not sure I want 
{tozyfu'e} to be the root for the {malfu'e} and {zanfu'e} variants - it 
would have to be {maltozyfu'e} and {zantozyfu'e} to avoid attaching -toz- 
to mabla/zabna; using -kem- is even longer.

Maybe {jinvi} is better?

$x_1$ is/does $x_2$ as much as possible.

## Notes

See also {tankomo} ‘… is as … as …’ (the general case) and {djirai} ‘… is as … as wanted’. «mi cumrai lo ka sutra» ‘I was as fast as I could’, «gau ko le pixra cu cumrai lo ka barda» ‘make the picture as large as possible’.

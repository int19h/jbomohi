$m_1=d_1$ is the little finger/toe/digit of $d_2$.

## Notes

Does not actually have to be little or smallest; just has to be homologous to the most-ulnar/most-medial digit in the human hand or most-lateral digit in the human foot (assuming standard anatomical position for humans). Confer: "{degji}".

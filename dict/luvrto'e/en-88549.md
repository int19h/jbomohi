$x_1$ is a compound made from negating a word with «to'e» having meaning $x_2$ with arguments $x_3$ built from phrase $x_4$.

## Notes

Especially used to refer to ones with mono-morphemic counterparts, e.g. {tolcando} instead of {olca} or {tolfa'u} instead of {cnasi}. See also {lujvo}, {to'e}.

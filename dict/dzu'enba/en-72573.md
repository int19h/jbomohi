$x_1$ and $x_2$ are mutual sibling nodes in a directed tree graph $x_6$ such that their shared parent node(s) $x_5$ is/are the most-recent common direct ancestor of $x_3$ and $x_4$, such that $x_1$ is a direct ancestor of or simply is $x_3$ (as the case may be), and such that $x_2$ is a direct ancestor of or simply is $x_4$ (as the case may be), all according to tree ordering relation $x_7$.

## Notes

$x_3$ and $x_4$ must be distinct nodes in the same directed tree; else this relationship is undefined. If $x_3$ is a direct ancestor of $x_4$, or vice-versa, then this relationship is – again – undefined. $x_6$ and $x_7$ define the relations/standards which are being applied. This word may be used in reference to non-familial tree graphs via analogy, despite references to 'siblings' etc. See also: "{tunba}", "{dzena}", "{treicu}", "{tseingu}", "{kraune}".

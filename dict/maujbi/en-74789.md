$x_1=j_1$ is closer to $x_2=j_2=z_1$ than ($x_1$ is to) $x_3=z_2$ in property / quantity $x_4=j_3$ by amount / excess $x_5=z_4$.

## Notes

This is not {jbimau}, which uses «-mau» to insert $zmadu_2$ at $x_2$ (and append $zmadu_4$), and means ‘closer to something than something else is’.  {maujbi} means ‘closer to something than something’.  The former compares $x_1$; the latter compares $x_2$.

$b_1$ is the brother-in-law of $b_2$

## Notes

$b_3$: $b_1$ is a man who is, or is a brother of, $s_1$, $s_1$ is married to $s_2$, $b_2$ is, or is a sibling of,  $s_2$, $b_1=s_1$ and $b_2=s_2$ is not true. Cf. {bruna} {speni}

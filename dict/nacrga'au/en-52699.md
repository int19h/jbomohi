$x_1$ is the Euler–Mascheroni constant, usually denoted by lowercase gamma (γ); approximately 0.5772156649 (in decimal).

## Notes

The difference in the limit as n becomes arbitrarily large between/of: the sum of (1/k) for all strictly positive integers k < (n+1), and the natural logarithm of n. See also: {ga'au}

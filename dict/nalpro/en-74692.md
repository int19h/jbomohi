$x_1=f_1$ is not opposed to / doesn't mind / doesn't object to / doesn't contend / balance against $x_2=f_2$ about $x_3=f_3$.

## Notes

Can be either neutral or in support of.  For consent, see {po'ecru}, which deals more closely with ownership and property rights.  It is possible to permit something you have ownership over while opposing it.  Depending on context, «nalpro» can imply consent (permission/license), e.g. if it's not explicit and reasonably inferrable from context.  See also: «tolpro» (non-neutral), «no'epro» (only neutral).

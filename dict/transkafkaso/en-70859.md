## Notes

Synonymous with {snanu} {zei} {kafkaso} and {nanrkafkaso} 'South Caucasian'. The Transcaucasia region is usually defined to include Georgia ({sakartyvelos}), Armenia ({xaiaSTAN}) and Azerbaijan ({azarbaiDJAN}), the nations south of the main range of the Caucasus mountains but above Anatolia (see {andolu}). See also {kartuli} 'Georgian', {xai'otso} 'Armenian', {zarbaija} 'Azarbaijani/Azeri', {softo} 'Soviet', {rusko} 'Russian', {tirkice} 'Turkish'.

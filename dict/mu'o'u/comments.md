Why not in selma'o RAUhO

This word attaches to the nearest quotation marks (which are words in
Lojban) such that it is outside of their scope; it can precede or follow
the quote if it is bi-marked, and its meaning depends on which side it is
on.

RAUhO must follow a quote (and its quotation mark/quotation-terminating
word) and applies to the whole quote, without dependence on the side to
which it is adjacent.

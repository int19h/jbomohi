number: sixteen, four to the second power.

## Notes

Unlike {paxa} it does not automatically imply a decimal base. See also {vo}, {te'a}, {re}, {kurtenfa}. Can also be expressed as «vopi'ivo» (four times four) or «bipi'ire» (eight times two).

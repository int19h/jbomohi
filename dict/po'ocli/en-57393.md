## Notes

$x_1$ poses poshlyi [Russian original term] morally worthless, cheap, sham, smutty, vulgar, common, commonplace, trivial, trite, banal qualities that are subject to being deflated and derided. $x_1$ is poshlyi / posljak  / posljacka / exposes poshlost' / poshlust. See {pocli} for an experimental gismu synonym.

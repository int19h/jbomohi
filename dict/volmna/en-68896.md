$x_1$ flies/swims through fluid $x_2$ using means of propulsion/lift $x_3$.

## Notes

Combines the notions of {vofli} and {limna} which are subtypes of this notion. This is done in the spirit of {xamsi}, which pertains to/can consist of all/any fluids. It is less general than {kesfi} (it is something like "kesfi be lo xamsi be fi $x_2$").

## Notes

Resultative, not-necessarily causal, change. See also {cenba} for non-resultative, {galfi} for causal, {stika} for non-resultative and non-causal change, {zasni}, na {stodi}, {banro}, {farvi}, {lidne}, {pruce}, {jalge}, {zifre}, {nibli}.

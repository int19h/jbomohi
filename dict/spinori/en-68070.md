$x_1$ is a spinor (mathematical object/number/operator) representing (object/information) $x_2$ with properties $x_3$.

## Notes

This definition refers to the object, not the representation. A given spinori is not changed by a change of base no matter how its appearance does, and two spinori are not the same, even if they are represented in equivalent forms in some bases (or even the same one).

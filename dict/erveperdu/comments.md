Why not "{vendu'a}"?

It could have the same meaning (modulo permutation of sumti slots), is
well-formed and is composed of gismu veljvo, begins with a consonant, and
is easier and quicker to say. Stick a "te" in front of it if you want to
permute the sumti slots in order to match; this alternative will have
predictable meaning and terbri structure, so such a conversion should be
encouraged.

We can have semantic overlap in the language, though. Maybe there is some
sort of poetic or wordplay motivation for the existence of this word,
which I am failing to recognize.

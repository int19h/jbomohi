$x_1$ is a pentagon.

## Notes

From {mu} + {jganu} + {tarmi} (so, 'five-corner-shape'). See {cibjgatai} 'triangle'. Names for polygons with any number of sides can be formed the same way: {vonjgatai} 'quadrilateral' (lit. 'tetragon', 'quadrangle'), {xavjgatai} 'hexagon'...

$x_1=v_1$ shaves/[removes hair] $x_2=k_1=v_2$ from $x_3=k_2=v_3$ 

## Notes

Applies to any form of hair removal, not necessarily with a blade. Can refer to self-shaving if using the same sumti for x1 and x3, or a barber if using separate sumti for x1 and x3.

$c_1$ is a snowshoe for covering/protecting feet/hooves $c_2$, and of material $c_3$.

## Notes

From {snime} {cutci}. Like its English gloss, si'ercutci denotes a shoe built to distribute the wearer's weight to keep her/him from sinking into snow.

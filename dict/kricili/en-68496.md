$x_1$ alieves, has alief that $x_2$ (clause); $x_1$ has an automatic or habitual belief-like attitude $x_2$ that is in tension with $x_1$'s explicit beliefs

## Notes

A person reading a book about fiction persons may alieve that they are real, while believing that they are not. See also {krici}, {lifri}

Aukward

Just a heads-up: the genus name of the great auk is "Pinguinus" and, in 
fact, the word "penguin" originally referred to these birds rather than the
Antarctic ones; the scientific names for various taxa of Antarctic penguins
use the element "Sphenisci-". While not many people talk about auks 
nowadays, the introduction of Linnaean names could make this work 
confusing.

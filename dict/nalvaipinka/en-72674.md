$p_1$ is bikeshedding about subject $p_2$ expressed by $p_3$ to audience $p_4$

## Notes

Bikeshedding is the phenomenon of delaying decisions about an important topic in favor of decisions about ancillary topics, e.g. procrastinating decisions about building a house in order to decide the color of the attached bike shed. From {na'e} {vajni} {pinka}.

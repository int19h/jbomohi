$x_1$ is cool/trendy/fashionable/in style/hip/chic/sophisticated-and-socially-desirable to $x_2$ (observer) by standard $x_3$.

## Notes

Collecting wine may be sophisticated, but it is not cool; there is a component of social relevance and desirability to conform. This is not the sense of "cool" which means "impressive, awesome/awe-inspiring, wicked, really good, wonderful (either sense), acceptable (downplaying the intensity of the word)"; for that sense, use: {xamgu} or .{u'enderfu}.

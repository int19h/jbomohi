Change / jump back to previous language; «jo'au lo prula'i».

## Notes

This shortcut jumps back to the previous language set by either {bau'o} or {jo'au}.  This is like ‘cd -’, not ‘popd’; like a jump and not a call in assembly.  (Technically two stacks can be maintained by other mechanisms.).  This might not be a commonly used feature, but like Identity, it allows {zoi'o} to switch back after being terminated by {li'u}.  See also: {jo'au'i} (push current language), {zoi'o} (quote and change language).

$x_1$ (amount of time) is the age of $x_2$.

## Notes

Technically using “lo nanca be li dau mi jbetei” is a better alternative to using “mi nanca li dau”, since this latter seems to imply that the whole duration of the life of the speaker is (exactly) ten years.

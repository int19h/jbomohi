$x_1$ is $x_2$ (li; default: 1) dimensionless units on scale/by standard $x_3$.

## Notes

This word follows the tradition of {grake} and {mitre} etc. except it is for the empty/trivial unit (something having dimensionality of '1'). This is not the same as the dimension itself, which is still {li} {pa}; just as the dimension of a quantity of meters is length/displacement.

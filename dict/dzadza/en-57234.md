$x_1$ (number) is the quantity of specimens in the domain of discourse that satisfy the property $x_2$

## Notes

Predicate equivalent of {da}. It enables rendering a quantifier's scope as a nested bridi level, and to terminate this scope explicitly by closing the sub-bridi. Synonymous with {kaidza}.

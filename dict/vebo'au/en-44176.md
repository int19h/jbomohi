## Notes

A node a A node is a point in/embedded in the medium ({sebo'ai}) that is transmitting the wave/vibrating whereat the signal/wave has minimal intensity (as defined by {tebo'au}). The intensity value of the wave at a node, although minimal by definition, can be more precisely determined by waveform ({tebo'ai}). See also: {boxna}, {bo'au}, {bo'ai}, {sebo'ai}, {tebo'ai}, {tebo'au}.

$x_1$ is a tosmabru word.

## Notes

This is a Lojban-specific concept. A “tosmabru word” is a sequence of characters which looks like a {lujvo}, but it falls apart into several {cmavo} and a {lujvo}/{gismu}. For details see http://mw.lojban.org/papri/tosmabru_test. See also {valslinku'i}. Note: You probably should use {lo'u}/{le'u} or {zoi} to quote $x_1$, as {zo} will probably not work here.

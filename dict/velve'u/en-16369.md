$x_{1}$ is the sales price of $x_{2}$ to $x_{3}$ from/at vendor $x_{4}$.

## Notes

$x_1$ is also the revenue taken in by $x_4$ for $x_2$ from $x_3$ under the condition of a sale (as opposed to ex-nihilo mining); one person's cost is the other person's revenue.

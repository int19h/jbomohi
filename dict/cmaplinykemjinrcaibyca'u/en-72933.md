## Notes

This word is a {brapagjvo} involving "{jinrcaibyca'u}". Technically, this word should only be used when the region may be described as an example of a jinrcaibyca'u; thus, this word does not apply to and cannot be used in order to describe 'clouds' of celestial objects (such as the Oort Cloud) or more complicated regions of space which may exist in star systems with multiple major massive objects (such as binary star systems). It can, however, be used for planetary rings. See also: "{belta}", "{cmaplini}".

$d_1$ desires/wants/wishes for future $d_2$ (event/state) for purpose $d_3$ with desired future point $b_1$. $d_1$ aspires.

## Notes

b1 refers to the point in time when the aspiring individual wants the desire to come true; not the time they expect it to come true.

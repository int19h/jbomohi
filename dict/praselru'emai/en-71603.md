$x_1=m_1=p_2$ are the means of production used/employed in process $x_2=p_1=c_3$ to produce $x_2=p_3=c_2$.

## Notes

Translates the term 'means of production' as it is used in Marxian economics and Marxism. The means of production include the instruments of labor (e.g. milling machinery) and the subjects/object of labor (e.g. grain).

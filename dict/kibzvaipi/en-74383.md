$x_1$ is located at IP address $x_2$.

## Notes

From {kibzva} and the English pronunciation of "IP". IP addresses, both v4 and v6, are read {me'o} + numbers separated by {pi'e}. Use hex digits ({dau}, {fei} ... {vai}), not letters, for IPv6.

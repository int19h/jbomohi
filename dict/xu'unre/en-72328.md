$x_1$ is to the red of $x_2$; of a wave: $x_1$ (wave, color, etc.) has greater wavelength/lesser frequency than $x_2$ (wave, color, etc.) in medium/under conditions/in frame of reference $x_3$.

## Notes

Infrared is to the red of red and all human-visible colors; red is to the red of orange; orange is to the red of violet; violet is to the red of ultraviolet. Not confined to just colors or electromagnetic radiation: low-pitched musical notes are to the red of high-pitched musical notes. See: "{zi'irpu}". Could be useful for referencing the concept of redshift, or appropriate Doppler shift.

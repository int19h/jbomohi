$x1$ is (n) more than $x2$ in metric $x3$ [ni, default cardinality]

## Notes

I.e. $|x2| + n = |x1|$ where $x3$ determines how to measure $|x|$.  Practically, this can be used, e.g. «djica tu'a le pa zau'ei be lei titla» for "I want one more candy". See also: {mei'ai}, {ze'au}, {ja'au} Etymology: {za'u}+{mei} (note that za'u ny. mei means something different).

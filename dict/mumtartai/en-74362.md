$x_1$ is a five-pointed star; $x_1$ is a pentagram.

## Notes

More pedantically, {mumjiptartai} 'five-point-star-shape'. Words for n-pointed stars can be formed with prefix numbers + -tartai, so {xavytartai} 'six-pointed star' (for ex. the star of David), or {bivytartai} for an eight-pointed one, and so on.

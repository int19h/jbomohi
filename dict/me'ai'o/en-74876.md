foreign quoted selbri while changing to another language.

## Notes

This is {zoi'o} but for selbri.  It works as «me'au la'e zoi'o».  See the notes in {zoi'o} for the language-switch style of foreign quotes.  See also: {me'a'o}, {me'oi}.

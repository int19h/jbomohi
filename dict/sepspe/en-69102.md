$x_1$ is a separated/distanced/estranged spouse of absent partner $x_2$, separated by situation/barrier/interval $x_3$, and married by law/custom $x_4$.

## Notes

This is somewhat weaker of a condition than widowhood and a less official or strong separation than divorce.

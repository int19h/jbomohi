$x_1$ is a metathesized form of $x_2$; $x_1$ exhibits metathesis from original form $x_2$.

## Notes

Itself metathesized from *metafesi > *metfasi > meftasi. Example: zo'oi third noi glico cu meftasi zo'oi þridda noi laldygli 'English third is metathesized from Old English þridda'

$x_1$ (record, movie) has the dimension $x_2$ (ka)

## Notes

$x_1$ can be {vreji}, {skina}, {draci}. $x_2$ can specify length in time, space dimensions of its frames ({kantu}). See also {tcidu}, {skicu}, {zgana}, {ze'a}, {temci}, {canlu}.

$x_{1}$ is the class of structure word $x_{2}$, which means or has function $x_{3}$ in language $x_{4}$.

## Notes

For example, the selma'o (class) of particles {pu}, {ca} and {ba} is PU. $x_2$ can, for some speakers, take not just cmavo but any word. See also {ma'oi}.

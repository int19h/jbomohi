$c_{1}$ (quoted word(s)) is a/the (proper) name identifying an individual person [not necessarily human] $c_{2}$=$p_{1}$ to/used-by namer/name-user $c_{3}$ (person).

## Notes

Personal name usually today comprises a given name bestowed at birth or at a young age plus a surname, but may vary culturally. Naming convention (=cmene cacyjva). See also {cmene} and {prenu}.

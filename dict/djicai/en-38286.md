$x_1=d_1=c_1$ intensely desires/craves/[needs] $x_2=d_2$ for purpose $x_3=d_3$ as seen/observed/measured by $x_4=c_3$.

## Notes

Composed of {djica}, {carmi}. Purely jvajvo, as opposed to (an old definition of) {caidji}, which is not. The use of {carmi} instead of {mutce} makes the intensity subjective, rather than objective.

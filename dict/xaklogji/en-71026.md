$x_1$ is linear logic, used to deduce/infer $x_2$ (du'u)

## Notes

A substructural logic proposed by Girard where propositions cannot be duplicated or deleted at will; they are like resources that you can and must "use up" (hence {xaksu}). See also {fadylogji}, {zbalogji}, {xakyziflogji}, {ra'arlogji}.

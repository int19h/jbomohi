$d_1=k_1$ is a myriad/at least ten thousands (10000) of $d_2=k_2$ in dimension/aspect $d_3=k_3$ (default is units).

## Notes

'Myriades' in classical Greek is similar to the use of 萬 or 万 in East Asian languages, it can also be used generically to denote any "numberless", "countless", or "infinite" large quantity.

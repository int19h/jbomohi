> Don't we need another place for the referents? i.e. "x1 is/are the x2

Well, retrieving the full collection of the UD members satisfying the
quantified property isn't the goal of counting quantifiers, those merely
perform a count much like {kancu} does.

If you want to gather all the UD members having a specific property P, you
can go one of the two following ways:
• lo selcmi be ro ckaji be P be'o .e no na ckaji be P
• lo se steci be P bei [current-UD]

(I don't know how to refer to the current UD in Lojban, but "lo dei smuni
se munje" might fit the purpose.)

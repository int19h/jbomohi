$x_1$ is the sensory media content of type/stimulating sense/containing experience information $x_2$ of recording/broadcast/media $x_3$ displayed/produced by medium/device $x_4$.

## Notes

Generalizes/encompasses both of {vidvi} and {snavi} (where $x_2$ is visual/video or audio, respectively), so-called '4D' experiences (which have jostling/kinetoceptive and olfactory stimuli), the feelies from Huxley's /Brave New World/, and other stuff. See also {tivni}, {cradi}, {skina}, {zgike}. Proposed rafsi: -gav-.

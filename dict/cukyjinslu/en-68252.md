$x_1$ is a sphincter (circular-annulic muscle)/muscular ring controlling $x_2$ [approximately: in orifice $x_2$] of/belonging to body $x_3$

## Notes

Sphincters usually must be relaxed in order to allow passage of substance, normally maintaining constriction. But this quality is not necessary for this definition (although it is hard to imagine a reverse scenario).

$x_1=m_1=c_3$ is romantic to $x_2=m_2$; $x_1$ is beautiful to $x_2$ because it follows or is based on some emotional ideal that may not actually reflect reality.

## Notes

Not "romantic" in the sense of "pertaining to intimate love" (={cinpamra'a}); rather, "romantic" in the sense of viewing emotions as a higher and more important quality with extraordinary properties. See also {cinmo}, {melbi}.

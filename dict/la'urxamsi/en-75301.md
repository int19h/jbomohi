$x_{1}$ is a lagoon of location $x_{2}$, of fluid $x_{3}$.

## Notes

A lagoon is a shallow body of water separated from a larger body of water by a narrow landform. Hence a lagoon is not a lake, but a sea. See also {daplu}, {djacu}, {lalxu}, {rirxe}, {tumla}, {zbani}, {ctaru}.

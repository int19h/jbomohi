$x_1$ is the shin/calf (body-part) of $x_2$.

## Notes

This is a whole region of the leg (between knee and ankle; the foot is not included as part of the 'leg' in this case), not any given structure in this region (such as the tibia or gastrocnemius). See also: {xadni'arno}.

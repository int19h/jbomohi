$x_1$ (possibly indirectly) cites $x_2$, via intermediate citation path $x_3$ (ordered list; ce'o), for information/statement $x_4$, such that said $x_2$ is the ultimate source (along path $x_3$) for said information/statement $x_4$.

## Notes

This word is equivalent to "{sity'utka}" but with the additional requirement that $x_2$ is the final 'link' in the citation chain described by $x_3$ (with $x_1$ and $x_2$ appended to their respective sides). This word is a {brapagjvo} with {zevla} ".{utkaro}".

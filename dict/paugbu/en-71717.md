$x_1$ (plural) each satisfying $x_2$ (ka), one by one, is how $x_3$ (nu) is brought about.

## Notes

Defined so that "{fi'o} {paugbu} ..." = "one ... at a time". ($x_3$ should describe the "gestalt action" formed by all the individual actions of $x_2$ being done to the $x_1$s one at a time.) See {pau'u}.

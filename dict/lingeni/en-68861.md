$x_1$ has [self-proclaimed] gender $x_2$ (property of $x_1$)

## Notes

$x_1$ identifies their social gender as $x_2$ [a man, a woman, non-binary (i.e. neither, but still exhibits gendered behaviour), agender (opts out of gender)]. Gender here denotes a social role loosely defined as a combination of how one relates to others socially/societally and how one presents oneself. Gender here is not determined by chromosomal makeup, genital configuration, or sexual/romantic orientation of $x_1$. $x_2$ can contain such verbs as {nanmu} - 'male', {ninmu} - 'female', {mlini'u} - 'demi-girl' (only loosely feminine), {mlinau} - 'demi-guy' (only loosely masculine), {cenba} - 'genderfluid', genderqueer, {nunmu} = 'non-binary' and 'agender'. See also: {tracinse}.

$x_{1}$ is flexible/supple/limber/bendable/non-resistant in direction $x_{2}$ against force $x_{3}$ under conditions $x_{4}$.

## Notes

Not limited to physical forces; e.g. mental flexibility. Synonym of {toltinsa}. See also {tinsa}, {ranti}, {bapli}, {jdari}, {frili}, {nandu}, {torni}, {trati}, {xarnu}, {danre}, {stodi}, {krobi'o}, {polje}.

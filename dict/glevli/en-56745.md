$x_1=v_1=g_1$ has the power to bring about sexual activity/event $x_2=v_2$ with $x_3=g_2$ under conditions $x_4=v_3$; $x_1$ is sexually powerful in aspect $x_2$

## Notes

Mutual symmetry between g1 and g2 is partially lost since only g1 is necessarily endowed with sexual power (even though the act of sex is mutually symmetric between them). Generalized sexual power (as in: self-empowerment) should have x3 erased or {gleborsezvli}; this word implies have sexual power over another (upon/over whom some sort of sexual power may be exercised).

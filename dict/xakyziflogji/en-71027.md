$x_1$ is affine logic, used to deduce/infer $x_2$ (du'u)

## Notes

Linear logic ({xaklogji}) with weakening. (Here, you are {xaksu} {zifre}: you don't *have* to "use up" propositions, you are allowed to just get rid of them. But you still can't duplicate them.) See also {fadylogji}, {zbalogji}, {ra'arlogji}.

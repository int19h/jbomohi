krinu

Justification.

{su'ai} is a powerful tool that shows reciprocity of members between two
places. However, it changes the place structure which might be hard to use
(remember that tavla4 turns into tavla3 etc.) This is of course fine since
this is what we need: a new brivla, a zi'o-like behavior. Thus "su'ai
tavla" becomes very similar to "casnu".

But what if we both want a reciprocal kansa/penmi/tavla and a
non-reciprocal one?

{su'ei} doesn't change the place structure but isn't gonna work with more
than two members of a mass.

Here is another solution: {zu'ai} that is KOhA7 and is similar to {lo
nei}:

"ca'e re do cinba zu'ai" - "now you two kiss each other!"

"ca'e re do cinba lo nei" - "now you two kiss each other and kiss
yourselves too!"

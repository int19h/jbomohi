$x_{1}$ is exactly/approximately $1/e^{(x_2)}$-th  of $x_3$ by standard $x_4$, where e is the natural exponential base; $x_1$ is $x_2$ [li; default: 1] e-fold decreases in $x_3$; $x_1$ is a portion of $x_3$ apportioned by a factor of the $(x_2)$-th power of 1/e

## Notes

If $x_2$ is positive, then the result is an e-fold decrease occurring that many times; if $x_2$ is negative, then the result is an e-fold increase occurring that many times. Modeled on "{xadba}". See also: "{xonba}" (this word is equivalent to "xonba be li {te'o} {te'a} $x_2$").

$x_1=skuP_1$ says $x_2=skuP_2=skuZ_2$ to $x_3=skuP_3$ via medium $x_4=skuP_4$, quoting $x_5=skuZ_1$ saying that to $x_6=skuZ_3$ via medium $x_7=skuZ_4$.

## Notes

skuZ is used for the left ({zunle}) {cusku}, and skuP for the right ({pritu}) one. Syn. {sitsku}, but a bit more precise (and with more places to use).

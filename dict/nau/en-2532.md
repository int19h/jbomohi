When used as a selbri tense, places the selbri event at the space and time of the speaker, rather than at some time and place relative to the context. In particular, it cancels out the presumption of 'story time', which remains intact with ca. When used as a sumti tcita, it introduces a description of the physical here-and-now of the speaker.

## Notes

Before 1991, was used to signal a reversion to default case shift for lerfu.

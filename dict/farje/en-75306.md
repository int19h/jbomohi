$x_1$ ferments [undergoes fermentation] with fermenting agent $x_2$.

## Notes

Proposed combining form -faj-. For agentive '$x_1$ ferments substance $x_2$', use {farjygau} (or even shorter, {fajgau}). This word was made to split the semantic spaces of 'ferment' ({farje}) and 'rot' ({fusra}); {kukfusra} 'delicious-rot' for 'ferment', while technically correct, is silly.

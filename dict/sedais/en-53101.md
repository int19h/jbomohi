Sedai (honorific/title/name/rank/organization)

## Notes

Can be appended to the end of a name. It is also the second part of the name/word "Aes Sedai", which is Lojbanized in full as ".aiz. sedais.". The meaning of "Aes Sedai" and its domain of application varies over the course of the series and over the course of in-universe history; this word can take any such meaning, based on context.

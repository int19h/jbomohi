$x_1$ is a glottal stop sound produced by $x_2$.

## Notes

Derivative of and synonymous with {denpa bu zei sance}; also synonymous with {deplersna}. The odd morphological form echoes that of {asnce}, {basnce} {casnce}, etc., where it serves to foreground the sounds that these words represent while preserving some material from {sance}.

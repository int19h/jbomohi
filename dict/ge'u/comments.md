## 2013-09-24T10:36:17Z — gusnikantu (comment 792)

removed "modal" from the definition

The English/Russian/Japanese definitions included an expression "end
relative/modal phrases", but {ge'u} does not terminate modal phrases
according to the formal grammar described in yacc and/or peg. I replaced
this part with an expression "end GOI relative phrases" so that the
definitions agree with the formal grammar.

$x_1$ is whole, entire, complete (has all of its parts in it).

## Notes

A more specialized version of {mulno}. A finished jigsaw puzzle is {pagmu'o}, as is the full moon (you can see the whole object, and not just a part of it). Also covers 'intact' [but not necessarily].

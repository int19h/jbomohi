$x_1$ is an angle that measures to be $x_2$ (li; default: 1) local angular pseudo-units by standard $x_3$, with subunits $x_4$

## Notes

Use for degrees, minutes, seconds, gradians, etc., where these measure angles or rotations. Full rotations might be such a unit in some situations, but that is a fairly natural unit and might warrant a separate word (possibly a lujvo with {radno} as a veljvo).
